Oven to air fryer conversion

The air fryer temperature and time for any oven recipe — fresh or frozen.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an oven recipe to an air fryer?

For fresh food, set the air fryer 20°C lower than the oven temperature in the recipe and cut the time by 20–25%: 200°C for 30 minutes becomes 180°C for 22–24 minutes. The same starting point works whether the recipe gives a conventional or a fan temperature.

What about frozen food?

Keep the temperature the same and cook for about half the time — 200°C for 20 minutes becomes 200°C for about 10 minutes — then check. Frozen food's own packet instructions for an air fryer come first if it has them.

Why do air fryers cook faster?

An air fryer is a small, powerful fan oven: the hot air moves fast around a small space, so food cooks and browns more quickly than in a full-size oven.

How do I know it's cooked?

Check it early, shake or turn it halfway through, and make sure it's piping hot all the way through before serving. A food thermometer in the thickest part is the surest check for meat and poultry.

Sources: Which?, Instant Pot UK, Liana's Kitchen. Ovens vary, so check your oven's manual — and check food is piping hot all the way through before serving.
