210°C oven in an air fryer

An oven recipe at 210°C cooks at about 190°C in an air fryer, for 20–25% less time.

Air fryer temperature190°C
Air fryer time22–24 minutes

Fresh food: 20°C lower and 20–25% less time. Frozen food: the same temperature for about half the time. Check early, shake or turn halfway, and make sure food is piping hot all the way through.

Frequently asked questions

What is 210°C in an air fryer?

About 190°C for fresh food, with 20–25% less time than the oven: 210°C for 30 minutes becomes 190°C for 22–24 minutes.

What if the recipe says 210°C fan?

Use the same starting point — 190°C — and check the food a little early.

What about frozen food at 210°C?

Keep it at 210°C and cook for about half the oven time, then check it's piping hot all the way through.

Sources: Which?, Instant Pot UK, Liana's Kitchen. Ovens vary, so check your oven's manual — and check food is piping hot all the way through before serving.
