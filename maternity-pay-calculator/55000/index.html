Statutory Maternity Pay on £55,000

A £55,000 salary is about £1,057.69 a week (salary ÷ 52). For the first 6 weeks you get 90% of that, £951.92 a week (£5,711.54 in all). For the next 33 weeks you get the £194.32 weekly rate, because 90% of your pay is more than that: £6,412.56. That's £12,124.10 over 39 weeks, before tax and National Insurance: about 29% of the £41,250.00 your salary would pay over the same 39 weeks.

Your employer may pay more under a company maternity scheme. Your actual average weekly earnings come from your pay in the 8 weeks (if paid weekly) or 2 months (if paid monthly) up to the last payday before the 15th week before the baby is due, so overtime or a pay rise then can change them. Above about £11,227 a year (£215.91 a week), weeks 7 to 39 are paid at the £194.32 rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much maternity pay will I get on £55,000?

About £951.92 a week for the first 6 weeks, then £194.32 a week for 33 weeks: £12,124.10 in total before tax (2026/27).

How much maternity pay a month on £55,000?

For the first 6 weeks it's the same as about £4,125.00 a month, then about £842.05 a month for weeks 7 to 39 (the weekly amounts × 52 ÷ 12), before tax.

Is maternity pay taxed?

Yes — tax and National Insurance are deducted from SMP in the usual way.

Can my employer pay more than SMP?

Yes. Your employer may pay more under a company maternity scheme (enhanced maternity pay).
