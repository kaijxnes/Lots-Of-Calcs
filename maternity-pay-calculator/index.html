Before tax and National Insurance, which come off SMP as they do from pay. To qualify you must earn on average at least £129 a week and have worked for your employer continuously for at least 26 weeks into the 15th week before the baby is due. Your employer may pay more (enhanced maternity pay). 2026/27 figures from GOV.UK (Maternity pay), checked 2026-11-21. A guide, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Statutory Maternity Pay?

90% of your average weekly earnings (before tax) for the first 6 weeks, then £194.32 or 90% of your average weekly earnings, whichever is lower, for the next 33 weeks.

How are average weekly earnings worked out?

From your pay in the 8 weeks (if paid weekly) or 2 months (if paid monthly) up to the last payday before the 15th week before the baby is due. Entering an annual salary here divides it by 52 as an estimate.

Is maternity pay taxed?

Yes — tax and National Insurance are deducted from SMP in the usual way.

What about paternity pay?

Statutory Paternity Pay is £194.32 a week or 90% of average weekly earnings, whichever is lower.
