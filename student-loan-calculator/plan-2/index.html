Frequently asked questions

What is the Plan 2 repayment threshold?

£29,385 a year for 2026/27 — about £2,449 a month. You repay 9% of anything you earn above it.

How much is the Plan 2 repayment on £40,000?

£79.61 a month, or £955 a year: 9% of £10,615 over the threshold.

What interest is charged on Plan 2?

4.1% to 6% depending on income: 4.1% up to £29,385, rising steadily to 6% at £52,885 and above, as GOV.UK lists it now. Rates are reset each September.

When is Plan 2 written off?

30 years after the April you were first due to repay. Anything still owed then is cancelled.

Who is on Plan 2?

English and Welsh students who started between September 2012 and July 2023.
