How the conversions work

Length, weight and volume are converted through a common base unit — metres, kilograms or litres. Your value is multiplied into the base unit and then divided into the unit you want, using the exact international definitions: an inch is exactly 2.54 cm, a pound exactly 0.45359237 kg, and a UK gallon exactly 4.54609 litres.

Temperature needs a formula rather than a multiplication, because the scales start from different zeros. To go from Celsius to Fahrenheit, multiply by 9, divide by 5 and add 32; to go back, take away 32, multiply by 5 and divide by 9. Kelvin is Celsius plus 273.15.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32. So 20C is 68F. Going the other way, subtract 32 first, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9.

How many kilometres is a mile?

One mile is about 1.609 kilometres, so a kilometre is roughly 0.621 miles. A quick mental estimate is to multiply miles by 1.6.

How many pounds are in a stone?

Fourteen. A stone is 14 pounds, or 6.35 kg, so 11st 7lb is 161 lb or 73.0 kg. Choose Stone in the weight tab to convert directly.

Is a UK pint the same as a US pint?

No. A UK (imperial) pint is 568 ml, about 20% more than a US pint of 473 ml. Gallons differ in the same way: 4.546 litres in the UK against 3.785 litres in the US.

How tall is 5 foot 10 in centimetres?

177.8 cm. Multiply the feet by 12 and add the inches to get inches (70), then multiply by 2.54.

How do I convert cups to millilitres?

A US cup, as used in most recipes online, is about 237 ml. Australian and some UK recipes use a 250 ml metric cup. For baking, weighing in grams is more reliable than cups, since a cup of flour can vary by 20 grams or more depending on how it's packed.
