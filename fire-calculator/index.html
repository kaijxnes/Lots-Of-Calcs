Frequently asked questions

What is FIRE?

Financial independence, retire early: saving and investing enough that the investments can pay for your spending for the rest of your life, so work becomes optional.

How much do I need to retire early?

A common rule of thumb is 25 times your yearly spending — the "4% rule". Spending £30,000 a year, that's £750,000. A lower withdrawal rate (3% to 3.5%) is safer for a retirement that may last 40 years or more.

Where does the 4% rule come from?

From US research in the 1990s on how much could be withdrawn from a stock and bond portfolio, rising with inflation, without running out over 30 years in past markets. It's a rough guide, not a guarantee, and early retirements usually need to last longer than 30 years.

What return should I use?

The return after inflation, because spending is in today's money. No rate is guaranteed and markets go through long bad patches, so it's worth trying a cautious figure as well as your central one.

Does this include the state pension?

No. A state or workplace pension later on reduces how much your own investments need to cover from that age, so the real figure may be lower.
