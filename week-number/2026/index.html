Frequently asked questions

How many weeks are there in 2026?

53 ISO weeks. Week 1 starts on Monday 29 Dec 2025 and week 53 ends on Sunday 3 Jan 2027.

How are week numbers counted?

By the ISO 8601 standard used across Europe and in most business software: weeks run Monday to Sunday, and week 1 is the week containing the year's first Thursday (the week with 4 January in it). So a few days at the start or end of January can belong to the previous or next year's weeks.

Why does my calendar show a different week number?

Some calendars and spreadsheets count week 1 as the week containing 1 January, starting on Sunday — Excel's WEEKNUM does this by default, while ISOWEEKNUM gives the ISO number. The two can differ by one.
