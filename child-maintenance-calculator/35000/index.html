Frequently asked questions

How much child maintenance do you pay on £35,000?

About £80.55 a week (£349.05 a month) for 1 child, £107.40 for 2 children and £127.53 for 3 or more, at the basic rate. £35,000 a year is £671.23 a week (÷ 365 × 7). That's before any shared care or other children living with you.

What if I have another child living with me?

One other child takes 11% off the income counted, so for 1 child it's £71.69 a week instead of £80.55.

How much less is it if the child stays over?

With 104 to 155 nights a year, two-sevenths comes off: £57.54 a week for 1 child. At 175 nights or more it's halved and £7 more comes off per child: £33.27.

Does pension come off my income?

Contributions taken off through the payroll before tax are already left out of the HMRC figure. Others, such as personal pension payments, can be taken off if you ask and give the details.
