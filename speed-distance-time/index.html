Frequently asked questions

What is the formula for speed, distance and time?

Speed = distance ÷ time; distance = speed × time; time = distance ÷ speed. 150 miles at 60 mph takes 2.5 hours — 2 hours 30 minutes.

How do I convert mph to km/h?

Multiply by 1.609344 (a mile is exactly 1,609.344 metres): 70 mph is 112.7 km/h. To go back, divide.

How long does it take to drive 100 miles?

At an average of 50 mph, 2 hours; at 60 mph, 1 hour 40 minutes. Real journeys average less than the speed limit once junctions, traffic and stops are included.

What is a knot?

One nautical mile (exactly 1,852 metres) an hour — about 1.15 mph.
