Benefit payment dates October 2036

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in October 2036, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2036 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in October 2036

Universal Credit due on the 4th and 5th: paid Friday 3 October; due on the 11th and 12th: paid Friday 10 October; due on the 18th and 19th: paid Friday 17 October; due on the 25th and 26th: paid Friday 24 October. (England and Wales.)

State Pension, PIP and other 4-weekly benefits

These are usually paid every 4 weeks on the same weekday, so they only move if that day is a bank holiday. There are no weekday bank holidays in October 2036.

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in October 2036?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In October 2036 (England and Wales): due on the 4th and 5th: paid Friday 3 October; due on the 11th and 12th: paid Friday 10 October; due on the 18th and 19th: paid Friday 17 October; due on the 25th and 26th: paid Friday 24 October.

Are benefits paid early in October 2036?

Only for weekends: there are no weekday bank holidays in October 2036 in England and Wales, so a payment due on a Saturday or Sunday is paid on the Friday before.

When is the State Pension paid in October 2036?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). No weekday in October 2036 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so it should arrive on your usual day.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
