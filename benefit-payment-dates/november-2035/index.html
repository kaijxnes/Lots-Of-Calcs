Benefit payment dates November 2035

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in November 2035, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2035 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in November 2035

Universal Credit due on the 3rd and 4th: paid Friday 2 November; due on the 10th and 11th: paid Friday 9 November; due on the 17th and 18th: paid Friday 16 November; due on the 24th and 25th: paid Friday 23 November. (England and Wales.)

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in November 2035?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In November 2035 (England and Wales): due on the 3rd and 4th: paid Friday 2 November; due on the 10th and 11th: paid Friday 9 November; due on the 17th and 18th: paid Friday 16 November; due on the 24th and 25th: paid Friday 23 November.

Are benefits paid early in November 2035?

Only for weekends: there are no weekday bank holidays in November 2035 in England and Wales, so a payment due on a Saturday or Sunday is paid on the Friday before.

When is the State Pension paid in November 2035?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). No weekday in November 2035 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so it should arrive on your usual day.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
