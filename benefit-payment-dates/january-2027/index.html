Benefit payment dates January 2027

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in January 2027, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Paid early in January 2027

Universal Credit due on the 1st, 2nd and 3rd: paid Thursday 31 December 2026; due on the 9th and 10th: paid Friday 8 January; due on the 16th and 17th: paid Friday 15 January; due on the 23rd and 24th: paid Friday 22 January; due on the 30th and 31st: paid Friday 29 January. (England and Wales.)

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in January 2027?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In January 2027 (England and Wales): due on the 1st, 2nd and 3rd: paid Thursday 31 December 2026; due on the 9th and 10th: paid Friday 8 January; due on the 16th and 17th: paid Friday 15 January; due on the 23rd and 24th: paid Friday 22 January; due on the 30th and 31st: paid Friday 29 January.

Are benefits paid early in January 2027?

Yes, around New Year's Day on Friday 1 January: a payment due that day is paid on Thursday 31 December 2026.

When is the State Pension paid in January 2027?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). If your payment falls on Friday 1 January, you might be paid earlier.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
