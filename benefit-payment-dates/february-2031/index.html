Benefit payment dates February 2031

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in February 2031, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2031 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in February 2031

Universal Credit due on the 1st and 2nd: paid Friday 31 January; due on the 8th and 9th: paid Friday 7 February; due on the 15th and 16th: paid Friday 14 February; due on the 22nd and 23rd: paid Friday 21 February. (England and Wales.)

State Pension, PIP and other 4-weekly benefits

These are usually paid every 4 weeks on the same weekday, so they only move if that day is a bank holiday. There are no weekday bank holidays in February 2031.

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in February 2031?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In February 2031 (England and Wales): due on the 1st and 2nd: paid Friday 31 January; due on the 8th and 9th: paid Friday 7 February; due on the 15th and 16th: paid Friday 14 February; due on the 22nd and 23rd: paid Friday 21 February.

Are benefits paid early in February 2031?

Only for weekends: there are no weekday bank holidays in February 2031 in England and Wales, so a payment due on a Saturday or Sunday is paid on the Friday before.

When is the State Pension paid in February 2031?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). No weekday in February 2031 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so it should arrive on your usual day.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
