The rule: if your payment date is on a weekend or a bank holiday, you'll usually be paid on the working day before. Your State Pension day depends on the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number:

Last 2 digits of your National Insurance numberPaid on a
00 to 19Monday
20 to 39Tuesday
40 to 59Wednesday
60 to 79Thursday
80 to 99Friday
