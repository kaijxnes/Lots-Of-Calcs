Benefit payment dates April 2036

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in April 2036, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2036 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in April 2036

Universal Credit due on the 5th and 6th: paid Friday 4 April; due on the 11th, 12th, 13th and 14th: paid Thursday 10 April; due on the 19th and 20th: paid Friday 18 April; due on the 26th and 27th: paid Friday 25 April. (England and Wales.)

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in April 2036?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In April 2036 (England and Wales): due on the 5th and 6th: paid Friday 4 April; due on the 11th, 12th, 13th and 14th: paid Thursday 10 April; due on the 19th and 20th: paid Friday 18 April; due on the 26th and 27th: paid Friday 25 April.

Are benefits paid early in April 2036?

Yes, around Good Friday on Friday 11 April and Easter Monday on Monday 14 April: a payment due that day is paid on Thursday 10 April and Thursday 10 April.

When is the State Pension paid in April 2036?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). If your payment falls on Friday 11 April and Monday 14 April, you might be paid earlier.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
