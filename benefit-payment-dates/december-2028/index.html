Benefit payment dates December 2028

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in December 2028, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in December 2028?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In December 2028 (England and Wales): due on the 2nd and 3rd: paid Friday 1 December; due on the 9th and 10th: paid Friday 8 December; due on the 16th and 17th: paid Friday 15 December; due on the 23rd, 24th, 25th and 26th: paid Friday 22 December; due on the 30th and 31st: paid Friday 29 December.

Are benefits paid early in December 2028?

Yes, around Christmas Day on Monday 25 December and Boxing Day on Tuesday 26 December: a payment due that day is paid on Friday 22 December and Friday 22 December.

When is the State Pension paid in December 2028?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). If your payment falls on Monday 25 December and Tuesday 26 December, you might be paid earlier.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
