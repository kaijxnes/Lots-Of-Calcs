Benefit payment dates 2028

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid early in 2028 because a payment falls on a bank holiday.

The rule

If your payment date is on a weekend or a bank holiday, you'll usually be paid on the working day before. Universal Credit is paid on the same date each month; most other benefits are paid every 2 or 4 weeks on the same weekday. Child Benefit may be different.

So a weekend payment arrives on the Friday, and a bank holiday payment on the last working day before it: the Thursday before Good Friday, the Friday before a bank holiday Monday.

State Pension and other 4-weekly benefits

The State Pension, Pension Credit, PIP, Attendance Allowance and Disability Living Allowance are usually paid every 4 weeks on the same weekday, so they only move when that weekday is a bank holiday: see the dates above. Your State Pension day depends on your National Insurance number:

Frequently asked questions

When are benefits paid early in 2028?

When a payment is due on a bank holiday, it's usually paid on the working day before. In England and Wales in 2028 that's 3 January (New Year's Day), 14 April (Good Friday), 17 April (Easter Monday), 1 May (Early May bank holiday), 29 May (Spring bank holiday), 28 August (Summer bank holiday), 25 December (Christmas Day) and 26 December (Boxing Day).

When is Universal Credit paid at Christmas 2028?

A payment due on Christmas Day, Monday 25 December, is paid on Friday 22 December, the working day before. The Christmas 2028 payment dates page goes day by day.

What day is my State Pension paid?

It depends on the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number: 00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday, usually every 4 weeks. If that day is a bank holiday you might be paid earlier.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
