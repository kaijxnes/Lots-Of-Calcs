Benefit payment dates November 2032

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in November 2032, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2032 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in November 2032

Universal Credit due on the 6th and 7th: paid Friday 5 November; due on the 13th and 14th: paid Friday 12 November; due on the 20th and 21st: paid Friday 19 November; due on the 27th and 28th: paid Friday 26 November. (England and Wales.)

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in November 2032?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In November 2032 (England and Wales): due on the 6th and 7th: paid Friday 5 November; due on the 13th and 14th: paid Friday 12 November; due on the 20th and 21st: paid Friday 19 November; due on the 27th and 28th: paid Friday 26 November.

Are benefits paid early in November 2032?

Only for weekends: there are no weekday bank holidays in November 2032 in England and Wales, so a payment due on a Saturday or Sunday is paid on the Friday before.

When is the State Pension paid in November 2032?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). No weekday in November 2032 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so it should arrive on your usual day.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
