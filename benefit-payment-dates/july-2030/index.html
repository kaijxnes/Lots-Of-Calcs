Benefit payment dates July 2030

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in July 2030, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2030 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in July 2030

Universal Credit due on the 6th and 7th: paid Friday 5 July; due on the 13th and 14th: paid Friday 12 July; due on the 20th and 21st: paid Friday 19 July; due on the 27th and 28th: paid Friday 26 July. (England and Wales.)

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in July 2030?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In July 2030 (England and Wales): due on the 6th and 7th: paid Friday 5 July; due on the 13th and 14th: paid Friday 12 July; due on the 20th and 21st: paid Friday 19 July; due on the 27th and 28th: paid Friday 26 July.

Are benefits paid early in July 2030?

Only for weekends: there are no weekday bank holidays in July 2030 in England and Wales, so a payment due on a Saturday or Sunday is paid on the Friday before.

When is the State Pension paid in July 2030?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). No weekday in July 2030 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so it should arrive on your usual day.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
