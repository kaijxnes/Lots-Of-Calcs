Benefit payment dates July 2029

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in July 2029, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2029 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in July 2029

Universal Credit due on the 1st: paid Friday 29 June; due on the 7th and 8th: paid Friday 6 July; due on the 14th and 15th: paid Friday 13 July; due on the 21st and 22nd: paid Friday 20 July; due on the 28th and 29th: paid Friday 27 July. (England and Wales.)

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in July 2029?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In July 2029 (England and Wales): due on the 1st: paid Friday 29 June; due on the 7th and 8th: paid Friday 6 July; due on the 14th and 15th: paid Friday 13 July; due on the 21st and 22nd: paid Friday 20 July; due on the 28th and 29th: paid Friday 27 July.

Are benefits paid early in July 2029?

Only for weekends: there are no weekday bank holidays in July 2029 in England and Wales, so a payment due on a Saturday or Sunday is paid on the Friday before.

When is the State Pension paid in July 2029?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). No weekday in July 2029 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so it should arrive on your usual day.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
