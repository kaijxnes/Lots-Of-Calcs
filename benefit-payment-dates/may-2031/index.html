Benefit payment dates May 2031

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in May 2031, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2031 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Paid early in May 2031

Universal Credit due on the 3rd, 4th and 5th: paid Friday 2 May; due on the 10th and 11th: paid Friday 9 May; due on the 17th and 18th: paid Friday 16 May; due on the 24th, 25th and 26th: paid Friday 23 May; due on the 31st: paid Friday 30 May. (England and Wales.)

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in May 2031?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In May 2031 (England and Wales): due on the 3rd, 4th and 5th: paid Friday 2 May; due on the 10th and 11th: paid Friday 9 May; due on the 17th and 18th: paid Friday 16 May; due on the 24th, 25th and 26th: paid Friday 23 May; due on the 31st: paid Friday 30 May.

Are benefits paid early in May 2031?

Yes, around Early May bank holiday on Monday 5 May and Spring bank holiday on Monday 26 May: a payment due that day is paid on Friday 2 May and Friday 23 May.

When is the State Pension paid in May 2031?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). If your payment falls on Monday 5 May and Monday 26 May, you might be paid earlier.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
