Christmas 2029 benefit payment dates

When benefits and pensions due over Christmas 2029 and New Year are paid: on the working day before each bank holiday and weekend.

Expected dates. GOV.UK hasn't published the bank holidays for 2029 yet, so these use the expected dates from the usual pattern; they'll change to the official list when it's out.

Making it last

An early payment isn't extra money: the next one comes on its usual date. If yours arrives on Monday 24 December, it has to cover the extra days until your next payment, so it's worth planning for the gap in January.

Frequently asked questions

When will I get my benefits at Christmas 2029?

A payment due on Christmas Day (Tuesday 25 December) or Boxing Day (Wednesday 26 December) is usually paid on Monday 24 December. One due on New Year's Day (Tuesday 1 January 2030) is paid on Monday 31 December 2029.

Is Universal Credit paid early at Christmas 2029?

Yes, if your payment date falls on a bank holiday or weekend over Christmas: it's paid on the working day before. The table shows the paid date for every day from 18 December to 5 January.

What are the bank holidays over Christmas 2029?

Tuesday 25 December (Christmas Day), Wednesday 26 December (Boxing Day) and Tuesday 1 January (New Year's Day) in England and Wales. Scotland also has 2 January.

Why does my money arrive early at Christmas?

Because banks don't process payments on bank holidays and weekends, so a payment due then is paid on the last working day before. It's the same money, not an extra payment, so it has to last longer before the next one.
