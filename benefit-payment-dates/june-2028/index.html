Benefit payment dates June 2028

When Universal Credit, the State Pension and other benefits are paid in June 2028, with payments due on a weekend or bank holiday paid on the working day before.

Paid early in June 2028

Universal Credit due on the 3rd and 4th: paid Friday 2 June; due on the 10th and 11th: paid Friday 9 June; due on the 17th and 18th: paid Friday 16 June; due on the 24th and 25th: paid Friday 23 June. (England and Wales.)

State Pension, PIP and other 4-weekly benefits

These are usually paid every 4 weeks on the same weekday, so they only move if that day is a bank holiday. There are no weekday bank holidays in June 2028.

Frequently asked questions

When is Universal Credit paid in June 2028?

On your usual payment date, unless it's a weekend or bank holiday: then it's paid on the working day before. In June 2028 (England and Wales): due on the 3rd and 4th: paid Friday 2 June; due on the 10th and 11th: paid Friday 9 June; due on the 17th and 18th: paid Friday 16 June; due on the 24th and 25th: paid Friday 23 June.

Are benefits paid early in June 2028?

Only for weekends: there are no weekday bank holidays in June 2028 in England and Wales, so a payment due on a Saturday or Sunday is paid on the Friday before.

When is the State Pension paid in June 2028?

Every 4 weeks on the weekday set by the last 2 digits of your National Insurance number (00 to 19 Monday, 20 to 39 Tuesday, 40 to 59 Wednesday, 60 to 79 Thursday, 80 to 99 Friday). No weekday in June 2028 is a bank holiday in England and Wales, so it should arrive on your usual day.

Is Child Benefit paid early for bank holidays?

It's usually paid every 4 weeks on a Monday or Tuesday, and GOV.UK says the weekend and bank holiday rule may be different for Child Benefit, so check your payment notice from HMRC.
