Take-home pay uses each place's 2026/27 rules with its usual defaults (in the UK, no student loan and no employee pension). For those, get the take-home figure from the salary calculator. Employer pension is a percentage of salary.

The example: £35,000 or £38,000?

Offer A pays £35,000 with a 5% employer pension and £120 a month in travel. Offer B pays £38,000 with 3% pension, £320 a month in travel and a 40-hour week instead of 37.5.

After tax and National Insurance the £3,000 gap shrinks to £2,160. Offer A's pension is worth £610 more and its commute costs £2,400 less, so Offer A comes out £850 a year ahead — and £1.34 more per hour worked.

Things that don't show up in the numbers

Holiday allowance, flexible or remote working, bonus history (a "target" bonus isn't guaranteed), share schemes, sick pay, career prospects and how secure the job is. A useful check: how much would you need to be paid to take the job you like less?

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare two job offers?

Compare what each leaves you with, not the headline salary: take-home pay after tax, plus what the employer puts into your pension, plus benefits with a cash value, minus the cost of getting to work. Then look at it per hour, since longer hours or a longer commute eat into a higher salary.

Is a higher salary always better?

No. In the example above, £38,000 sounds better than £35,000, but after tax the extra £3,000 becomes about £2,160; a 3% instead of 5% pension and £200 a month more commuting turn it into £850 a year less.

Why is the employer pension added on top?

Your employer's contribution is money paid for you that you'd otherwise have to save from take-home pay, and it isn't taxed as income now. Your own contribution is part of your salary, so it's already inside the take-home figure if you choose it in the salary calculator.

What counts as other benefits?

Anything with a clear yearly cash value you'd otherwise pay for: a car allowance, private medical insurance, a gym membership, free meals or a season ticket loan's interest saved. Extra holiday is worth roughly a day's pay per day.

Can I compare jobs in different countries?

Yes — pick each place and the tax is worked out under its own rules. The real values come out in each currency; convert one at today's exchange rate and allow for living costs, which can matter more than the tax.
