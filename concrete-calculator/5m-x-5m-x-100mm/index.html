Frequently asked questions

How much concrete do I need for a 5m × 5m slab 100mm deep?

2.5 cubic metres: 5 × 5 × 0.1 = 2.5 m³, or 2,500 litres. Allow a little extra for uneven ground and waste.

How many bags of concrete for a 5m × 5m slab?

Divide 2,500 litres by what one bag makes, which is printed on the bag. The calculator does it when you enter the bag's yield.

What about 150mm deep?

3.75 m³.
