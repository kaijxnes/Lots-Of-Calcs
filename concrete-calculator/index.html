Frequently asked questions

How do I work out how much concrete I need?

Multiply length × width × depth in the same units. For a 3m × 3m slab 100mm deep: 3 × 3 × 0.1 = 0.9 m³. In feet, divide the cubic feet by 27 for cubic yards: a 10 × 10 ft slab 4 inches deep is 33.33 cu ft, or 1.23 yd³.

How do I work out concrete for post holes?

Each hole is a cylinder: π × radius² × depth. A 300mm hole 600mm deep holds about 42 litres; the post itself takes up some of that, so the real figure is a little less.

How many bags of concrete do I need?

Divide the volume by what one bag makes — the bag says, and it varies by product and bag size. Enter it in the calculator.

How much extra should I allow?

Enough to cover uneven ground and spillage; it depends on the job and how level the base is. Add a percentage in the calculator to see the difference.
