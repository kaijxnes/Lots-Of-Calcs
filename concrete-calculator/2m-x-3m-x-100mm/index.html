Frequently asked questions

How much concrete do I need for a 2m × 3m slab 100mm deep?

0.6 cubic metres: 2 × 3 × 0.1 = 0.6 m³, or 600 litres. Allow a little extra for uneven ground and waste.

How many bags of concrete for a 2m × 3m slab?

Divide 600 litres by what one bag makes, which is printed on the bag. The calculator does it when you enter the bag's yield.

What about 150mm deep?

0.9 m³.
