Frequently asked questions

How much concrete do I need for a 12x24 slab 4 inches thick?

3.56 cubic yards: 12 × 24 × 4/12 = 96 cubic feet, ÷ 27 = 3.56 yd³. Ready-mix is ordered by the cubic yard, so round up and allow a little extra.

How many bags of concrete for a 12x24 slab?

Divide 96 cubic feet by what one bag makes, which is printed on the bag. Enter it in the calculator to get the number of bags.

What about 6 inches thick?

5.33 cubic yards (144 cubic feet).
