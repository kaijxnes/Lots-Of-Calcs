Frequently asked questions

How much is stamp duty on £400,000?

£10,000 in England and Northern Ireland if you're moving home: 2% on £125,000, 5% on £150,000. That's 2.5% of the price.

How much stamp duty does a first-time buyer pay on £400,000?

£5,000 in England and Northern Ireland: no tax up to £300,000 and 5% on the part from £300,001 to £500,000. That saves £5,000. In Scotland first-time buyers pay £12,750; Wales has no first-time buyer relief.

What about a second home or buy-to-let at £400,000?

£30,000 in England and Northern Ireland, with 5% added to every band. In Scotland the Additional Dwelling Supplement of 8% of the price makes it £45,350; Wales's higher rates make it £29,950.

When do I pay it?

In England and Northern Ireland the return and payment are due within 14 days of completion; your solicitor or conveyancer normally files it and pays it for you, out of the money you send them. Scotland and Wales use their own taxes with their own returns, also usually handled by the solicitor.

Buying at £400,000

With a 10% deposit you'd borrow about £360,000, which most lenders would want an income of around £80,000 or more for (4.5 times income).
