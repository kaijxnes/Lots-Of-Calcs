Frequently asked questions

What is £60,000 pro rata for 2 days a week?

£24,000 a year: 2 days out of 5 is 40% of the full-time salary. That's £2,000.00 a month before tax.

How much is £60,000 pro rata 2 days a week after tax?

£1,733.30 a month (£20,799.60 a year) in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and £1,736.61 a month in Scotland, with tax code 1257L and no student loan or pension. With a Plan 2 student loan it's £1,733.30 a month.

Why do I take home more than 40% of the full-time pay?

Because the first £12,570 is tax-free and National Insurance starts at the same point, a smaller salary loses a smaller share to tax. Full-time take-home on £60,000 is £3,779.78 a month; 2 days a week keeps 45.9% of it.

Is my hourly rate lower if I work part time?

No. Part-time workers should get the same pay rates as comparable full-time workers, so the hourly rate stays at about £30.77 on a 37.5-hour week.
