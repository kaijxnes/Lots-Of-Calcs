Frequently asked questions

How many watts is 1 amps?

230 W at 230 V and 120 W at 120 V. Multiply the amps by the volts.

How many watts can a 1 amp circuit or fuse take?

Up to 230 W at 230 V in theory. For appliances that run for hours (heaters, chargers) it's sensible to stay well under the limit — US practice, for example, keeps continuous loads to 80% of a breaker's rating, 96 W at 120 V.
