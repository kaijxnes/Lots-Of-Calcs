Frequently asked questions

How many amps is 100 watts?

0.43 A on a 230 V supply (UK and Europe) and 0.83 A on 120 V (US and Canada). Divide the watts by the volts.

How many amps is 100 watts in the UK?

0.43 A at the standard 230 V. Some appliances still quote 240 V, which gives 0.42 A. In the UK that's within a 3 A plug fuse.

Does this apply to every appliance?

It's exact for heaters, kettles, toasters and other resistive loads. Motors, compressors and some electronics draw a little more current than this for the same watts (their power factor is below 1), and many draw extra when they start.
