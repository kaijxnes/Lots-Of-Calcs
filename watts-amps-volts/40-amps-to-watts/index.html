Frequently asked questions

How many watts is 40 amps?

9,200 W at 230 V and 4,800 W at 120 V. Multiply the amps by the volts.

How many watts can a 40 amp circuit or fuse take?

Up to 9,200 W at 230 V in theory. For appliances that run for hours (heaters, chargers) it's sensible to stay well under the limit — US practice, for example, keeps continuous loads to 80% of a breaker's rating, 3,840 W at 120 V.
