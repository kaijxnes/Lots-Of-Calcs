Frequently asked questions

How many watts is 6 amps?

1,380 W at 230 V and 720 W at 120 V. Multiply the amps by the volts.

How many watts can a 6 amp circuit or fuse take?

Up to 1,380 W at 230 V in theory. For appliances that run for hours (heaters, chargers) it's sensible to stay well under the limit — US practice, for example, keeps continuous loads to 80% of a breaker's rating, 576 W at 120 V.
