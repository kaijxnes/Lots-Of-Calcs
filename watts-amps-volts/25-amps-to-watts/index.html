Frequently asked questions

How many watts is 25 amps?

5,750 W at 230 V and 3,000 W at 120 V. Multiply the amps by the volts.

How many watts can a 25 amp circuit or fuse take?

Up to 5,750 W at 230 V in theory. For appliances that run for hours (heaters, chargers) it's sensible to stay well under the limit — US practice, for example, keeps continuous loads to 80% of a breaker's rating, 2,400 W at 120 V.
