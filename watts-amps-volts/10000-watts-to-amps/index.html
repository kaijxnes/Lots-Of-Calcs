Frequently asked questions

How many amps is 10,000 watts?

43.48 A on a 230 V supply (UK and Europe) and 83.33 A on 120 V (US and Canada). Divide the watts by the volts.

How many amps is 10,000 watts in the UK?

43.48 A at the standard 230 V. Some appliances still quote 240 V, which gives 41.67 A. That's well beyond a single domestic socket circuit; at this size an electrician should size the cable and protection.

Does this apply to every appliance?

It's exact for heaters, kettles, toasters and other resistive loads. Motors, compressors and some electronics draw a little more current than this for the same watts (their power factor is below 1), and many draw extra when they start.
