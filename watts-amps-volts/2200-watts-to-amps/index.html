Frequently asked questions

How many amps is 2,200 watts?

9.57 A on a 230 V supply (UK and Europe) and 18.33 A on 120 V (US and Canada). Divide the watts by the volts.

How many amps is 2,200 watts in the UK?

9.57 A at the standard 230 V. Some appliances still quote 240 V, which gives 9.17 A. In the UK it needs a 13 A plug fuse — a standard plug can supply up to 13 A, about 3 kW.

Does this apply to every appliance?

It's exact for heaters, kettles, toasters and other resistive loads. Motors, compressors and some electronics draw a little more current than this for the same watts (their power factor is below 1), and many draw extra when they start.
