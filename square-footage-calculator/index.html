Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate square footage?

Measure the length and width in feet and multiply them: a 12 × 14 ft room is 168 square feet. For a circle, π × radius²; for a triangle, half the base × the height.

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Multiply by 0.0929 (exactly 0.09290304): 100 sq ft is 9.29 m². The other way, multiply square metres by 10.764.

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Split it into two rectangles, work out each, and add them together. Set 'How many of these areas' to add up several the same size.

How many square feet in an acre?

43,560 square feet, or about 4,047 square metres.
