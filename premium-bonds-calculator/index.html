Average vs typical winnings

Premium Bonds don't pay interest. The prize fund rate, 4.35% (variable), is what all holders win on average, but two £1 million prizes and other large ones a month pull that average up. Most of the prize money goes on £25, £50 and £100 prizes, so most holders win less than 4.35%. On £50,000, the average is £2,175 a year but a typical holder wins about £1,900. On £1,000, 56% of holders win nothing in a year. Each £1 Bond has a 21,000 to 1 chance in each monthly draw.

Frequently asked questions

How much will I win on Premium Bonds?

On average 4.35% a year, but most holders win less because big prizes pull the average up. With £50,000 the average is £2,175 and a typical year about £1,900; 8 in 10 holders would win between £1,350 and £2,750.

What are the odds of winning Premium Bonds?

21,000 to 1 for every £1 Bond in each monthly draw. With £50,000 that's about 28.6 prizes a year.

How much do you win with £1,000 in Premium Bonds?

On average £43 a year, but 56% of holders with £1,000 win nothing in a year, since they'd expect about 0.57 prizes.

Are Premium Bonds prizes taxed?

No. NS&I says prizes are free from UK Income Tax and Capital Gains Tax.

How much can I put in Premium Bonds?

From £25 up to £50,000. Anyone 16 or over with a UK bank account can buy them.
