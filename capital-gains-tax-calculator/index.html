Frequently asked questions

What are the capital gains tax rates for 2026/27?

18% on gains that fall within your basic rate band and 24% above it, after the £3,000 tax-free allowance. The same rates apply to residential property and other assets. Business Asset Disposal Relief is 18% from 6 April 2026.

How do I work out my gain?

Sale price minus what you paid, minus allowable costs such as buying and selling fees, stamp duty and the cost of improvements (not repairs or maintenance).

Do I pay capital gains tax on my home?

Usually not on your main home, thanks to Private Residence Relief, as long as it's been your main home throughout. Second homes and buy-to-lets are taxed.
