Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in September 2036?

22 in every part of the UK: 30 days less 8 weekend days.

What is the last working day of September 2036?

Tuesday 30 September 2036 in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. The first working day is Monday 1 September 2036.

How many working hours are in September 2036?

165 hours on a 7.5-hour day or 176 on an 8-hour day (22 days, England and Wales).

Are there any bank holidays in September 2036?

No — there are no weekday bank holidays in September 2036 anywhere in the UK.
