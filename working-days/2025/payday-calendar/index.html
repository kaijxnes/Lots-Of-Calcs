Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2025 if I'm paid on the last working day?

England and Wales: January — Friday 31 January; February — Friday 28 February; March — Monday 31 March; April — Wednesday 30 April; May — Friday 30 May; June — Monday 30 June; July — Thursday 31 July; August — Friday 29 August; September — Tuesday 30 September; October — Friday 31 October; November — Friday 28 November; December — Wednesday 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the 25th paid early in 2025?

January (Friday 24 January), May (Friday 23 May), August (Friday 22 August), October (Friday 24 October), December (Wednesday 24 December) — the 25th falls on a weekend or bank holiday in those months.
