Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in February 2025?

20 in every part of the UK: 28 days less 8 weekend days.

What is the last working day of February 2025?

Friday 28 February 2025 in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. The first working day is Monday 3 February 2025.

How many working hours are in February 2025?

150 hours on a 7.5-hour day or 160 on an 8-hour day (20 days, England and Wales).

Are there any bank holidays in February 2025?

No — there are no weekday bank holidays in February 2025 anywhere in the UK.
