Frequently asked questions

How do I count working days between two dates?

Count the weekdays (Monday to Friday) between them, then take off any bank holidays that fall on a weekday. The calculator does this for England and Wales, Scotland or Northern Ireland, counting both the first and last date.

How many working days until Christmas?

From Saturday 14 November 2026 to Christmas Eve, Thursday 24 December 2026, there are 29 working days in England and Wales (counting both days). The calculator starts from today when you open it.

Are bank holidays the same across the UK?

No. Scotland has 2 January and St Andrew's Day, and its summer bank holiday is at the start of August rather than the end; Northern Ireland adds St Patrick's Day and the Battle of the Boyne (Orangemen's Day).

How many working days are there in a year?

2026: 253, 2027: 253, 2028: 252 in England and Wales — usually between 250 and 254.
