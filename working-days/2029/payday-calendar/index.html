Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2029 if I'm paid on the last working day?

England and Wales: January — Wednesday 31 January; February — Wednesday 28 February; March — Thursday 29 March; April — Monday 30 April; May — Thursday 31 May; June — Friday 29 June; July — Tuesday 31 July; August — Friday 31 August; September — Friday 28 September; October — Wednesday 31 October; November — Friday 30 November; December — Monday 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the 25th paid early in 2029?

February (Friday 23 February), March (Friday 23 March), August (Friday 24 August), November (Friday 23 November), December (Monday 24 December) — the 25th falls on a weekend or bank holiday in those months.
