Frequently asked questions

When do I get paid in December 2029?

If you're paid on the last working day, Monday 31 December; the last Friday, Friday 28 December; the 25th, Monday 24 December, because Christmas Day is a bank holiday. Many employers pay earlier in December, before Christmas, so check your payslip or ask payroll.

When is the January 2030 payday?

On the last working day it's Thursday 31 January 2030: 31 days after December's. Paid on the 25th rule, the gap is 32 days.

Why does January feel so long?

Because December pay usually comes before Christmas and January's at the end of the month, and Christmas spending lands in between. If your December pay comes early, the gap to January's payday is longer still: worth planning the budget for it.

Is Christmas Eve a bank holiday?

No: Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve aren't bank holidays anywhere in the UK, so they're normal working days for payday. Tuesday 25 December, Wednesday 26 December, Tuesday 1 January and Wednesday 2 January are.
