Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in January 2027?

England and Wales: 20; Scotland: 19; Northern Ireland: 20.

What is the last working day of January 2027?

Friday 29 January 2027 in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. The first working day is Monday 4 January 2027 (Tuesday 5 January 2027 in Scotland).

How many working hours are in January 2027?

150 hours on a 7.5-hour day or 160 on an 8-hour day (20 days, England and Wales).

Are there any bank holidays in January 2027?

England and Wales: New Year's Day on Friday 1 January. Scotland: New Year's Day on Friday 1 January, 2nd January on Monday 4 January. Northern Ireland: New Year's Day on Friday 1 January.
