Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2027 if I'm paid on the last working day?

England and Wales: January — Friday 29 January; February — Friday 26 February; March — Wednesday 31 March; April — Friday 30 April; May — Friday 28 May; June — Wednesday 30 June; July — Friday 30 July; August — Tuesday 31 August; September — Thursday 30 September; October — Friday 29 October; November — Tuesday 30 November; December — Friday 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the 25th paid early in 2027?

April (Friday 23 April), July (Friday 23 July), September (Friday 24 September), December (Friday 24 December) — the 25th falls on a weekend or bank holiday in those months.
