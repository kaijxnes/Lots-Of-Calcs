Frequently asked questions

When do I get paid in December 2026?

If you're paid on the last working day, Thursday 31 December; the last Friday, Thursday 24 December; the 25th, Thursday 24 December, because Christmas Day is a bank holiday. Many employers pay earlier in December, before Christmas, so check your payslip or ask payroll.

When is the January 2027 payday?

On the last working day it's Friday 29 January 2027: 29 days after December's. Paid on the last friday rule, the gap is 36 days.

Why does January feel so long?

Because December pay usually comes before Christmas and January's at the end of the month, and Christmas spending lands in between. If your December pay comes early, the gap to January's payday is longer still: worth planning the budget for it.

Is Christmas Eve a bank holiday?

No: Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve aren't bank holidays anywhere in the UK, so they're normal working days for payday. Friday 25 December, Monday 28 December, Friday 1 January and Monday 4 January are.
