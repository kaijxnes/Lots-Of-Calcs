Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2026 if I'm paid on the last working day?

England and Wales: January — Friday 30 January; February — Friday 27 February; March — Tuesday 31 March; April — Thursday 30 April; May — Friday 29 May; June — Tuesday 30 June; July — Friday 31 July; August — Friday 28 August; September — Wednesday 30 September; October — Friday 30 October; November — Monday 30 November; December — Thursday 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the 25th paid early in 2026?

January (Friday 23 January), April (Friday 24 April), May (Friday 22 May), July (Friday 24 July), October (Friday 23 October), December (Thursday 24 December) — the 25th falls on a weekend or bank holiday in those months.
