Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in December 2026?

21 in every part of the UK: 31 days less 8 weekend days and 2 bank holidays.

What is the last working day of December 2026?

Thursday 31 December 2026 in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. The first working day is Tuesday 1 December 2026.

How many working hours are in December 2026?

157.5 hours on a 7.5-hour day or 168 on an 8-hour day (21 days, England and Wales).

Are there any bank holidays in December 2026?

England and Wales: Christmas Day on Friday 25 December, Boxing Day on Monday 28 December. Scotland: Christmas Day on Friday 25 December, Boxing Day on Monday 28 December. Northern Ireland: Christmas Day on Friday 25 December, Boxing Day on Monday 28 December.
