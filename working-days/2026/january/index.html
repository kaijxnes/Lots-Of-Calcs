Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in January 2026?

England and Wales: 21; Scotland: 20; Northern Ireland: 21.

What is the last working day of January 2026?

Friday 30 January 2026 in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. The first working day is Friday 2 January 2026 (Monday 5 January 2026 in Scotland).

How many working hours are in January 2026?

157.5 hours on a 7.5-hour day or 168 on an 8-hour day (21 days, England and Wales).

Are there any bank holidays in January 2026?

England and Wales: New Year's Day on Thursday 1 January. Scotland: New Year's Day on Thursday 1 January, 2nd January on Friday 2 January. Northern Ireland: New Year's Day on Thursday 1 January.
