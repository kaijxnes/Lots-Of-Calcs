Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in December 2028?

19 in every part of the UK: 31 days less 10 weekend days and 2 bank holidays.

What is the last working day of December 2028?

Friday 29 December 2028 in England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. The first working day is Friday 1 December 2028.

How many working hours are in December 2028?

142.5 hours on a 7.5-hour day or 152 on an 8-hour day (19 days, England and Wales).

Are there any bank holidays in December 2028?

England and Wales: Christmas Day on Monday 25 December, Boxing Day on Tuesday 26 December. Scotland: Christmas Day on Monday 25 December, Boxing Day on Tuesday 26 December. Northern Ireland: Christmas Day on Monday 25 December, Boxing Day on Tuesday 26 December.
