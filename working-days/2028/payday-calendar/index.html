Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2028 if I'm paid on the last working day?

England and Wales: January — Monday 31 January; February — Tuesday 29 February; March — Friday 31 March; April — Friday 28 April; May — Wednesday 31 May; June — Friday 30 June; July — Monday 31 July; August — Thursday 31 August; September — Friday 29 September; October — Tuesday 31 October; November — Thursday 30 November; December — Friday 29 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the 25th paid early in 2028?

March (Friday 24 March), June (Friday 23 June), November (Friday 24 November), December (Friday 22 December) — the 25th falls on a weekend or bank holiday in those months.

December and January: Christmas payday 2028, with the gap to January's payday.
