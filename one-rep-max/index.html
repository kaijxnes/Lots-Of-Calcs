Frequently asked questions

How do I estimate my one rep max?

Lift a weight you can do for a few clean reps, then use a formula: Epley's is weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30), Brzycki's is weight × 36 ÷ (37 − reps). 100 kg for 5 reps gives about 117 kg (Epley) or 113 kg (Brzycki).

Which formula is more accurate?

Both are estimates and are closest at low reps — roughly up to 10. Beyond that they drift apart; testing your actual one rep max (safely, with a spotter) is the only way to know it.

How do I use the percentages?

Training plans often give loads as a percentage of your one rep max. The table shows the weight for each percentage, and roughly how many reps that load allows by Epley's formula.
