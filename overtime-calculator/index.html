Frequently asked questions

How is overtime pay worked out?

Hourly rate × the overtime multiplier × overtime hours. At £15.00 an hour, time and a half is £22.50 an hour and double time £30.00.

How much of my overtime do I keep after tax?

Overtime is taxed at your top rate, because it sits on top of your normal pay. In the example, 5 hours a week at time and a half adds £112.50 a week before tax but £81.00 after income tax and National Insurance — 72% of it.

Is overtime taxed more?

No — it's taxed at the same rates as the rest of your pay. It can feel like more because it's all taxed at your highest rate, and a one-off big overtime month can put more through PAYE in that month, which usually evens out over the tax year.

Do I have to be paid for overtime?

In the UK employers don't have to pay for overtime, but your average pay for all the hours you work mustn't fall below the National Minimum Wage; your contract sets the rate (see GOV.UK's overtime rights page). In the US, the Fair Labor Standards Act requires at least time and a half over 40 hours a week for non-exempt employees.
