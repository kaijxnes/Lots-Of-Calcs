10 km = 6.21 miles

6.21 miles
10 km
In metres10,000 m

How: A mile is exactly 1.609344 km, so divide by 1.609 (or multiply by 0.6214). 10 ÷ 1.609344 = 6.214 miles.

In your head: Multiply by 5, then divide by 8: that gives 6.25 miles for 10 km — 0.04 miles over the exact 6.21 miles.
