5 km to Miles

5 km = 3.11 miles

3.11 miles
5 km
In metres5,000 m

How: A mile is exactly 1.609344 km, so divide by 1.609 (or multiply by 0.6214). 5 ÷ 1.609344 = 3.107 miles.

In your head: Multiply by 5, then divide by 8: that gives 3.13 miles for 5 km — 0.02 miles over the exact 3.11 miles.
