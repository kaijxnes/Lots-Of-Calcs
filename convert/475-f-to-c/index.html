475°F to °C

475 °F = 246.1 °C

246.1 °C
475 °F
In kelvin519.26 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (475 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 246.11 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 222.5 °C for 475 °F — 23.6 °C under the exact 246.1 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.

In the oven: Gas mark 9 — 240 °C (220 °C in a fan oven) or 475 °F, which Which?'s oven chart calls very hot. The oven temperature converter does fan and air fryer temperatures too.
