375°F to °C

375 °F = 190.6 °C

190.6 °C
375 °F
In kelvin463.71 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (375 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 190.56 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 172.5 °C for 375 °F — 18.1 °C under the exact 190.6 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.

In the oven: Gas mark 5 — 190 °C (170 °C in a fan oven) or 375 °F, which Which?'s oven chart calls moderately hot. The oven temperature converter does fan and air fryer temperatures too.
