How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (550 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 287.78 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 260 °C for 550 °F — 27.8 °C under the exact 287.8 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
