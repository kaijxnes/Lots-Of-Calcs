275°F to °C

275 °F = 135 °C

135 °C
275 °F
In kelvin408.15 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (275 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 135 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 122.5 °C for 275 °F — 12.5 °C under the exact 135 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.

In the oven: Gas mark 1 — 140 °C (120 °C in a fan oven) or 275 °F, which Which?'s oven chart calls cool. The oven temperature converter does fan and air fryer temperatures too.
