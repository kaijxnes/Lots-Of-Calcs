-14°F to °C

-14 °F = -25.6 °C

-25.6 °C

In kelvin247.59 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (-14 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = -25.56 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives -22 °C for -14 °F — 3.6 °C over the exact -25.6 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
