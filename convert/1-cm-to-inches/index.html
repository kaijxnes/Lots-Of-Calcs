1 cm to Inches

1 cm = 0.39 in

0.39 in
1 cm
In millimetres10 mm

How: Divide by 2.54, since an inch is exactly 2.54 cm. 1 ÷ 2.54 = 0.394 inches.

In your head: Multiply by 0.4 (the same as dividing by 2.5): that gives 0.4 in for 1 cm — 0.01 in over the exact 0.39 in.
