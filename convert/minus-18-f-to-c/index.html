-18°F to °C

-18 °F = -27.8 °C

-27.8 °C

In kelvin245.37 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (-18 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = -27.78 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives -24 °C for -18 °F — 3.8 °C over the exact -27.8 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
