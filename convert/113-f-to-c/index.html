113°F to °C

113 °F = 45 °C

45 °C
113 °F
In kelvin318.15 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (113 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 45 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 41.5 °C for 113 °F — 3.5 °C under the exact 45 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
