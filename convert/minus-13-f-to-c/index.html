-13°F to °C

-13 °F = -25 °C

-25 °C
-13 °F
In kelvin248.15 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (-13 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = -25 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives -21.5 °C for -13 °F — 3.5 °C over the exact -25 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
