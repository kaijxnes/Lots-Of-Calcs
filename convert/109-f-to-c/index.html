109°F to °C

109 °F = 42.8 °C

42.8 °C
109 °F
In kelvin315.93 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (109 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 42.78 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 39.5 °C for 109 °F — 3.3 °C under the exact 42.8 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
