250°F to °C

250 °F = 121.1 °C

121.1 °C
250 °F
In kelvin394.26 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (250 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 121.11 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 110 °C for 250 °F — 11.1 °C under the exact 121.1 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
