107°F to °C

107 °F = 41.7 °C

41.7 °C
107 °F
In kelvin314.82 K

How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (107 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = 41.67 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives 38.5 °C for 107 °F — 3.2 °C under the exact 41.7 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
