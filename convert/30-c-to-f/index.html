30 °C = 86 °F

86 °F
30 °C
In kelvin303.15 K

How: Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32 (°F = °C × 1.8 + 32). 30 × 1.8 + 32 = 86 °F.

In your head: Double it, then add 30: that gives 90 °F for 30 °C — 4 °F over the exact 86 °F. The shortcut is exact at 10 °C and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
