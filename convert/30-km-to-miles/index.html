18.64 miles

In metres30,000 m

How: A mile is exactly 1.609344 km, so divide by 1.609 (or multiply by 0.6214). 30 ÷ 1.609344 = 18.641 miles.

In your head: Multiply by 5, then divide by 8: that gives 18.75 miles for 30 km — 0.11 miles over the exact 18.64 miles.
