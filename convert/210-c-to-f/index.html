How: Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32 (°F = °C × 1.8 + 32). 210 × 1.8 + 32 = 410 °F.

In your head: Double it, then add 30: that gives 450 °F for 210 °C — 40 °F over the exact 410 °F. The shortcut is exact at 10 °C and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
