100°C to °F

100 °C = 212 °F

212 °F
100 °C
In kelvin373.15 K

How: Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32 (°F = °C × 1.8 + 32). 100 × 1.8 + 32 = 212 °F.

In your head: Double it, then add 30: that gives 230 °F for 100 °C — 18 °F over the exact 212 °F. The shortcut is exact at 10 °C and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
