How: Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32 (°F = °C × 1.8 + 32). 135 × 1.8 + 32 = 275 °F.

In your head: Double it, then add 30: that gives 300 °F for 135 °C — 25 °F over the exact 275 °F. The shortcut is exact at 10 °C and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
