1 km to Miles

1 km = 0.62 miles

0.62 miles
1 km
In metres1,000 m

How: A mile is exactly 1.609344 km, so divide by 1.609 (or multiply by 0.6214). 1 ÷ 1.609344 = 0.621 miles.

In your head: Multiply by 5, then divide by 8: that gives 0.63 miles for 1 km — spot on.
