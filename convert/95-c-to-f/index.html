95°C to °F

95 °C = 203 °F

How: Multiply by 9, divide by 5, then add 32 (°F = °C × 1.8 + 32). 95 × 1.8 + 32 = 203 °F.

In your head: Double it, then add 30: that gives 220 °F for 95 °C — 17 °F over the exact 203 °F. The shortcut is exact at 10 °C and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
