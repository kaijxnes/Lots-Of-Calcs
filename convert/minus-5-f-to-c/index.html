How: Subtract 32, then multiply by 5 and divide by 9 (°C = (°F − 32) ÷ 1.8). (-5 − 32) ÷ 1.8 = -20.56 °C.

In your head: Take away 30, then halve it: that gives -17.5 °C for -5 °F — 3.1 °C over the exact -20.6 °C. The shortcut is exact at 50 °F and drifts further out the further you go from it, so for anything much hotter or colder, use the exact formula.
