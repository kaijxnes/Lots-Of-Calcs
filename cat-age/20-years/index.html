Frequently asked questions

How old is a 20-year-old cat in human years?

About 96. Cats age about 15 human years in their first year and 9 more in their second, then roughly 4 human years for every year after that, so 20 works out at 96.

Is one cat year seven human years?

No — the seven-year rule would make a 20-year-old cat 140. It overstates older cats and badly understates kittens, which are adults by the age of one or two.

What life stage is a 20-year-old cat?

Geriatric. Vets commonly group cats as kittens to 1, juniors to 2, adults 3 to 6, mature 7 to 10, seniors 11 to 14 and geriatric from 15.
