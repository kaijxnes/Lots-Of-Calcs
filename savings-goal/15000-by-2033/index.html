How it's worked out

Without interest it's simply the goal divided by the months: £15,000 ÷ 85 = £176.47.

With interest, each deposit earns interest for the months left after it goes in, so you need to pay in less. The monthly amount is goal × i ÷ ((1 + i)n − 1), where i is the monthly rate (4% ÷ 12 = 0.333%) and n is 85 months: £152.94.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to save a month to have £15,000 by 2033?

£176.47 a month, starting in December 2026 and saving every month until December 2033 — 85 payments. That's about £40.72 a week.

How much less do I need to save with interest?

At 4% a year, compounded monthly, £152.94 a month gets you there, because the interest makes up £2,000 of the £15,000. At 2% it's £164.41 and at 5% £147.42.

What if I start later?

Every month you wait spreads the same total over fewer payments. Starting in December 2028 instead of December 2026, you'd need £245.90 a month without interest.

What if I already have some saved?

Take it off the goal first, then divide what's left by the months. With £3,750 already saved, you'd need £132.35 a month without interest. The savings goal calculator does this with interest on both.
