How it's worked out

Without interest it's simply the goal divided by the months: £25,000 ÷ 85 = £294.12.

With interest, each deposit earns interest for the months left after it goes in, so you need to pay in less. The monthly amount is goal × i ÷ ((1 + i)n − 1), where i is the monthly rate (4% ÷ 12 = 0.333%) and n is 85 months: £254.90.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to save a month to have £25,000 by 2033?

£294.12 a month, starting in December 2026 and saving every month until December 2033 — 85 payments. That's about £67.87 a week.

How much less do I need to save with interest?

At 4% a year, compounded monthly, £254.90 a month gets you there, because the interest makes up £3,333 of the £25,000. At 2% it's £274.02 and at 5% £245.71.

What if I start later?

Every month you wait spreads the same total over fewer payments. Starting in December 2028 instead of December 2026, you'd need £409.84 a month without interest.

What if I already have some saved?

Take it off the goal first, then divide what's left by the months. With £6,250 already saved, you'd need £220.59 a month without interest. The savings goal calculator does this with interest on both.
