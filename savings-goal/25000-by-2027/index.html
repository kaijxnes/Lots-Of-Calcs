How it's worked out

Without interest it's simply the goal divided by the months: £25,000 ÷ 13 = £1,923.08.

With interest, each deposit earns interest for the months left after it goes in, so you need to pay in less. The monthly amount is goal × i ÷ ((1 + i)n − 1), where i is the monthly rate (4% ÷ 12 = 0.333%) and n is 13 months: £1,884.91.

Frequently asked questions

How much do I need to save a month to have £25,000 by 2027?

£1,923.08 a month, starting in December 2026 and saving every month until December 2027 — 13 payments. That's about £443.79 a week.

How much less do I need to save with interest?

At 4% a year, compounded monthly, £1,884.91 a month gets you there, because the interest makes up £496 of the £25,000. At 2% it's £1,903.92 and at 5% £1,875.47.

What if I start later?

Every month you wait spreads the same total over fewer payments. Starting in June 2027 instead of December 2026, you'd need £3,571.43 a month without interest.

What if I already have some saved?

Take it off the goal first, then divide what's left by the months. With £6,250 already saved, you'd need £1,442.31 a month without interest. The savings goal calculator does this with interest on both.
