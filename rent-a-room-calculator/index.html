How the Rent a Room Scheme works

You can earn up to £7,500 a year tax-free from letting furnished accommodation in your only or main home, whether you own it or rent it, or from running a bed and breakfast or guest house. The limit is halved to £3,750 if someone else, such as a joint owner, also gets income from letting in the same property. It counts your gross receipts: the rent before expenses, plus anything paid for meals, cleaning, laundry or other services.

Under the limit the exemption is automatic and you don't need to do anything. Over it, you fill in a tax return and choose: Method A pays tax on your actual profit (receipts less expenses); Method B pays tax on the receipts over £7,500, with no expenses. HMRC uses Method A unless you tell it you want Method B. HMRC's example: receipts of £10,600 and expenses of £9,000 mean tax on £1,600 under Method A or £3,100 under Method B, so Method A is better.

You can't use the scheme for a home converted into separate flats, unfurnished accommodation, or a room let while you live abroad. If having a lodger means you no longer get a Council Tax single person discount, you must tell your council.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I earn from a lodger tax-free?

£7,500 a year (about £144.23 a week) under the Rent a Room Scheme, or £3,750 if someone else also gets letting income from the same home.

What if I earn more than £7,500 from a lodger?

You fill in a tax return and pay tax either on your profit after expenses (Method A) or on everything over £7,500 with no expenses (Method B), whichever you choose. HMRC uses Method A unless you tell it otherwise.

How much tax on £150 a week from a lodger?

£7,800 a year is £300 over the limit, so with no expenses and other income of £30,000, Method B gives £60.00 of tax (2026/27).

Do bills paid by my lodger count?

Yes: gross receipts include anything you get for meals, goods and services such as cleaning or laundry, as well as the rent.

Can I use Rent a Room if I rent my home?

Yes, as long as it's your only or main home and the room is furnished. You can't use it if the home has been converted into separate flats.
