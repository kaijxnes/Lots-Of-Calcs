Frequently asked questions

What salary do I need to take home £1,750 a month?

£24,279 a year (£2,023.25 a month before tax) in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and £24,224 in Scotland, for the 2026/27 tax year with no student loan or pension.

How much is £1,750 a month after tax a year?

£21,000 a year after tax, which takes a gross salary of £24,279: £2,341.80 goes in Income Tax and £936.72 in National Insurance.

What if I have a student loan?

With a Plan 2 loan you need £24,279 to take home £1,750 a month, £0 more, because repayments are 9% of pay over the threshold. The table above shows the other plans.

Does paying into a pension change the salary I need?

Yes: paying 5% of your salary into a pension through relief at source means you need £25,707 to keep £1,750 a month in your pocket. The contribution goes into your pension, so you're not losing it.
