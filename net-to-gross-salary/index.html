Frequently asked questions

How do I work out the gross salary for a take-home pay?

Tax isn't a flat percentage, so you can't just add a fixed amount back on: the calculator finds the lowest salary whose take-home, after every deduction, is at least the amount you want. The salary calculator does the same sum the other way.

What salary do I need to take home £2,000 a month?

£28,445 a year in England, Wales and Northern Ireland for 2026/27, with no student loan or pension.

What salary do I need to take home £3,000 a month?

£45,112 a year in England, Wales and Northern Ireland for 2026/27, or £45,953 in Scotland.

Why does each extra pound of take-home cost more salary as I earn more?

Because more of each extra pound goes in tax. Basic-rate taxpayers keep about 72p of each extra pound and higher-rate taxpayers about 58p, so to take home an extra £100 a month you need about £139 more a month on the basic rate and £173 on the higher rate.

Does it work outside the UK?

Yes: pick where you're paid. To take home $5,000 a month in Texas, single, you need about $72,737 a year.
