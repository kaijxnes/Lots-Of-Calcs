Frequently asked questions

What pace do I need for a 1 hour 20 minutes 10K?

8:00 per kilometre, or 12:52 per mile, held for the whole 10 km.

What speed is a 1 hour 20 minutes 10K?

7.50 km/h, or 4.66 mph — the number to set on a treadmill.

What would a 1 hour 20 minutes 10K predict for other distances?

Using Riegel's formula, a common predictor: 5K 38:22, Half marathon 2:56:31, Marathon 6:08:01. Predictions for longer races assume you've trained for the distance.
