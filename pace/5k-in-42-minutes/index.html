Frequently asked questions

What pace do I need for a 42 minutes 5K?

8:24 per kilometre, or 13:31 per mile, held for the whole 5 km.

What speed is a 42 minutes 5K?

7.14 km/h, or 4.44 mph — the number to set on a treadmill.

What would a 42 minutes 5K predict for other distances?

Using Riegel's formula, a common predictor: 10K 1:27:34, Half marathon 3:13:13, Marathon 6:42:50. Predictions for longer races assume you've trained for the distance.
