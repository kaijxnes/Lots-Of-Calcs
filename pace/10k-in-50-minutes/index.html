Frequently asked questions

What pace do I need for a 50 minutes 10K?

5:00 per kilometre, or 8:03 per mile, held for the whole 10 km.

What speed is a 50 minutes 10K?

12.00 km/h, or 7.46 mph — the number to set on a treadmill.

What would a 50 minutes 10K predict for other distances?

Using Riegel's formula, a common predictor: 5K 23:59, Half marathon 1:50:19, Marathon 3:50:01. Predictions for longer races assume you've trained for the distance.
