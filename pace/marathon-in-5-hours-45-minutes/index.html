Frequently asked questions

What pace do I need for a 5 hours 45 minutes marathon?

8:11 per kilometre, or 13:10 per mile, held for the whole 42.195 km.

What speed is a 5 hours 45 minutes marathon?

7.34 km/h, or 4.56 mph — the number to set on a treadmill.

What would a 5 hours 45 minutes marathon predict for other distances?

Using Riegel's formula, a common predictor: 5K 35:58, 10K 1:15:00, Half marathon 2:45:28. Predictions for longer races assume you've trained for the distance.
