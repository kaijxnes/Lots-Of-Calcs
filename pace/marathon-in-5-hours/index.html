Frequently asked questions

What pace do I need for a 5 hours marathon?

7:07 per kilometre, or 11:27 per mile, held for the whole 42.195 km.

What speed is a 5 hours marathon?

8.44 km/h, or 5.24 mph — the number to set on a treadmill.

What would a 5 hours marathon predict for other distances?

Using Riegel's formula, a common predictor: 5K 31:17, 10K 1:05:13, Half marathon 2:23:53. Predictions for longer races assume you've trained for the distance.
