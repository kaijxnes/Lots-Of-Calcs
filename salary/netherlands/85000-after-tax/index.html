How €85,000 after tax is worked out

Labour tax credit (arbeidskorting): €3,119.54. Builds up to €5,685 at €45,592 of pay, then falls by 6.51% of pay above that, reaching nothing at €132,920.

General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting): €0.00. €3,115, reduced by 6.398% of income above €29,736.

Income tax and national insurance (loonheffing): €28,887.61. The tax credits can only reduce the tax to zero.

Frequently asked questions

How much is €85,000 after tax in the Netherlands?

A €85,000 salary in the Netherlands leaves €56,112.39 a year after income tax, which is €4,676.03 a month or about €1,079.08 a week. The deductions are income tax and national insurance (loonheffing) €28,887.61 — €28,887.61 in all, or 34.0% of the gross.

How much is €85,000 a month after tax in the Netherlands?

€4,676.03 a month — the annual take-home of €56,112.39 divided by twelve. Actual pay slips can differ where pay is split into more than twelve instalments or where withholding is settled at the end of the year.

What is the marginal tax rate on €85,000 in the Netherlands?

56% — that is what is taken from the next unit you earn, against 34.0% of the salary overall. The marginal rate is the one that matters when weighing a pay rise or overtime.

What does this leave out?

Employee pension contributions, which depend on the pension fund (often 4–9% of pay above a franchise); The 30% ruling for incoming employees; Other credits (income-dependent combination credit for parents) and benefits (toeslagen); Box 2 and Box 3 income.
