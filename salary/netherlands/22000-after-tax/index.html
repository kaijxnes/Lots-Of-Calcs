How €22,000 after tax is worked out

Labour tax credit (arbeidskorting): €4,107.72. Builds up to €5,685 at €45,592 of pay, then falls by 6.51% of pay above that, reaching nothing at €132,920.

General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting): €3,115.00. €3,115, reduced by 6.398% of income above €29,736.

Taxable wage: €22,000.00. Employee pension premiums, and the tax-free allowance under the 30% ruling, come off pay before wage tax and the tax credits are worked out.

30% ruling tax-free allowance: €0.00. Under the 30% ruling (expatregeling) up to 30% of pay, including the allowance, is paid tax-free, on pay up to €262,000 (a maximum of €78,600 for a full year). The pay left after the allowance must stay above the salary norm — €48,013 in 2026, or €36,497 if you're under 30 with a master's degree — so near the norm the allowance is cut back to keep it there. It lasts up to 5 years.

Income tax and national insurance (loonheffing): €642.28. The tax credits can only reduce the tax to zero.

Frequently asked questions

How much is €22,000 after tax in the Netherlands?

A €22,000 salary in the Netherlands leaves €21,357.72 a year after income tax, which is €1,779.81 a month or about €410.73 a week. The deductions are income tax and national insurance (loonheffing) €642.28 — €642.28 in all, or 2.9% of the gross.

How much is €22,000 a month after tax in the Netherlands?

€1,779.81 a month — the annual take-home of €21,357.72 divided by twelve. Actual pay slips can differ where pay is split into more than twelve instalments or where withholding is settled at the end of the year.

What is the marginal tax rate on €22,000 in the Netherlands?

5% — that is what is taken from the next unit you earn, against 2.9% of the salary overall. The marginal rate is the one that matters when weighing a pay rise or overtime.

What is a 3% pay rise on €22,000 worth after tax in the Netherlands?

A 3% rise takes €22,000 to €22,660 — €660 more before tax. After deductions you keep €628.71 a year, or €52.39 a month.

What does this leave out?

The franchise and pensionable-salary rules of individual schemes (the percentage here applies to all pay); Other credits (income-dependent combination credit for parents) and benefits (toeslagen); Box 2 and Box 3 income; The 30% ruling for part of a year (the allowance and its €78,600 maximum are pro rata), its transitional rules, and the cut to 27% planned from 2027.
