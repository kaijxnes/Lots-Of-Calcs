What comes out of pay in the Netherlands

Labour tax credit (arbeidskorting): €5,398.04. Builds up to €5,685 at €45,592 of pay, then falls by 6.51% of pay above that, reaching nothing at €132,920.

General tax credit (algemene heffingskorting): €1,818.51. €3,115, reduced by 6.398% of income above €29,736.

Income tax and national insurance (loonheffing): €10,859.67. The tax credits can only reduce the tax to zero.

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Below state pension (AOW) age
  • The gross includes the 8% holiday allowance
  • Employment income only; each person is taxed individually

Leaves out:

  • Employee pension contributions, which depend on the pension fund (often 4–9% of pay above a franchise)
  • The 30% ruling for incoming employees
  • Other credits (income-dependent combination credit for parents) and benefits (toeslagen)
  • Box 2 and Box 3 income
