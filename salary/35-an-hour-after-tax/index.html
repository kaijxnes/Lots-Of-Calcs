How £35 an hour after tax is worked out

The hourly rate is turned into a yearly salary first — £35 × 37.5 hours × 52 weeks = £68,250 — because tax is worked out on yearly income. The take-home is then spread back over the year.

Personal allowance: £12,570.00. Withdrawn by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000, which is what creates the 60% effective band up to £125,140.

Income tax: £14,732.00. Thresholds are on taxable income, i.e. after the personal allowance.

National Insurance: £3,375.60. Class 1 employee contributions, charged on gross pay rather than taxable income. Weekly thresholds of £241.73 and £966.73 are £12,570 and £50,270 a year.

Frequently asked questions

How much is £35 an hour a year?

£35 an hour for 37.5 hours a week over 52 weeks is £68,250 a year before tax. After income tax and National Insurance in the UK, that leaves £50,142.40 a year.

How much is £35 an hour after tax a month?

£4,178.53 a month, or £964.28 a week, working 37.5 hours a week. Each hour worked takes home £25.71 once 26.5% has gone in deductions.

What if I work part-time at £35 an hour?

At 20 hours a week, £35 an hour is £36,400 a year and leaves £29,727.60 after tax, or £2,477.30 a month. Lower earnings are taxed at a lower average rate, so take-home doesn't fall quite in proportion to the hours. The table above covers other weekly hours.

How much of an overtime hour do I keep?

At this level of pay your marginal rate is 42%, so an extra hour at £35 leaves about £20.30 after tax. Overtime paid at a higher rate keeps the same share of the higher figure.

Is this the same in Scotland?

No. Scotland sets its own income tax bands and rates, so the income tax differs at most rates of pay; National Insurance is the same across the UK. These figures are for England, Wales and Northern Ireland.

What does this leave out?

Pension contributions (workplace or salary sacrifice); Tax codes other than the standard personal allowance; Benefits in kind, salary sacrifice and taxable expenses; Blind person's allowance, marriage allowance and other reliefs.
