What comes out of pay in Portugal

Taxable income (rendimento coletável): €44,500.00. Pay less the specific deduction: €4,587.09 (8.54 × the 2026 social support index), or social security paid if more.

Social security (Segurança Social): €5,500.00. 11% of pay, with no ceiling.

Income tax (IRS): €11,264.89. 2026 rates after the October 2026 cut (backdated to January) to the first six bands. Tax may not leave less than the €12,880 subsistence minimum.

Additional solidarity rate: €0.00. 2.5% of taxable income between €80,000 and €250,000, 5% above.

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Mainland Portugal (the Azores and Madeira have lower rates)
  • Employment (Category A) income only; the annual gross includes the 13th and 14th month payments
  • No dependants

Leaves out:

  • IRS Jovem (partial exemption for workers under 35)
  • Non-habitual resident and IFICI regimes
  • Personal deductions (general family expenses, health, education, housing)
  • The Azores and Madeira regional rates

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • Monthly withholding in 2026 used the earlier rates; the annual return settles the difference
