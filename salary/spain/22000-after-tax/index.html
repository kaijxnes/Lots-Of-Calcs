How €22,000 after tax is worked out

Personal and family minimum (state): €5,550.00. €5,550 for you, plus €2,400, €2,700, €4,000 and €4,500 for the first, second, third and each further child, and €2,800 more for each child under 3 (arts. 57 and 58).

Taxable base (base liquidable): €17,227.65. Net earnings less the reduction for employment income and any pension plan reduction. Married couples filing jointly take a further €3,400 off. Under the Beckham law it is simply your gross pay: no allowances or reductions.

Social security (Seguridad Social): €1,430.00. 4.70% common contingencies, 1.55% unemployment, 0.10% training and 0.15% intergenerational equity (MEI), on pay up to the maximum base of €5,101.20 a month.

Income tax — state share (IRPF estatal): €1,228.82. The state scale on the base, less the same scale applied to the personal and family minimum. Under the Beckham law the whole tax is 24% of gross pay up to €600,000 and 47% above, with half shown as the state share and half as the regional share, as the Modelo 151 return splits it.

Income tax — regional share (IRPF autonómico): €1,043.07. Each autonomous community sets its own scale for its half of IRPF. Valencia's 2026 scale is the one cut in July 2026 with effect from January (Ley 5/2026). Under the Beckham law the regional scale doesn't apply: this is the other half of the flat 24% / 47%.

Frequently asked questions

How much is €22,000 after tax in Spain?

A €22,000 salary in Spain leaves €18,298.11 a year after income tax and social contributions, which is €1,524.84 a month or about €351.89 a week. The deductions are Social security (Seguridad Social) €1,430.00, income tax — state share (IRPF estatal) €1,228.82, income tax — regional share (IRPF autonómico) €1,043.07 — €3,701.89 in all, or 16.8% of the gross. That is for individual; Region: Comunidad de Madrid.

How much is €22,000 a month after tax in Spain?

€1,524.84 a month — the annual take-home of €18,298.11 divided by twelve. Actual pay slips can differ where pay is split into more than twelve instalments or where withholding is settled at the end of the year.

What is the marginal tax rate on €22,000 in Spain?

52% — that is what is taken from the next unit you earn, against 16.8% of the salary overall. The marginal rate is the one that matters when weighing a pay rise or overtime.

What is a 3% pay rise on €22,000 worth after tax in Spain?

A 3% rise takes €22,000 to €22,660 — €660 more before tax. After deductions you keep €317.33 a year, or €26.44 a month.

How much is €22,000 after tax in Spain if I'm married?

€19,069.91 a year (€1,589.16 a month) as "Joint return, one earner", against €18,298.11 for a single person — a difference of €771.80. That assumes one spouse earns the whole salary.

Regions other than Madrid, Catalonia, Andalusia and Valencia, and the separate Basque and Navarre systems; Regional and state deductions (rent, family, donations) and higher regional personal minimums; The solidarity contribution on pay above the maximum base (a fraction of 1% for the employee); Employer pension contributions and the higher €8,500 limit for company plans; Regional deductions for children (e.g. Madrid's birth and childcare deductions); Beckham law: savings income (taxed at 19%–30%), foreign income, and the deductions for donations and double taxation.
