What comes out of pay in Spain

Taxable base (base liquidable): €44,750.00. Married couples filing jointly take a further €3,400 off.

Social security (Seguridad Social): €3,250.00. 4.70% common contingencies, 1.55% unemployment, 0.10% training and 0.15% intergenerational equity (MEI), on pay up to the maximum base of €5,101.20 a month.

Income tax — state share (IRPF estatal): €5,602.25. The state scale, less the same scale applied to the €5,550 personal minimum.

Income tax — regional share (IRPF autonómico): €4,992.10. Each autonomous community sets its own scale for its half of IRPF.

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • A permanent (indefinite) contract under the general social security scheme
  • No children, under 65
  • Employment income only

Leaves out:

  • Regions other than Madrid and Catalonia — including Andalusia and Valencia, whose 2026 scales could not be confirmed — and the separate Basque and Navarre systems
  • Regional and state deductions (rent, family, donations) and higher regional personal minimums
  • The solidarity contribution on pay above the maximum base (a fraction of 1% for the employee)
  • Pension plan contributions

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • The Catalan scale (eight bands from 9.5% to 25.5%, Decree-law 5/2025) comes from secondary sources that agree with each other; older tables starting at 10.5% are still in circulation
