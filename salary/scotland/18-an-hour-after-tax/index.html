How £18 an hour after tax is worked out

The hourly rate is turned into a yearly salary first — £18 × 37.5 hours × 52 weeks = £35,100 — because tax is worked out on yearly income. The take-home is then spread back over the year.

Personal allowance: £12,570.00. Withdrawn by £1 for every £2 of income over £100,000, which is what creates the 60% effective band up to £125,140.

Scottish income tax: £4,522.07. Replaces the rest-of-UK bands. Thresholds are on taxable income: with the full £12,570 allowance the bands start at £12,571, £16,538, £29,527, £43,663, £75,001 and £125,141 of gross pay. The personal allowance and National Insurance are set UK-wide and are unchanged.

National Insurance: £1,802.40. Class 1 employee contributions, charged on gross pay rather than taxable income. Weekly thresholds of £241.73 and £966.73 are £12,570 and £50,270 a year.

Frequently asked questions

How much is £18 an hour a year?

£18 an hour for 37.5 hours a week over 52 weeks is £35,100 a year before tax. After income tax and National Insurance in Scotland, that leaves £28,775.53 a year.

How much is £18 an hour after tax a month?

£2,397.96 a month, or £553.38 a week, working 37.5 hours a week. Each hour worked takes home £14.76 once 18.0% has gone in deductions.

What if I work part-time at £18 an hour?

At 20 hours a week, £18 an hour is £18,720 a year and leaves £17,037.67 after tax, or £1,419.81 a month. Lower earnings are taxed at a lower average rate, so take-home doesn't fall quite in proportion to the hours. The table above covers other weekly hours.

How much of an overtime hour do I keep?

At this level of pay your marginal rate is 29%, so an extra hour at £18 leaves about £12.78 after tax. Overtime paid at a higher rate keeps the same share of the higher figure.

What does this leave out?

Pension contributions (workplace or salary sacrifice); Tax codes other than the standard personal allowance; Benefits in kind, salary sacrifice and taxable expenses; Blind person's allowance, marriage allowance and other reliefs; Student loan and postgraduate loan repayments.
