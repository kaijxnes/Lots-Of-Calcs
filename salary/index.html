How it works

Pick where you're paid and type your salary before any deductions. The calculator applies that place's current tax year — income tax, social contributions and the local taxes most employees pay — and shows each one. Some places have options, such as filing status in the US or region in Spain; they appear under the salary when they apply.

The rates come from published sources listed on each location's own page. They have not yet been checked by a person against the official tax authority, so treat the figures as estimates and confirm them before relying on them.
