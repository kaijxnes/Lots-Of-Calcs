How $60 an hour after tax is worked out

The hourly rate is turned into a yearly salary first — $60 × 38 hours × 52 weeks = $118,560 — because tax is worked out on yearly income. The take-home is then spread back over the year.

Taxable income: $117,560.00. After the new $1,000 standard deduction for work-related expenses, which applies from 2026–27 unless you claim more in actual expenses.

Income tax: $25,788.00. Nothing to $18,200, 15% to $45,000 (cut from 16% on 1 July 2026), 30% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000, 45% above. Less the low income tax offset of up to $700.

Medicare levy: $2,351.20. 2% of taxable income, phased in from $28,011 at 10 cents per dollar.

Medicare levy surcharge: $0.00. Only without private hospital cover: 1% above $101,000, 1.25% above $118,000, 1.5% above $158,000.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $60 an hour a year?

$60 an hour for 38 hours a week over 52 weeks is $118,560 a year before tax. After income tax and social contributions in Australia, that leaves $90,420.80 a year.

How much is $60 an hour after tax a month?

$7,535.07 a month, or $1,738.86 a week, working 38 hours a week. Each hour worked takes home $45.76 once 23.7% has gone in deductions.

What if I work part-time at $60 an hour?

At 20 hours a week, $60 an hour is $62,400 a year and leaves $52,311.00 after tax, or $4,359.25 a month. Lower earnings are taxed at a lower average rate, so take-home doesn't fall quite in proportion to the hours. The table above covers other weekly hours.

How much of an overtime hour do I keep?

At this level of pay your marginal rate is 32%, so an extra hour at $60 leaves about $40.80 after tax. Overtime paid at a higher rate keeps the same share of the higher figure.

About these figures

They use the 2026-27 tax year, which runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Australian resident for tax purposes, under pension age
  • Employer superannuation is paid on top of the salary, not out of it
  • Single, no dependants

Leaves out:
