What comes out of pay in Lausanne, Switzerland

Cantonal base tax (Vaud): CHF 7,026.12. Vaud's 2026 tariff: 1% on the first CHF 1,600, rising in steps to 15.5% above CHF 291,700.

Taxable income (Vaud): CHF 85,792.00. Net salary less the professional costs lump sum and CHF 5,000 for health and accident insurance premiums.

Taxable income (federal): CHF 88,900.00. Net salary less the professional costs lump sum and the CHF 1,800 insurance premiums deduction, rounded down to CHF 100.

Other professional costs (lump sum): CHF 2,808.00. 3% of net salary, at least CHF 2,000 and at most CHF 4,000 — the same for federal and Zurich tax.

AHV/IV/EO (old age, disability, loss of earnings): CHF 5,300.00. 5.3% of all salary, with no ceiling (the employer pays another 5.3%).

Unemployment insurance (ALV): CHF 1,100.00. 1.1% of salary up to CHF 148,200; nothing on salary above that.

Federal income tax (direkte Bundessteuer): CHF 1,951.75. 2026 single tariff: nothing up to CHF 15,200, then a set amount plus a rate per CHF 100 in each band, rising to 13.2%; from CHF 794,000 a flat 11.5% of all taxable income.

Cantonal tax (Vaud): CHF 10,345.96. The base tax times the 155% cantonal coefficient, less the 5% reduction for 2026.

City tax (Lausanne): CHF 5,515.50. 78.5% of the cantonal base tax, Lausanne's rate for 2026. No church tax in Vaud.

Frequently asked questions

How much is CHF 100,000 after tax in Lausanne, Switzerland?

CHF 75,786.78 a year, or CHF 6,315.57 a month, after income tax and social contributions. The deductions are AHV/IV/EO (old age, disability, loss of earnings) CHF 5,300.00, unemployment insurance (ALV) CHF 1,100.00, federal income tax (direkte Bundessteuer) CHF 1,951.75, cantonal tax (Vaud) CHF 10,345.96, city tax (Lausanne) CHF 5,515.50 — 24.2% of the gross in all, and the next unit earned is taxed at 29%.

How is income tax worked out in Lausanne?

Federal tax is the same everywhere in Switzerland. Vaud works out a base tax — 1% on the first CHF 1,600, rising in steps to 15.5% above CHF 291,700 for 2026 — then charges 155% of it for the canton, less a 5% reduction for 2026, and Lausanne adds 78.5% of the base tax.

What deductions are allowed in Vaud?

Here: the professional costs lump sum (3% of net income, between CHF 2,000 and CHF 4,000) and up to CHF 5,000 of health and accident insurance premiums for a single person, within an overall CHF 6,600 limit that also covers life insurance and savings interest.

Is tax higher in Lausanne than in Zurich?

Yes, on these figures: the cantonal and city tax on the same salary is higher in Lausanne than in the city of Zurich. The 'in other Swiss cities' table on each page compares them at the same salary.

What about cross-border workers from France?

Workers who live in France and work in Vaud are usually taxed under the France–Switzerland cross-border arrangements, not like this. These figures are for residents of Lausanne.

What does this leave out?

Pillar 3a contributions and other personal deductions; Commuting and meal cost deductions; Non-occupational accident insurance (NBU), which depends on the employer's insurer; Daily sickness benefit insurance; Zurich's personal tax and church tax; Tax at source (Quellensteuer) tables; Other cantons and municipalities; Vaud's deduction for people on modest incomes; Family quotient (quotient familial) for couples and parents; Tax at source (Quellensteuer / impôt à la source) tables; Other Vaud municipalities.
