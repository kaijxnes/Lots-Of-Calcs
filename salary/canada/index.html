Frequently asked questions

Which province has the lowest taxes on a salary?

At $75,000, Nunavut leaves the most ($58,818.86 a year) and Nova Scotia the least ($53,423.01). The order changes with income: a province with a high basic personal amount does well at lower salaries, one with low top rates at higher ones, so check the columns for your pay.

What comes off a paycheque in Canada?

Federal income tax, provincial or territorial income tax, Canada Pension Plan contributions (5.95% of earnings between $3,500 and $74,600, plus 4% up to $85,000) and Employment Insurance premiums (1.63% of earnings up to $68,900). Quebec has its own pension plan and parental insurance instead, and a lower EI rate.

Why is federal tax lower in Quebec?

Quebec residents get the Quebec abatement: 16.5% off basic federal tax, because Quebec runs some programs that the federal government pays for elsewhere. Quebec income tax is higher to match, and is collected by Revenu Québec.
