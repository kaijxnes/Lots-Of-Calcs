What comes out of pay in France

Taxable income: €36,861.83. After the automatic 10% deduction for work expenses (at least €509, at most €14,555).

Basic state pension (assurance vieillesse): €3,516.14. 6.9% of pay up to the social security ceiling (PASS, €48,060 in 2026) plus 0.4% of all pay.

Complementary pension (Agirc-Arrco): €2,185.77. Agirc-Arrco 3.15% plus the 0.86% general balancing contribution up to the PASS, then 8.64% plus 1.08% up to 8 × PASS; and 0.14% (CET) on all pay up to 8 × PASS once pay exceeds the PASS.

CSG and CRDS: €4,765.12. CSG 9.2% (6.8% of it deductible from taxable income) and CRDS 0.5%.

Income tax (impôt sur le revenu): €4,163.00. Less the décote, which reduces small tax bills: €897 (€1,483 for a couple) minus 45.25% of the tax.

High income contribution (CEHR): €0.00. 3% of income between €250,000 and €500,000 and 4% above (thresholds doubled for couples).

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • A private-sector employee who is not a cadre, outside Alsace-Moselle
  • No children; married means one spouse earns the whole salary
  • Employment income only

Leaves out:

  • Employer-arranged health (mutuelle) and provident contributions, which typically add 1–2% to employee deductions
  • Children and additional household shares
  • The 2025–2026 minimum 20% tax on incomes over €250,000 (contribution différentielle)
  • Tax credits and reductions (home help, donations) and actual work expenses instead of the 10% deduction
  • Overtime exemptions and the Alsace-Moselle health contribution

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • France taxes each year's income the following spring with that year's finance law. This uses the latest scale (indexed 0.9% by the 2026 finance law, applied to 2025 income), which is also the basis for withholding through 2026; the scale for 2026 income will be set by the 2027 finance law.
