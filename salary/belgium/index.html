What comes out of pay in Belgium

Professional expenses (lump sum): €6,070.00. 30% of pay after social security, up to €6,070.

Social security (RSZ/ONSS): €6,535.00. 13.07% of gross pay, with no ceiling.

Income tax (federal and regional): €9,956.75. 25% to €16,720, 40% to €29,510, 45% to €51,070 and 50% above, less 25% of the €11,550 tax-free amount (€2,887.50).

Municipal surcharge: €718.88. A percentage of the income tax set by each municipality: 7.22% on average in Flanders for 2026.

Special social security contribution (BBSZ): €484.12. The withholding scale for someone taxed alone: nothing on pay up to €1,945.38 a month, then a set amount plus 4.22%, 1.10%, 3.38% and 1.10% bands, at most €182.82 a quarter (€731.28 a year).

Frequently asked questions

How much is €50,000 after tax in Belgium?

€32,305.25 a year, or €2,692.10 a month, after income tax and social contributions. The deductions are Social security (RSZ/ONSS) €6,535.00, income tax (federal and regional) €9,956.75, municipal surcharge €718.88, special social security contribution (BBSZ) €484.12 — 35.4% of the gross in all, and the next unit earned is taxed at 56%.

How much social security do employees pay in Belgium?

13.07% of gross pay, with no ceiling. On top of that comes the special social security contribution (bijzondere bijdrage), withheld from pay above €1,945.38 a month and at most €182.82 a quarter — €731.28 a year — for someone taxed alone.

How much can you earn tax-free in Belgium?

The tax-free amount for 2026 income (assessment year 2027) is €11,550. It works as a reduction of tax: 25% of €11,550, or €2,887.50, comes off the tax worked out on the bands of 25%, 40%, 45% and 50%.

What is the municipal surcharge?

Each municipality adds a percentage of your income tax. For 2026 the average across Flemish municipalities is 7.22%; Antwerp charges 7%, Ghent 6.5%, Leuven 6.7% and Bruges 6.9%. Pick yours in the calculator's options.

What are lump-sum professional expenses?

Unless you claim actual costs, 30% of your pay after social security is deducted as professional expenses before tax, up to €6,070 for 2026 income. Most salaries above about €23,000 reach the cap.

Are the 13th month and holiday pay included?

Enter your whole year's gross pay, including any 13th month and holiday pay; here they're taxed like ordinary pay. Payslips withhold tax on them differently, and the annual tax assessment settles the difference.

What does this leave out?

Werkbonus and the low-wage tax credit, which cut tax and contributions on lower salaries; Tax-free supplements for children and dependants; Meal vouchers, company cars and other benefits; Brussels and Walloon municipalities; The final settlement of the special social security contribution on the tax assessment, which uses household income; Pension savings, service vouchers and other tax reductions.
