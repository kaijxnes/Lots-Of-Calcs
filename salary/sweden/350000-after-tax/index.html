How kr 350,000 after tax is worked out

Basic allowance (grundavdrag): kr 29,000.00. Depends on income: up to 0.77 × the price base amount (SEK 59,200 in 2026), falling to 0.293 × at high incomes.

General pension contribution (allmän pensionsavgift): kr 24,500.00. 7% of income up to 8.07 × the income base amount (SEK 673,038). It is credited back against tax, so on its own it costs nothing.

Income tax: kr 41,219.81. Municipal and state tax, less the pension contribution credit and the earned income tax credit.

Burial fee (begravningsavgift): kr 937.32. 0.292% of taxable earned income everywhere except Stockholm (0.07%) and Tranås (0.285%), which set their own (2026).

Public service fee (public service-avgift): kr 1,184.00. 1% of taxable earned income, at most SEK 1,184 (1% of 1.42 × the SEK 83,400 income base amount) in 2026. Paid by everyone 18 or over.

Church fee (kyrkoavgift): kr 0.00. Church of Sweden members only: the 2026 national average of 1.05%; each parish sets its own.

Frequently asked questions

How much is kr 350,000 after tax in Sweden?

A kr 350,000 salary in Sweden leaves kr 282,158.87 a year after income tax and social contributions, which is kr 23,513.24 a month or about kr 5,426.13 a week. The deductions are general pension contribution (allmän pensionsavgift) kr 24,500.00, income tax kr 41,219.81, burial fee (begravningsavgift) kr 937.32, public service fee (public service-avgift) kr 1,184.00 — kr 67,841.13 in all, or 19.4% of the gross.

How much is kr 350,000 a month after tax in Sweden?

kr 23,513.24 a month — the annual take-home of kr 282,158.87 divided by twelve. Actual pay slips can differ where pay is split into more than twelve instalments or where withholding is settled at the end of the year.

What is the marginal tax rate on kr 350,000 in Sweden?

-8% — that is what is taken from the next unit you earn, against 19.4% of the salary overall. The marginal rate is the one that matters when weighing a pay rise or overtime.

What is a 3% pay rise on kr 350,000 worth after tax in Sweden?

A 3% rise takes kr 350,000 to kr 360,500 — kr 10,500 more before tax. After deductions you keep kr 7,919.89 a year, or kr 659.99 a month.
