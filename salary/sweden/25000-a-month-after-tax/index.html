How kr 25,000 a month after tax is worked out

Tax is worked out on the year, so the monthly figure is multiplied by twelve — kr 300,000 — the deductions are worked out on that, and the take-home is divided back by twelve. The yearly amounts below are for the whole year.

Basic allowance (grundavdrag): kr 34,000.00. Depends on income: up to 0.77 × the price base amount (SEK 59,200 in 2026), falling to 0.293 × at high incomes.

General pension contribution (allmän pensionsavgift): kr 21,000.00. 7% of income up to 8.07 × the income base amount (SEK 673,038). It is credited back against tax, so on its own it costs nothing.

Income tax: kr 32,593.50. Municipal and state tax, less the pension contribution credit and the earned income tax credit.
