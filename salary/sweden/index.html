What comes out of pay in Sweden

Basic allowance (grundavdrag): kr 17,400.00. Depends on income: up to 0.77 × the price base amount (SEK 59,200 in 2026), falling to 0.293 × at high incomes.

General pension contribution (allmän pensionsavgift): kr 35,000.00. 7% of income up to 8.07 × the income base amount (SEK 673,038). It is credited back against tax, so on its own it costs nothing.

Income tax: kr 68,875.56. Municipal and state tax, less the pension contribution credit and the earned income tax credit.

Burial fee (begravningsavgift): kr 1,409.19. 0.292% of taxable earned income everywhere except Stockholm (0.07%) and Tranås (0.285%), which set their own (2026).

Public service fee (public service-avgift): kr 1,184.00. 1% of taxable earned income, at most SEK 1,184 (1% of 1.42 × the SEK 83,400 income base amount) in 2026. Paid by everyone 18 or over.

Church fee (kyrkoavgift): kr 0.00. Church of Sweden members only: the 2026 national average of 1.05%; each parish sets its own.

Frequently asked questions

How much is kr 500,000 after tax in Sweden?

kr 393,531.25 a year, or kr 32,794.27 a month, after income tax and social contributions. The deductions are general pension contribution (allmän pensionsavgift) kr 35,000.00, income tax kr 68,875.56, burial fee (begravningsavgift) kr 1,409.19, public service fee (public service-avgift) kr 1,184.00 — 21.3% of the gross in all, and the next unit earned is taxed at 0%.

What does this leave out?

Individual municipal rates; Deductions for travel and other work expenses; Individual parish church fees and the Stockholm and Tranås burial fees.
