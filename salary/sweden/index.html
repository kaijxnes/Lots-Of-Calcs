What comes out of pay in Sweden

Basic allowance (grundavdrag): kr 17,400.00. Depends on income: up to 0.77 × the price base amount (SEK 59,200 in 2026), falling to 0.293 × at high incomes.

General pension contribution (allmän pensionsavgift): kr 35,000.00. 7% of income up to 8.07 × the income base amount (SEK 673,038). It is credited back against tax, so on its own it costs nothing.

Income tax: kr 68,875.56. Municipal and state tax, less the pension contribution credit and the earned income tax credit.

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Under 66 at the start of the year
  • Lives in a municipality at the national average rate
  • Employment income only; each person is taxed individually

Leaves out:

  • Individual municipal rates
  • Burial fee (about 0.3%) and church fee for Church of Sweden members
  • Public service fee (1% of income, capped at about SEK 1,250)
  • Deductions for travel and other work expenses

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • The earned income tax credit uses the formula from Skatteverket's 2025 technical description; it reproduces the SEK 52,390 2026 maximum quoted by a secondary source, but the 2026 edition was not read directly
