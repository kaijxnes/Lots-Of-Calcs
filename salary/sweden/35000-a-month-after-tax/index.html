How kr 35,000 a month after tax is worked out

Tax is worked out on the year, so the monthly figure is multiplied by twelve — kr 420,000 — the deductions are worked out on that, and the take-home is divided back by twelve. The yearly amounts below are for the whole year.

Basic allowance (grundavdrag): kr 22,000.00. Depends on income: up to 0.77 × the price base amount (SEK 59,200 in 2026), falling to 0.293 × at high incomes.

General pension contribution (allmän pensionsavgift): kr 29,400.00. 7% of income up to 8.07 × the income base amount (SEK 673,038). It is credited back against tax, so on its own it costs nothing.

Income tax: kr 53,296.64. Municipal and state tax, less the pension contribution credit and the earned income tax credit.

Burial fee (begravningsavgift): kr 1,162.16. 0.292% of taxable earned income everywhere except Stockholm (0.07%) and Tranås (0.285%), which set their own (2026).

Public service fee (public service-avgift): kr 1,184.00. 1% of taxable earned income, at most SEK 1,184 (1% of 1.42 × the SEK 83,400 income base amount) in 2026. Paid by everyone 18 or over.

Church fee (kyrkoavgift): kr 0.00. Church of Sweden members only: the 2026 national average of 1.05%; each parish sets its own.
