What comes out of pay in Finland

Earned income tax credit (työtulovähennys): €3,235.70. 18% of pay, at most €3,430, reduced by 2% of net earned income between €35,000 and €50,550 (so never below €3,119 once the full credit is reached).

Basic deduction (perusvähennys): €0.00. Up to €4,265 in municipal taxation, reduced by 18% of income above €4,265 (gone at about €27,960).

Net earned income (puhdas ansiotulo): €44,715.00. Pay less the automatic €750 deduction for work expenses and the employee contributions above, which are deductible.

Employee pension contribution (TyEL): €3,650.00. 7.30% of pay for all employees aged 17 to 64 in 2026.

Unemployment insurance contribution: €445.00. 0.89% of pay.

Health insurance daily allowance contribution: €440.00. 0.88% of pay for anyone earning €17,255 or more a year; nothing below that.

State income tax: €5,362.34. 12.64% up to €22,000 of net earned income, then 19%, 30.25% from €32,600, 33.25% from €40,100 and 37.5% from €52,100, less the earned income tax credit.

Municipal tax: €3,384.93. The 2026 weighted average of 7.57% for mainland Finland (municipalities range from 4.7% to 10.9%). Any credit left after state tax comes off this and the health care contribution in proportion.

Health care contribution (sairaanhoitomaksu): €491.87. 1.10% of income taxable in municipal taxation.

Public broadcasting tax (Yle-vero): €160.00. 2.5% of income above €15,150, at most €160.

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip. When every rate was last checked.

Assumes:

  • An employee aged 17 to 64 living in mainland Finland
  • The average municipal tax rate
  • Not a member of the Evangelical Lutheran or Orthodox church
  • Employment income only

Leaves out:

  • Church tax for members (about 1–2%, set by each parish)
  • Individual municipal tax rates
  • Holiday pay (lomaraha) and other payments taxed with pay
  • Travel, union and other deductions beyond the automatic €750
  • The higher earned income credit for over-65s and the extra for children
