What comes out of pay in Denmark

ATP pension contribution: kr 1,193.76. DKK 99.48 a month for full-time employees.

Labour market contribution (AM-bidrag): kr 47,904.50. 8% of pay after ATP.

Municipal tax (kommuneskat): kr 107,815.64. The national average rate, on taxable income above the DKK 54,100 personal allowance. Rates range from about 23.4% to 26.3%.

Church tax (kirkeskat): kr 0.00. Members of the Church of Denmark only; about 0.87% on average.

State tax (bundskat, mellemskat, topskat): kr 59,665.89. Bottom tax 12.01% above the allowance; middle tax 7.5% above DKK 641,200; top tax 7.5% above DKK 777,900; top-top tax 5% above DKK 2,592,700 (the new 2026 structure).

Frequently asked questions

How much is kr 600,000 after tax in Denmark?

kr 383,420.22 a year, or kr 31,951.68 a month, after income tax and social contributions. The deductions are ATP pension contribution kr 1,193.76, labour market contribution (AM-bidrag) kr 47,904.50, municipal tax (kommuneskat) kr 107,815.64, state tax (bundskat, mellemskat, topskat) kr 59,665.89 — 36.1% of the gross in all, and the next unit earned is taxed at 42%.

What does this leave out?

Individual municipal tax rates; Senior and single-parent employment deductions; Interest, union dues, commuting and other deductions; Private (non-employer) pension savings and the employer's share of contributions.
