What comes out of pay in Denmark

ATP pension contribution: kr 1,193.76. DKK 99.48 a month for full-time employees.

Labour market contribution (AM-bidrag): kr 47,904.50. 8% of pay after ATP.

Municipal tax (kommuneskat): kr 107,815.64. The national average rate, on taxable income above the DKK 54,100 personal allowance. Rates range from about 23.4% to 26.3%.

Church tax (kirkeskat): kr 0.00. Members of the Church of Denmark only; about 0.87% on average.

State tax (bundskat, mellemskat, topskat): kr 59,665.89. Bottom tax 12.01% above the allowance; middle tax 7.5% above DKK 641,200; top tax 7.5% above DKK 777,900; top-top tax 5% above DKK 2,592,700 (the new 2026 structure).

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Full-time employee aged 18 or over
  • No pension contributions through pay; each person is taxed individually

Leaves out:

  • Individual municipal tax rates
  • Labour-market and private pension contributions
  • Senior and single-parent employment deductions
  • Interest, union dues, commuting and other deductions

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • The national average municipal rate is given as 24.94%–25.43% by different sources; 25.05% is used
  • ATP amount is from a single secondary source
