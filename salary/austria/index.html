What comes out of pay in Austria

Taxable regular pay: €34,980.86. Twelve monthly payments less social insurance and the €132 allowance for work expenses.

Social insurance on monthly pay: €7,744.29. 18.07% (health 3.87%, pension 10.25%, unemployment 2.95%, chamber 0.5%, housing 0.5%) of each of the 12 monthly payments up to €6,930. Unemployment insurance is lower on monthly pay up to €2,630.

Social insurance on the 13th and 14th salaries: €1,219.29. The same rates less the chamber and housing contributions, on up to €13,860.

Income tax on monthly pay (Lohnsteuer): €5,091.26. 2026 tariff: nothing to €13,539, then 20%, 30%, 40%, 48% and 50%, and 55% above €1 million. Less the €496 commuter tax credit (Verkehrsabsetzbetrag) and its €804 supplement for lower incomes.

Income tax on the 13th and 14th salaries: €318.21. Taxed separately: the first €620 free, then 6% up to €25,000, 27% to €50,000, 35.75% to €83,333.

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Salaried employee (Angestellter) paid in 14 equal instalments, the usual Austrian pattern
  • No children; each person is taxed individually
  • No commuter allowance (Pendlerpauschale)

Leaves out:

  • Family bonus (Familienbonus Plus) and other credits for children or sole earners
  • Commuter allowance and the higher commuter credit
  • Refund of social insurance for very low incomes (negative tax)
  • The €2,100 exemption limit for small special payments

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • The unemployment insurance reduction thresholds (€2,225 / €2,427 / €2,630 a month) come from a single secondary source
  • Special-payment tax bands (§ 67 EStG) were not re-checked for 2026
