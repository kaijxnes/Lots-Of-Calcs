How $25 an hour after tax is worked out

The hourly rate is turned into a yearly salary first — $25 × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 — because tax is worked out on yearly income. The take-home is then spread back over the year.

Federal income tax: $4,060.00.

Social Security: $3,224.00. 6.2% of wages up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500.

Medicare: $754.00.

Additional Medicare tax: $0.00. 0.9% on wages above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly). Employers withhold it from $200,000 whatever the status; the threshold here is the liability.

New York State income tax: $2,211.00. 2026 rates, after the cuts to the five lowest brackets. Includes the supplemental tax (benefit recapture).

Disability insurance: $31.20. 0.5% of wages, capped at $0.60 a week.

Paid Family Leave: $224.64. 0.432% of wages, capped at $411.91 for 2026.

New York City income tax: $1,427.55. Charged on New York State taxable income, less the school tax credit ($63, or $125 married, up to $250,000 of income) and the school tax rate reduction credit.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $25 an hour a year?

$25 an hour for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks is $52,000 a year before tax. After federal and state taxes and payroll deductions in New York City, that leaves $40,067.61 a year.

How much is $25 an hour after tax a month?

$3,338.97 a month, or $770.53 a week, working 40 hours a week. Each hour worked takes home $19.26 once 22.9% has gone in deductions.

What if I work part-time at $25 an hour?

At 20 hours a week, $25 an hour is $26,000 a year and leaves $21,572.60 after tax, or $1,797.72 a month. Lower earnings are taxed at a lower average rate, so take-home doesn't fall quite in proportion to the hours. The table above covers other weekly hours.

How much of an overtime hour do I keep?

At this level of pay your marginal rate is 29%, so an extra hour at $25 leaves about $17.73 after tax. Overtime paid at a higher rate keeps the same share of the higher figure.
