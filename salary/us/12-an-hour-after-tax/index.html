How much is $12 an hour a year?

$12 an hour for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks is $24,960.00 a year before tax.

How much is $12 an hour after taxes?

It depends on the state. A single filer on the standard deduction takes home between $20,706.43 a year in Oregon and $22,164.56 in Florida; in the middle state, Montana, it is $21,748.14, or $1,812.35 a month.

Which states take the least from $12 an hour?

States with no tax on wages come out on top: Florida, North Dakota, New Hampshire, Nevada, Ohio lead the table. Federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare are the same in every state.
