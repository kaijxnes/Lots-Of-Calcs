How $100,000 after tax is worked out

Maryland taxable income: $93,450.00. Standard deduction of $3,350 ($6,700 married) and a $3,200 exemption each, which shrinks above $100,000 of income ($150,000 married).

Federal income tax: $13,170.00.

Social Security: $6,200.00. 6.2% of wages up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500.

Medicare: $1,450.00.

Additional Medicare tax: $0.00. 0.9% on wages above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly). Employers withhold it from $200,000 whatever the status; the threshold here is the liability.

Maryland income tax: $4,386.38.

County income tax: $2,990.40. Every Maryland county (and Baltimore City) levies its own tax on Maryland taxable income, from 2.25% to 3.3%.
