How $150,000 after tax is worked out

Connecticut personal exemption: $0.00. Reduced by $1,000 for each $1,000 (or part) of income above $30,000 ($48,000 married).

Federal income tax: $24,734.00.

Social Security: $9,300.00. 6.2% of wages up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500.

Medicare: $2,175.00.

Additional Medicare tax: $0.00. 0.9% on wages above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly). Employers withhold it from $200,000 whatever the status; the threshold here is the liability.

Connecticut income tax: $8,225.00. Less the personal tax credit, a percentage of the tax that falls from 75% to nothing as income rises.

Paid Leave: $750.00. 0.5% of wages up to the Social Security wage base.
