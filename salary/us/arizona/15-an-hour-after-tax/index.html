Frequently asked questions

How much is $15 an hour a year?

$15 an hour for 40 hours a week over 52 weeks is $31,200 a year before tax. After federal and state taxes and payroll deductions in Arizona, that leaves $26,871.70 a year.

How much is $15 an hour after tax a month?

$2,239.31 a month, or $516.76 a week, working 40 hours a week. Each hour worked takes home $12.92 once 13.9% has gone in deductions.

What if I work part-time at $15 an hour?

At 20 hours a week, $15 an hour is $15,600 a year and leaves $14,406.60 after tax, or $1,200.55 a month. Lower earnings are taxed at a lower average rate, so take-home doesn't fall quite in proportion to the hours. The table above covers other weekly hours.

How much of an overtime hour do I keep?

At this level of pay your marginal rate is 22%, so an extra hour at $15 leaves about $11.68 after tax. Overtime paid at a higher rate keeps the same share of the higher figure.
