What comes out of pay in Norway

Ordinary income (alminnelig inntekt) after allowances: kr 389,760.00. Pay less the minimum deduction (46%, at most NOK 95,700) and the NOK 114,540 personal allowance.

National insurance (trygdeavgift): kr 45,600.00. 7.6% of pay.

Tax on ordinary income: kr 85,747.20. 22%.

Bracket tax (trinnskatt): kr 12,835.40. On gross pay: 1.7% from NOK 226,100, 4.0% from 318,300, 13.7% from 725,050, 16.8% from 980,100 and 17.8% from 1,467,200.

About these figures

They use the 2026 tax year, which runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Resident employee in mainland Norway
  • Employment income only; each person is taxed individually

Leaves out:

  • The Finnmark and Nord-Troms action zone, which has lower rates
  • The low-income limit and phase-in for national insurance (only affects very small incomes)
  • Interest deductions, union dues, pension savings and other deductions
  • Wealth tax
