What comes out of pay in India

Taxable income: ₹11,25,000.00. After the ₹75,000 standard deduction for salaried employees.

Income tax and surcharge: ₹0.00. New tax regime (the default): nothing to ₹4 lakh, then 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25% in steps of ₹4 lakh, and 30% above ₹24 lakh.

Health and education cess: ₹0.00. 4% of income tax plus surcharge.

About these figures

They use the 2026-27 tax year, which runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip.

Assumes:

  • Resident individual under 60 on the new tax regime
  • Salary is the only income

Leaves out:

  • The old tax regime and its deductions (80C, HRA, home loan interest)
  • Employee Provident Fund contributions (12% of basic pay, which depends on how the salary is structured)
  • State professional tax (up to ₹2,500 a year)
  • Employer NPS contributions

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • Rates come from secondary sources that agree the 2026 Budget left the new-regime slabs unchanged under the Income Tax Act 2025

Sources:

  • Income Tax Department — tax rates for individuals
  • Income Tax Act, 2025
