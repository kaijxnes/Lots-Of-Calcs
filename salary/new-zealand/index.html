What comes out of pay in New Zealand

Independent earner tax credit: $0.00. $520 a year for incomes from $24,000 to $66,000, falling by 13 cents per dollar to nothing at $70,000.

Income tax (PAYE): $16,277.50. 10.5% to $15,600, 17.5% to $53,500, 30% to $78,100, 33% to $180,000, 39% above.

ACC earners' levy: $1,400.00. 1.75% of earnings up to $156,641 in 2026–27.

About these figures

They use the 2026-27 tax year, which runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. The rates have not yet been checked by a person against the official sources — check them here before relying on them. This is an estimate, not a payslip. When every rate was last checked.

Assumes:

  • Tax resident on a standard (M) tax code
  • Not receiving Working for Families, NZ Super or a main benefit (which rule out the independent earner credit)

Leaves out:

  • KiwiSaver contributions (employee default rate 3–4%)
  • Student loan repayments (12% above the threshold)
  • Secondary tax codes

Not yet confirmed for this year:

  • Figures are from secondary payroll and calculator sources that agree with each other; not checked against Inland Revenue or ACC directly
