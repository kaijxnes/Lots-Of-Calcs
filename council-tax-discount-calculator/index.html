The single person discount

A full Council Tax bill is based on at least 2 adults living in a home. You get 25% off if you pay Council Tax and either you live on your own or everyone else in your home is disregarded. You get 50% off if everyone living in your household is disregarded, and households where everyone is a full-time student don't have to pay at all. On a £2,000 bill, the single person discount saves £500 a year.

Who is disregarded: people under 18; people on certain apprentice schemes; 18 or 19 year olds in full-time education; full-time students at college or university; under 25s funded by the Education and Skills Funding Agency; student nurses; foreign language assistants registered with the British Council; people who are severely mentally impaired; live-in carers for someone who isn't their partner, spouse or child under 18; and diplomats.

You need to apply to your council for a discount, even if you're disregarded. If you get a discount by mistake, you must contact your council, or you could get a fine. Bills are usually split into 10 monthly payments; your council can spread them over 12 if you're having trouble paying.

Disabled Band Reduction Scheme

If you live in a larger home than you'd need if you or someone you live with weren't disabled (for example an extra bathroom, kitchen or room, or extra space for a wheelchair), your bill can be reduced to the next band down. In band A it's 17% off instead. Bands are charged in fixed proportions of band D:

Frequently asked questions

How much is the single person discount?

25% off your Council Tax if you're the only adult who counts in your home. On a £2,000 bill that's £500 a year, leaving £1,500.

Do students count for Council Tax?

No: full-time students are disregarded. If you live with only full-time students you get 25% off, and a home where everyone is a full-time student doesn't have to pay.

Do children count for Council Tax?

No: people under 18 are disregarded, so a single parent living only with children under 18 can get the 25% single person discount.

What if everyone in the home is disregarded?

You get 50% off, or 100% off if everyone is severely mentally impaired or a full-time student.

Do I have to apply for the single person discount?

Yes: you need to apply to your council, even if you're disregarded. If you get a discount by mistake you must contact your council, or you could get a fine, and the council may ask you to pay the discount back.
