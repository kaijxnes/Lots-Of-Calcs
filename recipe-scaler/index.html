Frequently asked questions

      
How do I scale a recipe?

      
Divide the servings you want by the servings the recipe makes, and multiply every quantity by that. A recipe for 4 made for 6 is × 1.5: 250g flour becomes 375g and 2 eggs become 3.

      
How do I halve a recipe with one egg?

      
Beat the egg and use half of it — about half the weight. Or keep the whole egg if the recipe is forgiving, such as pancakes.

      
Do cooking times change when I scale a recipe?

      
Often, but not in proportion. A bigger cake in a bigger tin can take longer; the same cakes in more tins take about the same time. Check it's cooked rather than multiplying the time.

      
What about tin sizes?

      
When you double a cake, use a tin with about twice the area, or two tins. The area of a round tin goes up with the square of its width, so a 23cm tin holds about a third more than a 20cm one.
