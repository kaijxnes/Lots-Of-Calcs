How to use it

This calculator handles the four basic operations — addition, subtraction, multiplication and division — plus percentages, all with full keyboard support.

Type numbers and operators directly, or click the on-screen keys; press Enter or = to see the result.

Like a pocket calculator, it works out each step as you go: press an operator and the sum so far is calculated straight away. That means 2 + 3 × 4 gives 20 — it adds 2 and 3, then multiplies by 4. If you want multiplication done before addition, the way it's written on paper (2 + 3 × 4 = 14), use the scientific calculator, which follows the order of operations.

Using the % key

The % key divides the number on screen by 100. Use it to take a percentage of something: 240 × 15 % = gives 36, which is 15% of 240.

To add a percentage, multiply by 1 plus the percentage: 240 plus 15% is 240 × 1.15 = 276. To take one off, multiply by 1 minus it: 240 less 15% is 240 × 0.85 = 204. For percentage change, “what percent of” and reverse percentages, the percentage calculator does each in one step.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my keyboard with this calculator?

Yes. Number keys and the operator keys work as you would expect, Enter acts as equals, Backspace deletes the last digit, and Escape clears the calculator.

How do I clear just the last digit instead of everything?

Press Backspace to remove the last digit you typed. Escape (or the clear button) resets the whole calculation.

Why does 2 + 3 × 4 give 20 instead of 14?

This is a step-by-step calculator, like most pocket calculators: each operation is completed when you press the next operator, so 2 + 3 is worked out before the × 4. For the order of operations taught in school (BODMAS), use the scientific calculator, which gives 14.

How do I work out a percentage of a number?

Type the number, press ×, type the percentage, then press % and =. 80 × 25 % = gives 20: the % key turns 25 into 0.25 before the multiplication.

Can I chain calculations together?

Yes. Keep pressing operators and numbers and the running total updates at each step: 12 + 8 × 3 − 10 = gives 50. After pressing =, type an operator to carry on from the answer, or press Escape to start a new sum.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type leaves your device.
