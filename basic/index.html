About this calculator

This calculator handles the four basic operations — addition, subtraction, multiplication and division — plus percentages, all with full keyboard support.

Type numbers and operators directly, or click the on-screen keys; press Enter or = to see the result.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my keyboard with this calculator?

Yes. Number keys and the operator keys work as you would expect, Enter acts as equals, Backspace deletes the last digit, and Escape clears the calculator.

How do I clear just the last digit instead of everything?

Press Backspace to remove the last digit you typed. Escape (or the clear button) resets the whole calculation.
