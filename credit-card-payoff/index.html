Frequently asked questions

How long will it take to pay off my credit card with minimum payments?

It depends on how your card works out the minimum. A £3,000 balance at 24.9% APR, paying 1% of the balance plus interest (at least £25), takes 15 years 5 months and costs £5,092 in interest. Paying a fixed £150 a month clears it in 2 years 3 months for £916.

Why do minimum payments take so long?

Because the minimum shrinks as the balance does: most of each payment goes on interest, and as the balance falls the minimum falls with it. Paying a fixed amount — even the first month's minimum, kept the same — clears it far sooner.

How is credit card interest worked out?

Roughly the APR ÷ 12 on the balance each month. Card companies work it out daily and the APR includes some fees, so your statement will differ slightly; this is close enough to compare plans.

Should I move the balance to a 0% card?

A 0% balance transfer can save a lot if you can clear the balance before the offer ends — set the APR to 0 here to see the monthly payment that does it, and remember the transfer fee.
