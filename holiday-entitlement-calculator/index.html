Frequently asked questions

How much holiday am I entitled to?

Almost all workers get 5.6 weeks' paid holiday a year. Working 5 days a week that's 28 days; it's capped at 28 days even if you work 6 days a week.

How much holiday do part-time workers get?

5.6 times the days you work a week: 16.8 days for a 3-day week, 22.4 days for 4 days. It's the same 5.6 weeks off as full-time staff.

How is holiday worked out for irregular hours?

For leave years starting on or after 1 April 2024, irregular-hours and part-year workers build up holiday at 12.07% of the hours they actually work in each pay period.

Are bank holidays included?

They can be: employers can count bank holidays as part of the 5.6 weeks. There's no legal right to bank holidays off on top.
