How it's worked out

The battery: the MG ZS EV Long Range has 68.3 kWh of usable capacity. Some energy is lost as heat while charging, so filling it from empty draws about 75.9 kWh from the grid at home.

The price: 75.9 kWh × 26.32p, the Ofgem price cap unit rate for 1 October to 31 December 2026 (Direct Debit, average for England, Scotland and Wales; no VAT on electricity from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027), is £19.97. Your tariff may be different; the table shows other prices.

Per mile: EV Database estimates 304 Wh a mile in real-world driving, so each mile takes 0.338 kWh from the grid, 8.9p at 26.32p.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to charge a MG ZS EV at home?

About £19.97 from empty to full at the Oct–Dec 2026 price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh: 75.9 kWh from the grid to put 68.3 kWh into the battery, allowing for 10% lost in charging. On an overnight EV tariff at 7.5p it would be £5.69.

How much does a MG ZS EV cost per mile?

About 8.9p a mile charged at home at the price cap rate, using EV Database's real-world estimate of 304 Wh per mile (3.3 miles per kWh). That's £889.03 for 10,000 miles a year.

How much is a rapid charge for a MG ZS EV?

Charging from 10% to 80% — the usual rapid-charger stop — puts in 47.8 kWh, about £39.76 at 79p per kWh. Rapid charger prices vary a lot by network and many have membership rates, so check the price on the charger.

How long does a MG ZS EV take to charge at home?

About 10h 15m from empty to full on a 7.4 kW home wallbox, or roughly 70% of that for a 10–80% top-up. A three-pin plug at around 2.3 kW takes about three times as long.

How far does a MG ZS EV go on a full charge?

273 miles on the official WLTP test, and about 225 miles in mixed real-world driving by EV Database's estimate. Cold weather and motorway speeds cut range most.
