Tax rates and when they were checked

Every tax and payroll rate set the salary calculators use.

Each country, state and region has its own file of rates and thresholds for its current tax year. They are checked every month against the tax authority's published figures and any change is put in straight away; 2 of 70 have been checked so far. New tax years are prepared as soon as their figures are published and switch on when the year starts.

Last checked is the last time the figures were compared with current published sources and found to be up to date. Verified means a person has also gone through every figure against the official source. Until then, treat the results as estimates. Not yet confirmed counts figures that had not been published for the year when the file was made, where the previous year's amount is used.
