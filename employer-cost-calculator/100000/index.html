Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to employ someone on £100,000?

About £115,570.90 a year (£9,630.91 a month) in 2026/27: the salary, £14,250.00 of employer National Insurance and £1,320.90 for the minimum 3% pension contribution. That's 15.6% on top of the salary.

How much employer National Insurance is there on £100,000?

£14,250.00 a year: 15% of the £95,000 above £5,000. For an employee under 21 or an apprentice under 25 it's £7,459.50.

How much does the employee take home on £100,000?

About £68,557.40 a year (£5,713.12 a month) in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, with tax code 1257L, before their own pension contribution or any student loan. The salary calculator has the full breakdown.

Does Employment Allowance reduce the cost?

If the employer is eligible, it takes up to £10,500 a year off its National Insurance bill across all staff, which would cover the £14,250.00 here.
