Frequently asked questions

How much protein do I need a day?

The UK Reference Nutrient Intake for adults is 0.75 g per kg of body weight: 45 g for a 60 kg adult, 60 g at 80 kg. It's set to cover the needs of nearly all adults.

Do I need more if I exercise?

Possibly — the British Heart Foundation says people who are more active may need more than the reference intake, which is set for the general population. For sports targets, ask a dietitian or your coach.

How much protein in pregnancy?

An extra 6 g a day on top of the adult figure, and an extra 11 g a day while breastfeeding in the first six months (8 g after).

An estimate for general information, not medical advice. Source: British Nutrition Foundation — Nutrition requirements; British Heart Foundation — Protein: how much do you need?.
