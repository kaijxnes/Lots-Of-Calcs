Protein for 100 kg

At 100 kg you need about 75 g of protein a day.

75 g
protein a day

The UK Reference Nutrient Intake is 0.75 g per kg of body weight a day — enough for nearly everyone — plus 6 g in pregnancy and 11 g (8 g after six months) when breastfeeding. For people over 65 the British Heart Foundation suggests 1–1.2 g per kg.

Frequently asked questions

How much protein do I need at 100 kg?

75 g a day: 100 × 0.75 g, the UK Reference Nutrient Intake for adults.

How much protein at 100 kg if I'm over 65?

100–120 g a day, at the 1–1.2 g per kg the British Heart Foundation suggests for older adults.

What about 100 kg and pregnant?

81 g a day — the adult figure plus 6 g.

An estimate for general information, not medical advice. Source: British Nutrition Foundation — Nutrition requirements; British Heart Foundation — Protein: how much do you need?.
