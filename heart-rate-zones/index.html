Frequently asked questions

How do I work out my maximum heart rate?

The usual estimate is 220 minus your age: 180 beats a minute at 40, 170 at 50. It's an average — your own maximum can be higher or lower.

What is my target heart rate for exercise?

The CDC gives 64–76% of your maximum for moderate-intensity exercise and 77–93% for vigorous. At 50 that's 109–129 bpm for moderate and 131–158 bpm for vigorous.

How do I check my heart rate?

Count your pulse at the wrist or neck for 30 seconds and double it, or use a heart rate monitor or watch.
