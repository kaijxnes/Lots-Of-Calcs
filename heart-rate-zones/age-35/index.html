Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum heart rate for a 35-year-old?

About 185 beats a minute: 220 − 35 = 185.

What is a good target heart rate at 35?

118–141 bpm for moderate-intensity exercise (64–76% of maximum) and 142–172 bpm for vigorous (77–93%), using the CDC's ranges.

An estimate for general information, not medical advice. Source: CDC — Target heart rate and estimated maximum heart rate.
