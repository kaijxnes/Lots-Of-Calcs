Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum heart rate for a 40-year-old?

About 180 beats a minute: 220 − 40 = 180.

What is a good target heart rate at 40?

115–137 bpm for moderate-intensity exercise (64–76% of maximum) and 139–167 bpm for vigorous (77–93%), using the CDC's ranges.
