Frequently asked questions

What is the maximum heart rate for a 20-year-old?

About 200 beats a minute: 220 − 20 = 200.

What is a good target heart rate at 20?

128–152 bpm for moderate-intensity exercise (64–76% of maximum) and 154–186 bpm for vigorous (77–93%), using the CDC's ranges.
