Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1935 as a year?

Nineteen thirty-five. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and thirty-five (US: one thousand nine hundred thirty-five).

How do you write 1935 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and thirty-five. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred thirty-five.

How do you write 1935 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and thirty-five pounds only" on the words line and £1935.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred thirty-five and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 1935?

1935th, written one thousand nine hundred and thirty-fifth.
