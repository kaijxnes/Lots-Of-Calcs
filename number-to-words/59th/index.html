Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 59th?

Fifty-ninth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (fifty-nine).

Why is it 59th?

The suffix follows the last word: ninth ends "th", so 59 is written 59th.

How do you write the 59th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 59th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her fifty-ninth birthday", "59th place".

What is 59th as a number?

59 — fifty-nine. 59th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
