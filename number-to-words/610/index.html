Frequently asked questions

How do you write 610 in words?

Six hundred and ten. In American English the "and" is usually left out: six hundred ten.

How do you write 610 on a cheque?

Write "Six hundred and ten pounds only" on the words line and £610.00 in the box. On a US check, "Six hundred ten and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "six hundred and ten" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (six hundred and ten); American English usually leaves it out (six hundred ten). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 610?

610th, written six hundred and tenth.
