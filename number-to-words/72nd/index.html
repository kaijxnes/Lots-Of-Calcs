Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 72nd?

Seventy-second. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (seventy-two).

Why is it 72nd?

The suffix follows the last word: second ends "nd", so 72 is written 72nd.

How do you write the 72nd in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 72nd is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her seventy-second birthday", "72nd place".

What is 72nd as a number?

72 — seventy-two. 72nd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
