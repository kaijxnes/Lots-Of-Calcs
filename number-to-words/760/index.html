Frequently asked questions

How do you write 760 in words?

Seven hundred and sixty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: seven hundred sixty.

How do you write 760 on a cheque?

Write "Seven hundred and sixty pounds only" on the words line and £760.00 in the box. On a US check, "Seven hundred sixty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "seven hundred and sixty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (seven hundred and sixty); American English usually leaves it out (seven hundred sixty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 760?

760th, written seven hundred and sixtieth.
