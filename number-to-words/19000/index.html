Frequently asked questions

How do you write 19,000 in words?

Nineteen thousand.

How do you write 19,000 on a cheque?

Write "Nineteen thousand pounds only" on the words line and £19,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Nineteen thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "nineteen thousand" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in nineteen thousand in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 19,000?

19000th, written nineteen thousandth.
