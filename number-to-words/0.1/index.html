Frequently asked questions

How do you write 0.1 in words?

Zero point one. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is one tenth.

How do you write £0.10 in words?

Ten pence only on a cheque; "tenp" when you say it. On a US check, $0.10 is "Zero and 10/100 dollars".

What is 0.1 as a fraction?

1/10. See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 0.1 as a percentage?

10% — multiply by 100.
