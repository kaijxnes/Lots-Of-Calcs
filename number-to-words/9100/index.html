Frequently asked questions

How do you write 9,100 in words?

Nine thousand one hundred.

How do you write 9,100 on a cheque?

Write "Nine thousand one hundred pounds only" on the words line and £9,100.00 in the box. On a US check, "Nine thousand one hundred and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "nine thousand one hundred" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in nine thousand one hundred in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 9,100?

9100th, written nine thousand one hundredth.
