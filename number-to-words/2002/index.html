Frequently asked questions

How do you say 2002 as a year?

Two thousand and two. Written out as a number in full it is two thousand and two (US: two thousand two).

How do you write 2002 in words?

Two thousand and two. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two thousand two.

How do you write 2002 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand and two pounds only" on the words line and £2002.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand two and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two thousand and two" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two thousand and two); American English usually leaves it out (two thousand two). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 2002?

2002nd, written two thousand and second.
