Frequently asked questions

How do you write 2,100 in words?

Two thousand one hundred.

How do you write 2,100 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand one hundred pounds only" on the words line and £2,100.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand one hundred and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two thousand one hundred" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in two thousand one hundred in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 2,100?

2100th, written two thousand one hundredth.
