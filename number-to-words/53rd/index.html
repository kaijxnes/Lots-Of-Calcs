Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 53rd?

Fifty-third. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (fifty-three).

Why is it 53rd?

The suffix follows the last word: third ends "rd", so 53 is written 53rd.

How do you write the 53rd in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 53rd is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her fifty-third birthday", "53rd place".

What is 53rd as a number?

53 — fifty-three. 53rd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
