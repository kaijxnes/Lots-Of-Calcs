Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 6th?

Sixth.

Why is it 6th?

The suffix follows the last word: sixth ends "th", so 6 is written 6th.

How do you write the 6th in a date?

In the UK, "the 6th of March" or "6 March"; in the US, "March 6th" or "March 6". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 6 March 2027.

What is 6th as a number?

6 — six. 6th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
