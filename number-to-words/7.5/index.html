Frequently asked questions

How do you write 7.5 in words?

Seven point five. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is seven and a half.

How do you write £7.50 in words?

Seven pounds and fifty pence only on a cheque; "seven pounds 50" when you say it. On a US check, $7.50 is "Seven and 50/100 dollars".

What is 7.5 as a fraction?

7 1/2 (15/2 as an improper fraction). See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 7.5 as a percentage?

750% — multiply by 100.
