Frequently asked questions

How do you write 220 in words?

Two hundred and twenty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred twenty.

How do you write 220 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and twenty pounds only" on the words line and £220.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred twenty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and twenty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and twenty); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred twenty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 220?

220th, written two hundred and twentieth.
