Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 35th?

Thirty-fifth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (thirty-five).

Why is it 35th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 35 is written 35th.

How do you write the 35th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 35th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her thirty-fifth birthday", "35th place".

What is 35th as a number?

35 — thirty-five. 35th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
