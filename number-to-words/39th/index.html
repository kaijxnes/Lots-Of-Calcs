Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 39th?

Thirty-ninth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (thirty-nine).

Why is it 39th?

The suffix follows the last word: ninth ends "th", so 39 is written 39th.

How do you write the 39th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 39th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her thirty-ninth birthday", "39th place".

What is 39th as a number?

39 — thirty-nine. 39th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
