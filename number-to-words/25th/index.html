Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 25th?

Twenty-fifth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (twenty-five).

Why is it 25th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 25 is written 25th.

How do you write the 25th in a date?

In the UK, "the 25th of March" or "25 March"; in the US, "March 25th" or "March 25". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 25 March 2027.

What is 25th as a number?

25 — twenty-five. 25th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
