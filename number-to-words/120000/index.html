Frequently asked questions

How do you write 120,000 in words?

One hundred and twenty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred twenty thousand. In India it is written one lakh twenty thousand (1,20,000).

How do you write 120,000 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and twenty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £120,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred twenty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and twenty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and twenty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred twenty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 120,000?

120000th, written one hundred and twenty thousandth.
