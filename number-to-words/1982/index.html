Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1982 as a year?

Nineteen eighty-two. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and eighty-two (US: one thousand nine hundred eighty-two).

How do you write 1982 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and eighty-two. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred eighty-two.

How do you write 1982 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and eighty-two pounds only" on the words line and £1982.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred eighty-two and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 1982?

1982nd, written one thousand nine hundred and eighty-second.
