Frequently asked questions

How do you write 4,500 in words?

Four thousand five hundred.

How do you write 4,500 on a cheque?

Write "Four thousand five hundred pounds only" on the words line and £4,500.00 in the box. On a US check, "Four thousand five hundred and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "four thousand five hundred" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in four thousand five hundred in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 4,500?

4500th, written four thousand five hundredth.
