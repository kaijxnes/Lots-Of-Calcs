Frequently asked questions

How do you write 90 in words?

Ninety.

How do you write 90 on a cheque?

Write "Ninety pounds only" on the words line and £90.00 in the box. On a US check, "Ninety and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "ninety" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in ninety in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 90?

90th, written ninetieth.
