Frequently asked questions

How do you write 250,000 in words?

Two hundred and fifty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred fifty thousand. In India it is written two lakh fifty thousand (2,50,000).

How do you write 250,000 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and fifty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £250,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred fifty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and fifty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and fifty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred fifty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 250,000?

250000th, written two hundred and fifty thousandth.
