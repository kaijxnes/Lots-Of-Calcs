Frequently asked questions

How do you write 58 in words?

Fifty-eight.

How do you write 58 on a cheque?

Write "Fifty-eight pounds only" on the words line and £58.00 in the box. On a US check, "Fifty-eight and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 58?

58th, written fifty-eighth.
