Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 30th?

Thirtieth.

Why is it 30th?

The suffix follows the last word: thirtieth ends "th", so 30 is written 30th.

How do you write the 30th in a date?

In the UK, "the 30th of March" or "30 March"; in the US, "March 30th" or "March 30". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 30 March 2027.

What is 30th as a number?

30 — thirty. 30th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
