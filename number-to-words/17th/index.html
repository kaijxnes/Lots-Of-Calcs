Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 17th?

Seventeenth.

Why is it 17th?

The suffix follows the last word: seventeenth ends "th", so 17 is written 17th.

How do you write the 17th in a date?

In the UK, "the 17th of March" or "17 March"; in the US, "March 17th" or "March 17". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 17 March 2027.

What is 17th as a number?

17 — seventeen. 17th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
