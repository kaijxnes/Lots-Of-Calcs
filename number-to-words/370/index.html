Frequently asked questions

How do you write 370 in words?

Three hundred and seventy. In American English the "and" is usually left out: three hundred seventy.

How do you write 370 on a cheque?

Write "Three hundred and seventy pounds only" on the words line and £370.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three hundred seventy and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "three hundred and seventy" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (three hundred and seventy); American English usually leaves it out (three hundred seventy). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 370?

370th, written three hundred and seventieth.
