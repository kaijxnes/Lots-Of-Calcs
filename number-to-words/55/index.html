Frequently asked questions

How do you write 55 in words?

Fifty-five.

How do you write 55 on a cheque?

Write "Fifty-five pounds only" on the words line and £55.00 in the box. On a US check, "Fifty-five and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 55?

55th, written fifty-fifth.
