Frequently asked questions

How do you say 2018 as a year?

Twenty eighteen. "Two thousand and eighteen" is also common, especially in formal writing. Written out as a number in full it is two thousand and eighteen (US: two thousand eighteen).

How do you write 2018 in words?

Two thousand and eighteen. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two thousand eighteen.

How do you write 2018 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand and eighteen pounds only" on the words line and £2018.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand eighteen and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two thousand and eighteen" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two thousand and eighteen); American English usually leaves it out (two thousand eighteen). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 2018?

2018th, written two thousand and eighteenth.
