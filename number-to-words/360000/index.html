Frequently asked questions

How do you write 360,000 in words?

Three hundred and sixty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: three hundred sixty thousand. In India it is written three lakh sixty thousand (3,60,000).

How do you write 360,000 on a cheque?

Write "Three hundred and sixty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £360,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three hundred sixty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "three hundred and sixty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (three hundred and sixty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (three hundred sixty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 360,000?

360000th, written three hundred and sixty thousandth.
