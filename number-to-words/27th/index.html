Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 27th?

Twenty-seventh. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (twenty-seven).

Why is it 27th?

The suffix follows the last word: seventh ends "th", so 27 is written 27th.

How do you write the 27th in a date?

In the UK, "the 27th of March" or "27 March"; in the US, "March 27th" or "March 27". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 27 March 2027.

What is 27th as a number?

27 — twenty-seven. 27th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
