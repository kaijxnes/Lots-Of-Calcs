Frequently asked questions

How do you write 870 in words?

Eight hundred and seventy. In American English the "and" is usually left out: eight hundred seventy.

How do you write 870 on a cheque?

Write "Eight hundred and seventy pounds only" on the words line and £870.00 in the box. On a US check, "Eight hundred seventy and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "eight hundred and seventy" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (eight hundred and seventy); American English usually leaves it out (eight hundred seventy). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 870?

870th, written eight hundred and seventieth.
