Frequently asked questions

How do you say 2004 as a year?

Two thousand and four. Written out as a number in full it is two thousand and four (US: two thousand four).

How do you write 2004 in words?

Two thousand and four. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two thousand four.

How do you write 2004 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand and four pounds only" on the words line and £2004.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand four and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two thousand and four" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two thousand and four); American English usually leaves it out (two thousand four). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 2004?

2004th, written two thousand and fourth.
