Frequently asked questions

How do you write 460 in words?

Four hundred and sixty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: four hundred sixty.

How do you write 460 on a cheque?

Write "Four hundred and sixty pounds only" on the words line and £460.00 in the box. On a US check, "Four hundred sixty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "four hundred and sixty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (four hundred and sixty); American English usually leaves it out (four hundred sixty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 460?

460th, written four hundred and sixtieth.
