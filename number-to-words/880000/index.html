Frequently asked questions

How do you write 880,000 in words?

Eight hundred and eighty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: eight hundred eighty thousand. In India it is written eight lakh eighty thousand (8,80,000).

How do you write 880,000 on a cheque?

Write "Eight hundred and eighty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £880,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Eight hundred eighty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "eight hundred and eighty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (eight hundred and eighty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (eight hundred eighty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 880,000?

880000th, written eight hundred and eighty thousandth.
