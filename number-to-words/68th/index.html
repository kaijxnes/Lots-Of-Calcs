Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 68th?

Sixty-eighth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (sixty-eight).

Why is it 68th?

The suffix follows the last word: eighth ends "th", so 68 is written 68th.

How do you write the 68th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 68th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her sixty-eighth birthday", "68th place".

What is 68th as a number?

68 — sixty-eight. 68th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
