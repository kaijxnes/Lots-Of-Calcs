Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 34th?

Thirty-fourth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (thirty-four).

Why is it 34th?

The suffix follows the last word: fourth ends "th", so 34 is written 34th.

How do you write the 34th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 34th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her thirty-fourth birthday", "34th place".

What is 34th as a number?

34 — thirty-four. 34th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
