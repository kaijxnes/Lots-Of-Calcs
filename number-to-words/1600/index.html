Frequently asked questions

How do you write 1,600 in words?

One thousand six hundred.

How do you write 1,600 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand six hundred pounds only" on the words line and £1,600.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand six hundred and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one thousand six hundred" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in one thousand six hundred in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 1,600?

1600th, written one thousand six hundredth.
