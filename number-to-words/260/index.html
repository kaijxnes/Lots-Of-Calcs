Frequently asked questions

How do you write 260 in words?

Two hundred and sixty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred sixty.

How do you write 260 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and sixty pounds only" on the words line and £260.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred sixty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and sixty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and sixty); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred sixty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 260?

260th, written two hundred and sixtieth.
