Frequently asked questions

How do you write 180,000 in words?

One hundred and eighty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred eighty thousand. In India it is written one lakh eighty thousand (1,80,000).

How do you write 180,000 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and eighty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £180,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred eighty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and eighty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and eighty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred eighty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 180,000?

180000th, written one hundred and eighty thousandth.
