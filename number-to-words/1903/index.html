Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1903 as a year?

Nineteen oh three. Some people say one thousand nine hundred and three instead. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and three (US: one thousand nine hundred three).

How do you write 1903 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and three. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred three.

How do you write 1903 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and three pounds only" on the words line and £1903.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred three and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one thousand nine hundred and three" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one thousand nine hundred and three); American English usually leaves it out (one thousand nine hundred three). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 1903?

1903rd, written one thousand nine hundred and third.
