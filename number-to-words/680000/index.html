Frequently asked questions

How do you write 680,000 in words?

Six hundred and eighty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: six hundred eighty thousand. In India it is written six lakh eighty thousand (6,80,000).

How do you write 680,000 on a cheque?

Write "Six hundred and eighty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £680,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Six hundred eighty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "six hundred and eighty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (six hundred and eighty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (six hundred eighty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 680,000?

680000th, written six hundred and eighty thousandth.
