Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 21st?

Twenty-first. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (twenty-one).

Why is it 21st?

The suffix follows the last word: first ends "st", so 21 is written 21st.

How do you write the 21st in a date?

In the UK, "the 21st of March" or "21 March"; in the US, "March 21st" or "March 21". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 21 March 2027.

What is 21st as a number?

21 — twenty-one. 21st is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
