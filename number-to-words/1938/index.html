Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1938 as a year?

Nineteen thirty-eight. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and thirty-eight (US: one thousand nine hundred thirty-eight).

How do you write 1938 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and thirty-eight. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred thirty-eight.

How do you write 1938 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and thirty-eight pounds only" on the words line and £1938.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred thirty-eight and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 1938?

1938th, written one thousand nine hundred and thirty-eighth.
