Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 29th?

Twenty-ninth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (twenty-nine).

Why is it 29th?

The suffix follows the last word: ninth ends "th", so 29 is written 29th.

How do you write the 29th in a date?

In the UK, "the 29th of March" or "29 March"; in the US, "March 29th" or "March 29". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 29 March 2027.

What is 29th as a number?

29 — twenty-nine. 29th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
