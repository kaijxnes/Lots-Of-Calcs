Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 43rd?

Forty-third. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (forty-three).

Why is it 43rd?

The suffix follows the last word: third ends "rd", so 43 is written 43rd.

How do you write the 43rd in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 43rd is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her forty-third birthday", "43rd place".

What is 43rd as a number?

43 — forty-three. 43rd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
