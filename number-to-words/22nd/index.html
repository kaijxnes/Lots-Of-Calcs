Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 22nd?

Twenty-second. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (twenty-two).

Why is it 22nd?

The suffix follows the last word: second ends "nd", so 22 is written 22nd.

How do you write the 22nd in a date?

In the UK, "the 22nd of March" or "22 March"; in the US, "March 22nd" or "March 22". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 22 March 2027.

What is 22nd as a number?

22 — twenty-two. 22nd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
