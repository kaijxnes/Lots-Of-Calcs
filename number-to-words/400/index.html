Frequently asked questions

How do you write 400 in words?

Four hundred.

How do you write 400 on a cheque?

Write "Four hundred pounds only" on the words line and £400.00 in the box. On a US check, "Four hundred and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "four hundred" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in four hundred in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 400?

400th, written four hundredth.
