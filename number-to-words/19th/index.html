Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 19th?

Nineteenth.

Why is it 19th?

The suffix follows the last word: nineteenth ends "th", so 19 is written 19th.

How do you write the 19th in a date?

In the UK, "the 19th of March" or "19 March"; in the US, "March 19th" or "March 19". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 19 March 2027.

What is 19th as a number?

19 — nineteen. 19th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
