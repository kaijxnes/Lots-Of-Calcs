Frequently asked questions

How do you write 1,000,000,000 in words?

One billion. In India it is written one hundred crore (1,00,00,00,000).

How do you write 1 billion in words?

1 billion is 1,000,000,000, written one billion. In India it's one hundred crore.

How do you write 1,000,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "One billion pounds only" on the words line and £1,000,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "One billion and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one billion" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in one billion in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 1,000,000,000?

1000000000th, written one billionth.

How many zeros are in a billion?

Nine: 1,000,000,000 is 10 to the power of 9 (10⁹). A billion is a thousand million in both British and American English today; the old British billion (a million million) is no longer used.
