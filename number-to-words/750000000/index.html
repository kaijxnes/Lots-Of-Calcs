Frequently asked questions

How do you write 750,000,000 in words?

Seven hundred and fifty million. In American English the "and" is usually left out: seven hundred fifty million. In India it is written seventy-five crore (75,00,00,000).

How do you write 750 million in words?

750 million is 750,000,000, written seven hundred and fifty million (US: seven hundred fifty million). In India it's seventy-five crore.

How do you write 750,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "Seven hundred and fifty million pounds only" on the words line and £750,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Seven hundred fifty million and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "seven hundred and fifty million" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (seven hundred and fifty million); American English usually leaves it out (seven hundred fifty million). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 750,000,000?

750000000th, written seven hundred and fifty millionth.
