Frequently asked questions

How do you write 240 in words?

Two hundred and forty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred forty.

How do you write 240 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and forty pounds only" on the words line and £240.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred forty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and forty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and forty); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred forty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 240?

240th, written two hundred and fortieth.
