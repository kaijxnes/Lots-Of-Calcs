Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 96th?

Ninety-sixth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (ninety-six).

Why is it 96th?

The suffix follows the last word: sixth ends "th", so 96 is written 96th.

How do you write the 96th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 96th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her ninety-sixth birthday", "96th place".

What is 96th as a number?

96 — ninety-six. 96th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
