Frequently asked questions

How do you write 0.2 in words?

Zero point two. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is one fifth.

How do you write £0.20 in words?

Twenty pence only on a cheque; "twentyp" when you say it. On a US check, $0.20 is "Zero and 20/100 dollars".

What is 0.2 as a fraction?
