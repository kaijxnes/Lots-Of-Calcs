Frequently asked questions

How do you write 0.9 in words?

Zero point nine. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is nine tenths.

How do you write £0.90 in words?

Ninety pence only on a cheque; "ninetyp" when you say it. On a US check, $0.90 is "Zero and 90/100 dollars".

What is 0.9 as a fraction?

9/10. See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 0.9 as a percentage?

90% — multiply by 100.
