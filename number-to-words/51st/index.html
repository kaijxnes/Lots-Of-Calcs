Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 51st?

Fifty-first. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (fifty-one).

Why is it 51st?

The suffix follows the last word: first ends "st", so 51 is written 51st.

How do you write the 51st in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 51st is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her fifty-first birthday", "51st place".

What is 51st as a number?

51 — fifty-one. 51st is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
