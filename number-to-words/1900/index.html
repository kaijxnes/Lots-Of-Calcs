Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1900 as a year?

Nineteen hundred. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred.

How do you write 1900 in words?

One thousand nine hundred.

How do you write 1900 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred pounds only" on the words line and £1900.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one thousand nine hundred" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in one thousand nine hundred in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 1900?

1900th, written one thousand nine hundredth.
