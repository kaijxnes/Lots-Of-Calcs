Frequently asked questions

How do you write 1.25 in words?

One point two five. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own — "point two five", not "point twenty-five". As a fraction it is one and a quarter.

How do you write £1.25 in words?

One pound and twenty-five pence only on a cheque; "one pound 25" when you say it. On a US check, $1.25 is "One and 25/100 dollars".

What is 1.25 as a fraction?

1 1/4 (5/4 as an improper fraction). See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 1.25 as a percentage?

125% — multiply by 100.
