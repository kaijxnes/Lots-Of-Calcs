Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 65th?

Sixty-fifth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (sixty-five).

Why is it 65th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 65 is written 65th.

How do you write the 65th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 65th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her sixty-fifth birthday", "65th place".

What is 65th as a number?

65 — sixty-five. 65th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
