Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 15th?

Fifteenth.

Why is it 15th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifteenth ends "th", so 15 is written 15th.

How do you write the 15th in a date?

In the UK, "the 15th of March" or "15 March"; in the US, "March 15th" or "March 15". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 15 March 2027.

What is 15th as a number?

15 — fifteen. 15th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
