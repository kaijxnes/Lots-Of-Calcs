Frequently asked questions

How do you write 10,000 in words?

Ten thousand.

How do you write 10,000 on a cheque?

Write "Ten thousand pounds only" on the words line and £10,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Ten thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "ten thousand" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in ten thousand in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 10,000?

10000th, written ten thousandth.
