Frequently asked questions

How do you write 410,000 in words?

Four hundred and ten thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: four hundred ten thousand. In India it is written four lakh ten thousand (4,10,000).

How do you write 410,000 on a cheque?

Write "Four hundred and ten thousand pounds only" on the words line and £410,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Four hundred ten thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "four hundred and ten thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (four hundred and ten thousand); American English usually leaves it out (four hundred ten thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 410,000?

410000th, written four hundred and ten thousandth.
