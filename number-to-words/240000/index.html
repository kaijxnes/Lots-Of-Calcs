Frequently asked questions

How do you write 240,000 in words?

Two hundred and forty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred forty thousand. In India it is written two lakh forty thousand (2,40,000).

How do you write 240,000 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and forty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £240,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred forty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and forty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and forty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred forty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 240,000?

240000th, written two hundred and forty thousandth.
