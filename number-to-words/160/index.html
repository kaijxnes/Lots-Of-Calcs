Frequently asked questions

How do you write 160 in words?

One hundred and sixty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred sixty.

How do you write 160 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and sixty pounds only" on the words line and £160.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred sixty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and sixty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and sixty); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred sixty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 160?

160th, written one hundred and sixtieth.
