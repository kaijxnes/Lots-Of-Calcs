Frequently asked questions

How do you write 375,000 in words?

Three hundred and seventy-five thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: three hundred seventy-five thousand. In India it is written three lakh seventy-five thousand (3,75,000).

How do you write 375,000 on a cheque?

Write "Three hundred and seventy-five thousand pounds only" on the words line and £375,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three hundred seventy-five thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 375,000?

375000th, written three hundred and seventy-five thousandth.
