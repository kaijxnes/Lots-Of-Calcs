Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 50th?

Fiftieth.

Why is it 50th?

The suffix follows the last word: fiftieth ends "th", so 50 is written 50th.

How do you write the 50th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 50th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her fiftieth birthday", "50th place".

What is 50th as a number?

50 — fifty. 50th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
