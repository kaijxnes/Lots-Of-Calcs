Frequently asked questions

How do you write 480,000 in words?

Four hundred and eighty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: four hundred eighty thousand. In India it is written four lakh eighty thousand (4,80,000).

How do you write 480,000 on a cheque?

Write "Four hundred and eighty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £480,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Four hundred eighty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "four hundred and eighty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (four hundred and eighty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (four hundred eighty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 480,000?

480000th, written four hundred and eighty thousandth.
