Frequently asked questions

How do you write 200,000,000 in words?

Two hundred million. In India it is written twenty crore (20,00,00,000).

How do you write 200 million in words?

200 million is 200,000,000, written two hundred million. In India it's twenty crore.

How do you write 200,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred million pounds only" on the words line and £200,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred million and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred million" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in two hundred million in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 200,000,000?

200000000th, written two hundred millionth.
