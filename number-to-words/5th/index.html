Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 5th?

Fifth.

Why is it 5th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 5 is written 5th.

How do you write the 5th in a date?

In the UK, "the 5th of March" or "5 March"; in the US, "March 5th" or "March 5". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 5 March 2027.

What is 5th as a number?

5 — five. 5th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
