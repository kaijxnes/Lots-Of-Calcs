Frequently asked questions

How do you write 510 in words?

Five hundred and ten. In American English the "and" is usually left out: five hundred ten.

How do you write 510 on a cheque?

Write "Five hundred and ten pounds only" on the words line and £510.00 in the box. On a US check, "Five hundred ten and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "five hundred and ten" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (five hundred and ten); American English usually leaves it out (five hundred ten). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 510?

510th, written five hundred and tenth.
