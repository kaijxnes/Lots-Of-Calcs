Frequently asked questions

How do you write 990 in words?

Nine hundred and ninety. In American English the "and" is usually left out: nine hundred ninety.

How do you write 990 on a cheque?

Write "Nine hundred and ninety pounds only" on the words line and £990.00 in the box. On a US check, "Nine hundred ninety and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "nine hundred and ninety" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (nine hundred and ninety); American English usually leaves it out (nine hundred ninety). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 990?

990th, written nine hundred and ninetieth.
