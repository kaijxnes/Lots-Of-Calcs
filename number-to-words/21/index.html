Frequently asked questions

How do you write 21 in words?

Twenty-one.

How do you write 21 on a cheque?

Write "Twenty-one pounds only" on the words line and £21.00 in the box. On a US check, "Twenty-one and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 21?

21st, written twenty-first.
