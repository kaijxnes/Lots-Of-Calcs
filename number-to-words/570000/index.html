Frequently asked questions

How do you write 570,000 in words?

Five hundred and seventy thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: five hundred seventy thousand. In India it is written five lakh seventy thousand (5,70,000).

How do you write 570,000 on a cheque?

Write "Five hundred and seventy thousand pounds only" on the words line and £570,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Five hundred seventy thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "five hundred and seventy thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (five hundred and seventy thousand); American English usually leaves it out (five hundred seventy thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 570,000?

570000th, written five hundred and seventy thousandth.
