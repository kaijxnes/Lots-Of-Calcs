Frequently asked questions

How do you write 4,000,000 in words?

Four million. In India it is written forty lakh (40,00,000).

How do you write 4,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "Four million pounds only" on the words line and £4,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Four million and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "four million" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in four million in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 4,000,000?

4000000th, written four millionth.
