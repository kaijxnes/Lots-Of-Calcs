Frequently asked questions

How do you write 200 in words?

Two hundred.

How do you write 200 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred pounds only" on the words line and £200.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in two hundred in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 200?

200th, written two hundredth.
