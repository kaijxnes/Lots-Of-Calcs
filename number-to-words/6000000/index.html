Frequently asked questions

How do you write 6,000,000 in words?

Six million. In India it is written sixty lakh (60,00,000).

How do you write 6,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "Six million pounds only" on the words line and £6,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Six million and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "six million" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in six million in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 6,000,000?

6000000th, written six millionth.
