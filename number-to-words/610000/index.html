Frequently asked questions

How do you write 610,000 in words?

Six hundred and ten thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: six hundred ten thousand. In India it is written six lakh ten thousand (6,10,000).

How do you write 610,000 on a cheque?

Write "Six hundred and ten thousand pounds only" on the words line and £610,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Six hundred ten thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "six hundred and ten thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (six hundred and ten thousand); American English usually leaves it out (six hundred ten thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 610,000?

610000th, written six hundred and ten thousandth.
