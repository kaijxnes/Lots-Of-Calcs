Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 3rd?

Third.

Why is it 3rd?

The suffix follows the last word: third ends "rd", so 3 is written 3rd.

How do you write the 3rd in a date?

In the UK, "the 3rd of March" or "3 March"; in the US, "March 3rd" or "March 3". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 3 March 2027.

What is 3rd as a number?

3 — three. 3rd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
