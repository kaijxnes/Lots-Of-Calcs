Frequently asked questions

How do you write 210 in words?

Two hundred and ten. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred ten.

How do you write 210 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and ten pounds only" on the words line and £210.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred ten and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and ten" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and ten); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred ten). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 210?

210th, written two hundred and tenth.
