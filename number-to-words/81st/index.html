Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 81st?

Eighty-first. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (eighty-one).

Why is it 81st?

The suffix follows the last word: first ends "st", so 81 is written 81st.

How do you write the 81st in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 81st is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her eighty-first birthday", "81st place".

What is 81st as a number?

81 — eighty-one. 81st is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
