Frequently asked questions

How do you write 630,000 in words?

Six hundred and thirty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: six hundred thirty thousand. In India it is written six lakh thirty thousand (6,30,000).

How do you write 630,000 on a cheque?

Write "Six hundred and thirty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £630,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Six hundred thirty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "six hundred and thirty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (six hundred and thirty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (six hundred thirty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 630,000?

630000th, written six hundred and thirty thousandth.
