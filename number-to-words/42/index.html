Frequently asked questions

How do you write 42 in words?

Forty-two.

How do you write 42 on a cheque?

Write "Forty-two pounds only" on the words line and £42.00 in the box. On a US check, "Forty-two and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 42?

42nd, written forty-second.
