Frequently asked questions

How do you write 130 in words?

One hundred and thirty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred thirty.

How do you write 130 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and thirty pounds only" on the words line and £130.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred thirty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and thirty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and thirty); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred thirty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 130?

130th, written one hundred and thirtieth.
