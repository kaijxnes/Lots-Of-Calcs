Frequently asked questions

How do you write 110,000 in words?

One hundred and ten thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred ten thousand. In India it is written one lakh ten thousand (1,10,000).

How do you write 110,000 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and ten thousand pounds only" on the words line and £110,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred ten thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and ten thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and ten thousand); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred ten thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 110,000?

110000th, written one hundred and ten thousandth.
