Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 44th?

Forty-fourth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (forty-four).

Why is it 44th?

The suffix follows the last word: fourth ends "th", so 44 is written 44th.

How do you write the 44th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 44th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her forty-fourth birthday", "44th place".

What is 44th as a number?

44 — forty-four. 44th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
