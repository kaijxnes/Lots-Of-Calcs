Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 20th?

Twentieth.

Why is it 20th?

The suffix follows the last word: twentieth ends "th", so 20 is written 20th.

How do you write the 20th in a date?

In the UK, "the 20th of March" or "20 March"; in the US, "March 20th" or "March 20". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 20 March 2027.

What is 20th as a number?

20 — twenty. 20th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
