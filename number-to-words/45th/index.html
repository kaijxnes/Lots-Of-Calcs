Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 45th?

Forty-fifth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (forty-five).

Why is it 45th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 45 is written 45th.

How do you write the 45th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 45th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her forty-fifth birthday", "45th place".

What is 45th as a number?

45 — forty-five. 45th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
