Frequently asked questions

How do you say 2029 as a year?

Twenty twenty-nine. "Two thousand and twenty-nine" is also common, especially in formal writing. Written out as a number in full it is two thousand and twenty-nine (US: two thousand twenty-nine).

How do you write 2029 in words?

Two thousand and twenty-nine. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two thousand twenty-nine.

How do you write 2029 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand and twenty-nine pounds only" on the words line and £2029.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand twenty-nine and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 2029?

2029th, written two thousand and twenty-ninth.
