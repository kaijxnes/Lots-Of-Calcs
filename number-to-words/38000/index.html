Frequently asked questions

How do you write 38,000 in words?

Thirty-eight thousand.

How do you write 38,000 on a cheque?

Write "Thirty-eight thousand pounds only" on the words line and £38,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Thirty-eight thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 38,000?

38000th, written thirty-eight thousandth.
