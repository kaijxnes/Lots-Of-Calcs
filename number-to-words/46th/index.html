Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 46th?

Forty-sixth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (forty-six).

Why is it 46th?

The suffix follows the last word: sixth ends "th", so 46 is written 46th.

How do you write the 46th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 46th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her forty-sixth birthday", "46th place".

What is 46th as a number?

46 — forty-six. 46th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
