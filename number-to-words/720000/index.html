Frequently asked questions

How do you write 720,000 in words?

Seven hundred and twenty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: seven hundred twenty thousand. In India it is written seven lakh twenty thousand (7,20,000).

How do you write 720,000 on a cheque?

Write "Seven hundred and twenty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £720,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Seven hundred twenty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "seven hundred and twenty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (seven hundred and twenty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (seven hundred twenty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 720,000?

720000th, written seven hundred and twenty thousandth.
