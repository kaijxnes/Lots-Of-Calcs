Frequently asked questions

How do you write 110 in words?

One hundred and ten. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred ten.

How do you write 110 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and ten pounds only" on the words line and £110.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred ten and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and ten" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and ten); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred ten). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 110?

110th, written one hundred and tenth.
