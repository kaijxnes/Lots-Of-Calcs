Frequently asked questions

How do you write 310 in words?

Three hundred and ten. In American English the "and" is usually left out: three hundred ten.

How do you write 310 on a cheque?

Write "Three hundred and ten pounds only" on the words line and £310.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three hundred ten and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "three hundred and ten" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (three hundred and ten); American English usually leaves it out (three hundred ten). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 310?

310th, written three hundred and tenth.
