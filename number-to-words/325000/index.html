Frequently asked questions

How do you write 325,000 in words?

Three hundred and twenty-five thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: three hundred twenty-five thousand. In India it is written three lakh twenty-five thousand (3,25,000).

How do you write 325,000 on a cheque?

Write "Three hundred and twenty-five thousand pounds only" on the words line and £325,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three hundred twenty-five thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 325,000?

325000th, written three hundred and twenty-five thousandth.
