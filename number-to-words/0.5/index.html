Frequently asked questions

How do you write 0.5 in words?

Zero point five. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is a half.

How do you write £0.50 in words?

Fifty pence only on a cheque; "fiftyp" when you say it. On a US check, $0.50 is "Zero and 50/100 dollars".

What is 0.5 as a fraction?

1/2. See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 0.5 as a percentage?

50% — multiply by 100.
