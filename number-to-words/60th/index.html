Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 60th?

Sixtieth.

Why is it 60th?

The suffix follows the last word: sixtieth ends "th", so 60 is written 60th.

How do you write the 60th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 60th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her sixtieth birthday", "60th place".

What is 60th as a number?

60 — sixty. 60th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
