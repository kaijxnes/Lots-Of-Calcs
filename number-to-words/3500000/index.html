Frequently asked questions

How do you write 3,500,000 in words?

Three million five hundred thousand. In India it is written thirty-five lakh (35,00,000).

How do you write 3,500,000 on a cheque?

Write "Three million five hundred thousand pounds only" on the words line and £3,500,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three million five hundred thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "three million five hundred thousand" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in three million five hundred thousand in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 3,500,000?

3500000th, written three million five hundred thousandth.
