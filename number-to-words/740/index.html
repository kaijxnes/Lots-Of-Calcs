Frequently asked questions

How do you write 740 in words?

Seven hundred and forty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: seven hundred forty.

How do you write 740 on a cheque?

Write "Seven hundred and forty pounds only" on the words line and £740.00 in the box. On a US check, "Seven hundred forty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "seven hundred and forty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (seven hundred and forty); American English usually leaves it out (seven hundred forty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 740?

740th, written seven hundred and fortieth.
