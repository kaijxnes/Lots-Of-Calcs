Frequently asked questions

How do you write 2.5 in words?

Two point five. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is two and a half.

How do you write £2.50 in words?

Two pounds and fifty pence only on a cheque; "two pounds 50" when you say it. On a US check, $2.50 is "Two and 50/100 dollars".

What is 2.5 as a fraction?

2 1/2 (5/2 as an improper fraction). See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 2.5 as a percentage?

250% — multiply by 100.
