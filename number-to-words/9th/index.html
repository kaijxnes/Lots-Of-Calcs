Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 9th?

Ninth.

Why is it 9th?

The suffix follows the last word: ninth ends "th", so 9 is written 9th.

How do you write the 9th in a date?

In the UK, "the 9th of March" or "9 March"; in the US, "March 9th" or "March 9". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 9 March 2027.

What is 9th as a number?

9 — nine. 9th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
