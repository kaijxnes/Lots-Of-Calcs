Frequently asked questions

How do you write 520 in words?

Five hundred and twenty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: five hundred twenty.

How do you write 520 on a cheque?

Write "Five hundred and twenty pounds only" on the words line and £520.00 in the box. On a US check, "Five hundred twenty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "five hundred and twenty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (five hundred and twenty); American English usually leaves it out (five hundred twenty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 520?

520th, written five hundred and twentieth.
