Frequently asked questions

How do you write 175,000 in words?

One hundred and seventy-five thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred seventy-five thousand. In India it is written one lakh seventy-five thousand (1,75,000).

How do you write 175,000 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and seventy-five thousand pounds only" on the words line and £175,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred seventy-five thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 175,000?

175000th, written one hundred and seventy-five thousandth.
