Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1911 as a year?

Nineteen eleven. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and eleven (US: one thousand nine hundred eleven).

How do you write 1911 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and eleven. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred eleven.

How do you write 1911 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and eleven pounds only" on the words line and £1911.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred eleven and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one thousand nine hundred and eleven" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one thousand nine hundred and eleven); American English usually leaves it out (one thousand nine hundred eleven). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 1911?

1911th, written one thousand nine hundred and eleventh.
