Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 92nd?

Ninety-second. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (ninety-two).

Why is it 92nd?

The suffix follows the last word: second ends "nd", so 92 is written 92nd.

How do you write the 92nd in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 92nd is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her ninety-second birthday", "92nd place".

What is 92nd as a number?

92 — ninety-two. 92nd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
