Frequently asked questions

How do you write 680 in words?

Six hundred and eighty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: six hundred eighty.

How do you write 680 on a cheque?

Write "Six hundred and eighty pounds only" on the words line and £680.00 in the box. On a US check, "Six hundred eighty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "six hundred and eighty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (six hundred and eighty); American English usually leaves it out (six hundred eighty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 680?

680th, written six hundred and eightieth.
