Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 33rd?

Thirty-third. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (thirty-three).

Why is it 33rd?

The suffix follows the last word: third ends "rd", so 33 is written 33rd.

How do you write the 33rd in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 33rd is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her thirty-third birthday", "33rd place".

What is 33rd as a number?

33 — thirty-three. 33rd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
