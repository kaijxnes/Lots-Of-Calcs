Frequently asked questions

How do you write 0.25 in words?

Zero point two five. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own — "point two five", not "point twenty-five". As a fraction it is a quarter.

How do you write £0.25 in words?

Twenty-five pence only on a cheque; "twenty-fivep" when you say it. On a US check, $0.25 is "Zero and 25/100 dollars".

What is 0.25 as a fraction?

1/4. See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 0.25 as a percentage?
