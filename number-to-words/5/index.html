Frequently asked questions

How do you write 5 in words?

Five.

How do you write 5 on a cheque?

Write "Five pounds only" on the words line and £5.00 in the box. On a US check, "Five and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "five" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in five in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 5?

5th, written fifth.
