Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 31st?

Thirty-first. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (thirty-one).

Why is it 31st?

The suffix follows the last word: first ends "st", so 31 is written 31st.

How do you write the 31st in a date?

In the UK, "the 31st of March" or "31 March"; in the US, "March 31st" or "March 31". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 31 March 2027.

What is 31st as a number?

31 — thirty-one. 31st is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
