Frequently asked questions

How do you write 46 in words?

Forty-six.

How do you write 46 on a cheque?

Write "Forty-six pounds only" on the words line and £46.00 in the box. On a US check, "Forty-six and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 46?

46th, written forty-sixth.
