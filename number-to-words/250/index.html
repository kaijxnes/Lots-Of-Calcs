Frequently asked questions

How do you write 250 in words?

Two hundred and fifty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred fifty.

How do you write 250 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and fifty pounds only" on the words line and £250.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred fifty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and fifty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and fifty); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred fifty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 250?

250th, written two hundred and fiftieth.
