Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 93rd?

Ninety-third. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (ninety-three).

Why is it 93rd?

The suffix follows the last word: third ends "rd", so 93 is written 93rd.

How do you write the 93rd in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 93rd is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her ninety-third birthday", "93rd place".

What is 93rd as a number?

93 — ninety-three. 93rd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
