Frequently asked questions

How do you write 870,000 in words?

Eight hundred and seventy thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: eight hundred seventy thousand. In India it is written eight lakh seventy thousand (8,70,000).

How do you write 870,000 on a cheque?

Write "Eight hundred and seventy thousand pounds only" on the words line and £870,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Eight hundred seventy thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "eight hundred and seventy thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (eight hundred and seventy thousand); American English usually leaves it out (eight hundred seventy thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 870,000?

870000th, written eight hundred and seventy thousandth.
