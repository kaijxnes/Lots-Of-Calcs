Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 47th?

Forty-seventh. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (forty-seven).

Why is it 47th?

The suffix follows the last word: seventh ends "th", so 47 is written 47th.

How do you write the 47th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 47th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her forty-seventh birthday", "47th place".

What is 47th as a number?

47 — forty-seven. 47th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
