Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 94th?

Ninety-fourth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (ninety-four).

Why is it 94th?

The suffix follows the last word: fourth ends "th", so 94 is written 94th.

How do you write the 94th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 94th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her ninety-fourth birthday", "94th place".

What is 94th as a number?

94 — ninety-four. 94th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
