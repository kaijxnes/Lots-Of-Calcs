Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 49th?

Forty-ninth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (forty-nine).

Why is it 49th?

The suffix follows the last word: ninth ends "th", so 49 is written 49th.

How do you write the 49th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 49th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her forty-ninth birthday", "49th place".

What is 49th as a number?

49 — forty-nine. 49th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
