Frequently asked questions

How do you write 830 in words?

Eight hundred and thirty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: eight hundred thirty.

How do you write 830 on a cheque?

Write "Eight hundred and thirty pounds only" on the words line and £830.00 in the box. On a US check, "Eight hundred thirty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "eight hundred and thirty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (eight hundred and thirty); American English usually leaves it out (eight hundred thirty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 830?

830th, written eight hundred and thirtieth.
