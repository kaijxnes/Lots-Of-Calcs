Frequently asked questions

How do you write 1.5 in words?

One point five. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is one and a half.

How do you write £1.50 in words?

One pound and fifty pence only on a cheque; "one pound 50" when you say it. On a US check, $1.50 is "One and 50/100 dollars".

What is 1.5 as a fraction?

1 1/2 (3/2 as an improper fraction). See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 1.5 as a percentage?

150% — multiply by 100.
