Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 40th?

Fortieth.

Why is it 40th?

The suffix follows the last word: fortieth ends "th", so 40 is written 40th.

How do you write the 40th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 40th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her fortieth birthday", "40th place".

What is 40th as a number?

40 — forty. 40th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
