Frequently asked questions

How do you write 790 in words?

Seven hundred and ninety. In American English the "and" is usually left out: seven hundred ninety.

How do you write 790 on a cheque?

Write "Seven hundred and ninety pounds only" on the words line and £790.00 in the box. On a US check, "Seven hundred ninety and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "seven hundred and ninety" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (seven hundred and ninety); American English usually leaves it out (seven hundred ninety). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 790?

790th, written seven hundred and ninetieth.
