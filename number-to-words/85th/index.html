Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 85th?

Eighty-fifth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (eighty-five).

Why is it 85th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 85 is written 85th.

How do you write the 85th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 85th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her eighty-fifth birthday", "85th place".

What is 85th as a number?

85 — eighty-five. 85th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
