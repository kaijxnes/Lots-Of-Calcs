Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 7th?

Seventh.

Why is it 7th?

The suffix follows the last word: seventh ends "th", so 7 is written 7th.

How do you write the 7th in a date?

In the UK, "the 7th of March" or "7 March"; in the US, "March 7th" or "March 7". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 7 March 2027.

What is 7th as a number?

7 — seven. 7th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
