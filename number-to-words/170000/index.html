Frequently asked questions

How do you write 170,000 in words?

One hundred and seventy thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred seventy thousand. In India it is written one lakh seventy thousand (1,70,000).

How do you write 170,000 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and seventy thousand pounds only" on the words line and £170,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred seventy thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and seventy thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and seventy thousand); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred seventy thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 170,000?

170000th, written one hundred and seventy thousandth.
