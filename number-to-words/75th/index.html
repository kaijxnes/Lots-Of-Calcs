Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 75th?

Seventy-fifth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (seventy-five).

Why is it 75th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 75 is written 75th.

How do you write the 75th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 75th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her seventy-fifth birthday", "75th place".

What is 75th as a number?

75 — seventy-five. 75th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
