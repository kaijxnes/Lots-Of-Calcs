Frequently asked questions

How do you write 280 in words?

Two hundred and eighty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two hundred eighty.

How do you write 280 on a cheque?

Write "Two hundred and eighty pounds only" on the words line and £280.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two hundred eighty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "two hundred and eighty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (two hundred and eighty); American English usually leaves it out (two hundred eighty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 280?

280th, written two hundred and eightieth.
