Frequently asked questions

How do you write 67 in words?

Sixty-seven.

How do you write 67 on a cheque?

Write "Sixty-seven pounds only" on the words line and £67.00 in the box. On a US check, "Sixty-seven and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 67?

67th, written sixty-seventh.
