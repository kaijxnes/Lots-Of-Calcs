Frequently asked questions

How do you write 150,000,000 in words?

One hundred and fifty million. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred fifty million. In India it is written fifteen crore (15,00,00,000).

How do you write 150 million in words?

150 million is 150,000,000, written one hundred and fifty million (US: one hundred fifty million). In India it's fifteen crore.

How do you write 150,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and fifty million pounds only" on the words line and £150,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred fifty million and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and fifty million" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and fifty million); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred fifty million). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 150,000,000?

150000000th, written one hundred and fifty millionth.
