Frequently asked questions

How do you write 50,000,000,000 in words?

Fifty billion. In India it is written five thousand crore (50,00,00,00,000).

How do you write 50 billion in words?

50 billion is 50,000,000,000, written fifty billion. In India it's five thousand crore.

How do you write 50,000,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "Fifty billion pounds only" on the words line and £50,000,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Fifty billion and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "fifty billion" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in fifty billion in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 50,000,000,000?

50000000000th, written fifty billionth.
