Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 52nd?

Fifty-second. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (fifty-two).

Why is it 52nd?

The suffix follows the last word: second ends "nd", so 52 is written 52nd.

How do you write the 52nd in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 52nd is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her fifty-second birthday", "52nd place".

What is 52nd as a number?

52 — fifty-two. 52nd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
