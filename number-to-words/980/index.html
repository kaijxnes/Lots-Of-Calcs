Frequently asked questions

How do you write 980 in words?

Nine hundred and eighty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: nine hundred eighty.

How do you write 980 on a cheque?

Write "Nine hundred and eighty pounds only" on the words line and £980.00 in the box. On a US check, "Nine hundred eighty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "nine hundred and eighty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (nine hundred and eighty); American English usually leaves it out (nine hundred eighty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 980?

980th, written nine hundred and eightieth.
