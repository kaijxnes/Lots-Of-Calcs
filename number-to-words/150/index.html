Frequently asked questions

How do you write 150 in words?

One hundred and fifty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred fifty.

How do you write 150 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and fifty pounds only" on the words line and £150.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred fifty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and fifty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and fifty); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred fifty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 150?

150th, written one hundred and fiftieth.
