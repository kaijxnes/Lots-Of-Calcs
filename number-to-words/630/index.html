Frequently asked questions

How do you write 630 in words?

Six hundred and thirty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: six hundred thirty.

How do you write 630 on a cheque?

Write "Six hundred and thirty pounds only" on the words line and £630.00 in the box. On a US check, "Six hundred thirty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "six hundred and thirty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (six hundred and thirty); American English usually leaves it out (six hundred thirty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 630?

630th, written six hundred and thirtieth.
