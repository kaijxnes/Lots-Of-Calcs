Frequently asked questions

How do you write 70,000,000 in words?

Seventy million. In India it is written seven crore (7,00,00,000).

How do you write 70 million in words?

70 million is 70,000,000, written seventy million. In India it's seven crore.

How do you write 70,000,000 on a cheque?

Write "Seventy million pounds only" on the words line and £70,000,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Seventy million and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "seventy million" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in seventy million in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 70,000,000?

70000000th, written seventy millionth.
