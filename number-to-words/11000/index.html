Frequently asked questions

How do you write 11,000 in words?

Eleven thousand.

How do you write 11,000 on a cheque?

Write "Eleven thousand pounds only" on the words line and £11,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Eleven thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "eleven thousand" with an "and"?

There is no "and" in eleven thousand in either British or American English.

What is the ordinal of 11,000?

11000th, written eleven thousandth.
