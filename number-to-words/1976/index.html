Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1976 as a year?

Nineteen seventy-six. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and seventy-six (US: one thousand nine hundred seventy-six).

How do you write 1976 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and seventy-six. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred seventy-six.

How do you write 1976 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and seventy-six pounds only" on the words line and £1976.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred seventy-six and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 1976?

1976th, written one thousand nine hundred and seventy-sixth.
