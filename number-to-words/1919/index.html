Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1919 as a year?

Nineteen nineteen. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and nineteen (US: one thousand nine hundred nineteen).

How do you write 1919 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and nineteen. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred nineteen.

How do you write 1919 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and nineteen pounds only" on the words line and £1919.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred nineteen and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one thousand nine hundred and nineteen" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one thousand nine hundred and nineteen); American English usually leaves it out (one thousand nine hundred nineteen). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 1919?

1919th, written one thousand nine hundred and nineteenth.
