Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 90th?

Ninetieth.

Why is it 90th?

The suffix follows the last word: ninetieth ends "th", so 90 is written 90th.

How do you write the 90th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 90th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her ninetieth birthday", "90th place".

What is 90th as a number?

90 — ninety. 90th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
