Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 48th?

Forty-eighth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (forty-eight).

Why is it 48th?

The suffix follows the last word: eighth ends "th", so 48 is written 48th.

How do you write the 48th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 48th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her forty-eighth birthday", "48th place".

What is 48th as a number?

48 — forty-eight. 48th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
