Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 13th?

Thirteenth.

Why is it 13th?

13 ends in 13, and 11, 12 and 13 always take "th" (eleventh, twelfth, thirteenth), even though they end in 1, 2 or 3.

How do you write the 13th in a date?

In the UK, "the 13th of March" or "13 March"; in the US, "March 13th" or "March 13". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 13 March 2027.

What is 13th as a number?

13 — thirteen. 13th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
