Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 89th?

Eighty-ninth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (eighty-nine).

Why is it 89th?

The suffix follows the last word: ninth ends "th", so 89 is written 89th.

How do you write the 89th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 89th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her eighty-ninth birthday", "89th place".

What is 89th as a number?

89 — eighty-nine. 89th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
