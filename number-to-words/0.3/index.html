Frequently asked questions

How do you write 0.3 in words?

Zero point three. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is three tenths.

How do you write £0.30 in words?

Thirty pence only on a cheque; "thirtyp" when you say it. On a US check, $0.30 is "Zero and 30/100 dollars".

What is 0.3 as a fraction?

3/10. See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 0.3 as a percentage?

30% — multiply by 100.
