Frequently asked questions

How do you say 2057 as a year?

Twenty fifty-seven. "Two thousand and fifty-seven" is also common, especially in formal writing. Written out as a number in full it is two thousand and fifty-seven (US: two thousand fifty-seven).

How do you write 2057 in words?

Two thousand and fifty-seven. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two thousand fifty-seven.

How do you write 2057 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand and fifty-seven pounds only" on the words line and £2057.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand fifty-seven and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 2057?

2057th, written two thousand and fifty-seventh.
