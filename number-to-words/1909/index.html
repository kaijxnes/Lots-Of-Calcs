Frequently asked questions

How do you say 1909 as a year?

Nineteen oh nine. Some people say one thousand nine hundred and nine instead. Written out as a number in full it is one thousand nine hundred and nine (US: one thousand nine hundred nine).

How do you write 1909 in words?

One thousand nine hundred and nine. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one thousand nine hundred nine.

How do you write 1909 on a cheque?

Write "One thousand nine hundred and nine pounds only" on the words line and £1909.00 in the box. On a US check, "One thousand nine hundred nine and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one thousand nine hundred and nine" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one thousand nine hundred and nine); American English usually leaves it out (one thousand nine hundred nine). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 1909?

1909th, written one thousand nine hundred and ninth.
