Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 1st?

First.

Why is it 1st?

The suffix follows the last word: first ends "st", so 1 is written 1st.

How do you write the 1st in a date?

In the UK, "the 1st of March" or "1 March"; in the US, "March 1st" or "March 1". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 1 March 2027.

What is 1st as a number?

1 — one. 1st is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
