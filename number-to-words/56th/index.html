Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 56th?

Fifty-sixth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (fifty-six).

Why is it 56th?

The suffix follows the last word: sixth ends "th", so 56 is written 56th.

How do you write the 56th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 56th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her fifty-sixth birthday", "56th place".

What is 56th as a number?

56 — fifty-six. 56th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
