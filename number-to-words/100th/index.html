Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 100th?

One hundredth.

Why is it 100th?

The suffix follows the last word: hundredth ends "th", so 100 is written 100th.

How do you write the 100th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 100th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her one hundredth birthday", "100th place".

What is 100th as a number?

100 — one hundred. 100th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
