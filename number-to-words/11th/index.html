Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 11th?

Eleventh.

Why is it 11th?

11 ends in 11, and 11, 12 and 13 always take "th" (eleventh, twelfth, thirteenth), even though they end in 1, 2 or 3.

How do you write the 11th in a date?

In the UK, "the 11th of March" or "11 March"; in the US, "March 11th" or "March 11". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 11 March 2027.

What is 11th as a number?

11 — eleven. 11th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
