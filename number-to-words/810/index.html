Frequently asked questions

How do you write 810 in words?

Eight hundred and ten. In American English the "and" is usually left out: eight hundred ten.

How do you write 810 on a cheque?

Write "Eight hundred and ten pounds only" on the words line and £810.00 in the box. On a US check, "Eight hundred ten and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "eight hundred and ten" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (eight hundred and ten); American English usually leaves it out (eight hundred ten). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 810?

810th, written eight hundred and tenth.
