Frequently asked questions

How do you write 320 in words?

Three hundred and twenty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: three hundred twenty.

How do you write 320 on a cheque?

Write "Three hundred and twenty pounds only" on the words line and £320.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three hundred twenty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "three hundred and twenty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (three hundred and twenty); American English usually leaves it out (three hundred twenty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 320?

320th, written three hundred and twentieth.
