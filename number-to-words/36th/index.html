Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 36th?

Thirty-sixth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (thirty-six).

Why is it 36th?

The suffix follows the last word: sixth ends "th", so 36 is written 36th.

How do you write the 36th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 36th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her thirty-sixth birthday", "36th place".

What is 36th as a number?

36 — thirty-six. 36th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
