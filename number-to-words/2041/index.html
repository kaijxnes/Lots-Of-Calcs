Frequently asked questions

How do you say 2041 as a year?

Twenty forty-one. "Two thousand and forty-one" is also common, especially in formal writing. Written out as a number in full it is two thousand and forty-one (US: two thousand forty-one).

How do you write 2041 in words?

Two thousand and forty-one. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two thousand forty-one.

How do you write 2041 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand and forty-one pounds only" on the words line and £2041.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand forty-one and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 2041?

2041st, written two thousand and forty-first.
