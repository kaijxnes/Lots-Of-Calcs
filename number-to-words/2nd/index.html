Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 2nd?

Second.

Why is it 2nd?

The suffix follows the last word: second ends "nd", so 2 is written 2nd.

How do you write the 2nd in a date?

In the UK, "the 2nd of March" or "2 March"; in the US, "March 2nd" or "March 2". In formal writing the date is often written without the suffix: 2 March 2027.

What is 2nd as a number?

2 — two. 2nd is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
