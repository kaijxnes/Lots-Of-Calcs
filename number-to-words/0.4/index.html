Frequently asked questions

How do you write 0.4 in words?

Zero point four. After the decimal point, each digit is read on its own. As a fraction it is two fifths.

How do you write £0.40 in words?

Forty pence only on a cheque; "fortyp" when you say it. On a US check, $0.40 is "Zero and 40/100 dollars".

What is 0.4 as a fraction?

2/5. See the <a href="/decimal-to-fraction/">decimal to fraction converter</a> for any other decimal.

What is 0.4 as a percentage?

40% — multiply by 100.
