Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 80th?

Eightieth.

Why is it 80th?

The suffix follows the last word: eightieth ends "th", so 80 is written 80th.

How do you write the 80th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 80th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her eightieth birthday", "80th place".

What is 80th as a number?

80 — eighty. 80th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
