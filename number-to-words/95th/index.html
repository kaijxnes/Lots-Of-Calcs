Frequently asked questions

How do you spell 95th?

Ninety-fifth. It's hyphenated, like the number itself (ninety-five).

Why is it 95th?

The suffix follows the last word: fifth ends "th", so 95 is written 95th.

How do you write the 95th in a date?

Dates only go up to the 31st, but 95th is used for anniversaries, birthdays and positions: "her ninety-fifth birthday", "95th place".

What is 95th as a number?

95 — ninety-five. 95th is its ordinal form, used for order or position.
