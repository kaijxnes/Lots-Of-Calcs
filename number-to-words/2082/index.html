Frequently asked questions

How do you say 2082 as a year?

Twenty eighty-two. "Two thousand and eighty-two" is also common, especially in formal writing. Written out as a number in full it is two thousand and eighty-two (US: two thousand eighty-two).

How do you write 2082 in words?

Two thousand and eighty-two. In American English the "and" is usually left out: two thousand eighty-two.

How do you write 2082 on a cheque?

Write "Two thousand and eighty-two pounds only" on the words line and £2082.00 in the box. On a US check, "Two thousand eighty-two and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Why is there a hyphen?

Numbers from twenty-one to ninety-nine are hyphenated when written out (twenty-one, forty-five), wherever they appear in a larger number.

What is the ordinal of 2082?

2082nd, written two thousand and eighty-second.
