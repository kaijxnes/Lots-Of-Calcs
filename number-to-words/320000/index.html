Frequently asked questions

How do you write 320,000 in words?

Three hundred and twenty thousand. In American English the "and" is usually left out: three hundred twenty thousand. In India it is written three lakh twenty thousand (3,20,000).

How do you write 320,000 on a cheque?

Write "Three hundred and twenty thousand pounds only" on the words line and £320,000.00 in the box. On a US check, "Three hundred twenty thousand and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "three hundred and twenty thousand" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (three hundred and twenty thousand); American English usually leaves it out (three hundred twenty thousand). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 320,000?

320000th, written three hundred and twenty thousandth.
