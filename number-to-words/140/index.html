Frequently asked questions

How do you write 140 in words?

One hundred and forty. In American English the "and" is usually left out: one hundred forty.

How do you write 140 on a cheque?

Write "One hundred and forty pounds only" on the words line and £140.00 in the box. On a US check, "One hundred forty and 00/100", with a line drawn to the end so nothing can be added.

Is it "one hundred and forty" with an "and"?

In British English, "and" goes before the last part of a number (one hundred and forty); American English usually leaves it out (one hundred forty). Both are correct in their own style.

What is the ordinal of 140?

140th, written one hundred and fortieth.
