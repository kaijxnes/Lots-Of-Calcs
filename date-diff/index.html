How the difference is counted

The years, months and days are counted the way you would on a calendar: whole years first, then whole months, then the days left over. Months aren't treated as a fixed 30 days, so the answer matches what you'd get counting on a wall calendar.

Worked example: from 14 March 2024 to 2 September 2026. Two whole years takes you to 14 March 2026; five more whole months to 14 August 2026; and 19 days to 2 September. The answer is 2 years, 5 months and 19 days — 902 days in total, or 128 weeks and 6 days.

The total days, weeks and months are also shown, because “how many days until” and “how many years since” are often different questions about the same two dates.

Counting tips

Inclusive or not. The calculator gives the gap between the dates, so Monday to Friday is 4 days. If you're counting days of a holiday or a hire period where both the first and last day count, add one: Monday to Friday is 5 days inclusive.

Month ends. Calendar months differ in length, so one month after 31 January has no exact answer — there is no 31 February. Counting from the end of a long month into a short one can give a result that looks a day out; total days are always exact.

Working days. The totals include weekends and bank holidays. For a rough count of weekdays, multiply the total weeks by five.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculation include the end date?

It measures the span between the two dates, so the finish date is the endpoint rather than an extra counted day. Add one if you need to count both the first and last day inclusively.

Are leap years accounted for?

Yes. The difference is worked out from the real calendar, so 29 February and the varying lengths of months are handled correctly.

How many days are there between two dates?

Enter both dates and read the total days figure. From 14 March 2024 to 2 September 2026 is 902 days. For a date in the past, put it first; the order only changes whether the answer counts forwards or back.

How many weeks is it until a date?

Put today as the start date and the date you're counting to as the end. The total weeks figure gives whole weeks plus a fraction — 59 days is 8.4 weeks, or 8 weeks and 3 days.

How do I work out someone's age on a particular date?

Use their date of birth as the start and the date you're interested in as the end; the years figure is their age on that day. For age today, plus the weekday they were born and a countdown to their birthday, use the birthday calculator.

Does it count working days?

No — it counts every calendar day, including weekends and bank holidays. Multiply the total weeks by five for an approximate number of weekdays.
