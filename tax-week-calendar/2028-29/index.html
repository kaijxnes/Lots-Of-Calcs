Frequently asked questions

When does tax week 1 start in 2028/29?

Tax week 1 runs from Thursday 6 April 2028 to Wednesday 12 April 2028. Every tax year starts on 6 April, so week 1 always starts on 6 April whatever day of the week that is.

What is week 53 in 2028/29?

Week 53 is Thursday 5 April 2029: the one day left over after 52 full weeks. Employees paid weekly on a Thursday get a week 53 payment, which goes on the final FPS as week 53 (54 if paid fortnightly, 56 if paid every 4 weeks).

What tax week and tax month is Christmas Day 2028?

Monday 25 December 2028 is in tax week 38 and tax month 9.

What tax week is 1 January 2029?

Monday 1 January 2029 is in tax week 39 and tax month 9.

When is PAYE due for tax month 1 of 2028/29?

Tax month 1 runs from 6 April to 5 May 2028. PAYE for it is due by 22 May 2028 if you pay electronically, or must reach HMRC by 19 May 2028 by cheque. Each later month follows the same pattern: the 22nd of the next month.

How are tax weeks and tax months defined?

By the Income Tax (PAYE) Regulations 2003: tax week 1 is 6 to 12 April and each following 7 days is the next week, with week 53 being just the last day of the tax year (the last two days in a leap year). A tax month runs from the 6th of one month to the 5th of the next, so 6 October is always the first day of tax month 7.

Definitions from the Income Tax (PAYE) Regulations 2003, regulation 2; payment dates from GOV.UK's Pay employers' PAYE and Payroll: annual reporting.
