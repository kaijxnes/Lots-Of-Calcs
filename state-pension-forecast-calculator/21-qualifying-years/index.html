Frequently asked questions

How much State Pension will I get with 21 years of National Insurance?

About £144.78 a week (£7,528.56 a year): 21/35 of the full £241.30, in 2026/27 money. That's the rule if your record started after April 2016; your State Pension forecast shows your own amount.

How many more years do I need for the full State Pension?

14 more years, to reach 35. Each one adds £6.89 a week, or £358.50 a year.

Is it worth paying voluntary National Insurance?

A year of Class 3 costs £956.80 (£18.40 a week). If it adds a qualifying year that raises your pension, you get £358.50 a year back, so it pays for itself in about 2.7 years. Check your forecast first: it doesn't always increase your pension.

Will I keep building qualifying years?

Yes, while you work and pay National Insurance or get NI credits, until State Pension age. If you keep working after State Pension age you stop paying National Insurance.
