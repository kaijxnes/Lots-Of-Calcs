Frequently asked questions

How much tax do I pay on £15,000 of side income?

With a £30,000 job and no expenses, £2,800.00 of Income Tax and £85.80 of Class 4 National Insurance after the £1,000 trading allowance, so you keep £12,114.20 (2026/27). With no other job, £371.80.

Do I need to tell HMRC about £15,000 of side income?

For trading income of £15,000, register for Self Assessment by 5 October after the tax year ends.

Should I claim expenses or the £1,000 allowance on £15,000?

Whichever is more: you can't have both. With £4,500 of expenses on £15,000, claiming them leaves £10,500.00 taxable and £2,100.00 of tax and NI on a £30,000 job.

Is £15,000 of rental income taxed the same way?

Property income gets its own £1,000 allowance and doesn't pay Class 4 National Insurance: £2,800.00 of Income Tax on a £30,000 job. A room let in your own home can use the Rent a Room Scheme instead.
