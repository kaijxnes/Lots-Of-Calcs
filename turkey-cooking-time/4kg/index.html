From Food Standards Scotland's turkey cooking guide (the same times the Food Standards Agency gives), for an unstuffed turkey in an oven preheated to 180°C (350°F, Gas Mark 4). Times are a guide: the turkey is cooked when the thickest part (between the breast and the thigh) reaches 75°C, the meat is steaming hot with no pink, and the juices run clear (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA's minimum is 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost part of the thigh and wing.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a 4 kg turkey take to cook?

3 hours 20 minutes in an oven preheated to 180°C (350°F, Gas Mark 4), unstuffed, using Food Standards Scotland's turkey cooking guide (the same times the Food Standards Agency gives): 45 minutes per kg plus 20 minutes (under 4.5 kg). Cover it with foil and take the foil off for the last 30 minutes to brown the skin. Fan ovens may cook it more quickly.

What about the US timing?

The USDA timetable at 325°F gives 2¾ to 3 hours unstuffed or 3 to 3½ hours stuffed for a 8 to 12 lb bird (8.8 lb is 4 kg). It uses a cooler oven, so the times are longer.

How long does it take to defrost a 4 kg turkey?

About 1½ to 2 days in a fridge at 4°C — allow 10 to 12 hours per kg (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA allows about 24 hours per 5 lb in the fridge (2 days for 9 lb), or 30 minutes per pound in cold water changed every 30 minutes. Cook it as soon as it has thawed.

How do I know when it's cooked?

Times are a guide: the turkey is cooked when the thickest part (between the breast and the thigh) reaches 75°C, the meat is steaming hot with no pink, and the juices run clear (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA's minimum is 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost part of the thigh and wing.

Should I let it rest?

Yes — the USDA says to let it stand 20 minutes before taking out any stuffing and carving. Cover it loosely with foil.
