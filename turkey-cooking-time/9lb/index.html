How long to cook a 9 lb turkey

2¾ to 3 hours at 325°F unstuffed, 3 to 3½ hours stuffed. That's 4.1 kg — enough for about 9 people at 1 lb each (USDA).

Frequently asked questions

How long does a 9 lb turkey take to cook?

2¾ to 3 hours unstuffed, or 3 to 3½ hours stuffed, in an oven at 325°F (about 160°C) — the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service roasting timetable, which groups 8 to 12 lb birds together. Start checking with a thermometer at the shorter time.

What is that in the UK?

4.1 kg at 180°C (Gas Mark 4), unstuffed, takes 3 hours 24 minutes by Food Standards Scotland's guide: 45 minutes per kg plus 20 minutes (under 4.5 kg).

What time should I put a 9 lb turkey in?

Allowing the longer 3 hours unstuffed and 20 minutes' standing, put it in at 10:40 for dinner at 2pm, 12:40 for 4pm or 14:40 for 6pm. Check the temperature from the shorter time.

When should I start thawing a 9 lb turkey for Thanksgiving?

At 24 hours per 5 lb, a 9 lb turkey needs about 2 days in the fridge, so for Thanksgiving on Thursday 26 November 2026 move it to the fridge by 12:00 on Tuesday 24 November. Short of time, thawing in cold water takes about 4 hours 30 minutes (30 minutes per pound, changing the water every 30 minutes).

How long does it take to defrost a 4.1 kg turkey?

About 1½ to 2 days in a fridge at 4°C — allow 10 to 12 hours per kg (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA allows about 24 hours per 5 lb in the fridge (2 days for 9 lb), or 30 minutes per pound in cold water changed every 30 minutes. Cook it as soon as it has thawed.

How do I know when it's cooked?

Times are a guide: the turkey is cooked when the thickest part (between the breast and the thigh) reaches 75°C, the meat is steaming hot with no pink, and the juices run clear (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA's minimum is 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost part of the thigh and wing.

Should I let it rest?

Yes — the USDA says to let it stand 20 minutes before taking out any stuffing and carving. Cover it loosely with foil.
