2 hours 58 minutes at 180°C (Gas Mark 4), or 160°C in a fan oven (the equivalent Which?'s oven chart gives for 180°C), then 20 minutes resting under foil before carving.

Defrosting for Christmas: a frozen 3.5 kg turkey needs 1½ to 2 days in the fridge, so move it from the freezer by 14:00 on Wednesday 23 December to have it thawed by the morning of Friday 25 December.

Feeds: about 8 people, at the USDA's 1 lb (about 450 g) per person.

From Food Standards Scotland's turkey cooking guide (the same times the Food Standards Agency gives), for an unstuffed turkey in an oven preheated to 180°C (350°F, Gas Mark 4). Times are a guide: the turkey is cooked when the thickest part (between the breast and the thigh) reaches 75°C, the meat is steaming hot with no pink, and the juices run clear (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA's minimum is 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost part of the thigh and wing.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a 3.5 kg turkey take to cook?

2 hours 58 minutes in an oven preheated to 180°C (350°F, Gas Mark 4), unstuffed, using Food Standards Scotland's turkey cooking guide (the same times the Food Standards Agency gives): 45 minutes per kg plus 20 minutes (under 4.5 kg). Cover it with foil and take the foil off for the last 30 minutes to brown the skin. Fan ovens may cook it more quickly.

What time should I put a 3.5 kg turkey in for Christmas dinner?

For dinner at 2pm, put it in at 10:43: 2 hours 58 minutes in the oven, then 20 minutes resting. For 1pm, 09:43; for 3pm, 11:43. Preheat the oven first.

When should I take a 3.5 kg turkey out of the freezer for Christmas?

Defrosting in the fridge takes 1½ to 2 days at 10 to 12 hours per kg, so for Christmas Day (Friday 25 December 2026) move it to the fridge by 14:00 on Wednesday 23 December, to be thawed by Christmas morning.

How many people does a 3.5 kg turkey feed?

About 8, using the USDA's guide of 1 lb (about 450 g) of whole turkey per person.

How long does it take to defrost a 3.5 kg turkey?

About 1½ to 2 days in a fridge at 4°C — allow 10 to 12 hours per kg (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA allows about 24 hours per 5 lb in the fridge (1½ days for 8 lb), or 30 minutes per pound in cold water changed every 30 minutes. Cook it as soon as it has thawed.

How do I know when it's cooked?

Times are a guide: the turkey is cooked when the thickest part (between the breast and the thigh) reaches 75°C, the meat is steaming hot with no pink, and the juices run clear (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA's minimum is 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost part of the thigh and wing.

Should I let it rest?

Yes — the USDA says to let it stand 20 minutes before taking out any stuffing and carving. Cover it loosely with foil.
