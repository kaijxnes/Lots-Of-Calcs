How long to roast and defrost your turkey, and what time to put it in for dinner.

Frequently asked questions

How long do you cook a turkey per kg?

At 180°C (Gas Mark 4), unstuffed: 45 minutes per kg plus 20 minutes under 4.5 kg, 40 minutes per kg from 4.5 to 6.5 kg, and 35 minutes per kg over 6.5 kg (Food Standards Scotland). A 5 kg turkey takes 3 hours 20 minutes.

How long do you cook a turkey per pound?

The USDA gives times by weight band at 325°F rather than per pound: 2¾ to 3 hours for 8 to 12 lb, 3 to 3¾ hours for 12 to 14 lb, 3¾ to 4¼ for 14 to 18 lb, 4¼ to 4½ for 18 to 20 lb and 4½ to 5 hours for 20 to 24 lb, unstuffed.

How long does a turkey take to defrost?

In the fridge, about 10 to 12 hours per kg (Food Standards Scotland), or 24 hours per 5 lb (USDA) — a large bird can take three or four days, so take it out of the freezer in good time.

How big a turkey do I need?

The USDA suggests 1 lb (about 450 g) per person, so a 5 kg turkey feeds about 11 and a 16 lb turkey about 16.

How do I know when it's cooked?

Times are a guide: the turkey is cooked when the thickest part (between the breast and the thigh) reaches 75°C, the meat is steaming hot with no pink, and the juices run clear (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA's minimum is 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost part of the thigh and wing.
