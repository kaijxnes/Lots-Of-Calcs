How long to cook a 15 lb turkey

3¾ to 4¼ hours at 325°F unstuffed, 4 to 4¼ hours stuffed. That's 6.8 kg — enough for about 15 people at 1 lb each (USDA).

Frequently asked questions

How long does a 15 lb turkey take to cook?

3¾ to 4¼ hours unstuffed, or 4 to 4¼ hours stuffed, in an oven at 325°F (about 160°C) — the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service roasting timetable, which groups 14 to 18 lb birds together. Start checking with a thermometer at the shorter time.

What is that in the UK?

6.8 kg at 180°C (Gas Mark 4), unstuffed, takes 3 hours 58 minutes by Food Standards Scotland's guide: 35 minutes per kg (over 6.5 kg).

How long does it take to defrost a 6.8 kg turkey?

About 3 to 3½ days in a fridge at 4°C — allow 10 to 12 hours per kg (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA allows about 24 hours per 5 lb in the fridge (3 days for 15 lb), or 30 minutes per pound in cold water changed every 30 minutes. Cook it as soon as it has thawed.

How do I know when it's cooked?

Times are a guide: the turkey is cooked when the thickest part (between the breast and the thigh) reaches 75°C, the meat is steaming hot with no pink, and the juices run clear (Food Standards Scotland). The USDA's minimum is 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast and the innermost part of the thigh and wing.

Should I let it rest?

Yes — the USDA says to let it stand 20 minutes before taking out any stuffing and carving. Cover it loosely with foil.
