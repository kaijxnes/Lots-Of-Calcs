Frequently asked questions

How does the word counter count words?

Anything separated by spaces or line breaks counts as a word, including numbers; hyphenated words like 'well-known' count once. Most word processors count the same way, though a few treat dashes and symbols differently.

How is reading time worked out?

Words ÷ reading speed. It uses 200 words a minute for reading and 130 for speaking aloud, round figures often used for estimates; change them to your own pace.

Is my text saved or sent anywhere?

No. It's counted in your browser and never leaves your device.
