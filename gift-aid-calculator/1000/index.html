Frequently asked questions

How much is Gift Aid on £1,000?

£250.00: the charity claims 25p for every £1, so your £1,000 is worth £1,250.00 to it. It doesn't cost you anything extra.

How much can I claim back on a £1,000 donation?

A higher rate (40%) taxpayer can claim back £250.00 and an additional rate (45%) taxpayer £312.50, on a Self Assessment tax return or by asking HMRC to change their tax code. Basic rate taxpayers don't get anything back: the charity gets the relief.

What does a £1,000 donation really cost a higher rate taxpayer?

£750.00 after claiming back £250.00 (2026/27), for a gift worth £1,250.00 to the charity.

Do I pay enough tax for Gift Aid?

Your donations qualify as long as they're not more than 4 times the tax you've paid in the tax year. For £1,000, that means paying at least £250.00 of Income Tax or Capital Gains Tax.
