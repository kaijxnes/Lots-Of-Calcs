2027 federal income tax brackets

The IRS sets the 2027 brackets in Revenue Procedure 2026-35. The seven rates stay at 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35% and 37%; the income levels they start at rise with inflation. They apply to income earned in 2027, so to the tax return you file in 2028, and to withholding from your paychecks from January 2027.

The 2027 standard deduction

Filing status20272026

Married filing jointly$33,200$32,200

Taxable income is your income less the standard deduction (or your itemized deductions if they're bigger). If you're 65 or older or blind, the standard deduction is $1,700 more for each, or $2,100 if you're also unmarried and not a surviving spouse.

How much tax you'll pay

Brackets are marginal: each rate applies only to the slice of income inside its band. A single filer earning $75,000 in wages has $58,400 of taxable income after the $16,600 standard deduction: 10% on the first $12,800, 12% up to $52,025 and 22% on the rest, $7,389.50 in all. That's 9.9% of their pay, although their top rate is 22%.

Other 2027 figures

  • 401(k): you can defer up to $25,500 (from $24,500).
  • Child Tax Credit: up to $2,300 a child under 17, of which up to $1,800 can be refunded.
  • Social Security: 6.2% on wages up to $192,300 (from $184,500); Medicare 1.45% on all wages, plus 0.9% above $200,000.
  • Long-term capital gains and qualified dividends: 0% up to the amounts below, 15% up to the next, 20% above.

Frequently asked questions

What are the 2027 tax brackets?

For single filers: 10% up to $12,800 of taxable income, 12% to $52,025, 22% to $109,125, 24% to $208,325, 32% to $264,550, 35% to $661,375 and 37% above. Married couples filing jointly: 10% to $25,600, 12% to $104,050, 22% to $218,250, 24% to $416,650, 32% to $529,100, 35% to $793,650 and 37% above.

What is the 2027 standard deduction?

$16,600 for single filers and married filing separately, $33,200 for married couples filing jointly and $24,925 for heads of household.

How much federal tax will I pay on $75,000 in 2027?

As a single filer with only wages and the standard deduction, $7,389.50 of federal income tax, plus Social Security and Medicare of $5,737.50.

When do the 2027 tax brackets apply?

To income earned from 1 January to 31 December 2027, reported on the tax return you file in 2028. Paycheck withholding switches to them in January 2027.

What is the 2027 401(k) contribution limit?

$25,500 of elective deferrals, up from $24,500 in 2026.

From IRS Revenue Procedure 2026-35 (Tables 1 to 4, sections .03, .05 and .15), IRS Notice 2026-66 and the SSA's 2027 wage base notice. Not tax advice.
