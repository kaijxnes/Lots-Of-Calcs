Before the tax year ends on 5 April 2027

The allowances you lose if you don't use them, and the claims to make while you still can.

Claim Marriage Allowance, including the backdated years

If you're married or in a civil partnership and one of you earns less than the Personal Allowance, the lower earner can transfer £1,260 of it, cutting the other's tax by up to £252 a year. GOV.UK lets you backdate a claim to 6 April 2022 (the 2022 to 2023 tax year) for any year you were both eligible: 2022/23, 2023/24, 2024/25, 2025/26, up to £1,008 on top of this year. Backdating only reaches back a limited number of years, so if you've been eligible since 2022 and haven't claimed, applying before 5 April 2027 is the safe way to make sure 2022/23 is included. The Marriage Allowance calculator shows what you'd get.

Use this year's ISA allowance

Every tax year you can save up to £20,000 in ISAs (at most £4,000 of it in a Lifetime ISA), and the tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April, so money meant for the 2026/27 allowance has to go in by 5 April 2027. Interest, dividends and gains inside an ISA are tax-free, and the accounts don't close when the tax year ends.

Top up your pension

Pension contributions get tax relief within the £60,000 annual allowance for the tax year, and you might be able to carry over allowance you didn't use in the previous 3 tax years. Contributions also reduce your adjusted net income, which matters in two places:

  • Between £100,000 and £125,140, where you lose £1 of Personal Allowance for every £2: paying in enough to bring your income back to £100,000 keeps it. See the bonus tax calculator and salary sacrifice calculator.
  • Above £60,000, where the High Income Child Benefit Charge takes back 1% of Child Benefit for every £200: the Child Benefit charge calculator shows how much a contribution saves.

The pension tax relief calculator works out the relief on a contribution.

Use your other yearly allowances

The capital gains tax-free allowance is £3,000 and the dividend allowance £500 for 2026/27. Both are for the tax year, so if you're selling investments outside an ISA, gains made before 5 April 2027 use this year's allowance and gains after it use next year's. The capital gains tax calculator and dividend tax calculator show the tax either side.

Claim job expenses and mileage you've paid for

You can claim tax relief on things you had to pay for to do your job, such as business mileage in your own vehicle, uniforms, tools and professional fees, for the current tax year and the 4 previous tax years. HMRC's approved rate for cars is 55p a mile for the first 10,000 business miles from 6 April 2026 (45p before), and if your employer pays less you can claim relief on the difference: the mileage allowance calculator works it out. If you've been on the wrong tax code, the tax refund calculator estimates what you're owed.

Self Assessment

The online tax return for the 2025/26 tax year, and any tax owed for it, are due by 31 January 2027. The Self Assessment guide covers the deadlines, payments on account and penalties.

Frequently asked questions

How far back can I claim Marriage Allowance?

GOV.UK lets you backdate a claim to 6 April 2022 (the 2022 to 2023 tax year) for any year you were eligible, which with this year is up to £1,260.

When is the ISA deadline for 2026/27?

5 April 2027, the last day of the tax year. You can save up to £20,000 in ISAs each tax year.

Can I use unused pension allowance from previous years?

You might be able to carry over annual allowance you didn't use in the previous 3 tax years, once you've used all of this year's £60,000.

How far back can I claim tax relief on work expenses?

For the current tax year and the 4 previous tax years, including mileage in your own vehicle.

When does the tax year end?

On 5 April. The 2026/27 tax year ends on 5 April 2027 and the next one starts on 6 April.
