Self Assessment explained (2025/26 return)

Who needs to send a tax return, every deadline up to 31 January 2027, what you'll pay in January, and what being late costs.

Who needs to send a return

You need to send a Self Assessment tax return for the tax year that ended on 5 April if, for example, you were self-employed and earned more than £1,000, were a partner in a business partnership, have Capital Gains Tax to pay, or have to pay the High Income Child Benefit Charge and don't pay it through PAYE. Untaxed income such as rent, tips and commission, savings interest, dividends or foreign income can mean a return too. GOV.UK has a checker if you're not sure.

The deadlines for the 2025/26 return

The 2025/26 tax year ran from 6 April 2025 to 5 April 2026. Each deadline is at 11:59pm:

WhatDeadline
Tell HMRC you need to send a return (if you haven't before)5 October 2026
Paper tax return31 October 2026
Online return, to have the bill collected through your tax code30 December 2026
Online tax return31 January 2027
Pay the tax owed for 2025/26 (and the first payment on account for 2026/27)31 January 2027
Second payment on account for 2026/2731 July 2027

If you register after 5 October, HMRC gives you a different deadline for the return (3 months from its letter), but the tax is still due by 31 January. The Self Assessment countdown shows how long is left.

What you pay in January

The 31 January bill is usually two things: the balancing payment (what's left of last year's bill once your payments on account are taken off) and the first payment on account towards this year, which is half of last year's bill. On GOV.UK's example, a £3,000 bill with £1,800 already paid on account means £1,200 plus £1,500: £2,700 by 31 January and £1,500 by 31 July.

You don't make payments on account if last year's bill was under £1,000, or if more than 80% of your tax was taken at source. The payments on account calculator works out your own figures, and the self-employed tax calculator estimates the bill itself.

Penalties and interest

Miss the return deadline and there's a £100 penalty straight away. After 3 months, £10 a day up to £900; after 6 months, 5% of the tax due or £300, whichever is more; after 12 months, the same again. Paying late adds 5% of the tax unpaid at 30 days, 6 months and 12 months, plus interest at HMRC's late payment rate, 8% a year at the moment.

So a return and £2,000 of tax both still outstanding at the end of May cost about £562.60: £410.00 in filing penalties, £100.00 in payment penalties and £52.60 interest. The Self Assessment penalty calculator works it out for your dates. If you had a reasonable excuse, you can appeal.

Making Tax Digital

Sole traders and landlords already registered for Self Assessment may need to keep digital records and send updates with Making Tax Digital for Income Tax software. GOV.UK says it applies if your self-employment and property income before expenses was over £50,000 for 2024 to 2025, may be over £30,000 for 2025 to 2026, or may be over £20,000 for 2026 to 2027. Check GOV.UK for when it starts for you.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Self Assessment deadline for 2025/26?

31 January 2027 at 11:59pm, for both the online return and paying what you owe. Paper returns were due by 31 October 2026.

What happens if I miss the Self Assessment deadline?

A £100 late filing penalty straight away, then daily penalties after 3 months and more at 6 and 12 months. Paying late adds 5% penalties at 30 days, 6 months and 12 months, plus interest at 8%.

Why is my January tax bill so high?

It usually includes the balancing payment for last year and the first payment on account for this year, half of last year's bill again. In your first year there are no payments on account to take off, so it can be half as much again as the year's tax.

Can I pay my Self Assessment through my tax code?

You may be able to, if you file your return online by 30 December 2026. After that, you pay it another way.

When is the second payment on account due?

31 July 2027: the second half of your payments on account towards 2026/27.
