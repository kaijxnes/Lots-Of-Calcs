How to work out 35% off £20

Step 1, the saving: £20 × 35 ÷ 100 = £7.

Step 2, the sale price: £20 − £7 = £13.

In one go: with 35% off you pay 65% of the price, so multiply by 0.65: £20 × 0.65 = £13.

In the shop: 10% of £20 is £2 and 5% is £1, so 35% is £7 off. You pay £13.

Frequently asked questions

What is 35% off £20?

£13. 35% of £20 is £7, and £20 − £7 = £13. Or in one step: £20 × 0.65 = £13.

How much do I save with 35% off £20?

£7: £20 × 35 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 35% off in my head?

10% of £20 is £2 and 5% is £1, so 35% is £7 off. You pay £13.

Is 35% off and then 10% off the same as 45% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 35% off £20 is £13; another 10% off that is £11.70, a total saving of £8.30 or 41.5% — a little less than 45% off (£11).
