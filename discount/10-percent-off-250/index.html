How to work out 10% off £250

Step 1, the saving: £250 × 10 ÷ 100 = £25.

Step 2, the sale price: £250 − £25 = £225.

In one go: with 10% off you pay 90% of the price, so multiply by 0.9: £250 × 0.9 = £225.

In the shop: 10% is a tenth: £250 ÷ 10 = £25 off, so you pay £225.

Frequently asked questions

What is 10% off £250?

£225. 10% of £250 is £25, and £250 − £25 = £225. Or in one step: £250 × 0.9 = £225.

How much do I save with 10% off £250?

£25: £250 × 10 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 10% off in my head?

10% is a tenth: £250 ÷ 10 = £25 off, so you pay £225.

Is 10% off and then 10% off the same as 20% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 10% off £250 is £225; another 10% off that is £202.50, a total saving of £47.50 or 19% — a little less than 20% off (£200).
