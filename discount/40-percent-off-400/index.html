Frequently asked questions

What is 40% off £400?

£240. 40% of £400 is £160, and £400 − £160 = £240. Or in one step: £400 × 0.6 = £240.

How much do I save with 40% off £400?

£160: £400 × 40 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 40% off in my head?

10% of £400 is £40, so 40% is 4 × £40 = £160 off. You pay £240.

Is 40% off and then 10% off the same as 50% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 40% off £400 is £240; another 10% off that is £216, a total saving of £184 or 46% — a little less than 50% off (£200).
