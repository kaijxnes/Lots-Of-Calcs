How to work out 10% off £125

Step 2, the sale price: £125 − £12.50 = £112.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 10% off £125?

£112.50. 10% of £125 is £12.50, and £125 − £12.50 = £112.50. Or in one step: £125 × 0.9 = £112.50.

How much do I save with 10% off £125?

£12.50: £125 × 10 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 10% off in my head?

10% is a tenth: £125 ÷ 10 = £12.50 off, so you pay £112.50.

Is 10% off and then 10% off the same as 20% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 10% off £125 is £112.50; another 10% off that is £101.25, a total saving of £23.75 or 19% — a little less than 20% off (£100).
