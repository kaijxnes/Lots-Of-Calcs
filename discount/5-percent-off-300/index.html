How to work out 5% off £300

Step 1, the saving: £300 × 5 ÷ 100 = £15.

Step 2, the sale price: £300 − £15 = £285.

In one go: with 5% off you pay 95% of the price, so multiply by 0.95: £300 × 0.95 = £285.

In the shop: 10% of £300 is £30; 5% is half that, £15. You pay £285.

Frequently asked questions

What is 5% off £300?

£285. 5% of £300 is £15, and £300 − £15 = £285. Or in one step: £300 × 0.95 = £285.

How much do I save with 5% off £300?

£15: £300 × 5 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 5% off in my head?

10% of £300 is £30; 5% is half that, £15. You pay £285.

Is 5% off and then 10% off the same as 15% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 5% off £300 is £285; another 10% off that is £256.50, a total saving of £43.50 or 14.5% — a little less than 15% off (£255).
