How to work out 35% off £150

Frequently asked questions

What is 35% off £150?

£97.50. 35% of £150 is £52.50, and £150 − £52.50 = £97.50. Or in one step: £150 × 0.65 = £97.50.

How much do I save with 35% off £150?

£52.50: £150 × 35 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 35% off in my head?

Is 35% off and then 10% off the same as 45% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 35% off £150 is £97.50; another 10% off that is £87.75, a total saving of £62.25 or 41.5% — a little less than 45% off (£82.50).
