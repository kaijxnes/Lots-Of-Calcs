How to work out 50% off £35

Step 1, the saving: £35 × 50 ÷ 100 = £17.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £35 − £17.50 = £17.50.

In one go: with 50% off you pay 50% of the price, so multiply by 0.5: £35 × 0.5 = £17.50.

In the shop: 50% off is half price: £35 ÷ 2 = £17.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 50% off £35?

£17.50. 50% of £35 is £17.50, and £35 − £17.50 = £17.50. Or in one step: £35 × 0.5 = £17.50.

How much do I save with 50% off £35?

£17.50: £35 × 50 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 50% off in my head?

50% off is half price: £35 ÷ 2 = £17.50.

Is 50% off and then 10% off the same as 60% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 50% off £35 is £17.50; another 10% off that is £15.75, a total saving of £19.25 or 55% — a little less than 60% off (£14).
