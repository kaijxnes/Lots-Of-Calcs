How to work out 20% off £125

Step 1, the saving: £125 × 20 ÷ 100 = £25.

Step 2, the sale price: £125 − £25 = £100.

In one go: with 20% off you pay 80% of the price, so multiply by 0.8: £125 × 0.8 = £100.

In the shop: 10% of £125 is £12.50, so 20% is 2 × £12.50 = £25 off. You pay £100.

Frequently asked questions

What is 20% off £125?

£100. 20% of £125 is £25, and £125 − £25 = £100. Or in one step: £125 × 0.8 = £100.

How much do I save with 20% off £125?

£25: £125 × 20 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 20% off in my head?

10% of £125 is £12.50, so 20% is 2 × £12.50 = £25 off. You pay £100.

Is 20% off and then 10% off the same as 30% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 20% off £125 is £100; another 10% off that is £90, a total saving of £35 or 28% — a little less than 30% off (£87.50).
