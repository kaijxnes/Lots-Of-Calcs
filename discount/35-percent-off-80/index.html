How to work out 35% off £80

Step 1, the saving: £80 × 35 ÷ 100 = £28.

Step 2, the sale price: £80 − £28 = £52.

In one go: with 35% off you pay 65% of the price, so multiply by 0.65: £80 × 0.65 = £52.

In the shop: 10% of £80 is £8 and 5% is £4, so 35% is £28 off. You pay £52.

Frequently asked questions

What is 35% off £80?

£52. 35% of £80 is £28, and £80 − £28 = £52. Or in one step: £80 × 0.65 = £52.

How much do I save with 35% off £80?

£28: £80 × 35 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 35% off in my head?

10% of £80 is £8 and 5% is £4, so 35% is £28 off. You pay £52.

Is 35% off and then 10% off the same as 45% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 35% off £80 is £52; another 10% off that is £46.80, a total saving of £33.20 or 41.5% — a little less than 45% off (£44).
