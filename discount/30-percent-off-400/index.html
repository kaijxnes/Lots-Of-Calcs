How to work out 30% off £400

Step 1, the saving: £400 × 30 ÷ 100 = £120.

Step 2, the sale price: £400 − £120 = £280.

In one go: with 30% off you pay 70% of the price, so multiply by 0.7: £400 × 0.7 = £280.

In the shop: 10% of £400 is £40, so 30% is 3 × £40 = £120 off. You pay £280.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30% off £400?

£280. 30% of £400 is £120, and £400 − £120 = £280. Or in one step: £400 × 0.7 = £280.

How much do I save with 30% off £400?

£120: £400 × 30 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 30% off in my head?

10% of £400 is £40, so 30% is 3 × £40 = £120 off. You pay £280.

Is 30% off and then 10% off the same as 40% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 30% off £400 is £280; another 10% off that is £252, a total saving of £148 or 37% — a little less than 40% off (£240).
