15% off £100

15% off £100 is £85 — you save £15.

How to work out 15% off £100

Step 1, the saving: £100 × 15 ÷ 100 = £15.

Step 2, the sale price: £100 − £15 = £85.

In one go: with 15% off you pay 85% of the price, so multiply by 0.85: £100 × 0.85 = £85.

In the shop: 10% of £100 is £10 and 5% is £5, so 15% off saves £15. You pay £85.

Frequently asked questions

What is 15% off £100?

£85. 15% of £100 is £15, and £100 − £15 = £85. Or in one step: £100 × 0.85 = £85.

How much do I save with 15% off £100?

£15: £100 × 15 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 15% off in my head?

10% of £100 is £10 and 5% is £5, so 15% off saves £15. You pay £85.

Is 15% off and then 10% off the same as 25% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 15% off £100 is £85; another 10% off that is £76.50, a total saving of £23.50 or 23.5% — a little less than 25% off (£75).
