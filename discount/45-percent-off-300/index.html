Frequently asked questions

What is 45% off £300?

£165. 45% of £300 is £135, and £300 − £135 = £165. Or in one step: £300 × 0.55 = £165.

How much do I save with 45% off £300?

£135: £300 × 45 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 45% off in my head?

10% of £300 is £30 and 5% is £15, so 45% is £135 off. You pay £165.

Is 45% off and then 10% off the same as 55% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 45% off £300 is £165; another 10% off that is £148.50, a total saving of £151.50 or 50.5% — a little less than 55% off (£135).
