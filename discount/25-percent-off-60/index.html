How to work out 25% off £60

Step 1, the saving: £60 × 25 ÷ 100 = £15.

Step 2, the sale price: £60 − £15 = £45.

In one go: with 25% off you pay 75% of the price, so multiply by 0.75: £60 × 0.75 = £45.

In the shop: 25% is a quarter. A quarter of £60 is £15, so you pay £45.

Frequently asked questions

What is 25% off £60?

£45. 25% of £60 is £15, and £60 − £15 = £45. Or in one step: £60 × 0.75 = £45.

How much do I save with 25% off £60?

£15: £60 × 25 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 25% off in my head?

25% is a quarter. A quarter of £60 is £15, so you pay £45.

Is 25% off and then 10% off the same as 35% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 25% off £60 is £45; another 10% off that is £40.50, a total saving of £19.50 or 32.5% — a little less than 35% off (£39).
