How to work out 10% off £50

Step 1, the saving: £50 × 10 ÷ 100 = £5.

Step 2, the sale price: £50 − £5 = £45.

In one go: with 10% off you pay 90% of the price, so multiply by 0.9: £50 × 0.9 = £45.

In the shop: 10% is a tenth: £50 ÷ 10 = £5 off, so you pay £45.

Frequently asked questions

What is 10% off £50?

£45. 10% of £50 is £5, and £50 − £5 = £45. Or in one step: £50 × 0.9 = £45.

How much do I save with 10% off £50?

£5: £50 × 10 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 10% off in my head?

10% is a tenth: £50 ÷ 10 = £5 off, so you pay £45.

Is 10% off and then 10% off the same as 20% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 10% off £50 is £45; another 10% off that is £40.50, a total saving of £9.50 or 19% — a little less than 20% off (£40).
