How to work out 40% off £500

Step 1, the saving: £500 × 40 ÷ 100 = £200.

Step 2, the sale price: £500 − £200 = £300.

In one go: with 40% off you pay 60% of the price, so multiply by 0.6: £500 × 0.6 = £300.

In the shop: 10% of £500 is £50, so 40% is 4 × £50 = £200 off. You pay £300.

Frequently asked questions

What is 40% off £500?

£300. 40% of £500 is £200, and £500 − £200 = £300. Or in one step: £500 × 0.6 = £300.

How much do I save with 40% off £500?

£200: £500 × 40 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 40% off in my head?

10% of £500 is £50, so 40% is 4 × £50 = £200 off. You pay £300.

Is 40% off and then 10% off the same as 50% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 40% off £500 is £300; another 10% off that is £270, a total saving of £230 or 46% — a little less than 50% off (£250).
