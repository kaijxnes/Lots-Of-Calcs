How to work out 33% off £75

Step 2, the sale price: £75 − £24.75 = £50.25.

Is 33% off and then 10% off the same as 43% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 33% off £75 is £50.25; another 10% off that is £45.23, a total saving of £29.77 or 39.7% — a little less than 43% off (£42.75).
