How to work out 25% off £200

Step 1, the saving: £200 × 25 ÷ 100 = £50.

Step 2, the sale price: £200 − £50 = £150.

In one go: with 25% off you pay 75% of the price, so multiply by 0.75: £200 × 0.75 = £150.

In the shop: 25% is a quarter. A quarter of £200 is £50, so you pay £150.

Frequently asked questions

What is 25% off £200?

£150. 25% of £200 is £50, and £200 − £50 = £150. Or in one step: £200 × 0.75 = £150.

How much do I save with 25% off £200?

£50: £200 × 25 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 25% off in my head?

25% is a quarter. A quarter of £200 is £50, so you pay £150.

Is 25% off and then 10% off the same as 35% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 25% off £200 is £150; another 10% off that is £135, a total saving of £65 or 32.5% — a little less than 35% off (£130).
