Frequently asked questions

What is 45% off £60?

£33. 45% of £60 is £27, and £60 − £27 = £33. Or in one step: £60 × 0.55 = £33.

How much do I save with 45% off £60?

£27: £60 × 45 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 45% off in my head?

10% of £60 is £6 and 5% is £3, so 45% is £27 off. You pay £33.

Is 45% off and then 10% off the same as 55% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 45% off £60 is £33; another 10% off that is £29.70, a total saving of £30.30 or 50.5% — a little less than 55% off (£27).
