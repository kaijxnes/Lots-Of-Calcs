How to work out 75% off £45

Step 1, the saving: £45 × 75 ÷ 100 = £33.75.

Step 2, the sale price: £45 − £33.75 = £11.25.

In one go: with 75% off you pay 25% of the price, so multiply by 0.25: £45 × 0.25 = £11.25.

In the shop: 75% off means you pay a quarter: £45 ÷ 4 = £11.25.

Frequently asked questions

What is 75% off £45?

£11.25. 75% of £45 is £33.75, and £45 − £33.75 = £11.25. Or in one step: £45 × 0.25 = £11.25.

How much do I save with 75% off £45?

£33.75: £45 × 75 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 75% off in my head?

75% off means you pay a quarter: £45 ÷ 4 = £11.25.

Is 75% off and then 10% off the same as 85% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 75% off £45 is £11.25; another 10% off that is £10.13, a total saving of £34.87 or 77.5% — a little less than 85% off (£6.75).
