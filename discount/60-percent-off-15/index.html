How to work out 60% off £15

Step 1, the saving: £15 × 60 ÷ 100 = £9.

Step 2, the sale price: £15 − £9 = £6.

In one go: with 60% off you pay 40% of the price, so multiply by 0.4: £15 × 0.4 = £6.

In the shop: 10% of £15 is £1.50, so 60% is 6 × £1.50 = £9 off. You pay £6.

Frequently asked questions

What is 60% off £15?

£6. 60% of £15 is £9, and £15 − £9 = £6. Or in one step: £15 × 0.4 = £6.

How much do I save with 60% off £15?

£9: £15 × 60 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 60% off in my head?

10% of £15 is £1.50, so 60% is 6 × £1.50 = £9 off. You pay £6.

Is 60% off and then 10% off the same as 70% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 60% off £15 is £6; another 10% off that is £5.40, a total saving of £9.60 or 64% — a little less than 70% off (£4.50).
