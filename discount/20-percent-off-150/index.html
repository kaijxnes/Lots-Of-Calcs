How to work out 20% off £150

Step 1, the saving: £150 × 20 ÷ 100 = £30.

Step 2, the sale price: £150 − £30 = £120.

In one go: with 20% off you pay 80% of the price, so multiply by 0.8: £150 × 0.8 = £120.

In the shop: 10% of £150 is £15, so 20% is 2 × £15 = £30 off. You pay £120.

Frequently asked questions

What is 20% off £150?

£120. 20% of £150 is £30, and £150 − £30 = £120. Or in one step: £150 × 0.8 = £120.

How much do I save with 20% off £150?

£30: £150 × 20 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 20% off in my head?

10% of £150 is £15, so 20% is 2 × £15 = £30 off. You pay £120.

Is 20% off and then 10% off the same as 30% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 20% off £150 is £120; another 10% off that is £108, a total saving of £42 or 28% — a little less than 30% off (£105).
