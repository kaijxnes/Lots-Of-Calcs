How to work out 30% off £1,000

Step 1, the saving: £1,000 × 30 ÷ 100 = £300.

Step 2, the sale price: £1,000 − £300 = £700.

In one go: with 30% off you pay 70% of the price, so multiply by 0.7: £1,000 × 0.7 = £700.

In the shop: 10% of £1,000 is £100, so 30% is 3 × £100 = £300 off. You pay £700.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30% off £1,000?

£700. 30% of £1,000 is £300, and £1,000 − £300 = £700. Or in one step: £1,000 × 0.7 = £700.

How much do I save with 30% off £1,000?

£300: £1,000 × 30 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 30% off in my head?

10% of £1,000 is £100, so 30% is 3 × £100 = £300 off. You pay £700.

Is 30% off and then 10% off the same as 40% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 30% off £1,000 is £700; another 10% off that is £630, a total saving of £370 or 37% — a little less than 40% off (£600).
