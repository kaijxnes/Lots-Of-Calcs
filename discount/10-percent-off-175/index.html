How to work out 10% off £175

Step 1, the saving: £175 × 10 ÷ 100 = £17.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £175 − £17.50 = £157.50.

In one go: with 10% off you pay 90% of the price, so multiply by 0.9: £175 × 0.9 = £157.50.

In the shop: 10% is a tenth: £175 ÷ 10 = £17.50 off, so you pay £157.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 10% off £175?

£157.50. 10% of £175 is £17.50, and £175 − £17.50 = £157.50. Or in one step: £175 × 0.9 = £157.50.

How much do I save with 10% off £175?

£17.50: £175 × 10 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 10% off in my head?

10% is a tenth: £175 ÷ 10 = £17.50 off, so you pay £157.50.

Is 10% off and then 10% off the same as 20% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 10% off £175 is £157.50; another 10% off that is £141.75, a total saving of £33.25 or 19% — a little less than 20% off (£140).
