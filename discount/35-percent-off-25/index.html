How to work out 35% off £25

Step 1, the saving: £25 × 35 ÷ 100 = £8.75.

Step 2, the sale price: £25 − £8.75 = £16.25.

In one go: with 35% off you pay 65% of the price, so multiply by 0.65: £25 × 0.65 = £16.25.

Frequently asked questions

What is 35% off £25?

£16.25. 35% of £25 is £8.75, and £25 − £8.75 = £16.25. Or in one step: £25 × 0.65 = £16.25.

How much do I save with 35% off £25?

£8.75: £25 × 35 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 35% off in my head?

10% of £25 is £2.50 and 5% is £1.25, so 35% is £8.75 off. You pay £16.25.

Is 35% off and then 10% off the same as 45% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 35% off £25 is £16.25; another 10% off that is £14.63, a total saving of £10.37 or 41.5% — a little less than 45% off (£13.75).
