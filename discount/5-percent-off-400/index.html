How to work out 5% off £400

Step 1, the saving: £400 × 5 ÷ 100 = £20.

Step 2, the sale price: £400 − £20 = £380.

In one go: with 5% off you pay 95% of the price, so multiply by 0.95: £400 × 0.95 = £380.

In the shop: 10% of £400 is £40; 5% is half that, £20. You pay £380.

Frequently asked questions

What is 5% off £400?

£380. 5% of £400 is £20, and £400 − £20 = £380. Or in one step: £400 × 0.95 = £380.

How much do I save with 5% off £400?

£20: £400 × 5 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 5% off in my head?

10% of £400 is £40; 5% is half that, £20. You pay £380.

Is 5% off and then 10% off the same as 15% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 5% off £400 is £380; another 10% off that is £342, a total saving of £58 or 14.5% — a little less than 15% off (£340).
