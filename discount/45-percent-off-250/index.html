Frequently asked questions

What is 45% off £250?

£137.50. 45% of £250 is £112.50, and £250 − £112.50 = £137.50. Or in one step: £250 × 0.55 = £137.50.

How much do I save with 45% off £250?

£112.50: £250 × 45 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 45% off in my head?

Is 45% off and then 10% off the same as 55% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 45% off £250 is £137.50; another 10% off that is £123.75, a total saving of £126.25 or 50.5% — a little less than 55% off (£112.50).
