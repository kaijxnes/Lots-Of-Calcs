How to work out 33% off £175

Step 2, the sale price: £175 − £57.75 = £117.25.

In the shop: 33% is just under a third. A third of £175 is £58.33; 33% exactly is £57.75, so you pay £117.25.

What is 33% off £175?

How do I work out 33% off in my head?

Is 33% off and then 10% off the same as 43% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 33% off £175 is £117.25; another 10% off that is £105.53, a total saving of £69.47 or 39.7% — a little less than 43% off (£99.75).
