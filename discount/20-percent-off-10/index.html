How to work out 20% off £10

Step 1, the saving: £10 × 20 ÷ 100 = £2.

Step 2, the sale price: £10 − £2 = £8.

In one go: with 20% off you pay 80% of the price, so multiply by 0.8: £10 × 0.8 = £8.

In the shop: 10% of £10 is £1, so 20% is 2 × £1 = £2 off. You pay £8.

Frequently asked questions

What is 20% off £10?

£8. 20% of £10 is £2, and £10 − £2 = £8. Or in one step: £10 × 0.8 = £8.

How much do I save with 20% off £10?

£2: £10 × 20 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 20% off in my head?

10% of £10 is £1, so 20% is 2 × £1 = £2 off. You pay £8.

Is 20% off and then 10% off the same as 30% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 20% off £10 is £8; another 10% off that is £7.20, a total saving of £2.80 or 28% — a little less than 30% off (£7).
