How to work out 15% off £10

Step 1, the saving: £10 × 15 ÷ 100 = £1.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £10 − £1.50 = £8.50.

In one go: with 15% off you pay 85% of the price, so multiply by 0.85: £10 × 0.85 = £8.50.

In the shop: 10% of £10 is £1 and 5% is £0.50, so 15% off saves £1.50. You pay £8.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 15% off £10?

£8.50. 15% of £10 is £1.50, and £10 − £1.50 = £8.50. Or in one step: £10 × 0.85 = £8.50.

How much do I save with 15% off £10?

£1.50: £10 × 15 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 15% off in my head?

10% of £10 is £1 and 5% is £0.50, so 15% off saves £1.50. You pay £8.50.

Is 15% off and then 10% off the same as 25% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 15% off £10 is £8.50; another 10% off that is £7.65, a total saving of £2.35 or 23.5% — a little less than 25% off (£7.50).
