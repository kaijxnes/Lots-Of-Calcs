How to work out 15% off £30

Step 1, the saving: £30 × 15 ÷ 100 = £4.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £30 − £4.50 = £25.50.

In one go: with 15% off you pay 85% of the price, so multiply by 0.85: £30 × 0.85 = £25.50.

In the shop: 10% of £30 is £3 and 5% is £1.50, so 15% off saves £4.50. You pay £25.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 15% off £30?

£25.50. 15% of £30 is £4.50, and £30 − £4.50 = £25.50. Or in one step: £30 × 0.85 = £25.50.

How much do I save with 15% off £30?

£4.50: £30 × 15 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 15% off in my head?

10% of £30 is £3 and 5% is £1.50, so 15% off saves £4.50. You pay £25.50.

Is 15% off and then 10% off the same as 25% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 15% off £30 is £25.50; another 10% off that is £22.95, a total saving of £7.05 or 23.5% — a little less than 25% off (£22.50).
