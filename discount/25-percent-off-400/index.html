How to work out 25% off £400

Step 1, the saving: £400 × 25 ÷ 100 = £100.

Step 2, the sale price: £400 − £100 = £300.

In one go: with 25% off you pay 75% of the price, so multiply by 0.75: £400 × 0.75 = £300.

In the shop: 25% is a quarter. A quarter of £400 is £100, so you pay £300.

Frequently asked questions

What is 25% off £400?

£300. 25% of £400 is £100, and £400 − £100 = £300. Or in one step: £400 × 0.75 = £300.

How much do I save with 25% off £400?

£100: £400 × 25 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 25% off in my head?

25% is a quarter. A quarter of £400 is £100, so you pay £300.

Is 25% off and then 10% off the same as 35% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 25% off £400 is £300; another 10% off that is £270, a total saving of £130 or 32.5% — a little less than 35% off (£260).
