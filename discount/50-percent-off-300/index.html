How to work out 50% off £300

Step 1, the saving: £300 × 50 ÷ 100 = £150.

Step 2, the sale price: £300 − £150 = £150.

Frequently asked questions

What is 50% off £300?

£150. 50% of £300 is £150, and £300 − £150 = £150. Or in one step: £300 × 0.5 = £150.

How much do I save with 50% off £300?

£150: £300 × 50 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 50% off in my head?

50% off is half price: £300 ÷ 2 = £150.

Is 50% off and then 10% off the same as 60% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 50% off £300 is £150; another 10% off that is £135, a total saving of £165 or 55% — a little less than 60% off (£120).
