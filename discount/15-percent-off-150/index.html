Frequently asked questions

What is 15% off £150?

£127.50. 15% of £150 is £22.50, and £150 − £22.50 = £127.50. Or in one step: £150 × 0.85 = £127.50.

How much do I save with 15% off £150?

How do I work out 15% off in my head?

Is 15% off and then 10% off the same as 25% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 15% off £150 is £127.50; another 10% off that is £114.75, a total saving of £35.25 or 23.5% — a little less than 25% off (£112.50).
