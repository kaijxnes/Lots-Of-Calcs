How to work out 70% off £175

Step 1, the saving: £175 × 70 ÷ 100 = £122.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £175 − £122.50 = £52.50.

In one go: with 70% off you pay 30% of the price, so multiply by 0.3: £175 × 0.3 = £52.50.

In the shop: 10% of £175 is £17.50, so 70% is 7 × £17.50 = £122.50 off. You pay £52.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 70% off £175?

£52.50. 70% of £175 is £122.50, and £175 − £122.50 = £52.50. Or in one step: £175 × 0.3 = £52.50.

How much do I save with 70% off £175?

£122.50: £175 × 70 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 70% off in my head?

10% of £175 is £17.50, so 70% is 7 × £17.50 = £122.50 off. You pay £52.50.

Is 70% off and then 10% off the same as 80% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 70% off £175 is £52.50; another 10% off that is £47.25, a total saving of £127.75 or 73% — a little less than 80% off (£35).
