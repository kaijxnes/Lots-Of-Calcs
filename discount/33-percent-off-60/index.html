How to work out 33% off £60

Step 1, the saving: £60 × 33 ÷ 100 = £19.80.

Step 2, the sale price: £60 − £19.80 = £40.20.

In one go: with 33% off you pay 67% of the price, so multiply by 0.67: £60 × 0.67 = £40.20.

In the shop: 33% is just under a third. A third of £60 is £20; 33% exactly is £19.80, so you pay £40.20.

Frequently asked questions

What is 33% off £60?

£40.20. 33% of £60 is £19.80, and £60 − £19.80 = £40.20. Or in one step: £60 × 0.67 = £40.20.

How much do I save with 33% off £60?

£19.80: £60 × 33 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 33% off in my head?

33% is just under a third. A third of £60 is £20; 33% exactly is £19.80, so you pay £40.20.

Is 33% off and then 10% off the same as 43% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 33% off £60 is £40.20; another 10% off that is £36.18, a total saving of £23.82 or 39.7% — a little less than 43% off (£34.20).
