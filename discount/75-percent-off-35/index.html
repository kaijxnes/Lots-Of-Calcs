How to work out 75% off £35

Step 1, the saving: £35 × 75 ÷ 100 = £26.25.

Step 2, the sale price: £35 − £26.25 = £8.75.

In one go: with 75% off you pay 25% of the price, so multiply by 0.25: £35 × 0.25 = £8.75.

In the shop: 75% off means you pay a quarter: £35 ÷ 4 = £8.75.

Frequently asked questions

What is 75% off £35?

£8.75. 75% of £35 is £26.25, and £35 − £26.25 = £8.75. Or in one step: £35 × 0.25 = £8.75.

How much do I save with 75% off £35?

£26.25: £35 × 75 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 75% off in my head?

75% off means you pay a quarter: £35 ÷ 4 = £8.75.

Is 75% off and then 10% off the same as 85% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 75% off £35 is £8.75; another 10% off that is £7.88, a total saving of £27.12 or 77.5% — a little less than 85% off (£5.25).
