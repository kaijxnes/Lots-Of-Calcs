How to work out 33% off £20

Step 1, the saving: £20 × 33 ÷ 100 = £6.60.

Step 2, the sale price: £20 − £6.60 = £13.40.

In one go: with 33% off you pay 67% of the price, so multiply by 0.67: £20 × 0.67 = £13.40.

In the shop: 33% is just under a third. A third of £20 is £6.67; 33% exactly is £6.60, so you pay £13.40.

Frequently asked questions

What is 33% off £20?

£13.40. 33% of £20 is £6.60, and £20 − £6.60 = £13.40. Or in one step: £20 × 0.67 = £13.40.

How much do I save with 33% off £20?

£6.60: £20 × 33 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 33% off in my head?

33% is just under a third. A third of £20 is £6.67; 33% exactly is £6.60, so you pay £13.40.

Is 33% off and then 10% off the same as 43% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 33% off £20 is £13.40; another 10% off that is £12.06, a total saving of £7.94 or 39.7% — a little less than 43% off (£11.40).
