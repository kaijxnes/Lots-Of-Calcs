How to work out 25% off £100

Step 1, the saving: £100 × 25 ÷ 100 = £25.

Step 2, the sale price: £100 − £25 = £75.

In one go: with 25% off you pay 75% of the price, so multiply by 0.75: £100 × 0.75 = £75.

In the shop: 25% is a quarter. A quarter of £100 is £25, so you pay £75.

Frequently asked questions

What is 25% off £100?

£75. 25% of £100 is £25, and £100 − £25 = £75. Or in one step: £100 × 0.75 = £75.

How much do I save with 25% off £100?

£25: £100 × 25 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 25% off in my head?

25% is a quarter. A quarter of £100 is £25, so you pay £75.

Is 25% off and then 10% off the same as 35% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 25% off £100 is £75; another 10% off that is £67.50, a total saving of £32.50 or 32.5% — a little less than 35% off (£65).
