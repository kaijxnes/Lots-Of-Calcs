What is 45% off £175?

£96.25. 45% of £175 is £78.75, and £175 − £78.75 = £96.25. Or in one step: £175 × 0.55 = £96.25.

How much do I save with 45% off £175?

How do I work out 45% off in my head?

Is 45% off and then 10% off the same as 55% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 45% off £175 is £96.25; another 10% off that is £86.63, a total saving of £88.37 or 50.5% — a little less than 55% off (£78.75).
