How to work out 50% off £20

Step 1, the saving: £20 × 50 ÷ 100 = £10.

Step 2, the sale price: £20 − £10 = £10.

In one go: with 50% off you pay 50% of the price, so multiply by 0.5: £20 × 0.5 = £10.

In the shop: 50% off is half price: £20 ÷ 2 = £10.

Frequently asked questions

What is 50% off £20?

£10. 50% of £20 is £10, and £20 − £10 = £10. Or in one step: £20 × 0.5 = £10.

How much do I save with 50% off £20?

£10: £20 × 50 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 50% off in my head?

50% off is half price: £20 ÷ 2 = £10.

Is 50% off and then 10% off the same as 60% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 50% off £20 is £10; another 10% off that is £9, a total saving of £11 or 55% — a little less than 60% off (£8).
