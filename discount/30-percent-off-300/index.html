How to work out 30% off £300

Step 1, the saving: £300 × 30 ÷ 100 = £90.

Step 2, the sale price: £300 − £90 = £210.

In one go: with 30% off you pay 70% of the price, so multiply by 0.7: £300 × 0.7 = £210.

In the shop: 10% of £300 is £30, so 30% is 3 × £30 = £90 off. You pay £210.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30% off £300?

£210. 30% of £300 is £90, and £300 − £90 = £210. Or in one step: £300 × 0.7 = £210.

How much do I save with 30% off £300?

£90: £300 × 30 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 30% off in my head?

10% of £300 is £30, so 30% is 3 × £30 = £90 off. You pay £210.

Is 30% off and then 10% off the same as 40% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 30% off £300 is £210; another 10% off that is £189, a total saving of £111 or 37% — a little less than 40% off (£180).
