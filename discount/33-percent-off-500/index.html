How to work out 33% off £500

Step 1, the saving: £500 × 33 ÷ 100 = £165.

Step 2, the sale price: £500 − £165 = £335.

In one go: with 33% off you pay 67% of the price, so multiply by 0.67: £500 × 0.67 = £335.

In the shop: 33% is just under a third. A third of £500 is £166.67; 33% exactly is £165, so you pay £335.

Frequently asked questions

What is 33% off £500?

£335. 33% of £500 is £165, and £500 − £165 = £335. Or in one step: £500 × 0.67 = £335.

How much do I save with 33% off £500?

£165: £500 × 33 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 33% off in my head?

33% is just under a third. A third of £500 is £166.67; 33% exactly is £165, so you pay £335.

Is 33% off and then 10% off the same as 43% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 33% off £500 is £335; another 10% off that is £301.50, a total saving of £198.50 or 39.7% — a little less than 43% off (£285).
