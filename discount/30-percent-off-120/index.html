How to work out 30% off £120

Step 1, the saving: £120 × 30 ÷ 100 = £36.

Step 2, the sale price: £120 − £36 = £84.

In one go: with 30% off you pay 70% of the price, so multiply by 0.7: £120 × 0.7 = £84.

In the shop: 10% of £120 is £12, so 30% is 3 × £12 = £36 off. You pay £84.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30% off £120?

£84. 30% of £120 is £36, and £120 − £36 = £84. Or in one step: £120 × 0.7 = £84.

How much do I save with 30% off £120?

£36: £120 × 30 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 30% off in my head?

10% of £120 is £12, so 30% is 3 × £12 = £36 off. You pay £84.

Is 30% off and then 10% off the same as 40% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 30% off £120 is £84; another 10% off that is £75.60, a total saving of £44.40 or 37% — a little less than 40% off (£72).
