How to work out 70% off £50

Step 1, the saving: £50 × 70 ÷ 100 = £35.

Step 2, the sale price: £50 − £35 = £15.

In one go: with 70% off you pay 30% of the price, so multiply by 0.3: £50 × 0.3 = £15.

In the shop: 10% of £50 is £5, so 70% is 7 × £5 = £35 off. You pay £15.

Frequently asked questions

What is 70% off £50?

£15. 70% of £50 is £35, and £50 − £35 = £15. Or in one step: £50 × 0.3 = £15.

How much do I save with 70% off £50?

£35: £50 × 70 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 70% off in my head?

10% of £50 is £5, so 70% is 7 × £5 = £35 off. You pay £15.

Is 70% off and then 10% off the same as 80% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 70% off £50 is £15; another 10% off that is £13.50, a total saving of £36.50 or 73% — a little less than 80% off (£10).
