How to work out 10% off £200

Step 1, the saving: £200 × 10 ÷ 100 = £20.

Step 2, the sale price: £200 − £20 = £180.

In one go: with 10% off you pay 90% of the price, so multiply by 0.9: £200 × 0.9 = £180.

In the shop: 10% is a tenth: £200 ÷ 10 = £20 off, so you pay £180.

Frequently asked questions

What is 10% off £200?

£180. 10% of £200 is £20, and £200 − £20 = £180. Or in one step: £200 × 0.9 = £180.

How much do I save with 10% off £200?

£20: £200 × 10 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 10% off in my head?

10% is a tenth: £200 ÷ 10 = £20 off, so you pay £180.

Is 10% off and then 10% off the same as 20% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 10% off £200 is £180; another 10% off that is £162, a total saving of £38 or 19% — a little less than 20% off (£160).
