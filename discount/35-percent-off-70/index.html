How to work out 35% off £70

Step 2, the sale price: £70 − £24.50 = £45.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 35% off £70?

£45.50. 35% of £70 is £24.50, and £70 − £24.50 = £45.50. Or in one step: £70 × 0.65 = £45.50.

How much do I save with 35% off £70?

£24.50: £70 × 35 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 35% off in my head?

10% of £70 is £7 and 5% is £3.50, so 35% is £24.50 off. You pay £45.50.

Is 35% off and then 10% off the same as 45% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 35% off £70 is £45.50; another 10% off that is £40.95, a total saving of £29.05 or 41.5% — a little less than 45% off (£38.50).
