Frequently asked questions

What is 60% off £750?

£300. 60% of £750 is £450, and £750 − £450 = £300. Or in one step: £750 × 0.4 = £300.

How much do I save with 60% off £750?

£450: £750 × 60 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 60% off in my head?

10% of £750 is £75, so 60% is 6 × £75 = £450 off. You pay £300.

Is 60% off and then 10% off the same as 70% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 60% off £750 is £300; another 10% off that is £270, a total saving of £480 or 64% — a little less than 70% off (£225).
