How to work out 25% off £750

Step 2, the sale price: £750 − £187.50 = £562.50.

In the shop: 25% is a quarter. A quarter of £750 is £187.50, so you pay £562.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 25% off £750?

£562.50. 25% of £750 is £187.50, and £750 − £187.50 = £562.50. Or in one step: £750 × 0.75 = £562.50.

How much do I save with 25% off £750?

£187.50: £750 × 25 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 25% off in my head?

Is 25% off and then 10% off the same as 35% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 25% off £750 is £562.50; another 10% off that is £506.25, a total saving of £243.75 or 32.5% — a little less than 35% off (£487.50).
