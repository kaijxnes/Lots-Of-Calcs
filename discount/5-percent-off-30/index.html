How to work out 5% off £30

Step 1, the saving: £30 × 5 ÷ 100 = £1.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £30 − £1.50 = £28.50.

In one go: with 5% off you pay 95% of the price, so multiply by 0.95: £30 × 0.95 = £28.50.

In the shop: 10% of £30 is £3; 5% is half that, £1.50. You pay £28.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 5% off £30?

£28.50. 5% of £30 is £1.50, and £30 − £1.50 = £28.50. Or in one step: £30 × 0.95 = £28.50.

How much do I save with 5% off £30?

£1.50: £30 × 5 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 5% off in my head?

10% of £30 is £3; 5% is half that, £1.50. You pay £28.50.

Is 5% off and then 10% off the same as 15% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 5% off £30 is £28.50; another 10% off that is £25.65, a total saving of £4.35 or 14.5% — a little less than 15% off (£25.50).
