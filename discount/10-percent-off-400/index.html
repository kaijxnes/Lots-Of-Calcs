Frequently asked questions

What is 10% off £400?

£360. 10% of £400 is £40, and £400 − £40 = £360. Or in one step: £400 × 0.9 = £360.

How much do I save with 10% off £400?

£40: £400 × 10 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 10% off in my head?

10% is a tenth: £400 ÷ 10 = £40 off, so you pay £360.

Is 10% off and then 10% off the same as 20% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 10% off £400 is £360; another 10% off that is £324, a total saving of £76 or 19% — a little less than 20% off (£320).
