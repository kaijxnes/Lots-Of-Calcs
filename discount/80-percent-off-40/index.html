How to work out 80% off £40

Step 1, the saving: £40 × 80 ÷ 100 = £32.

Step 2, the sale price: £40 − £32 = £8.

In one go: with 80% off you pay 20% of the price, so multiply by 0.2: £40 × 0.2 = £8.

In the shop: 10% of £40 is £4, so 80% is 8 × £4 = £32 off. You pay £8.

Frequently asked questions

What is 80% off £40?

£8. 80% of £40 is £32, and £40 − £32 = £8. Or in one step: £40 × 0.2 = £8.

How much do I save with 80% off £40?

£32: £40 × 80 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 80% off in my head?

10% of £40 is £4, so 80% is 8 × £4 = £32 off. You pay £8.

Is 80% off and then 10% off the same as 90% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 80% off £40 is £8; another 10% off that is £7.20, a total saving of £32.80 or 82% — a little less than 90% off (£4).
