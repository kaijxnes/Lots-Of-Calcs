Frequently asked questions

What is 15% off £400?

£340. 15% of £400 is £60, and £400 − £60 = £340. Or in one step: £400 × 0.85 = £340.

How much do I save with 15% off £400?

£60: £400 × 15 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 15% off in my head?

Is 15% off and then 10% off the same as 25% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 15% off £400 is £340; another 10% off that is £306, a total saving of £94 or 23.5% — a little less than 25% off (£300).
