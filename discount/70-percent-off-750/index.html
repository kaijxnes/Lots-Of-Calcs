Frequently asked questions

What is 70% off £750?

How much do I save with 70% off £750?

How do I work out 70% off in my head?

Is 70% off and then 10% off the same as 80% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 70% off £750 is £225; another 10% off that is £202.50, a total saving of £547.50 or 73% — a little less than 80% off (£150).
