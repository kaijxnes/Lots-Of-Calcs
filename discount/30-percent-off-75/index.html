How to work out 30% off £75

Step 1, the saving: £75 × 30 ÷ 100 = £22.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £75 − £22.50 = £52.50.

In one go: with 30% off you pay 70% of the price, so multiply by 0.7: £75 × 0.7 = £52.50.

In the shop: 10% of £75 is £7.50, so 30% is 3 × £7.50 = £22.50 off. You pay £52.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30% off £75?

£52.50. 30% of £75 is £22.50, and £75 − £22.50 = £52.50. Or in one step: £75 × 0.7 = £52.50.

How much do I save with 30% off £75?

£22.50: £75 × 30 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 30% off in my head?

10% of £75 is £7.50, so 30% is 3 × £7.50 = £22.50 off. You pay £52.50.

Is 30% off and then 10% off the same as 40% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 30% off £75 is £52.50; another 10% off that is £47.25, a total saving of £27.75 or 37% — a little less than 40% off (£45).
