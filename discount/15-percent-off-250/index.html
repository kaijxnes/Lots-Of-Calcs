How to work out 15% off £250

Step 1, the saving: £250 × 15 ÷ 100 = £37.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £250 − £37.50 = £212.50.

In one go: with 15% off you pay 85% of the price, so multiply by 0.85: £250 × 0.85 = £212.50.

In the shop: 10% of £250 is £25 and 5% is £12.50, so 15% off saves £37.50. You pay £212.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 15% off £250?

£212.50. 15% of £250 is £37.50, and £250 − £37.50 = £212.50. Or in one step: £250 × 0.85 = £212.50.

How much do I save with 15% off £250?

£37.50: £250 × 15 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 15% off in my head?

10% of £250 is £25 and 5% is £12.50, so 15% off saves £37.50. You pay £212.50.

Is 15% off and then 10% off the same as 25% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 15% off £250 is £212.50; another 10% off that is £191.25, a total saving of £58.75 or 23.5% — a little less than 25% off (£187.50).
