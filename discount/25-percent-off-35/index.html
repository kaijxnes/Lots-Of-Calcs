How to work out 25% off £35

Step 1, the saving: £35 × 25 ÷ 100 = £8.75.

Step 2, the sale price: £35 − £8.75 = £26.25.

In one go: with 25% off you pay 75% of the price, so multiply by 0.75: £35 × 0.75 = £26.25.

In the shop: 25% is a quarter. A quarter of £35 is £8.75, so you pay £26.25.

Frequently asked questions

What is 25% off £35?

£26.25. 25% of £35 is £8.75, and £35 − £8.75 = £26.25. Or in one step: £35 × 0.75 = £26.25.

How much do I save with 25% off £35?

£8.75: £35 × 25 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 25% off in my head?

25% is a quarter. A quarter of £35 is £8.75, so you pay £26.25.

Is 25% off and then 10% off the same as 35% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 25% off £35 is £26.25; another 10% off that is £23.63, a total saving of £11.37 or 32.5% — a little less than 35% off (£22.75).
