How to work out 40% off £45

Step 1, the saving: £45 × 40 ÷ 100 = £18.

Step 2, the sale price: £45 − £18 = £27.

Frequently asked questions

What is 40% off £45?

£27. 40% of £45 is £18, and £45 − £18 = £27. Or in one step: £45 × 0.6 = £27.

How much do I save with 40% off £45?

£18: £45 × 40 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 40% off in my head?

10% of £45 is £4.50, so 40% is 4 × £4.50 = £18 off. You pay £27.

Is 40% off and then 10% off the same as 50% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 40% off £45 is £27; another 10% off that is £24.30, a total saving of £20.70 or 46% — a little less than 50% off (£22.50).
