How to work out 30% off £15

Step 1, the saving: £15 × 30 ÷ 100 = £4.50.

Step 2, the sale price: £15 − £4.50 = £10.50.

In one go: with 30% off you pay 70% of the price, so multiply by 0.7: £15 × 0.7 = £10.50.

In the shop: 10% of £15 is £1.50, so 30% is 3 × £1.50 = £4.50 off. You pay £10.50.

Frequently asked questions

What is 30% off £15?

£10.50. 30% of £15 is £4.50, and £15 − £4.50 = £10.50. Or in one step: £15 × 0.7 = £10.50.

How much do I save with 30% off £15?

£4.50: £15 × 30 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 30% off in my head?

10% of £15 is £1.50, so 30% is 3 × £1.50 = £4.50 off. You pay £10.50.

Is 30% off and then 10% off the same as 40% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 30% off £15 is £10.50; another 10% off that is £9.45, a total saving of £5.55 or 37% — a little less than 40% off (£9).
