How to work out 75% off £1,000

Step 1, the saving: £1,000 × 75 ÷ 100 = £750.

Step 2, the sale price: £1,000 − £750 = £250.

In one go: with 75% off you pay 25% of the price, so multiply by 0.25: £1,000 × 0.25 = £250.

In the shop: 75% off means you pay a quarter: £1,000 ÷ 4 = £250.

Frequently asked questions

What is 75% off £1,000?

£250. 75% of £1,000 is £750, and £1,000 − £750 = £250. Or in one step: £1,000 × 0.25 = £250.

How much do I save with 75% off £1,000?

£750: £1,000 × 75 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 75% off in my head?

75% off means you pay a quarter: £1,000 ÷ 4 = £250.

Is 75% off and then 10% off the same as 85% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 75% off £1,000 is £250; another 10% off that is £225, a total saving of £775 or 77.5% — a little less than 85% off (£150).
