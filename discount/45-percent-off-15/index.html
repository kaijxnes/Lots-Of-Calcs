How to work out 45% off £15

Step 1, the saving: £15 × 45 ÷ 100 = £6.75.

Step 2, the sale price: £15 − £6.75 = £8.25.

In one go: with 45% off you pay 55% of the price, so multiply by 0.55: £15 × 0.55 = £8.25.

In the shop: 10% of £15 is £1.50 and 5% is £0.75, so 45% is £6.75 off. You pay £8.25.

Frequently asked questions

What is 45% off £15?

£8.25. 45% of £15 is £6.75, and £15 − £6.75 = £8.25. Or in one step: £15 × 0.55 = £8.25.

How much do I save with 45% off £15?

£6.75: £15 × 45 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 45% off in my head?

10% of £15 is £1.50 and 5% is £0.75, so 45% is £6.75 off. You pay £8.25.

Is 45% off and then 10% off the same as 55% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 45% off £15 is £8.25; another 10% off that is £7.43, a total saving of £7.57 or 50.5% — a little less than 55% off (£6.75).
