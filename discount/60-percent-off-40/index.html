How to work out 60% off £40

Step 1, the saving: £40 × 60 ÷ 100 = £24.

Step 2, the sale price: £40 − £24 = £16.

In one go: with 60% off you pay 40% of the price, so multiply by 0.4: £40 × 0.4 = £16.

In the shop: 10% of £40 is £4, so 60% is 6 × £4 = £24 off. You pay £16.

Frequently asked questions

What is 60% off £40?

£16. 60% of £40 is £24, and £40 − £24 = £16. Or in one step: £40 × 0.4 = £16.

How much do I save with 60% off £40?

£24: £40 × 60 ÷ 100.

How do I work out 60% off in my head?

10% of £40 is £4, so 60% is 6 × £4 = £24 off. You pay £16.

Is 60% off and then 10% off the same as 70% off?

No — the second discount comes off the already-reduced price. 60% off £40 is £16; another 10% off that is £14.40, a total saving of £25.60 or 64% — a little less than 70% off (£12).
