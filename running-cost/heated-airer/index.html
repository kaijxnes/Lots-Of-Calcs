Frequently asked questions

How much does a heated airer cost to run?

About 31.6p per 4 hours, using 1.2 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 3 times a week, that's around £4.11 a month or £49.27 a year.

How much does a heated airer cost per hour?

A 300 W heated airer uses 0.3 kWh an hour, which costs 7.9p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

Much cheaper per load than a tumble dryer, though slower.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
