Frequently asked questions

How much does an iron cost to run?

About 31.6p per 30 minutes, using 1.2 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 1 times a week, that's around £1.37 a month or £16.42 a year.

How much does an iron cost per hour?

A 2,400 W iron uses 2.4 kWh an hour, which costs 63.2p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

Irons cycle their element on and off, so they draw full power only part of the time.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
