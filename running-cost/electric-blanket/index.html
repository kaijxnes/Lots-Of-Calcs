Frequently asked questions

How much does an electric blanket cost to run?

About 1.6p per hour, using 0.06 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 7 times a week, that's around 47.9p a month or £5.75 a year.

How much does an electric blanket cost per hour?

A 60 W electric blanket uses 0.06 kWh an hour, which costs 1.6p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

Warming the bed, not the room, is one of the cheapest ways to keep warm at night.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
