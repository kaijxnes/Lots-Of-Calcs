Frequently asked questions

How much does a vacuum cleaner cost to run?

About 11.8p per 30 minutes, using 0.45 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 2 times a week, that's around £1.03 a month or £12.32 a year.

How much does a vacuum cleaner cost per hour?

A 900 W vacuum cleaner uses 0.9 kWh an hour, which costs 23.7p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

Mains vacuums sold in the UK are limited to 900 W.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
