Frequently asked questions

How much does a laptop cost to run?

About 1.3p per hour, using 0.05 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 35 times a week, that's around £2.00 a month or £23.95 a year.

How much does a laptop cost per hour?

A 50 W laptop uses 0.05 kWh an hour, which costs 1.3p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

Laptops use a fraction of a desktop's power.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
