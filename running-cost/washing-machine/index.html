Frequently asked questions

How much does a washing machine cost to run?

About 21.1p per 40°C wash, using 0.8 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 4 times a week, that's around £3.65 a month or £43.80 a year.

How much energy does a washing machine use?

About 0.8 kWh per 40°C wash for a typical model; the energy label gives the figure for yours.

How can I make it cheaper?

Most of the energy goes on heating water: a 30°C wash uses roughly a third less than 40°C.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
