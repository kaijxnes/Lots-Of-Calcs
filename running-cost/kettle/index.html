Frequently asked questions

How much does a kettle cost to run?

About 3.9p per boil, using 0.15 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 28 times a week, that's around £4.79 a month or £57.48 a year.

How much does a kettle cost per hour?

A 3,000 W kettle uses 3 kWh an hour, which costs 79p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

Only boil the water you need: a full kettle takes about four times as long as a mug's worth.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
