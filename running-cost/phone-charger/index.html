Frequently asked questions

How much does a phone charger cost to run?

About 0.4p per full charge, using 0.015 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 7 times a week, that's around 12p a month or £1.44 a year.

How much energy does a phone charger use?

About 0.015 kWh per full charge for a typical model; the energy label gives the figure for yours.

How can I make it cheaper?

A full phone charge costs well under a penny.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
