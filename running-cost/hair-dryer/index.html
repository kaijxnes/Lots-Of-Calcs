Frequently asked questions

How much does a hair dryer cost to run?

About 8.8p per 10-minute dry, using 0.333 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 5 times a week, that's around £1.90 a month or £22.81 a year.

How much does a hair dryer cost per hour?

A 2,000 W hair dryer uses 2 kWh an hour, which costs 52.6p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

Towel-drying first cuts the time on high heat.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
