Frequently asked questions

How much does an electric oven cost to run?

About 39.5p per hour of cooking, using 1.5 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 4 times a week, that's around £6.84 a month or £82.12 a year.

How much energy does an electric oven use?

About 1.5 kWh per hour of cooking for a typical model; the energy label gives the figure for yours.

How can I make it cheaper?

An oven draws full power while heating up, then cycles on and off; batch cooking spreads the heat-up across more food.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
