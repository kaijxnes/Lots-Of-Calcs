Frequently asked questions

How much does a fridge freezer cost to run?

About 18.4p per day, using 0.7 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 7 times a week, that's around £5.59 a month or £67.06 a year.

How much energy does a fridge freezer use?

About 0.7 kWh per day for a typical model; the energy label gives the figure for yours.

How can I make it cheaper?

Running all the time, so the yearly figure on the energy label is the one to check: about 250 kWh for a typical model.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
