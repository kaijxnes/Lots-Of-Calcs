Frequently asked questions

How much does an LED light bulb cost to run?

About 0.2p per hour, using 0.008 kWh at the energy price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh (1 October to 31 December 2026). Used 35 times a week, that's around 31.9p a month or £3.83 a year.

How much does an LED light bulb cost per hour?

An 8 W LED light bulb uses 0.008 kWh an hour, which costs 0.2p at 26.32p per kWh. Check the wattage on its label or plug and multiply: kW × hours × price per kWh.

How can I make it cheaper?

An 8 W LED gives about the light of an old 60 W bulb.

Does this include the standing charge?

No. The standing charge (54.83p a day under the price cap) is paid whatever you use, so it doesn't change with any one appliance. Fixed tariffs and some regions charge a different unit rate — put yours in the calculator.
