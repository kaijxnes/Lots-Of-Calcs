Frequently asked questions

What months have 3 paychecks in 2030?

Paid every other Friday from Friday January 4, 2030: March and August. From Friday January 11, 2030: May and November.

How many pay periods are in 2030?

26 for the cycle starting 4 Jan and 26 for the one starting 11 Jan. Neither Friday cycle gets a 27th paycheck in 2030.

What if I'm not paid on Fridays?

Each weekday has two biweekly cycles too; the table on this page lists the three-paycheck months for every one of them in 2030. Or put any one of your paydays into the biweekly pay schedule calculator.

Which months have 5 paychecks in 2030 if I'm paid weekly?

Paid every Friday: March, May, August and November (52 paydays in the year). Other weekdays are in the table.
