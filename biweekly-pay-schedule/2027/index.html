Frequently asked questions

What months have 3 paychecks in 2027?

Paid every other Friday from Friday January 1, 2027: January, July and December. From Friday January 8, 2027: April and October.

How many pay periods are in 2027?

27 for the cycle starting 1 Jan and 26 for the one starting 8 Jan. So 2027 is a 27-paycheck year for one of the Friday cycles.

What if I'm not paid on Fridays?

Put any one of your paydays into the biweekly pay schedule calculator and it lists every payday in the year, whatever the weekday.
