Frequently asked questions

What months have 3 paychecks in 2028?

Paid every other Friday from Friday January 7, 2028: March and September. From Friday January 14, 2028: June and December.

How many pay periods are in 2028?

26 for the cycle starting 7 Jan and 26 for the one starting 14 Jan. Neither Friday cycle gets a 27th paycheck in 2028.

What if I'm not paid on Fridays?

Put any one of your paydays into the biweekly pay schedule calculator and it lists every payday in the year, whatever the weekday.
