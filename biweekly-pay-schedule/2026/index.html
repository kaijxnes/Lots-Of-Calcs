Frequently asked questions

What months have 3 paychecks in 2026?

Paid every other Friday from Friday January 2, 2026: January and July. From Friday January 9, 2026: May and October.

How many pay periods are in 2026?

26 for the cycle starting 2 Jan and 26 for the one starting 9 Jan. Neither Friday cycle gets a 27th paycheck in 2026.

What if I'm not paid on Fridays?

Put any one of your paydays into the biweekly pay schedule calculator and it lists every payday in the year, whatever the weekday.
