Frequently asked questions

Which months have three paychecks in 2027?

It depends on your pay cycle. Paid on Fridays starting Friday January 1, 2027, it's January, July and December; starting Friday January 8, 2027, it's April and October. Enter any of your paydays above for your own dates.

How many paychecks are there in a year if I'm paid biweekly?

Usually 26. A year has 52 weeks and one day (two in a leap year), so every 11 years or so the extra days add up and one biweekly cycle gets 27 paydays. The table below shows which years.

Is biweekly the same as twice a month?

No. Biweekly is every two weeks — 26 paychecks a year, two most months and three in two of them. Twice a month (semi-monthly) is 24 paychecks on fixed dates, often the 15th and the last day of the month, so each paycheck is a bit larger.

How should I budget with biweekly pay?

Plan your monthly bills around two paychecks. The months with a third paycheck then give you an extra one to save, pay down debt or cover yearly costs, and the 50/30/20 budget calculator shows how to split the rest.
