Frequently asked questions

What is 90 in Roman numerals?

XC. It breaks down as XC (90) = 90.

How do you read XC?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XC is 100 − 10 = 90.

Can 90 be written another way?

XC is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
