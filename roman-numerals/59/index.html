Frequently asked questions

What is 59 in Roman numerals?

LIX. It breaks down as L (50) + IX (9) = 59.

How do you read LIX?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: IX is 10 − 1 = 9.

Can 59 be written another way?

LIX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
