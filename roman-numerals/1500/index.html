Frequently asked questions

What is 1500 in Roman numerals?

MD. It breaks down as M (1,000) + D (500) = 1500.

How do you read MD?

Add the symbols from left to right: 1000 + 500 = 1500. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 1500 be written another way?

MD is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1500 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MD. For example, 14 March 1500 is XIV.III.MD written day first, or III.XIV.MD month first as in the US.
