Frequently asked questions

What is 96 in Roman numerals?

XCVI. It breaks down as XC (90) + V (5) + I (1) = 96.

How do you read XCVI?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XC is 100 − 10 = 90.

Can 96 be written another way?

XCVI is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
