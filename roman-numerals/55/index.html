Frequently asked questions

What is 55 in Roman numerals?

LV. It breaks down as L (50) + V (5) = 55.

How do you read LV?

Add the symbols from left to right: 50 + 5 = 55. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 55 be written another way?

LV is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
