Frequently asked questions

What is 840 in Roman numerals?

DCCCXL. It breaks down as D (500) + C (100) + C (100) + C (100) + XL (40) = 840.

How do you read DCCCXL?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XL is 50 − 10 = 40.

Can 840 be written another way?

DCCCXL is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
