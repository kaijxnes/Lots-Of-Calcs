Frequently asked questions

What is 2016 in Roman numerals?

MMXVI. It breaks down as M (1,000) + M (1,000) + X (10) + V (5) + I (1) = 2016.

How do you read MMXVI?

Add the symbols from left to right: 1000 + 1000 + 10 + 5 + 1 = 2016. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 2016 be written another way?

MMXVI is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 2016 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MMXVI. For example, 14 March 2016 is XIV.III.MMXVI written day first, or III.XIV.MMXVI month first as in the US.
