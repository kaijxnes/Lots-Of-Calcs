Frequently asked questions

What is 35 in Roman numerals?

XXXV. It breaks down as X (10) + X (10) + X (10) + V (5) = 35.

How do you read XXXV?

Add the symbols from left to right: 10 + 10 + 10 + 5 = 35. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 35 be written another way?

XXXV is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
