Frequently asked questions

What is 44 in Roman numerals?

XLIV. It breaks down as XL (40) + IV (4) = 44.

How do you read XLIV?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XL is 50 − 10 = 40; IV is 5 − 1 = 4.

Can 44 be written another way?

XLIV is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
