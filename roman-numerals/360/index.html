Frequently asked questions

What is 360 in Roman numerals?

CCCLX. It breaks down as C (100) + C (100) + C (100) + L (50) + X (10) = 360.

How do you read CCCLX?

Add the symbols from left to right: 100 + 100 + 100 + 50 + 10 = 360. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 360 be written another way?

CCCLX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
