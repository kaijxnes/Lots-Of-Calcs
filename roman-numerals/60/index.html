Frequently asked questions

What is 60 in Roman numerals?

LX. It breaks down as L (50) + X (10) = 60.

How do you read LX?

Add the symbols from left to right: 50 + 10 = 60. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 60 be written another way?

LX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
