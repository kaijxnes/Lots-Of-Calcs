Frequently asked questions

What is 250 in Roman numerals?

CCL. It breaks down as C (100) + C (100) + L (50) = 250.

How do you read CCL?

Add the symbols from left to right: 100 + 100 + 50 = 250. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 250 be written another way?

CCL is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
