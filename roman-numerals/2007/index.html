Frequently asked questions

What is 2007 in Roman numerals?

MMVII. It breaks down as M (1,000) + M (1,000) + V (5) + I (1) + I (1) = 2007.

How do you read MMVII?

Add the symbols from left to right: 1000 + 1000 + 5 + 1 + 1 = 2007. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 2007 be written another way?

MMVII is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 2007 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MMVII. For example, 14 March 2007 is XIV.III.MMVII written day first, or III.XIV.MMVII month first as in the US.
