Frequently asked questions

What is 68 in Roman numerals?

LXVIII. It breaks down as L (50) + X (10) + V (5) + I (1) + I (1) + I (1) = 68.

How do you read LXVIII?

Add the symbols from left to right: 50 + 10 + 5 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 68. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 68 be written another way?

LXVIII is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
