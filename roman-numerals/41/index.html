Frequently asked questions

What is 41 in Roman numerals?

XLI. It breaks down as XL (40) + I (1) = 41.

How do you read XLI?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XL is 50 − 10 = 40.

Can 41 be written another way?

XLI is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
