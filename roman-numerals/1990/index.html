Frequently asked questions

What is 1990 in Roman numerals?

MCMXC. It breaks down as M (1,000) + CM (900) + XC (90) = 1990.

How do you read MCMXC?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900; XC is 100 − 10 = 90.

Can 1990 be written another way?

MCMXC is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1990 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MCMXC. For example, 14 March 1990 is XIV.III.MCMXC written day first, or III.XIV.MCMXC month first as in the US.
