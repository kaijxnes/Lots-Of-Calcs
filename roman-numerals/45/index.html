Frequently asked questions

What is 45 in Roman numerals?

XLV. It breaks down as XL (40) + V (5) = 45.

How do you read XLV?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XL is 50 − 10 = 40.

Can 45 be written another way?

XLV is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
