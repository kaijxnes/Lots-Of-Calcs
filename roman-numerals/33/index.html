Frequently asked questions

What is 33 in Roman numerals?

XXXIII. It breaks down as X (10) + X (10) + X (10) + I (1) + I (1) + I (1) = 33.

How do you read XXXIII?

Add the symbols from left to right: 10 + 10 + 10 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 33. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 33 be written another way?

XXXIII is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
