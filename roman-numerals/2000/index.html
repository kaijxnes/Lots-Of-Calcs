Frequently asked questions

What is 2000 in Roman numerals?

MM. It breaks down as M (1,000) + M (1,000) = 2000.

How do you read MM?

Add the symbols from left to right: 1000 + 1000 = 2000. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 2000 be written another way?

MM is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 2000 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MM. For example, 14 March 2000 is XIV.III.MM written day first, or III.XIV.MM month first as in the US.
