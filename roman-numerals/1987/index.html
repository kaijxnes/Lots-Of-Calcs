Frequently asked questions

What is 1987 in Roman numerals?

MCMLXXXVII. It breaks down as M (1,000) + CM (900) + L (50) + X (10) + X (10) + X (10) + V (5) + I (1) + I (1) = 1987.

How do you read MCMLXXXVII?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900.

Can 1987 be written another way?

MCMLXXXVII is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1987 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MCMLXXXVII. For example, 14 March 1987 is XIV.III.MCMLXXXVII written day first, or III.XIV.MCMLXXXVII month first as in the US.
