Frequently asked questions

What is 1909 in Roman numerals?

MCMIX. It breaks down as M (1,000) + CM (900) + IX (9) = 1909.

How do you read MCMIX?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900; IX is 10 − 1 = 9.

Can 1909 be written another way?

MCMIX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1909 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MCMIX. For example, 14 March 1909 is XIV.III.MCMIX written day first, or III.XIV.MCMIX month first as in the US.
