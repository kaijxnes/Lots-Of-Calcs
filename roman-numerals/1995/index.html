Frequently asked questions

What is 1995 in Roman numerals?

MCMXCV. It breaks down as M (1,000) + CM (900) + XC (90) + V (5) = 1995.

How do you read MCMXCV?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900; XC is 100 − 10 = 90.

Can 1995 be written another way?

MCMXCV is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1995 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MCMXCV. For example, 14 March 1995 is XIV.III.MCMXCV written day first, or III.XIV.MCMXCV month first as in the US.
