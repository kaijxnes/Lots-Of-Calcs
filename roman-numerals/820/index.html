Frequently asked questions

What is 820 in Roman numerals?

DCCCXX. It breaks down as D (500) + C (100) + C (100) + C (100) + X (10) + X (10) = 820.

How do you read DCCCXX?

Add the symbols from left to right: 500 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 10 + 10 = 820. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 820 be written another way?

DCCCXX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
