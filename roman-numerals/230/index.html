Frequently asked questions

What is 230 in Roman numerals?

CCXXX. It breaks down as C (100) + C (100) + X (10) + X (10) + X (10) = 230.

How do you read CCXXX?

Add the symbols from left to right: 100 + 100 + 10 + 10 + 10 = 230. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 230 be written another way?

CCXXX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
