Frequently asked questions

What is 240 in Roman numerals?

CCXL. It breaks down as C (100) + C (100) + XL (40) = 240.

How do you read CCXL?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XL is 50 − 10 = 40.

Can 240 be written another way?

CCXL is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
