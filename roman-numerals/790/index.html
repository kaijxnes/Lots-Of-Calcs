Frequently asked questions

What is 790 in Roman numerals?

DCCXC. It breaks down as D (500) + C (100) + C (100) + XC (90) = 790.

How do you read DCCXC?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XC is 100 − 10 = 90.

Can 790 be written another way?

DCCXC is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
