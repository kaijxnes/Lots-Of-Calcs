Frequently asked questions

What is 2019 in Roman numerals?

MMXIX. It breaks down as M (1,000) + M (1,000) + X (10) + IX (9) = 2019.

How do you read MMXIX?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: IX is 10 − 1 = 9.

Can 2019 be written another way?

MMXIX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 2019 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MMXIX. For example, 14 March 2019 is XIV.III.MMXIX written day first, or III.XIV.MMXIX month first as in the US.
