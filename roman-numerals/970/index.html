Frequently asked questions

What is 970 in Roman numerals?

CMLXX. It breaks down as CM (900) + L (50) + X (10) + X (10) = 970.

How do you read CMLXX?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900.

Can 970 be written another way?

CMLXX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
