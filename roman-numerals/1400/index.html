Frequently asked questions

What is 1400 in Roman numerals?

MCD. It breaks down as M (1,000) + CD (400) = 1400.

How do you read MCD?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CD is 500 − 100 = 400.

Can 1400 be written another way?

MCD is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1400 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MCD. For example, 14 March 1400 is XIV.III.MCD written day first, or III.XIV.MCD month first as in the US.
