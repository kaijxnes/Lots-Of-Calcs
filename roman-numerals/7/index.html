Frequently asked questions

What is 7 in Roman numerals?

VII. It breaks down as V (5) + I (1) + I (1) = 7.

How do you read VII?

Add the symbols from left to right: 5 + 1 + 1 = 7. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 7 be written another way?

VII is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
