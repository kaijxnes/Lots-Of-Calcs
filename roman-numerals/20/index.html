What is 20 in Roman numerals?

How do you read XX?

Add the symbols from left to right: 10 + 10 = 20. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 20 be written another way?

XX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
