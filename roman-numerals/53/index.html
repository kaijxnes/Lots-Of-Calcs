Frequently asked questions

What is 53 in Roman numerals?

LIII. It breaks down as L (50) + I (1) + I (1) + I (1) = 53.

How do you read LIII?

Add the symbols from left to right: 50 + 1 + 1 + 1 = 53. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 53 be written another way?

LIII is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
