Frequently asked questions

What is 550 in Roman numerals?

DL. It breaks down as D (500) + L (50) = 550.

How do you read DL?

Add the symbols from left to right: 500 + 50 = 550. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 550 be written another way?

DL is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
