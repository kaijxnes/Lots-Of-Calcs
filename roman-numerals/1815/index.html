Frequently asked questions

What is 1815 in Roman numerals?

MDCCCXV. It breaks down as M (1,000) + D (500) + C (100) + C (100) + C (100) + X (10) + V (5) = 1815.

How do you read MDCCCXV?

Add the symbols from left to right: 1000 + 500 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 10 + 5 = 1815. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 1815 be written another way?

MDCCCXV is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1815 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MDCCCXV. For example, 14 March 1815 is XIV.III.MDCCCXV written day first, or III.XIV.MDCCCXV month first as in the US.
