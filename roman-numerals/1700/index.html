Frequently asked questions

What is 1700 in Roman numerals?

MDCC. It breaks down as M (1,000) + D (500) + C (100) + C (100) = 1700.

How do you read MDCC?

Add the symbols from left to right: 1000 + 500 + 100 + 100 = 1700. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 1700 be written another way?

MDCC is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.

How do you write a date in 1700 in Roman numerals?

Write each part separately: the day and month as numerals and the year as MDCC. For example, 14 March 1700 is XIV.III.MDCC written day first, or III.XIV.MDCC month first as in the US.
