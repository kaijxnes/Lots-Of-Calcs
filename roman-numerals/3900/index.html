Frequently asked questions

What is 3,900 in Roman numerals?

MMMCM. It breaks down as M (1,000) + M (1,000) + M (1,000) + CM (900) = 3,900.

How do you read MMMCM?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900.

Can 3,900 be written another way?

MMMCM is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
