Frequently asked questions

What is 690 in Roman numerals?

DCXC. It breaks down as D (500) + C (100) + XC (90) = 690.

How do you read DCXC?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: XC is 100 − 10 = 90.

Can 690 be written another way?

DCXC is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
