Frequently asked questions

What is 440 in Roman numerals?

CDXL. It breaks down as CD (400) + XL (40) = 440.

How do you read CDXL?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CD is 500 − 100 = 400; XL is 50 − 10 = 40.

Can 440 be written another way?

CDXL is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
