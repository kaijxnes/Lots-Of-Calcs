Frequently asked questions

What is 850 in Roman numerals?

DCCCL. It breaks down as D (500) + C (100) + C (100) + C (100) + L (50) = 850.

How do you read DCCCL?

Add the symbols from left to right: 500 + 100 + 100 + 100 + 50 = 850. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 850 be written another way?

DCCCL is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
