Frequently asked questions

What is 560 in Roman numerals?

DLX. It breaks down as D (500) + L (50) + X (10) = 560.

How do you read DLX?

Add the symbols from left to right: 500 + 50 + 10 = 560. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 560 be written another way?

DLX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
