Frequently asked questions

What is 24 in Roman numerals?

XXIV. It breaks down as X (10) + X (10) + IV (4) = 24.

How do you read XXIV?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: IV is 5 − 1 = 4.

Can 24 be written another way?

XXIV is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
