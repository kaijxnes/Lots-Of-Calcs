Frequently asked questions

What is 3,999 in Roman numerals?

MMMCMXCIX. It breaks down as M (1,000) + M (1,000) + M (1,000) + CM (900) + XC (90) + IX (9) = 3,999.

How do you read MMMCMXCIX?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900; XC is 100 − 10 = 90; IX is 10 − 1 = 9.

Can 3,999 be written another way?

MMMCMXCIX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
