Frequently asked questions

What is 940 in Roman numerals?

CMXL. It breaks down as CM (900) + XL (40) = 940.

How do you read CMXL?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CM is 1,000 − 100 = 900; XL is 50 − 10 = 40.

Can 940 be written another way?

CMXL is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
