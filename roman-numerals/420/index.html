Frequently asked questions

What is 420 in Roman numerals?

CDXX. It breaks down as CD (400) + X (10) + X (10) = 420.

How do you read CDXX?

Read left to right, adding each symbol — except where a smaller symbol sits before a larger one, which means subtract it: CD is 500 − 100 = 400.

Can 420 be written another way?

CDXX is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
