Frequently asked questions

What is 2,700 in Roman numerals?

MMDCC. It breaks down as M (1,000) + M (1,000) + D (500) + C (100) + C (100) = 2,700.

How do you read MMDCC?

Add the symbols from left to right: 1000 + 1000 + 500 + 100 + 100 = 2,700. There is no subtraction in this one.

Can 2,700 be written another way?

MMDCC is the standard form. Clocks often show 4 as IIII rather than IV, and older inscriptions vary, but modern usage — film credits, document numbering, monarchs and years — uses the subtractive form shown here.
