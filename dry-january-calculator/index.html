Frequently asked questions

How much will I save doing Dry January?

Add up what you'd normally spend in a week and multiply by 4.4 (January has 31 days). Four pints at £5.50 and three glasses of wine at £7 is £43.00 a week, or about £190 over January.

How many units are in a drink?

Strength (ABV) × volume in ml ÷ 1,000. A pint of 5.2% lager is about 3 units, a 175ml glass of 12% wine 2.1, and a 25ml single of 40% spirit 1 (NHS).

How many units a week is safe?

The NHS advises men and women not to drink more than 14 units a week on a regular basis, spread over three or more days, with several drink-free days each week.

How many calories are in alcohol?

Alcohol has about 7 calories per gram, and a unit is 8g, so each unit is about 56 calories before any sugar in the drink or mixer — a pint of 5.2% lager has at least 165 from the alcohol alone.
