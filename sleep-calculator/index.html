Frequently asked questions

How much sleep do adults need?

The CDC recommends 7 or more hours a night for adults aged 18 to 60, 7 to 9 hours at 61 to 64, and 7 to 8 hours from 65.

How much sleep do children need?

The CDC recommends 9 to 12 hours for children aged 6 to 12 and 8 to 10 hours for teenagers; younger children need more, including naps. The full table is above.

What time should I go to bed to wake up at 7am?

For 7 hours' sleep, about 11:45pm if you take 15 minutes to fall asleep; for 8 hours, 10:45pm; for 9 hours, 9:45pm.

What about sleep cycles?

You'll often read that sleep runs in 90-minute cycles and that you should time your alarm to the end of one. Neither the NHS nor the CDC gives that advice; both focus on getting enough hours and going to bed and getting up at the same time every day.
