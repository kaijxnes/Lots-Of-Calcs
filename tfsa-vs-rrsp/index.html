How it works

The comparison assumes the RRSP refund is invested too — $500 from take-home at a 30% rate funds about $714 of RRSP contributions. If the refund gets spent, the TFSA usually comes out ahead. RRSP withdrawals can also reduce income-tested benefits such as OAS and GIS, which this leaves out.

Limits for 2026

TFSA: $7,000 of new room in 2026. Unused room carries forward from 2009 (or the year you turned 18), and withdrawals are added back the next year.

RRSP: 18% of the previous year's earned income, up to $33,810 for 2026, less any pension adjustment; unused room carries forward.

RRSP withdrawals are taxed as income; by the end of the year you turn 71 the RRSP has to become a RRIF or an annuity.

Frequently asked questions

Is a TFSA or a RRSP better?

It comes down to your tax rate now against your tax rate when you take the money out. For the same cost to you, the RRSP wins if you'll pay less tax later than you save now; the TFSA wins if you'll pay more. At 30% now the two break even at about 30.0% later. In the example, $500 a month for 25 years leaves $292,867 in the TFSA and $334,705 from the RRSP after tax.

Why does the same monthly amount buy a bigger contribution?

Because RRSP contributions are made before tax. Paying $500 from take-home at a 30% rate funds $714 a month of contributions, so the RRSP grows to $418,382 — but $83,676 of that is tax when it comes out.

What are the limits?

TFSA: $7,000 of new room in 2026. Unused room carries forward from 2009 (or the year you turned 18), and withdrawals are added back the next year. RRSP: 18% of the previous year's earned income, up to $33,810 for 2026, less any pension adjustment; unused room carries forward. RRSP withdrawals are taxed as income; by the end of the year you turn 71 the RRSP has to become a RRIF or an annuity.

Should I just use both?

Many people do: the RRSP for money taxed at a high rate now, the TFSA for flexibility, since TFSA withdrawals are tax-free and the room comes back. If you aren't sure what your tax rate will be later, splitting between them hedges the guess.
