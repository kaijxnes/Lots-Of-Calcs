Frequently asked questions

How much is £100 from 1984 worth today?

About £473.34 in September 2026 money, using the long-run Retail Prices Index. Something that cost £100 in 1984 would cost £473.34 now if its price had risen in line with inflation.

How much have prices gone up since 1984?

By 373.3% in all, an average of 3.8% a year.

What would £100 today have been worth in 1984?

£21.13. Today's money buys that much less than the same amount did in 1984.

Which inflation measure is used?

The ONS long-run Retail Prices Index series, which goes back to 1800, as CPI figures only start in 1988. RPI tends to run a little higher than CPI.
