Frequently asked questions

How much is £100 from 1909 worth today?

About £17,528 in September 2026 money, using the long-run Retail Prices Index. Something that cost £100 in 1909 would cost £17,528 now if its price had risen in line with inflation.

How much have prices gone up since 1909?

By 17428.4% in all, an average of 4.5% a year.

What would £100 today have been worth in 1909?

£0.57. Today's money buys that much less than the same amount did in 1909.

Which inflation measure is used?

The ONS long-run Retail Prices Index series, which goes back to 1800, as CPI figures only start in 1988. RPI tends to run a little higher than CPI.
