How Pension Credit works

If you've reached State Pension age and live in England, Scotland or Wales, Pension Credit's Guarantee Credit tops your weekly income up to £238.00 if you're single or £363.25 for a couple. The amount you're topped up to rises by £86.05 a week if you have a severe disability, £48.15 for each of you who gets Carer's Allowance, and £69.98 a week for each child you're responsible for (£81.07 for a first child born before 6 April 2017), plus £37.93 or £118.46 for a disabled child. There can be extra for housing costs such as ground rent and service charges.

If you reached State Pension age before 6 April 2016 and saved for retirement, you might also get Savings Credit: up to £17.96 a week if you're single or £20.10 for a couple, even without Guarantee Credit. This calculator doesn't work that part out.

Why it's worth claiming even a small amount: getting Pension Credit means you automatically get cold weather payments, and you can apply for a free TV licence if you're 75 or over and get help with NHS costs (with Guarantee Credit), as well as Council Tax Reduction, Housing Benefit and help through the Warm Home Discount. Claims can only be backdated 3 months. For couples, the severe disability rules are more involved: the Pension Service can confirm what you'd get.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Pension Credit?

It tops your weekly income up to £238.00 if you're single or £363.25 for a couple (2026/27), more with a severe disability, as a carer or with children. On £180 a week, a single person would get £58.00 a week.

Can I get Pension Credit with the full State Pension?

Not on its own: the full new State Pension is £241.30 a week, above the £238.00 single guarantee. With a severe disability addition (£86.05) or as a carer, you could.

How do savings affect Pension Credit?

Savings of £10,000 or less don't affect it. Above that, each £500 (or part of £500) counts as £1 a week of income: £11,000 counts as £2 a week.

What counts as income for Pension Credit?

Your State Pension, other pensions, earnings, and most benefits such as Carer's Allowance. Attendance Allowance, PIP, DLA, Child Benefit, Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction and the Winter Fuel Payment don't count.

What else do you get with Pension Credit?

Cold weather payments automatically, and you can apply for a free TV licence if you're 75 or over and get help with NHS costs. It can also help you get Housing Benefit, Council Tax Reduction and the Warm Home Discount.
