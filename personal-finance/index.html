Common questions

Do the take-home pay calculators use this year's tax rates?

Yes. Each country's rates are checked every month against the tax authority's published figures, and every salary page shows the date they were last checked. The tax rates page lists them all.

Which countries are covered?

The UK (with Scotland's own income tax bands), every US state, Canada's provinces and territories, most of Western Europe, Australia, New Zealand, India and Japan.

Do these include pension and student loan deductions?

The salary calculator does: add a pension contribution or student loan plan and the take-home figure updates. The budget and pay rise calculators start from the same figures.
