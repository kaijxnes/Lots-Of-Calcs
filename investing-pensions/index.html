Common questions

Is CAGR the same as average annual return?

No. A simple average ignores compounding and overstates growth after volatile years; CAGR is the single smoothed rate that takes the start value to the end value.

How much do fund fees really cost?

More than the percentage suggests, because the fee comes off every year's growth too. Over 30 years a 1% fee can take a fifth or more of the final pot; the fund fee calculator shows it for your numbers.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. The calculators use the growth rate you enter as if it were steady; real returns vary, can be negative, and past performance doesn't predict them.
