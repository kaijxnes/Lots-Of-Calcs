Frequently asked questions

How old would I be on Mars?

A Mars year is 687 Earth days, about 1.88 Earth years, so divide your age by 1.88. Someone born on 1 January 1990 is 19 in Mars years.

Where would I be oldest?

Mercury, which goes round the Sun every 88 days: the same person is 153 in Mercury years. On Neptune and Pluto, whose years last 164 and 248 Earth years, nobody has yet reached their first birthday.

What is a planet's year?

The time it takes to go once round the Sun. Planets further out travel further, and more slowly, so their years are longer.

Is a day the same length too?

No — a day (sunrise to sunrise) is 24.7 hours on Mars, 9.9 on Jupiter, and 2,802 hours on Venus, where a day is longer than its year.
