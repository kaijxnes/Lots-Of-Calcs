How the 50/30/20 rule works

The 50/30/20 rule splits what you take home, after tax, into three:

50% for needs — what you'd have to pay whatever happened: rent or mortgage, council tax, energy and water, food shopping, insurance, getting to work, minimum debt repayments and childcare.

30% for wants — everything you choose: eating out, takeaways, holidays, subscriptions, clothes beyond the basics, hobbies and nights out.

20% for savings — building an emergency fund first, then saving for goals, paying off debt faster than the minimum, and investing or pension top-ups.

It's a starting point, not a rule. Where rent is high, needs often take 60–70%, so 70/20/10 or 60/20/20 is more realistic; on a higher income, 40/30/30 saves more. The buttons above switch between them.
