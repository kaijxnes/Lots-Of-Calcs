Frequently asked questions

How many hours is 6am to 6pm?

12 hours. With a 30-minute unpaid break that's 11 hours 30 minutes worked (11.5 hours); with an hour's break, 11 hours.

How many hours a week is 6am to 6pm, five days a week?

60 hours with no breaks, or 57 hours 30 minutes (57.5 hours) with a 30-minute unpaid break each day.

What is 6am to 6pm in 24-hour time?

06:00 to 18:00.
