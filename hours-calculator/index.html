Frequently asked questions

How do I work out hours worked?

Take the start time from the end time, then take off any unpaid break. 8:30am to 5pm is 8 hours 30 minutes; with a 30-minute lunch, that's 8 hours worked. If the shift crosses midnight, add 24 hours to the end time first.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60: 15 minutes is 0.25 hours, 30 minutes 0.5, 45 minutes 0.75 and 20 minutes 0.33. So 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours — the figure payroll and timesheets usually want.

How do I work out pay for the hours?

Multiply the decimal hours by the hourly rate: 7.5 hours at £12 is £90. Enter a rate in the calculator and it does it for the shift and the week.

How are night shifts counted?

When the end time is earlier than the start, the calculator treats it as the next day: 10pm to 6am is 8 hours.
