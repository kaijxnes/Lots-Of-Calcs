About this calculator

The equal split divides the total bill (plus tip) evenly across everyone.

The itemized split instead works out each person's share of the tip in proportion to what they actually spent — so someone who ordered a £30 meal pays more of the tip than someone who ordered a £10 side, even though the tip percentage is the same for the whole table.

Frequently asked questions

How should we split a bill when people ordered very different amounts?

Use the itemised split: enter what each person actually ordered and the tip is shared in proportion to their spend, so nobody subsidises a much larger order.

Should the tip be split evenly or by what each person spent?

Splitting the tip in proportion to spend is the fairer default, since the tip is a percentage of the bill. Splitting it evenly is simpler and fine when everyone ordered roughly the same.
