Frequently asked questions

Is take-home pay higher in Spain or the UK?

On £50,000 (€59,712), UK leaves more after tax: £39,520 in the UK against €42,011 in Spain (£35,178). The gap changes with salary — see the table.

Does this compare living costs?

No — only tax and social contributions on the same gross salary. Rent, prices and typical salaries differ a lot between countries, and salaries for the same job are rarely the same, so use this alongside what a job actually pays in each place.

What's included?

Income tax and employee social contributions (National Insurance, social security, health insurance and so on) for a single employee with each place's default options and no pension contributions, using the current tax year's rates.
