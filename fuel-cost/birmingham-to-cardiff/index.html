Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to drive from Birmingham to Cardiff?

About £18.16 in petrol each way for a car doing 45 mpg, at the UK average of 167.07p a litre (week of 16 November 2026). A return trip is £36.33. In a diesel doing 50 mpg it's £19.25 each way.

How far is Birmingham to Cardiff by car?

About 108 miles (173 km) on the fastest route, roughly 2 h 16 min without traffic or stops.

How much would Birmingham to Cardiff cost in an electric car?

About £8.09 charged at home at the price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh, for a car doing 3.5 miles per kWh. Public rapid chargers cost several times as much per kWh.

How is the fuel cost worked out?

Miles ÷ mpg gives gallons; × 4.54609 gives litres; × the price per litre gives the cost. 108 ÷ 45 × 4.54609 × 167.07p = £18.16.
