Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to drive from Leeds to York?

About £4.14 in petrol each way for a car doing 45 mpg, at the UK average of 167.07p a litre (week of 16 November 2026). A return trip is £8.29. In a diesel doing 50 mpg it's £4.39 each way.

How far is Leeds to York by car?

About 25 miles (40 km) on the fastest route, roughly 41 min without traffic or stops.

How much would Leeds to York cost in an electric car?

About £1.85 charged at home at the price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh, for a car doing 3.5 miles per kWh. Public rapid chargers cost several times as much per kWh.

How is the fuel cost worked out?

Miles ÷ mpg gives gallons; × 4.54609 gives litres; × the price per litre gives the cost. 25 ÷ 45 × 4.54609 × 167.07p = £4.14.
