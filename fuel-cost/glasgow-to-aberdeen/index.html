Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to drive from Glasgow to Aberdeen?

About £24.57 in petrol each way for a car doing 45 mpg, at the UK average of 167.07p a litre (week of 16 November 2026). A return trip is £49.14. In a diesel doing 50 mpg it's £26.05 each way.

How far is Glasgow to Aberdeen by car?

About 146 miles (234 km) on the fastest route, roughly 2 h 48 min without traffic or stops.

How much would Glasgow to Aberdeen cost in an electric car?

About £10.95 charged at home at the price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh, for a car doing 3.5 miles per kWh. Public rapid chargers cost several times as much per kWh.

How is the fuel cost worked out?

Miles ÷ mpg gives gallons; × 4.54609 gives litres; × the price per litre gives the cost. 146 ÷ 45 × 4.54609 × 167.07p = £24.57.
