Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to drive from Birmingham to Newquay?

About £41.12 in petrol each way for a car doing 45 mpg, at the UK average of 167.07p a litre (week of 16 November 2026). A return trip is £82.24. In a diesel doing 50 mpg it's £43.59 each way.

How far is Birmingham to Newquay by car?

About 244 miles (392 km) on the fastest route, roughly 4 h 35 min without traffic or stops.

How much would Birmingham to Newquay cost in an electric car?

About £18.32 charged at home at the price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh, for a car doing 3.5 miles per kWh. Public rapid chargers cost several times as much per kWh.

How is the fuel cost worked out?

Miles ÷ mpg gives gallons; × 4.54609 gives litres; × the price per litre gives the cost. 244 ÷ 45 × 4.54609 × 167.07p = £41.12.
