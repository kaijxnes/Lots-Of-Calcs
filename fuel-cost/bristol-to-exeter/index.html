Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to drive from Bristol to Exeter?

About £13.35 in petrol each way for a car doing 45 mpg, at the UK average of 167.07p a litre (week of 16 November 2026). A return trip is £26.70. In a diesel doing 50 mpg it's £14.15 each way.

How far is Bristol to Exeter by car?

About 79 miles (127 km) on the fastest route, roughly 1 h 41 min without traffic or stops.

How much would Bristol to Exeter cost in an electric car?

About £5.95 charged at home at the price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh, for a car doing 3.5 miles per kWh. Public rapid chargers cost several times as much per kWh.

How is the fuel cost worked out?

Miles ÷ mpg gives gallons; × 4.54609 gives litres; × the price per litre gives the cost. 79 ÷ 45 × 4.54609 × 167.07p = £13.35.
