Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to drive from Birmingham to Liverpool?

About £16.59 in petrol each way for a car doing 45 mpg, at the UK average of 167.07p a litre (week of 16 November 2026). A return trip is £33.18. In a diesel doing 50 mpg it's £17.59 each way.

How far is Birmingham to Liverpool by car?

About 98 miles (158 km) on the fastest route, roughly 1 h 55 min without traffic or stops.

How much would Birmingham to Liverpool cost in an electric car?

About £7.39 charged at home at the price cap rate of 26.32p per kWh, for a car doing 3.5 miles per kWh. Public rapid chargers cost several times as much per kWh.

How is the fuel cost worked out?

Miles ÷ mpg gives gallons; × 4.54609 gives litres; × the price per litre gives the cost. 98 ÷ 45 × 4.54609 × 167.07p = £16.59.
