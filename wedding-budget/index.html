Frequently asked questions

How should I split my wedding budget?

There's no right answer, but the venue and food are usually the biggest share, because they scale with the number of guests. Start from a split like the one here, change the percentages to match what matters to you, and keep something back for the unexpected.

How do I cut the cost of a wedding?

The guest list drives most of the cost, so it's the biggest lever: every guest adds food, drink, a seat and often a favour. A weekday or off-season date, a shorter drinks reception and fewer printed extras also make a real difference.

What is the cost per guest?

The total divided by guests: £20,000 for 80 guests is £250 a head. Venue and catering on their own come to £113 a head on this split.
