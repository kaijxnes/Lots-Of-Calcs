Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an hourly wage to a yearly salary?

Multiply the hourly rate by the hours you work a week, then by 52. At 40 hours a week that's the hourly rate × 2,080; at 37.5 hours it's × 1,950.

How do I convert a salary to an hourly rate?

Divide the yearly salary by 52, then by your weekly hours. At 40 hours a week, divide by 2,080: $50,000 is $24.04 an hour.

How many working hours are in a year?

2,080 at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, or 1,950 at 37.5 hours. Paid holiday is included, because you're paid for it; if it's unpaid, use fewer weeks.

Is this before or after tax?

Before tax. For take-home pay, use the salary calculator or the after-tax pages linked from each rate.
