Frequently asked questions

$10 an hour is how much a year?

$20,800 before tax: $10 × 40 hours × 52 weeks.

$10 an hour is how much a month?

$1,733.33 before tax — a year's pay of $20,800 divided by 12. Months aren't all the same length, so a monthly figure is an average.

$10 an hour is how much a week?

$400.00 for 40 hours. Every two weeks that's $800.00.

What if my holiday is unpaid?

With two weeks off unpaid you're paid for 50 weeks: $20,000 a year.

What's $10 an hour part-time?

What is time and a half on $10 an hour?
