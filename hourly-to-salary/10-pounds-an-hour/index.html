Frequently asked questions

£10 an hour is how much a year?

£19,500 before tax: £10 × 37.5 hours × 52 weeks.

£10 an hour is how much a month?

£1,625.00 before tax — a year's pay of £19,500 divided by 12. Months aren't all the same length, so a monthly figure is an average.

£10 an hour is how much a week?

£375.00 for 37.5 hours.

What if my holiday is unpaid?

With two weeks off unpaid you're paid for 50 weeks: £18,750 a year.

What's £10 an hour part-time?

What is time and a half on £10 an hour?
