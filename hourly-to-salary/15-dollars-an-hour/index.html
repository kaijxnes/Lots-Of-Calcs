Frequently asked questions

$15 an hour is how much a year?

$31,200 before tax: $15 × 40 hours × 52 weeks.

$15 an hour is how much a month?

$2,600.00 before tax — a year's pay of $31,200 divided by 12. Months aren't all the same length, so a monthly figure is an average.

$15 an hour is how much a week?

$600.00 for 40 hours. Every two weeks that's $1,200.00.

What if my holiday is unpaid?

With two weeks off unpaid you're paid for 50 weeks: $30,000 a year.

What's $15 an hour part-time?

At 20 hours a week, $15,600 a year. The table above has other weekly hours.

What is time and a half on $15 an hour?

$22.50 an hour ($15 × 1.5); double time is $30.00.
