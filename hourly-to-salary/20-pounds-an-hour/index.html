£20 an hour is how much a month?

£3,250.00 before tax — a year's pay of £39,000 divided by 12. Months aren't all the same length, so a monthly figure is an average.

What if my holiday is unpaid?

With two weeks off unpaid you're paid for 50 weeks: £37,500 a year.
