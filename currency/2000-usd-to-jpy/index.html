Frequently asked questions

How much is 2,000 US dollars in yen?

Will I get this rate?

Not quite. This is the mid-market reference rate; banks, bureaux and card providers add a margin or fee, which can be anything from under 1% for some cards to 5% or more at airport kiosks. Compare what you'd actually receive.

How often is the rate updated?

The ECB publishes reference rates every working day; this page uses the rate for 20 November 2026 and is refreshed monthly, as is the currency converter.
