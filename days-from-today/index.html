Frequently asked questions

How do I work out the date 90 days from today?

Count forward from today, with today as day 0. The calculator does it from your device's date, and handles month lengths and leap years.

Can I count months or years instead?

Yes — pick months or years. Adding a month keeps the same day of the month where it exists; from 31 January, one month later is the last day of February.

What about working days?

This counts every calendar day. The working days calculator counts weekdays less UK bank holidays.
