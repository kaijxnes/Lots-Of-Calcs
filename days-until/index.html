Frequently asked questions

How many days until Christmas?

Christmas Day is Friday 25 December 2026. The Christmas countdown shows the days, hours and sleeps left, worked out live from your device's clock.

When is Easter?

Easter Sunday is next on Sunday 28 March 2027. It moves each year: it's the first Sunday after the first full moon on or after 21 March, so it falls between 22 March and 25 April.

How are the countdowns worked out?

Live in your browser from your device's date and time, counting to midnight at the start of the day, so they're right in any time zone. The dates themselves follow each day's rule, such as the fourth Thursday in November for US Thanksgiving.

What about working days?

The working days calculator counts weekdays less UK bank holidays between today and any date — for example, the working days left before Christmas.
