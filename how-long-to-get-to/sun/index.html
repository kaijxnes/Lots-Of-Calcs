These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get to the Sun?

86 days for NASA's Parker Solar Probe, which launched 12 August 2018 and made its first close pass of the Sun on 6 November 2018, after a flyby of Venus. Flying in a straight line at Apollo 10's top speed (24,791 mph) would take 156 days on average.

How far away is the Sun?

At its closest, 147.1 million km (91.4 million miles); at its farthest, 152.1 million km (94.5 million miles); on average 149.6 million km (93 million miles). The orbit isn't a perfect circle, so the distance changes through the year.

How long would it take to drive to the Sun?

151 years at 70 mph without stopping on average, or 154 years at its farthest.

How long does light take to get to the Sun?

8 minutes 19 seconds on average and 8 minutes 27 seconds at its farthest. That is also how long a radio message takes.
