Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to walk to Saturn?

27,504 years at its closest, covering 1.2 billion km (749.1 million miles) non-stop at 5 km/h (3.1 mph). At its farthest, 1.7 billion km (1 billion miles), it would take 37,842 years.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 1.2 billion km ÷ 5 km/h = 241,100,000 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Saturn?

About 6 years 8 months for NASA's Cassini, which launched 15 October 1997 and went into orbit on 1 July 2004, using flybys of Venus, Earth and Jupiter.

How long does light take to get to Saturn?

1 hour 7 minutes at its closest.
