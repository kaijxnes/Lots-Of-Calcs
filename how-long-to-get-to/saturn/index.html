How long it really took: about 6 years 8 months. NASA's Cassini launched 15 October 1997 and went into orbit on 1 July 2004, using flybys of Venus, Earth and Jupiter.

These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get to Saturn?

About 6 years 8 months for NASA's Cassini, which launched 15 October 1997 and went into orbit on 1 July 2004, using flybys of Venus, Earth and Jupiter. Flying in a straight line at Apollo 10's top speed (24,791 mph) would take 3.4 years at its closest.

How far away is Saturn?

At its closest, 1.2 billion km (749.1 million miles); at its farthest, 1.7 billion km (1 billion miles). The distance changes as Earth and Saturn go round the Sun at different speeds.

How long would it take to drive to Saturn?

1,221 years at 70 mph without stopping at its closest, or 1,680 years at its farthest.

How long does light take to get to Saturn?

1 hour 7 minutes at its closest and 1 hour 32 minutes at its farthest. That is also how long a radio message takes.
