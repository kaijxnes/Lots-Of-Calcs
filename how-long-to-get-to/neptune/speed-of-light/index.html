Frequently asked questions

How long does light take to get to Neptune?

4 hours at its closest, covering 4.3 billion km (2.7 billion miles) non-stop at 299,792 km/s. At its farthest, 4.7 billion km (2.9 billion miles), it would take 4 hours 22 minutes.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 4.3 billion km ÷ 1,079,252,848.8 km/h = 4 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Neptune?

About 12 years for NASA's Voyager 2, which launched 20 August 1977 and flew past Neptune on 25 August 1989 (the only visit so far).
