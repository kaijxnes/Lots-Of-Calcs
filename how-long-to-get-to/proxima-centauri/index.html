Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get to Proxima Centauri?

At the speed of the fastest spacecraft yet, Parker Solar Probe (430,000 mph), it would take 6,623 years. Even light takes 4.2 years.

How far away is Proxima Centauri?

4.2465 light-years, or about 40.2 trillion km (25 trillion miles). It is the closest star to the Sun.

How long would it take to drive to Proxima Centauri?

40.7 million years at 70 mph without stopping.

How long does light take to get to Proxima Centauri?

4.2 years. That is also how long a radio message takes — and how old the starlight you see is.
