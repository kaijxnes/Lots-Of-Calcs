Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to get to Proxima Centauri at Apollo speed?

114,871 years, covering 40.2 trillion km (25 trillion miles) non-stop at 39,897 km/h (24,791 mph).

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 40.2 trillion km ÷ 39,897.25 km/h = 1,006,961,504 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long does light take to get to Proxima Centauri?

4.2 years.
