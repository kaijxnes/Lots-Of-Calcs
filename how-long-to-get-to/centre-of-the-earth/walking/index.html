Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to walk to the centre of the Earth?

53 days, covering 6,371 km (3,959 miles) non-stop at 5 km/h (3.1 mph).

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 6,371 km ÷ 5 km/h = 1,274 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long does light take to get to the centre of the Earth?

21 milliseconds.
