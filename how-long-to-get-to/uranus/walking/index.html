Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to walk to Uranus?

58,877 years at its closest, covering 2.6 billion km (1.6 billion miles) non-stop at 5 km/h (3.1 mph). At its farthest, 3.2 billion km (2 billion miles), it would take 71,948 years.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 2.6 billion km ÷ 5 km/h = 516,120,000 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Uranus?

About 8 years 5 months for NASA's Voyager 2, which launched 20 August 1977 and flew past Uranus on 24 January 1986 (the only visit so far).

How long does light take to get to Uranus?

2 hours 23 minutes at its closest.
