Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to drive to Venus?

38.7 years at its closest, covering 38.2 million km (23.7 million miles) non-stop at 70 mph (113 km/h). At its farthest, 261 million km (162.2 million miles), it would take 264 years.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 38.2 million km ÷ 112.65 km/h = 339,091 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Venus?

About 15 months for NASA's Magellan, which launched 4 May 1989 and went into orbit on 10 August 1990.

How long does light take to get to Venus?

2 minutes 7 seconds at its closest.
