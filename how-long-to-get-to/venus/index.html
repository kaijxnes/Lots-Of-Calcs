How long it really took: about 15 months. NASA's Magellan launched 4 May 1989 and went into orbit on 10 August 1990.

These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get to Venus?

About 15 months for NASA's Magellan, which launched 4 May 1989 and went into orbit on 10 August 1990. Flying in a straight line at Apollo 10's top speed (24,791 mph) would take 40 days at its closest.

How far away is Venus?

At its closest, 38.2 million km (23.7 million miles); at its farthest, 261 million km (162.2 million miles). The distance changes as Earth and Venus go round the Sun at different speeds.

How long would it take to drive to Venus?

38.7 years at 70 mph without stopping at its closest, or 264 years at its farthest.

How long does light take to get to Venus?

2 minutes 7 seconds at its closest and 14 minutes 31 seconds at its farthest. That is also how long a radio message takes.
