Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to get to Mercury at the speed of sound?

7.1 years at its closest, covering 77.3 million km (48 million miles) non-stop at 1,235 km/h (767 mph). At its farthest, 221.9 million km (137.9 million miles), it would take 20.5 years.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 77.3 million km ÷ 1,234.8 km/h = 62,601 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Mercury?

About 6 years 7 months for NASA's MESSENGER, which launched 3 August 2004 and went into orbit on 18 March 2011, after flybys of Earth, Venus and Mercury to slow it down.

How long does light take to get to Mercury?

4 minutes 18 seconds at its closest.
