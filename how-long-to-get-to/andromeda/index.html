Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get to the Andromeda Galaxy?

At the speed of the fastest spacecraft yet, Parker Solar Probe (430,000 mph), it would take 3.9 billion years. Even light takes 2.5 million years.

How far away is the Andromeda Galaxy?

About 2.5 million light-years (NASA), or about 23.7 quintillion km (14.7 quintillion miles). It is the nearest large galaxy to our own.

How long would it take to drive to the Andromeda Galaxy?

24 trillion years at 70 mph without stopping.

How long does light take to get to the Andromeda Galaxy?

2.5 million years. That is also how long a radio message takes.
