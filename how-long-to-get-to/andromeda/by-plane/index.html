Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to fly to the Andromeda Galaxy in a plane?

3 trillion years, covering 23.7 quintillion km (14.7 quintillion miles) non-stop at 900 km/h (559 mph).

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 23.7 quintillion km ÷ 900 km/h = 26,279,806,868,280,000 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long does light take to get to the Andromeda Galaxy?

2.5 million years.
