Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to get to Mars?

NASA's Perseverance rover took 203 days. In a straight line at its closest, 54.6 million km (33.9 million miles), it would take 55.3 years by car, 57 days at Apollo 10's top speed, and 3 minutes 2 seconds for light.

Why don't spacecraft go in a straight line?

These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

What is the fastest spacecraft?

NASA's Parker Solar Probe, which reached about 430,000 mph (692,000 km/h) on its closest pass of the Sun in December 2024 — faster than any other object people have made.

How long does light from the Sun take to reach us?

About 8 minutes 19 seconds, so you see the Sun as it was that long ago. Moonlight takes 1.3 seconds.
