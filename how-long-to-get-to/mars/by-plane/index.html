Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to fly to Mars in a plane?

6.9 years at its closest, covering 54.6 million km (33.9 million miles) non-stop at 900 km/h (559 mph). At its farthest, 401.4 million km (249.4 million miles), it would take 50.9 years.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 54.6 million km ÷ 900 km/h = 60,667 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Mars?

203 days for NASA's Perseverance, which launched 30 July 2020 and landed on 18 February 2021, travelling about 471 million km on its curved path.

How long does light take to get to Mars?
