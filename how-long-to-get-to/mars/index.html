How long it really took: 203 days. NASA's Perseverance launched 30 July 2020 and landed on 18 February 2021, travelling about 471 million km on its curved path.

These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to get to Mars?

203 days for NASA's Perseverance, which launched 30 July 2020 and landed on 18 February 2021, travelling about 471 million km on its curved path. Flying in a straight line at Apollo 10's top speed (24,791 mph) would take 57 days at its closest.

How far away is Mars?

At its closest, 54.6 million km (33.9 million miles); at its farthest, 401.4 million km (249.4 million miles). The distance changes as Earth and Mars go round the Sun at different speeds.

How long would it take to drive to Mars?

55.3 years at 70 mph without stopping at its closest, or 406 years at its farthest.

How long does light take to get to Mars?

3 minutes 2 seconds at its closest and 22 minutes 19 seconds at its farthest. That is also how long a radio message takes.
