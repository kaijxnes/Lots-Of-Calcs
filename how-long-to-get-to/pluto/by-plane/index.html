Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to fly to Pluto in a plane?

543 years at its closest, covering 4.3 billion km (2.7 billion miles) non-stop at 900 km/h (559 mph). At its farthest, 7.5 billion km (4.7 billion miles), it would take 954 years.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 4.3 billion km ÷ 900 km/h = 4,760,778 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Pluto?

About 9 years 6 months for NASA's New Horizons, which launched 19 January 2006 and flew past Pluto on 14 July 2015, with a speed boost from Jupiter on the way.

How long does light take to get to Pluto?

3 hours 58 minutes at its closest.
