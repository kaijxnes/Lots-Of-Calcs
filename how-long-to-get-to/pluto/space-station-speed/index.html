Frequently asked questions

How long would it take to get to Pluto at space station speed?

17.4 years at its closest, covering 4.3 billion km (2.7 billion miles) non-stop at 28,164 km/h (17,500 mph). At its farthest, 7.5 billion km (4.7 billion miles), it would take 30.5 years.

How is that worked out?

Distance divided by speed: 4.3 billion km ÷ 28,163.52 km/h = 152,137 hours. These are straight-line times at a steady speed, as if the planets stood still. Real spacecraft can't fly straight there: they launch into a curved path round the Sun that meets the planet where it will be, so the route is longer, and they coast most of the way.

How long did it actually take to get to Pluto?

About 9 years 6 months for NASA's New Horizons, which launched 19 January 2006 and flew past Pluto on 14 July 2015, with a speed boost from Jupiter on the way.
