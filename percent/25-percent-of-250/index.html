What is 25% of 250?

25% of 250 is 62.5. 250 plus 25% is 312.5; 250 minus 25% is 187.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 250 × 25 ÷ 100 = 62.5.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 250 × 0.25 = 62.5.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 250 by 4: 62.5.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (312.5) or by 0.75 to take it off (187.5).
