What is 12% of 20?

12% of 20 is 2.4. 20 plus 12% is 22.4; 20 minus 12% is 17.6.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 20 × 12 ÷ 100 = 2.4.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 20 × 0.12 = 2.4.

As a fraction: 12% is 3/25 (12/100 simplified), so it's 20 × 3 ÷ 25 = 2.4.

In your head: 1% of 20 is 0.2, so 12% is 12 × 0.2 = 2.4.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (22.4) or by 0.88 to take it off (17.6).

Working backwards: if 2.4 is 12% of a number, the number is 2.4 ÷ 12 × 100 = 20. And if 22.4 is a price with 12% added, the price before was 22.4 ÷ 1.12 = 20.
