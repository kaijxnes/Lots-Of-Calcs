What is 75% of 500?

75% of 500 is 375. 500 plus 75% is 875; 500 minus 75% is 125.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 500 × 75 ÷ 100 = 375.

As a decimal: 75% is 0.75, so 500 × 0.75 = 375.

As a fraction: 75% is 3/4 (75/100 simplified), so it's 500 × 3 ÷ 4 = 375.

In your head: 75% is three quarters: a quarter of 500 is 125, and three of those make 375.

Adding or taking off 75%: multiply by 1.75 to add it (875) or by 0.25 to take it off (125).

Working backwards: if 375 is 75% of a number, the number is 375 ÷ 75 × 100 = 500. And if 875 is a price with 75% added, the price before was 875 ÷ 1.75 = 500.
