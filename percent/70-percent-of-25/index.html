What is 70% of 25?

70% of 25 is 17.5. 25 plus 70% is 42.5; 25 minus 70% is 7.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 25 × 70 ÷ 100 = 17.5.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 25 × 0.7 = 17.5.

As a fraction: 70% is 7/10 (70/100 simplified), so it's 25 × 7 ÷ 10 = 17.5.

In your head: 10% of 25 is 2.5, so 70% is 7 × 2.5 = 17.5.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (42.5) or by 0.3 to take it off (7.5).

Working backwards: if 17.5 is 70% of a number, the number is 17.5 ÷ 70 × 100 = 25. And if 42.5 is a price with 70% added, the price before was 42.5 ÷ 1.7 = 25.
