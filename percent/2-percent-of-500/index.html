What is 2% of 500?

2% of 500 is 10. 500 plus 2% is 510; 500 minus 2% is 490.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 500 × 2 ÷ 100 = 10.

As a decimal: 2% is 0.02, so 500 × 0.02 = 10.

In your head: 1% of 500 is 5, so 2% is 2 × 5 = 10.

Adding or taking off 2%: multiply by 1.02 to add it (510) or by 0.98 to take it off (490).
