What is 70% of 600?

70% of 600 is 420. 600 plus 70% is 1,020; 600 minus 70% is 180.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 600 × 70 ÷ 100 = 420.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 600 × 0.7 = 420.

In your head: 10% of 600 is 60, so 70% is 7 × 60 = 420.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (1,020) or by 0.3 to take it off (180).
