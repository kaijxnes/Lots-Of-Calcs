What is 70% of 400?

70% of 400 is 280. 400 plus 70% is 680; 400 minus 70% is 120.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 400 × 70 ÷ 100 = 280.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 400 × 0.7 = 280.

In your head: 10% of 400 is 40, so 70% is 7 × 40 = 280.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (680) or by 0.3 to take it off (120).
