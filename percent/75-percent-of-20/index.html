What is 75% of 20?

75% of 20 is 15. 20 plus 75% is 35; 20 minus 75% is 5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 20 × 75 ÷ 100 = 15.

As a decimal: 75% is 0.75, so 20 × 0.75 = 15.

As a fraction: 75% is 3/4 (75/100 simplified), so it's 20 × 3 ÷ 4 = 15.

In your head: 75% is three quarters: a quarter of 20 is 5, and three of those make 15.

Adding or taking off 75%: multiply by 1.75 to add it (35) or by 0.25 to take it off (5).

Working backwards: if 15 is 75% of a number, the number is 15 ÷ 75 × 100 = 20. And if 35 is a price with 75% added, the price before was 35 ÷ 1.75 = 20.
