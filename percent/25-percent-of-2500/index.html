What is 25% of 2,500?

25% of 2,500 is 625. 2,500 plus 25% is 3,125; 2,500 minus 25% is 1,875.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 2,500 × 25 ÷ 100 = 625.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 2,500 × 0.25 = 625.

As a fraction: 25% is 1/4 (25/100 simplified), so it's 2,500 × 1 ÷ 4 = 625.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 2,500 by 4: 625.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (3,125) or by 0.75 to take it off (1,875).

Working backwards: if 625 is 25% of a number, the number is 625 ÷ 25 × 100 = 2,500. And if 3,125 is a price with 25% added, the price before was 3,125 ÷ 1.25 = 2,500.
