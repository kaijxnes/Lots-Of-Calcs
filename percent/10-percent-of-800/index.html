What is 10% of 800?

10% of 800 is 80. 800 plus 10% is 880; 800 minus 10% is 720.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 800 × 10 ÷ 100 = 80.

As a decimal: 10% is 0.1, so 800 × 0.1 = 80.

As a fraction: 10% is 1/10 (10/100 simplified), so it's 800 × 1 ÷ 10 = 80.

In your head: 10% is a tenth, so divide 800 by 10: 80.

Adding or taking off 10%: multiply by 1.1 to add it (880) or by 0.9 to take it off (720).

Working backwards: if 80 is 10% of a number, the number is 80 ÷ 10 × 100 = 800. And if 880 is a price with 10% added, the price before was 880 ÷ 1.1 = 800.
