What is 50% of 10?

50% of 10 is 5. 10 plus 50% is 15; 10 minus 50% is 5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 10 × 50 ÷ 100 = 5.

As a decimal: 50% is 0.5, so 10 × 0.5 = 5.

In your head: 50% is half, so halve 10: 5.

Adding or taking off 50%: multiply by 1.5 to add it (15) or by 0.5 to take it off (5).
