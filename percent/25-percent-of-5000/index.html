What is 25% of 5,000?

25% of 5,000 is 1,250. 5,000 plus 25% is 6,250; 5,000 minus 25% is 3,750.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 5,000 × 25 ÷ 100 = 1,250.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 5,000 × 0.25 = 1,250.

As a fraction: 25% is 1/4 (25/100 simplified), so it's 5,000 × 1 ÷ 4 = 1,250.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 5,000 by 4: 1,250.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (6,250) or by 0.75 to take it off (3,750).

Working backwards: if 1,250 is 25% of a number, the number is 1,250 ÷ 25 × 100 = 5,000. And if 6,250 is a price with 25% added, the price before was 6,250 ÷ 1.25 = 5,000.
