What is 40% of 250?

40% of 250 is 100. 250 plus 40% is 350; 250 minus 40% is 150.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 250 × 40 ÷ 100 = 100.

As a decimal: 40% is 0.4, so 250 × 0.4 = 100.

As a fraction: 40% is 2/5 (40/100 simplified), so it's 250 × 2 ÷ 5 = 100.

In your head: 10% of 250 is 25, so 40% is 4 × 25 = 100.

Adding or taking off 40%: multiply by 1.4 to add it (350) or by 0.6 to take it off (150).

Working backwards: if 100 is 40% of a number, the number is 100 ÷ 40 × 100 = 250. And if 350 is a price with 40% added, the price before was 350 ÷ 1.4 = 250.
