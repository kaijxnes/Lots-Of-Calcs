What is 60% of 2,000?

60% of 2,000 is 1,200. 2,000 plus 60% is 3,200; 2,000 minus 60% is 800.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 2,000 × 60 ÷ 100 = 1,200.

As a decimal: 60% is 0.6, so 2,000 × 0.6 = 1,200.

As a fraction: 60% is 3/5 (60/100 simplified), so it's 2,000 × 3 ÷ 5 = 1,200.

In your head: 10% of 2,000 is 200, so 60% is 6 × 200 = 1,200.

Adding or taking off 60%: multiply by 1.6 to add it (3,200) or by 0.4 to take it off (800).

Working backwards: if 1,200 is 60% of a number, the number is 1,200 ÷ 60 × 100 = 2,000. And if 3,200 is a price with 60% added, the price before was 3,200 ÷ 1.6 = 2,000.
