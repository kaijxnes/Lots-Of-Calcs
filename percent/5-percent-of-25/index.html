What is 5% of 25?

5% of 25 is 1.25. 25 plus 5% is 26.25; 25 minus 5% is 23.75.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 25 × 5 ÷ 100 = 1.25.

As a decimal: 5% is 0.05, so 25 × 0.05 = 1.25.

As a fraction: 5% is 1/20 (5/100 simplified), so it's 25 × 1 ÷ 20 = 1.25.

In your head: 10% of 25 is 2.5, and 5% is half that: 1.25.

Adding or taking off 5%: multiply by 1.05 to add it (26.25) or by 0.95 to take it off (23.75).

Working backwards: if 1.25 is 5% of a number, the number is 1.25 ÷ 5 × 100 = 25. And if 26.25 is a price with 5% added, the price before was 26.25 ÷ 1.05 = 25.
