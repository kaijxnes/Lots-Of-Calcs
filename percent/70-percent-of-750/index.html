What is 70% of 750?

70% of 750 is 525. 750 plus 70% is 1,275; 750 minus 70% is 225.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 750 × 70 ÷ 100 = 525.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 750 × 0.7 = 525.

As a fraction: 70% is 7/10 (70/100 simplified), so it's 750 × 7 ÷ 10 = 525.

In your head: 10% of 750 is 75, so 70% is 7 × 75 = 525.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (1,275) or by 0.3 to take it off (225).

Working backwards: if 525 is 70% of a number, the number is 525 ÷ 70 × 100 = 750. And if 1,275 is a price with 70% added, the price before was 1,275 ÷ 1.7 = 750.
