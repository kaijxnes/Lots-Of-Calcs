What is 12% of 10?

12% of 10 is 1.2. 10 plus 12% is 11.2; 10 minus 12% is 8.8.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 10 × 12 ÷ 100 = 1.2.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 10 × 0.12 = 1.2.

As a fraction: 12% is 3/25 (12/100 simplified), so it's 10 × 3 ÷ 25 = 1.2.

In your head: 1% of 10 is 0.1, so 12% is 12 × 0.1 = 1.2.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (11.2) or by 0.88 to take it off (8.8).

Working backwards: if 1.2 is 12% of a number, the number is 1.2 ÷ 12 × 100 = 10. And if 11.2 is a price with 12% added, the price before was 11.2 ÷ 1.12 = 10.

Frequently asked questions

What is 12% of 10?

1.2. Multiply 10 by 12 and divide by 100, or multiply by 0.12.

What is 10 plus 12%?

11.2: 10 + 1.2, or 10 × 1.12.

What is 10 minus 12%?

8.8: 10 − 1.2, or 10 × 0.88. That's the price after a 12% discount.

How do I work out 12% of 10 in my head?

1% of 10 is 0.1, so 12% is 12 × 0.1 = 1.2.
