What is 90% of 250?

90% of 250 is 225. 250 plus 90% is 475; 250 minus 90% is 25.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 250 × 90 ÷ 100 = 225.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 250 × 0.9 = 225.

As a fraction: 90% is 9/10 (90/100 simplified), so it's 250 × 9 ÷ 10 = 225.

In your head: 10% of 250 is 25, so 90% is 9 × 25 = 225.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (475) or by 0.1 to take it off (25).

Working backwards: if 225 is 90% of a number, the number is 225 ÷ 90 × 100 = 250. And if 475 is a price with 90% added, the price before was 475 ÷ 1.9 = 250.
