What is 80% of 250?

80% of 250 is 200. 250 plus 80% is 450; 250 minus 80% is 50.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 250 × 80 ÷ 100 = 200.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 250 × 0.8 = 200.

As a fraction: 80% is 4/5 (80/100 simplified), so it's 250 × 4 ÷ 5 = 200.

In your head: 10% of 250 is 25, so 80% is 8 × 25 = 200.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (450) or by 0.2 to take it off (50).

Working backwards: if 200 is 80% of a number, the number is 200 ÷ 80 × 100 = 250. And if 450 is a price with 80% added, the price before was 450 ÷ 1.8 = 250.
