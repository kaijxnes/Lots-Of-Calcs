What is 5% of 50?

5% of 50 is 2.5. 50 plus 5% is 52.5; 50 minus 5% is 47.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 50 × 5 ÷ 100 = 2.5.

As a decimal: 5% is 0.05, so 50 × 0.05 = 2.5.

In your head: 10% of 50 is 5, and 5% is half that: 2.5.

Adding or taking off 5%: multiply by 1.05 to add it (52.5) or by 0.95 to take it off (47.5).
