What is 70% of 500?

70% of 500 is 350. 500 plus 70% is 850; 500 minus 70% is 150.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 500 × 70 ÷ 100 = 350.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 500 × 0.7 = 350.

In your head: 10% of 500 is 50, so 70% is 7 × 50 = 350.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (850) or by 0.3 to take it off (150).
