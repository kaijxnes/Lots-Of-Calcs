What is 75% of 1,000?

75% of 1,000 is 750. 1,000 plus 75% is 1,750; 1,000 minus 75% is 250.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 1,000 × 75 ÷ 100 = 750.

As a decimal: 75% is 0.75, so 1,000 × 0.75 = 750.

As a fraction: 75% is 3/4 (75/100 simplified), so it's 1,000 × 3 ÷ 4 = 750.

In your head: 75% is three quarters: a quarter of 1,000 is 250, and three of those make 750.

Adding or taking off 75%: multiply by 1.75 to add it (1,750) or by 0.25 to take it off (250).

Working backwards: if 750 is 75% of a number, the number is 750 ÷ 75 × 100 = 1,000. And if 1,750 is a price with 75% added, the price before was 1,750 ÷ 1.75 = 1,000.
