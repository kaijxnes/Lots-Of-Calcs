What is 40% of 100?

40% of 100 is 40. 100 plus 40% is 140; 100 minus 40% is 60.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 100 × 40 ÷ 100 = 40.

As a decimal: 40% is 0.4, so 100 × 0.4 = 40.

As a fraction: 40% is 2/5 (40/100 simplified), so it's 100 × 2 ÷ 5 = 40.

In your head: 10% of 100 is 10, so 40% is 4 × 10 = 40.

Adding or taking off 40%: multiply by 1.4 to add it (140) or by 0.6 to take it off (60).

Working backwards: if 40 is 40% of a number, the number is 40 ÷ 40 × 100 = 100. And if 140 is a price with 40% added, the price before was 140 ÷ 1.4 = 100.

Frequently asked questions

What is 40% of 100?

40. Multiply 100 by 40 and divide by 100, or multiply by 0.4.

What is 100 plus 40%?

140: 100 + 40, or 100 × 1.4.

What is 100 minus 40%?

60: 100 − 40, or 100 × 0.6. That's the price after a 40% discount.

How do I work out 40% of 100 in my head?

10% of 100 is 10, so 40% is 4 × 10 = 40.

40 is 40% of what number?

100. To work backwards from a percentage, divide by the percentage and multiply by 100: 40 ÷ 40 × 100 = 100.
