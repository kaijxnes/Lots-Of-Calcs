What is 80% of 75?

80% of 75 is 60. 75 plus 80% is 135; 75 minus 80% is 15.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 75 × 80 ÷ 100 = 60.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 75 × 0.8 = 60.

In your head: 10% of 75 is 7.5, so 80% is 8 × 7.5 = 60.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (135) or by 0.2 to take it off (15).
