What is 2% of 30?

2% of 30 is 0.6. 30 plus 2% is 30.6; 30 minus 2% is 29.4.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 30 × 2 ÷ 100 = 0.6.

As a decimal: 2% is 0.02, so 30 × 0.02 = 0.6.

In your head: 1% of 30 is 0.3, so 2% is 2 × 0.3 = 0.6.

Adding or taking off 2%: multiply by 1.02 to add it (30.6) or by 0.98 to take it off (29.4).
