What is 50% of 20?

50% of 20 is 10. 20 plus 50% is 30; 20 minus 50% is 10.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 20 × 50 ÷ 100 = 10.

As a decimal: 50% is 0.5, so 20 × 0.5 = 10.

As a fraction: 50% is 1/2 (50/100 simplified), so it's 20 × 1 ÷ 2 = 10.

In your head: 50% is half, so halve 20: 10.

Adding or taking off 50%: multiply by 1.5 to add it (30) or by 0.5 to take it off (10).

Working backwards: if 10 is 50% of a number, the number is 10 ÷ 50 × 100 = 20. And if 30 is a price with 50% added, the price before was 30 ÷ 1.5 = 20.
