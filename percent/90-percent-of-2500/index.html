What is 90% of 2,500?

90% of 2,500 is 2,250. 2,500 plus 90% is 4,750; 2,500 minus 90% is 250.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 2,500 × 90 ÷ 100 = 2,250.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 2,500 × 0.9 = 2,250.

As a fraction: 90% is 9/10 (90/100 simplified), so it's 2,500 × 9 ÷ 10 = 2,250.

In your head: 10% of 2,500 is 250, so 90% is 9 × 250 = 2,250.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (4,750) or by 0.1 to take it off (250).

Working backwards: if 2,250 is 90% of a number, the number is 2,250 ÷ 90 × 100 = 2,500. And if 4,750 is a price with 90% added, the price before was 4,750 ÷ 1.9 = 2,500.
