What is 80% of 5,000?

80% of 5,000 is 4,000. 5,000 plus 80% is 9,000; 5,000 minus 80% is 1,000.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 5,000 × 80 ÷ 100 = 4,000.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 5,000 × 0.8 = 4,000.

In your head: 10% of 5,000 is 500, so 80% is 8 × 500 = 4,000.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (9,000) or by 0.2 to take it off (1,000).
