What is 1% of 80?

1% of 80 is 0.8. 80 plus 1% is 80.8; 80 minus 1% is 79.2.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 80 × 1 ÷ 100 = 0.8.

As a decimal: 1% is 0.01, so 80 × 0.01 = 0.8.

In your head: 1% is a hundredth, so divide 80 by 100: 0.8.

Adding or taking off 1%: multiply by 1.01 to add it (80.8) or by 0.99 to take it off (79.2).
