What is 90% of 25?

90% of 25 is 22.5. 25 plus 90% is 47.5; 25 minus 90% is 2.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 25 × 90 ÷ 100 = 22.5.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 25 × 0.9 = 22.5.

In your head: 10% of 25 is 2.5, so 90% is 9 × 2.5 = 22.5.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (47.5) or by 0.1 to take it off (2.5).
