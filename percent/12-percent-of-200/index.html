What is 12% of 200?

12% of 200 is 24. 200 plus 12% is 224; 200 minus 12% is 176.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 200 × 12 ÷ 100 = 24.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 200 × 0.12 = 24.

As a fraction: 12% is 3/25 (12/100 simplified), so it's 200 × 3 ÷ 25 = 24.

In your head: 1% of 200 is 2, so 12% is 12 × 2 = 24.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (224) or by 0.88 to take it off (176).

Working backwards: if 24 is 12% of a number, the number is 24 ÷ 12 × 100 = 200. And if 224 is a price with 12% added, the price before was 224 ÷ 1.12 = 200.
