What is 80% of 80?

80% of 80 is 64. 80 plus 80% is 144; 80 minus 80% is 16.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 80 × 80 ÷ 100 = 64.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 80 × 0.8 = 64.

As a fraction: 80% is 4/5 (80/100 simplified), so it's 80 × 4 ÷ 5 = 64.

In your head: 10% of 80 is 8, so 80% is 8 × 8 = 64.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (144) or by 0.2 to take it off (16).

Working backwards: if 64 is 80% of a number, the number is 64 ÷ 80 × 100 = 80. And if 144 is a price with 80% added, the price before was 144 ÷ 1.8 = 80.
