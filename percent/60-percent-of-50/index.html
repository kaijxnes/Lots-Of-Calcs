What is 60% of 50?

60% of 50 is 30. 50 plus 60% is 80; 50 minus 60% is 20.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 50 × 60 ÷ 100 = 30.

As a decimal: 60% is 0.6, so 50 × 0.6 = 30.

As a fraction: 60% is 3/5 (60/100 simplified), so it's 50 × 3 ÷ 5 = 30.

In your head: 10% of 50 is 5, so 60% is 6 × 5 = 30.

Adding or taking off 60%: multiply by 1.6 to add it (80) or by 0.4 to take it off (20).

Working backwards: if 30 is 60% of a number, the number is 30 ÷ 60 × 100 = 50. And if 80 is a price with 60% added, the price before was 80 ÷ 1.6 = 50.
