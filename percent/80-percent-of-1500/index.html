What is 80% of 1,500?

80% of 1,500 is 1,200. 1,500 plus 80% is 2,700; 1,500 minus 80% is 300.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 1,500 × 80 ÷ 100 = 1,200.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 1,500 × 0.8 = 1,200.

As a fraction: 80% is 4/5 (80/100 simplified), so it's 1,500 × 4 ÷ 5 = 1,200.

In your head: 10% of 1,500 is 150, so 80% is 8 × 150 = 1,200.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (2,700) or by 0.2 to take it off (300).

Working backwards: if 1,200 is 80% of a number, the number is 1,200 ÷ 80 × 100 = 1,500. And if 2,700 is a price with 80% added, the price before was 2,700 ÷ 1.8 = 1,500.
