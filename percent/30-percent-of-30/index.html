What is 30% of 30?

30% of 30 is 9. 30 plus 30% is 39; 30 minus 30% is 21.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 30 × 30 ÷ 100 = 9.

As a decimal: 30% is 0.3, so 30 × 0.3 = 9.

In your head: 10% of 30 is 3, so 30% is 3 × 3 = 9.

Adding or taking off 30%: multiply by 1.3 to add it (39) or by 0.7 to take it off (21).
