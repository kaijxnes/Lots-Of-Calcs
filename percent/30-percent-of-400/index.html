What is 30% of 400?

30% of 400 is 120. 400 plus 30% is 520; 400 minus 30% is 280.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 400 × 30 ÷ 100 = 120.

As a decimal: 30% is 0.3, so 400 × 0.3 = 120.

In your head: 10% of 400 is 40, so 30% is 3 × 40 = 120.

Adding or taking off 30%: multiply by 1.3 to add it (520) or by 0.7 to take it off (280).
