What is 90% of 10?

90% of 10 is 9. 10 plus 90% is 19; 10 minus 90% is 1.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 10 × 90 ÷ 100 = 9.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 10 × 0.9 = 9.

As a fraction: 90% is 9/10 (90/100 simplified), so it's 10 × 9 ÷ 10 = 9.

In your head: 10% of 10 is 1, so 90% is 9 × 1 = 9.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (19) or by 0.1 to take it off (1).

Working backwards: if 9 is 90% of a number, the number is 9 ÷ 90 × 100 = 10. And if 19 is a price with 90% added, the price before was 19 ÷ 1.9 = 10.
