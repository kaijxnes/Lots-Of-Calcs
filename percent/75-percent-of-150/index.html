What is 75% of 150?

75% of 150 is 112.5. 150 plus 75% is 262.5; 150 minus 75% is 37.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 150 × 75 ÷ 100 = 112.5.

As a decimal: 75% is 0.75, so 150 × 0.75 = 112.5.

As a fraction: 75% is 3/4 (75/100 simplified), so it's 150 × 3 ÷ 4 = 112.5.

In your head: 75% is three quarters: a quarter of 150 is 37.5, and three of those make 112.5.

Adding or taking off 75%: multiply by 1.75 to add it (262.5) or by 0.25 to take it off (37.5).

Working backwards: if 112.5 is 75% of a number, the number is 112.5 ÷ 75 × 100 = 150. And if 262.5 is a price with 75% added, the price before was 262.5 ÷ 1.75 = 150.
