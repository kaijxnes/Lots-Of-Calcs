What is 20% of 750?

20% of 750 is 150. 750 plus 20% is 900; 750 minus 20% is 600.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 750 × 20 ÷ 100 = 150.

As a decimal: 20% is 0.2, so 750 × 0.2 = 150.

In your head: 10% of 750 is 75, so 20% is 2 × 75 = 150.

Adding or taking off 20%: multiply by 1.2 to add it (900) or by 0.8 to take it off (600).
