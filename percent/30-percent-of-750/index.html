What is 30% of 750?

30% of 750 is 225. 750 plus 30% is 975; 750 minus 30% is 525.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 750 × 30 ÷ 100 = 225.

As a decimal: 30% is 0.3, so 750 × 0.3 = 225.

As a fraction: 30% is 3/10 (30/100 simplified), so it's 750 × 3 ÷ 10 = 225.

In your head: 10% of 750 is 75, so 30% is 3 × 75 = 225.

Adding or taking off 30%: multiply by 1.3 to add it (975) or by 0.7 to take it off (525).

Working backwards: if 225 is 30% of a number, the number is 225 ÷ 30 × 100 = 750. And if 975 is a price with 30% added, the price before was 975 ÷ 1.3 = 750.
