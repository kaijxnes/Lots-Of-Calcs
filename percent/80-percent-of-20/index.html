What is 80% of 20?

80% of 20 is 16. 20 plus 80% is 36; 20 minus 80% is 4.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 20 × 80 ÷ 100 = 16.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 20 × 0.8 = 16.

As a fraction: 80% is 4/5 (80/100 simplified), so it's 20 × 4 ÷ 5 = 16.

In your head: 10% of 20 is 2, so 80% is 8 × 2 = 16.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (36) or by 0.2 to take it off (4).

Working backwards: if 16 is 80% of a number, the number is 16 ÷ 80 × 100 = 20. And if 36 is a price with 80% added, the price before was 36 ÷ 1.8 = 20.
