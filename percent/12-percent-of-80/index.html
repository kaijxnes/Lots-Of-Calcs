What is 12% of 80?

12% of 80 is 9.6. 80 plus 12% is 89.6; 80 minus 12% is 70.4.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 80 × 12 ÷ 100 = 9.6.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 80 × 0.12 = 9.6.

In your head: 1% of 80 is 0.8, so 12% is 12 × 0.8 = 9.6.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (89.6) or by 0.88 to take it off (70.4).
