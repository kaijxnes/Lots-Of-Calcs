What is 10% of 2,500?

10% of 2,500 is 250. 2,500 plus 10% is 2,750; 2,500 minus 10% is 2,250.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 2,500 × 10 ÷ 100 = 250.

As a decimal: 10% is 0.1, so 2,500 × 0.1 = 250.

In your head: 10% is a tenth, so divide 2,500 by 10: 250.

Adding or taking off 10%: multiply by 1.1 to add it (2,750) or by 0.9 to take it off (2,250).
