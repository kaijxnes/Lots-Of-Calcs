What is 12% of 800?

12% of 800 is 96. 800 plus 12% is 896; 800 minus 12% is 704.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 800 × 12 ÷ 100 = 96.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 800 × 0.12 = 96.

As a fraction: 12% is 3/25 (12/100 simplified), so it's 800 × 3 ÷ 25 = 96.

In your head: 1% of 800 is 8, so 12% is 12 × 8 = 96.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (896) or by 0.88 to take it off (704).

Working backwards: if 96 is 12% of a number, the number is 96 ÷ 12 × 100 = 800. And if 896 is a price with 12% added, the price before was 896 ÷ 1.12 = 800.
