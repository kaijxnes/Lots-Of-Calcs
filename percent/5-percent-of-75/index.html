What is 5% of 75?

5% of 75 is 3.75. 75 plus 5% is 78.75; 75 minus 5% is 71.25.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 75 × 5 ÷ 100 = 3.75.

As a decimal: 5% is 0.05, so 75 × 0.05 = 3.75.

As a fraction: 5% is 1/20 (5/100 simplified), so it's 75 × 1 ÷ 20 = 3.75.

In your head: 10% of 75 is 7.5, and 5% is half that: 3.75.

Adding or taking off 5%: multiply by 1.05 to add it (78.75) or by 0.95 to take it off (71.25).

Working backwards: if 3.75 is 5% of a number, the number is 3.75 ÷ 5 × 100 = 75. And if 78.75 is a price with 5% added, the price before was 78.75 ÷ 1.05 = 75.
