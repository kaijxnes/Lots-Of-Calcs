What is 60% of 400?

60% of 400 is 240. 400 plus 60% is 640; 400 minus 60% is 160.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 400 × 60 ÷ 100 = 240.

As a decimal: 60% is 0.6, so 400 × 0.6 = 240.

As a fraction: 60% is 3/5 (60/100 simplified), so it's 400 × 3 ÷ 5 = 240.

In your head: 10% of 400 is 40, so 60% is 6 × 40 = 240.

Adding or taking off 60%: multiply by 1.6 to add it (640) or by 0.4 to take it off (160).

Working backwards: if 240 is 60% of a number, the number is 240 ÷ 60 × 100 = 400. And if 640 is a price with 60% added, the price before was 640 ÷ 1.6 = 400.
