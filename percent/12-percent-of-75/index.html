What is 12% of 75?

12% of 75 is 9. 75 plus 12% is 84; 75 minus 12% is 66.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 75 × 12 ÷ 100 = 9.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 75 × 0.12 = 9.

As a fraction: 12% is 3/25 (12/100 simplified), so it's 75 × 3 ÷ 25 = 9.

In your head: 1% of 75 is 0.75, so 12% is 12 × 0.75 = 9.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (84) or by 0.88 to take it off (66).

Working backwards: if 9 is 12% of a number, the number is 9 ÷ 12 × 100 = 75. And if 84 is a price with 12% added, the price before was 84 ÷ 1.12 = 75.
