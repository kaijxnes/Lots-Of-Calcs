What is 1% of 10?

1% of 10 is 0.1. 10 plus 1% is 10.1; 10 minus 1% is 9.9.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 10 × 1 ÷ 100 = 0.1.

As a decimal: 1% is 0.01, so 10 × 0.01 = 0.1.

As a fraction: 1% is 1/100, so it's 10 × 1 ÷ 100 = 0.1.

In your head: 1% is a hundredth, so divide 10 by 100: 0.1.

Adding or taking off 1%: multiply by 1.01 to add it (10.1) or by 0.99 to take it off (9.9).

Working backwards: if 0.1 is 1% of a number, the number is 0.1 ÷ 1 × 100 = 10. And if 10.1 is a price with 1% added, the price before was 10.1 ÷ 1.01 = 10.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1% of 10?

0.1. Multiply 10 by 1 and divide by 100, or multiply by 0.01.

What is 10 plus 1%?

10.1: 10 + 0.1, or 10 × 1.01.

What is 10 minus 1%?

9.9: 10 − 0.1, or 10 × 0.99. That's the price after a 1% discount.

How do I work out 1% of 10 in my head?

1% is a hundredth, so divide 10 by 100: 0.1.

0.1 is 1% of what number?

10. To work backwards from a percentage, divide by the percentage and multiply by 100: 0.1 ÷ 1 × 100 = 10.

What percentage of 10 is 0.1?
