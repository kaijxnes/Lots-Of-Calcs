What is 90% of 30?

90% of 30 is 27. 30 plus 90% is 57; 30 minus 90% is 3.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 30 × 90 ÷ 100 = 27.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 30 × 0.9 = 27.

As a fraction: 90% is 9/10 (90/100 simplified), so it's 30 × 9 ÷ 10 = 27.

In your head: 10% of 30 is 3, so 90% is 9 × 3 = 27.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (57) or by 0.1 to take it off (3).

Working backwards: if 27 is 90% of a number, the number is 27 ÷ 90 × 100 = 30. And if 57 is a price with 90% added, the price before was 57 ÷ 1.9 = 30.
