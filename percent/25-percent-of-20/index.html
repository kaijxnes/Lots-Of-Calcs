What is 25% of 20?

25% of 20 is 5. 20 plus 25% is 25; 20 minus 25% is 15.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 20 × 25 ÷ 100 = 5.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 20 × 0.25 = 5.

As a fraction: 25% is 1/4 (25/100 simplified), so it's 20 × 1 ÷ 4 = 5.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 20 by 4: 5.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (25) or by 0.75 to take it off (15).

Working backwards: if 5 is 25% of a number, the number is 5 ÷ 25 × 100 = 20. And if 25 is a price with 25% added, the price before was 25 ÷ 1.25 = 20.
