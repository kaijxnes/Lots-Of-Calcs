What is 25% of 80?

25% of 80 is 20. 80 plus 25% is 100; 80 minus 25% is 60.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 80 × 25 ÷ 100 = 20.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 80 × 0.25 = 20.

As a fraction: 25% is 1/4 (25/100 simplified), so it's 80 × 1 ÷ 4 = 20.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 80 by 4: 20.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (100) or by 0.75 to take it off (60).

Working backwards: if 20 is 25% of a number, the number is 20 ÷ 25 × 100 = 80. And if 100 is a price with 25% added, the price before was 100 ÷ 1.25 = 80.
