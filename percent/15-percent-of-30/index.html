What is 15% of 30?

15% of 30 is 4.5. 30 plus 15% is 34.5; 30 minus 15% is 25.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 30 × 15 ÷ 100 = 4.5.

As a decimal: 15% is 0.15, so 30 × 0.15 = 4.5.

As a fraction: 15% is 3/20 (15/100 simplified), so it's 30 × 3 ÷ 20 = 4.5.

In your head: 10% of 30 is 3 and 5% is half that, 1.5, so 15% is 4.5.

Adding or taking off 15%: multiply by 1.15 to add it (34.5) or by 0.85 to take it off (25.5).

Working backwards: if 4.5 is 15% of a number, the number is 4.5 ÷ 15 × 100 = 30. And if 34.5 is a price with 15% added, the price before was 34.5 ÷ 1.15 = 30.
