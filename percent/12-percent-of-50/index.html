What is 12% of 50?

12% of 50 is 6. 50 plus 12% is 56; 50 minus 12% is 44.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 50 × 12 ÷ 100 = 6.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 50 × 0.12 = 6.

In your head: 1% of 50 is 0.5, so 12% is 12 × 0.5 = 6.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (56) or by 0.88 to take it off (44).
