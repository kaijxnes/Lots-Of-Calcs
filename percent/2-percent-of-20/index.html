What is 2% of 20?

2% of 20 is 0.4. 20 plus 2% is 20.4; 20 minus 2% is 19.6.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 20 × 2 ÷ 100 = 0.4.

As a decimal: 2% is 0.02, so 20 × 0.02 = 0.4.

As a fraction: 2% is 1/50 (2/100 simplified), so it's 20 × 1 ÷ 50 = 0.4.

In your head: 1% of 20 is 0.2, so 2% is 2 × 0.2 = 0.4.

Adding or taking off 2%: multiply by 1.02 to add it (20.4) or by 0.98 to take it off (19.6).

Working backwards: if 0.4 is 2% of a number, the number is 0.4 ÷ 2 × 100 = 20. And if 20.4 is a price with 2% added, the price before was 20.4 ÷ 1.02 = 20.
