What is 1% of 50?

1% of 50 is 0.5. 50 plus 1% is 50.5; 50 minus 1% is 49.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 50 × 1 ÷ 100 = 0.5.

As a decimal: 1% is 0.01, so 50 × 0.01 = 0.5.

In your head: 1% is a hundredth, so divide 50 by 100: 0.5.

Adding or taking off 1%: multiply by 1.01 to add it (50.5) or by 0.99 to take it off (49.5).
