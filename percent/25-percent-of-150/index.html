What is 25% of 150?

25% of 150 is 37.5. 150 plus 25% is 187.5; 150 minus 25% is 112.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 150 × 25 ÷ 100 = 37.5.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 150 × 0.25 = 37.5.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 150 by 4: 37.5.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (187.5) or by 0.75 to take it off (112.5).
