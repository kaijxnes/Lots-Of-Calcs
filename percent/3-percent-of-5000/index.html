What is 3% of 5,000?

3% of 5,000 is 150. 5,000 plus 3% is 5,150; 5,000 minus 3% is 4,850.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 5,000 × 3 ÷ 100 = 150.

As a decimal: 3% is 0.03, so 5,000 × 0.03 = 150.

As a fraction: 3% is 3/100, so it's 5,000 × 3 ÷ 100 = 150.

In your head: 1% of 5,000 is 50, so 3% is 3 × 50 = 150.

Adding or taking off 3%: multiply by 1.03 to add it (5,150) or by 0.97 to take it off (4,850).

Working backwards: if 150 is 3% of a number, the number is 150 ÷ 3 × 100 = 5,000. And if 5,150 is a price with 3% added, the price before was 5,150 ÷ 1.03 = 5,000.
