What is 25% of 750?

25% of 750 is 187.5. 750 plus 25% is 937.5; 750 minus 25% is 562.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 750 × 25 ÷ 100 = 187.5.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 750 × 0.25 = 187.5.

As a fraction: 25% is 1/4 (25/100 simplified), so it's 750 × 1 ÷ 4 = 187.5.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 750 by 4: 187.5.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (937.5) or by 0.75 to take it off (562.5).

Working backwards: if 187.5 is 25% of a number, the number is 187.5 ÷ 25 × 100 = 750. And if 937.5 is a price with 25% added, the price before was 937.5 ÷ 1.25 = 750.
