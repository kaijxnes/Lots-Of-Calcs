What is 70% of 300?

70% of 300 is 210. 300 plus 70% is 510; 300 minus 70% is 90.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 300 × 70 ÷ 100 = 210.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 300 × 0.7 = 210.

As a fraction: 70% is 7/10 (70/100 simplified), so it's 300 × 7 ÷ 10 = 210.

In your head: 10% of 300 is 30, so 70% is 7 × 30 = 210.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (510) or by 0.3 to take it off (90).

Working backwards: if 210 is 70% of a number, the number is 210 ÷ 70 × 100 = 300. And if 510 is a price with 70% added, the price before was 510 ÷ 1.7 = 300.
