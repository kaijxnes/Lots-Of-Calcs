What is 3% of 80?

3% of 80 is 2.4. 80 plus 3% is 82.4; 80 minus 3% is 77.6.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 80 × 3 ÷ 100 = 2.4.

As a decimal: 3% is 0.03, so 80 × 0.03 = 2.4.

In your head: 1% of 80 is 0.8, so 3% is 3 × 0.8 = 2.4.

Adding or taking off 3%: multiply by 1.03 to add it (82.4) or by 0.97 to take it off (77.6).
