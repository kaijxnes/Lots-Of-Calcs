What is 15% of 5,000?

15% of 5,000 is 750. 5,000 plus 15% is 5,750; 5,000 minus 15% is 4,250.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 5,000 × 15 ÷ 100 = 750.

As a decimal: 15% is 0.15, so 5,000 × 0.15 = 750.

As a fraction: 15% is 3/20 (15/100 simplified), so it's 5,000 × 3 ÷ 20 = 750.

In your head: 10% of 5,000 is 500 and 5% is half that, 250, so 15% is 750.

Adding or taking off 15%: multiply by 1.15 to add it (5,750) or by 0.85 to take it off (4,250).

Working backwards: if 750 is 15% of a number, the number is 750 ÷ 15 × 100 = 5,000. And if 5,750 is a price with 15% added, the price before was 5,750 ÷ 1.15 = 5,000.
