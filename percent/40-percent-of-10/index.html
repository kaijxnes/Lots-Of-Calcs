What is 40% of 10?

40% of 10 is 4. 10 plus 40% is 14; 10 minus 40% is 6.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 10 × 40 ÷ 100 = 4.

As a decimal: 40% is 0.4, so 10 × 0.4 = 4.

In your head: 10% of 10 is 1, so 40% is 4 × 1 = 4.

Adding or taking off 40%: multiply by 1.4 to add it (14) or by 0.6 to take it off (6).
