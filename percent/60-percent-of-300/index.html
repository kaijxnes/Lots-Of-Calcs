What is 60% of 300?

60% of 300 is 180. 300 plus 60% is 480; 300 minus 60% is 120.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 300 × 60 ÷ 100 = 180.

As a decimal: 60% is 0.6, so 300 × 0.6 = 180.

As a fraction: 60% is 3/5 (60/100 simplified), so it's 300 × 3 ÷ 5 = 180.

In your head: 10% of 300 is 30, so 60% is 6 × 30 = 180.

Adding or taking off 60%: multiply by 1.6 to add it (480) or by 0.4 to take it off (120).

Working backwards: if 180 is 60% of a number, the number is 180 ÷ 60 × 100 = 300. And if 480 is a price with 60% added, the price before was 480 ÷ 1.6 = 300.
