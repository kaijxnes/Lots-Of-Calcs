What is 20% of 75?

20% of 75 is 15. 75 plus 20% is 90; 75 minus 20% is 60.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 75 × 20 ÷ 100 = 15.

As a decimal: 20% is 0.2, so 75 × 0.2 = 15.

As a fraction: 20% is 1/5 (20/100 simplified), so it's 75 × 1 ÷ 5 = 15.

In your head: 10% of 75 is 7.5, so 20% is 2 × 7.5 = 15.

Adding or taking off 20%: multiply by 1.2 to add it (90) or by 0.8 to take it off (60).

Working backwards: if 15 is 20% of a number, the number is 15 ÷ 20 × 100 = 75. And if 90 is a price with 20% added, the price before was 90 ÷ 1.2 = 75.
