What is 20% of 100?

20% of 100 is 20. 100 plus 20% is 120; 100 minus 20% is 80.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 100 × 20 ÷ 100 = 20.

As a decimal: 20% is 0.2, so 100 × 0.2 = 20.

As a fraction: 20% is 1/5 (20/100 simplified), so it's 100 × 1 ÷ 5 = 20.

In your head: 10% of 100 is 10, so 20% is 2 × 10 = 20.

Adding or taking off 20%: multiply by 1.2 to add it (120) or by 0.8 to take it off (80).

Working backwards: if 20 is 20% of a number, the number is 20 ÷ 20 × 100 = 100. And if 120 is a price with 20% added, the price before was 120 ÷ 1.2 = 100.

Frequently asked questions

What is 20% of 100?

20. Multiply 100 by 20 and divide by 100, or multiply by 0.2.

What is 100 plus 20%?

120: 100 + 20, or 100 × 1.2.

What is 100 minus 20%?

80: 100 − 20, or 100 × 0.8. That's the price after a 20% discount.

How do I work out 20% of 100 in my head?

10% of 100 is 10, so 20% is 2 × 10 = 20.

20 is 20% of what number?

100. To work backwards from a percentage, divide by the percentage and multiply by 100: 20 ÷ 20 × 100 = 100.
