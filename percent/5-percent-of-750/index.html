What is 5% of 750?

5% of 750 is 37.5. 750 plus 5% is 787.5; 750 minus 5% is 712.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 750 × 5 ÷ 100 = 37.5.

As a decimal: 5% is 0.05, so 750 × 0.05 = 37.5.

As a fraction: 5% is 1/20 (5/100 simplified), so it's 750 × 1 ÷ 20 = 37.5.

In your head: 10% of 750 is 75, and 5% is half that: 37.5.

Adding or taking off 5%: multiply by 1.05 to add it (787.5) or by 0.95 to take it off (712.5).

Working backwards: if 37.5 is 5% of a number, the number is 37.5 ÷ 5 × 100 = 750. And if 787.5 is a price with 5% added, the price before was 787.5 ÷ 1.05 = 750.
