What is 50% of 400?

50% of 400 is 200. 400 plus 50% is 600; 400 minus 50% is 200.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 400 × 50 ÷ 100 = 200.

As a decimal: 50% is 0.5, so 400 × 0.5 = 200.

As a fraction: 50% is 1/2 (50/100 simplified), so it's 400 × 1 ÷ 2 = 200.

In your head: 50% is half, so halve 400: 200.

Adding or taking off 50%: multiply by 1.5 to add it (600) or by 0.5 to take it off (200).

Working backwards: if 200 is 50% of a number, the number is 200 ÷ 50 × 100 = 400. And if 600 is a price with 50% added, the price before was 600 ÷ 1.5 = 400.
