What is 90% of 600?

90% of 600 is 540. 600 plus 90% is 1,140; 600 minus 90% is 60.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 600 × 90 ÷ 100 = 540.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 600 × 0.9 = 540.

In your head: 10% of 600 is 60, so 90% is 9 × 60 = 540.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (1,140) or by 0.1 to take it off (60).
