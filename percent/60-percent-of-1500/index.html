What is 60% of 1,500?

60% of 1,500 is 900. 1,500 plus 60% is 2,400; 1,500 minus 60% is 600.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 1,500 × 60 ÷ 100 = 900.

As a decimal: 60% is 0.6, so 1,500 × 0.6 = 900.

As a fraction: 60% is 3/5 (60/100 simplified), so it's 1,500 × 3 ÷ 5 = 900.

In your head: 10% of 1,500 is 150, so 60% is 6 × 150 = 900.

Adding or taking off 60%: multiply by 1.6 to add it (2,400) or by 0.4 to take it off (600).

Working backwards: if 900 is 60% of a number, the number is 900 ÷ 60 × 100 = 1,500. And if 2,400 is a price with 60% added, the price before was 2,400 ÷ 1.6 = 1,500.
