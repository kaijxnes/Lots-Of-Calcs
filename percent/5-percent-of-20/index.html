What is 5% of 20?

5% of 20 is 1. 20 plus 5% is 21; 20 minus 5% is 19.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 20 × 5 ÷ 100 = 1.

As a decimal: 5% is 0.05, so 20 × 0.05 = 1.

In your head: 10% of 20 is 2, and 5% is half that: 1.

Adding or taking off 5%: multiply by 1.05 to add it (21) or by 0.95 to take it off (19).
