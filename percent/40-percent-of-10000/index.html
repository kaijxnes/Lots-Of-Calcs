What is 40% of 10,000?

40% of 10,000 is 4,000. 10,000 plus 40% is 14,000; 10,000 minus 40% is 6,000.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 10,000 × 40 ÷ 100 = 4,000.

As a decimal: 40% is 0.4, so 10,000 × 0.4 = 4,000.

As a fraction: 40% is 2/5 (40/100 simplified), so it's 10,000 × 2 ÷ 5 = 4,000.

In your head: 10% of 10,000 is 1,000, so 40% is 4 × 1,000 = 4,000.

Adding or taking off 40%: multiply by 1.4 to add it (14,000) or by 0.6 to take it off (6,000).

Working backwards: if 4,000 is 40% of a number, the number is 4,000 ÷ 40 × 100 = 10,000. And if 14,000 is a price with 40% added, the price before was 14,000 ÷ 1.4 = 10,000.
