What is 70% of 150?

70% of 150 is 105. 150 plus 70% is 255; 150 minus 70% is 45.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 150 × 70 ÷ 100 = 105.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 150 × 0.7 = 105.

In your head: 10% of 150 is 15, so 70% is 7 × 15 = 105.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (255) or by 0.3 to take it off (45).
