What is 12% of 5,000?

12% of 5,000 is 600. 5,000 plus 12% is 5,600; 5,000 minus 12% is 4,400.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 5,000 × 12 ÷ 100 = 600.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 5,000 × 0.12 = 600.

As a fraction: 12% is 3/25 (12/100 simplified), so it's 5,000 × 3 ÷ 25 = 600.

In your head: 1% of 5,000 is 50, so 12% is 12 × 50 = 600.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (5,600) or by 0.88 to take it off (4,400).

Working backwards: if 600 is 12% of a number, the number is 600 ÷ 12 × 100 = 5,000. And if 5,600 is a price with 12% added, the price before was 5,600 ÷ 1.12 = 5,000.
