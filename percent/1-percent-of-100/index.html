What is 1% of 100?

1% of 100 is 1. 100 plus 1% is 101; 100 minus 1% is 99.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 100 × 1 ÷ 100 = 1.

As a decimal: 1% is 0.01, so 100 × 0.01 = 1.

As a fraction: 1% is 1/100, so it's 100 × 1 ÷ 100 = 1.

In your head: 1% is a hundredth, so divide 100 by 100: 1.

Adding or taking off 1%: multiply by 1.01 to add it (101) or by 0.99 to take it off (99).

Working backwards: if 1 is 1% of a number, the number is 1 ÷ 1 × 100 = 100. And if 101 is a price with 1% added, the price before was 101 ÷ 1.01 = 100.

What is 1% of 100?

101: 100 + 1, or 100 × 1.01.

What is 100 minus 1%?

99: 100 − 1, or 100 × 0.99. That's the price after a 1% discount.

1 is 1% of what number?

100. To work backwards from a percentage, divide by the percentage and multiply by 100: 1 ÷ 1 × 100 = 100.
