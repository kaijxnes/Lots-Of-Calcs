What is 60% of 200?

60% of 200 is 120. 200 plus 60% is 320; 200 minus 60% is 80.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 200 × 60 ÷ 100 = 120.

As a decimal: 60% is 0.6, so 200 × 0.6 = 120.

As a fraction: 60% is 3/5 (60/100 simplified), so it's 200 × 3 ÷ 5 = 120.

In your head: 10% of 200 is 20, so 60% is 6 × 20 = 120.

Adding or taking off 60%: multiply by 1.6 to add it (320) or by 0.4 to take it off (80).

Working backwards: if 120 is 60% of a number, the number is 120 ÷ 60 × 100 = 200. And if 320 is a price with 60% added, the price before was 320 ÷ 1.6 = 200.

320: 200 + 120, or 200 × 1.6.

What is 200 minus 60%?

80: 200 − 120, or 200 × 0.4. That's the price after a 60% discount.

10% of 200 is 20, so 60% is 6 × 20 = 120.
