What is 15% of 750?

15% of 750 is 112.5. 750 plus 15% is 862.5; 750 minus 15% is 637.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 750 × 15 ÷ 100 = 112.5.

As a decimal: 15% is 0.15, so 750 × 0.15 = 112.5.

As a fraction: 15% is 3/20 (15/100 simplified), so it's 750 × 3 ÷ 20 = 112.5.

In your head: 10% of 750 is 75 and 5% is half that, 37.5, so 15% is 112.5.

Adding or taking off 15%: multiply by 1.15 to add it (862.5) or by 0.85 to take it off (637.5).

Working backwards: if 112.5 is 15% of a number, the number is 112.5 ÷ 15 × 100 = 750. And if 862.5 is a price with 15% added, the price before was 862.5 ÷ 1.15 = 750.
