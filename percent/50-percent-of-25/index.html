What is 50% of 25?

50% of 25 is 12.5. 25 plus 50% is 37.5; 25 minus 50% is 12.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 25 × 50 ÷ 100 = 12.5.

As a decimal: 50% is 0.5, so 25 × 0.5 = 12.5.

In your head: 50% is half, so halve 25: 12.5.

Adding or taking off 50%: multiply by 1.5 to add it (37.5) or by 0.5 to take it off (12.5).
