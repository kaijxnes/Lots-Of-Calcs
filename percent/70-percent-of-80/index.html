What is 70% of 80?

70% of 80 is 56. 80 plus 70% is 136; 80 minus 70% is 24.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 80 × 70 ÷ 100 = 56.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 80 × 0.7 = 56.

As a fraction: 70% is 7/10 (70/100 simplified), so it's 80 × 7 ÷ 10 = 56.

In your head: 10% of 80 is 8, so 70% is 7 × 8 = 56.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (136) or by 0.3 to take it off (24).

Working backwards: if 56 is 70% of a number, the number is 56 ÷ 70 × 100 = 80. And if 136 is a price with 70% added, the price before was 136 ÷ 1.7 = 80.
