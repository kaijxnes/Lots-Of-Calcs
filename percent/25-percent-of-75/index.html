What is 25% of 75?

25% of 75 is 18.75. 75 plus 25% is 93.75; 75 minus 25% is 56.25.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 75 × 25 ÷ 100 = 18.75.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 75 × 0.25 = 18.75.

As a fraction: 25% is 1/4 (25/100 simplified), so it's 75 × 1 ÷ 4 = 18.75.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 75 by 4: 18.75.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (93.75) or by 0.75 to take it off (56.25).

Working backwards: if 18.75 is 25% of a number, the number is 18.75 ÷ 25 × 100 = 75. And if 93.75 is a price with 25% added, the price before was 93.75 ÷ 1.25 = 75.
