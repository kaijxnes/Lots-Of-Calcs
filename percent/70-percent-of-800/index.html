What is 70% of 800?

70% of 800 is 560. 800 plus 70% is 1,360; 800 minus 70% is 240.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 800 × 70 ÷ 100 = 560.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 800 × 0.7 = 560.

As a fraction: 70% is 7/10 (70/100 simplified), so it's 800 × 7 ÷ 10 = 560.

In your head: 10% of 800 is 80, so 70% is 7 × 80 = 560.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (1,360) or by 0.3 to take it off (240).

Working backwards: if 560 is 70% of a number, the number is 560 ÷ 70 × 100 = 800. And if 1,360 is a price with 70% added, the price before was 1,360 ÷ 1.7 = 800.
