What is 80% of 800?

80% of 800 is 640. 800 plus 80% is 1,440; 800 minus 80% is 160.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 800 × 80 ÷ 100 = 640.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 800 × 0.8 = 640.

In your head: 10% of 800 is 80, so 80% is 8 × 80 = 640.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (1,440) or by 0.2 to take it off (160).
