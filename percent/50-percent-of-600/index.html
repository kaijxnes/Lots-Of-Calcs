What is 50% of 600?

50% of 600 is 300. 600 plus 50% is 900; 600 minus 50% is 300.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 600 × 50 ÷ 100 = 300.

As a decimal: 50% is 0.5, so 600 × 0.5 = 300.

In your head: 50% is half, so halve 600: 300.

Adding or taking off 50%: multiply by 1.5 to add it (900) or by 0.5 to take it off (300).
