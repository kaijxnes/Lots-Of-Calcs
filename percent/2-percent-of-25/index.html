What is 2% of 25?

2% of 25 is 0.5. 25 plus 2% is 25.5; 25 minus 2% is 24.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 25 × 2 ÷ 100 = 0.5.

As a decimal: 2% is 0.02, so 25 × 0.02 = 0.5.

As a fraction: 2% is 1/50 (2/100 simplified), so it's 25 × 1 ÷ 50 = 0.5.

In your head: 1% of 25 is 0.25, so 2% is 2 × 0.25 = 0.5.

Adding or taking off 2%: multiply by 1.02 to add it (25.5) or by 0.98 to take it off (24.5).

Working backwards: if 0.5 is 2% of a number, the number is 0.5 ÷ 2 × 100 = 25. And if 25.5 is a price with 2% added, the price before was 25.5 ÷ 1.02 = 25.
