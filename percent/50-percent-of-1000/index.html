What is 50% of 1,000?

50% of 1,000 is 500. 1,000 plus 50% is 1,500; 1,000 minus 50% is 500.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 1,000 × 50 ÷ 100 = 500.

As a decimal: 50% is 0.5, so 1,000 × 0.5 = 500.

In your head: 50% is half, so halve 1,000: 500.

Adding or taking off 50%: multiply by 1.5 to add it (1,500) or by 0.5 to take it off (500).
