What is 30% of 5,000?

30% of 5,000 is 1,500. 5,000 plus 30% is 6,500; 5,000 minus 30% is 3,500.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 5,000 × 30 ÷ 100 = 1,500.

As a decimal: 30% is 0.3, so 5,000 × 0.3 = 1,500.

As a fraction: 30% is 3/10 (30/100 simplified), so it's 5,000 × 3 ÷ 10 = 1,500.

In your head: 10% of 5,000 is 500, so 30% is 3 × 500 = 1,500.

Adding or taking off 30%: multiply by 1.3 to add it (6,500) or by 0.7 to take it off (3,500).

Working backwards: if 1,500 is 30% of a number, the number is 1,500 ÷ 30 × 100 = 5,000. And if 6,500 is a price with 30% added, the price before was 6,500 ÷ 1.3 = 5,000.
