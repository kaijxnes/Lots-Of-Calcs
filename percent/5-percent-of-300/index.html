What is 5% of 300?

5% of 300 is 15. 300 plus 5% is 315; 300 minus 5% is 285.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 300 × 5 ÷ 100 = 15.

As a decimal: 5% is 0.05, so 300 × 0.05 = 15.

As a fraction: 5% is 1/20 (5/100 simplified), so it's 300 × 1 ÷ 20 = 15.

In your head: 10% of 300 is 30, and 5% is half that: 15.

Adding or taking off 5%: multiply by 1.05 to add it (315) or by 0.95 to take it off (285).

Working backwards: if 15 is 5% of a number, the number is 15 ÷ 5 × 100 = 300. And if 315 is a price with 5% added, the price before was 315 ÷ 1.05 = 300.
