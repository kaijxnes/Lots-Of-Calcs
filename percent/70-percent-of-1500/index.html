What is 70% of 1,500?

70% of 1,500 is 1,050. 1,500 plus 70% is 2,550; 1,500 minus 70% is 450.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 1,500 × 70 ÷ 100 = 1,050.

As a decimal: 70% is 0.7, so 1,500 × 0.7 = 1,050.

In your head: 10% of 1,500 is 150, so 70% is 7 × 150 = 1,050.

Adding or taking off 70%: multiply by 1.7 to add it (2,550) or by 0.3 to take it off (450).
