What is 3% of 800?

3% of 800 is 24. 800 plus 3% is 824; 800 minus 3% is 776.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 800 × 3 ÷ 100 = 24.

As a decimal: 3% is 0.03, so 800 × 0.03 = 24.

As a fraction: 3% is 3/100, so it's 800 × 3 ÷ 100 = 24.

In your head: 1% of 800 is 8, so 3% is 3 × 8 = 24.

Adding or taking off 3%: multiply by 1.03 to add it (824) or by 0.97 to take it off (776).

Working backwards: if 24 is 3% of a number, the number is 24 ÷ 3 × 100 = 800. And if 824 is a price with 3% added, the price before was 824 ÷ 1.03 = 800.
