What is 50% of 80?

50% of 80 is 40. 80 plus 50% is 120; 80 minus 50% is 40.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 80 × 50 ÷ 100 = 40.

As a decimal: 50% is 0.5, so 80 × 0.5 = 40.

As a fraction: 50% is 1/2 (50/100 simplified), so it's 80 × 1 ÷ 2 = 40.

In your head: 50% is half, so halve 80: 40.

Adding or taking off 50%: multiply by 1.5 to add it (120) or by 0.5 to take it off (40).

Working backwards: if 40 is 50% of a number, the number is 40 ÷ 50 × 100 = 80. And if 120 is a price with 50% added, the price before was 120 ÷ 1.5 = 80.
