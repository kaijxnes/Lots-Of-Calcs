What is 75% of 400?

75% of 400 is 300. 400 plus 75% is 700; 400 minus 75% is 100.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 400 × 75 ÷ 100 = 300.

As a decimal: 75% is 0.75, so 400 × 0.75 = 300.

As a fraction: 75% is 3/4 (75/100 simplified), so it's 400 × 3 ÷ 4 = 300.

In your head: 75% is three quarters: a quarter of 400 is 100, and three of those make 300.

Adding or taking off 75%: multiply by 1.75 to add it (700) or by 0.25 to take it off (100).

Working backwards: if 300 is 75% of a number, the number is 300 ÷ 75 × 100 = 400. And if 700 is a price with 75% added, the price before was 700 ÷ 1.75 = 400.
