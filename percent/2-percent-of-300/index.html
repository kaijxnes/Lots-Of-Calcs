What is 2% of 300?

2% of 300 is 6. 300 plus 2% is 306; 300 minus 2% is 294.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 300 × 2 ÷ 100 = 6.

As a decimal: 2% is 0.02, so 300 × 0.02 = 6.

As a fraction: 2% is 1/50 (2/100 simplified), so it's 300 × 1 ÷ 50 = 6.

In your head: 1% of 300 is 3, so 2% is 2 × 3 = 6.

Adding or taking off 2%: multiply by 1.02 to add it (306) or by 0.98 to take it off (294).

Working backwards: if 6 is 2% of a number, the number is 6 ÷ 2 × 100 = 300. And if 306 is a price with 2% added, the price before was 306 ÷ 1.02 = 300.
