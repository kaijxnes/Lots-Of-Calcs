What is 90% of 400?

90% of 400 is 360. 400 plus 90% is 760; 400 minus 90% is 40.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 400 × 90 ÷ 100 = 360.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 400 × 0.9 = 360.

As a fraction: 90% is 9/10 (90/100 simplified), so it's 400 × 9 ÷ 10 = 360.

In your head: 10% of 400 is 40, so 90% is 9 × 40 = 360.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (760) or by 0.1 to take it off (40).

Working backwards: if 360 is 90% of a number, the number is 360 ÷ 90 × 100 = 400. And if 760 is a price with 90% added, the price before was 760 ÷ 1.9 = 400.
