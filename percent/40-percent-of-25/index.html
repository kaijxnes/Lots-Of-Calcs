What is 40% of 25?

40% of 25 is 10. 25 plus 40% is 35; 25 minus 40% is 15.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 25 × 40 ÷ 100 = 10.

As a decimal: 40% is 0.4, so 25 × 0.4 = 10.

As a fraction: 40% is 2/5 (40/100 simplified), so it's 25 × 2 ÷ 5 = 10.

In your head: 10% of 25 is 2.5, so 40% is 4 × 2.5 = 10.

Adding or taking off 40%: multiply by 1.4 to add it (35) or by 0.6 to take it off (15).

Working backwards: if 10 is 40% of a number, the number is 10 ÷ 40 × 100 = 25. And if 35 is a price with 40% added, the price before was 35 ÷ 1.4 = 25.
