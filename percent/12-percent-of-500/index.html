What is 12% of 500?

12% of 500 is 60. 500 plus 12% is 560; 500 minus 12% is 440.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 500 × 12 ÷ 100 = 60.

As a decimal: 12% is 0.12, so 500 × 0.12 = 60.

As a fraction: 12% is 3/25 (12/100 simplified), so it's 500 × 3 ÷ 25 = 60.

In your head: 1% of 500 is 5, so 12% is 12 × 5 = 60.

Adding or taking off 12%: multiply by 1.12 to add it (560) or by 0.88 to take it off (440).

Working backwards: if 60 is 12% of a number, the number is 60 ÷ 12 × 100 = 500. And if 560 is a price with 12% added, the price before was 560 ÷ 1.12 = 500.

Frequently asked questions

What is 12% of 500?

60. Multiply 500 by 12 and divide by 100, or multiply by 0.12.

What is 500 plus 12%?

560: 500 + 60, or 500 × 1.12.

What is 500 minus 12%?

440: 500 − 60, or 500 × 0.88. That's the price after a 12% discount.

How do I work out 12% of 500 in my head?

1% of 500 is 5, so 12% is 12 × 5 = 60.

60 is 12% of what number?

500. To work backwards from a percentage, divide by the percentage and multiply by 100: 60 ÷ 12 × 100 = 500.
