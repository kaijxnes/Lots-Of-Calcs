What is 90% of 100?

90% of 100 is 90. 100 plus 90% is 190; 100 minus 90% is 10.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 100 × 90 ÷ 100 = 90.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 100 × 0.9 = 90.

In your head: 10% of 100 is 10, so 90% is 9 × 10 = 90.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (190) or by 0.1 to take it off (10).
