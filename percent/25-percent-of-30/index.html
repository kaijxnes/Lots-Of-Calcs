What is 25% of 30?

25% of 30 is 7.5. 30 plus 25% is 37.5; 30 minus 25% is 22.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 30 × 25 ÷ 100 = 7.5.

As a decimal: 25% is 0.25, so 30 × 0.25 = 7.5.

In your head: 25% is a quarter, so divide 30 by 4: 7.5.

Adding or taking off 25%: multiply by 1.25 to add it (37.5) or by 0.75 to take it off (22.5).
