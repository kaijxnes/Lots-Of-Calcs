What is 75% of 300?

75% of 300 is 225. 300 plus 75% is 525; 300 minus 75% is 75.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 300 × 75 ÷ 100 = 225.

As a decimal: 75% is 0.75, so 300 × 0.75 = 225.

In your head: 75% is three quarters: a quarter of 300 is 75, and three of those make 225.

Adding or taking off 75%: multiply by 1.75 to add it (525) or by 0.25 to take it off (75).
