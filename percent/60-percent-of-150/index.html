What is 60% of 150?

60% of 150 is 90. 150 plus 60% is 240; 150 minus 60% is 60.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 150 × 60 ÷ 100 = 90.

As a decimal: 60% is 0.6, so 150 × 0.6 = 90.

As a fraction: 60% is 3/5 (60/100 simplified), so it's 150 × 3 ÷ 5 = 90.

In your head: 10% of 150 is 15, so 60% is 6 × 15 = 90.

Adding or taking off 60%: multiply by 1.6 to add it (240) or by 0.4 to take it off (60).

Working backwards: if 90 is 60% of a number, the number is 90 ÷ 60 × 100 = 150. And if 240 is a price with 60% added, the price before was 240 ÷ 1.6 = 150.
