What is 80% of 100?

80% of 100 is 80. 100 plus 80% is 180; 100 minus 80% is 20.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 100 × 80 ÷ 100 = 80.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 100 × 0.8 = 80.

As a fraction: 80% is 4/5 (80/100 simplified), so it's 100 × 4 ÷ 5 = 80.

In your head: 10% of 100 is 10, so 80% is 8 × 10 = 80.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (180) or by 0.2 to take it off (20).

Working backwards: if 80 is 80% of a number, the number is 80 ÷ 80 × 100 = 100. And if 180 is a price with 80% added, the price before was 180 ÷ 1.8 = 100.

Frequently asked questions

What is 80% of 100?

80. Multiply 100 by 80 and divide by 100, or multiply by 0.8.

What is 100 plus 80%?

180: 100 + 80, or 100 × 1.8.

What is 100 minus 80%?

20: 100 − 80, or 100 × 0.2. That's the price after a 80% discount.

How do I work out 80% of 100 in my head?

10% of 100 is 10, so 80% is 8 × 10 = 80.

80 is 80% of what number?

100. To work backwards from a percentage, divide by the percentage and multiply by 100: 80 ÷ 80 × 100 = 100.

What percentage of 100 is 80?

80%: 80 ÷ 100 × 100 = 80.
