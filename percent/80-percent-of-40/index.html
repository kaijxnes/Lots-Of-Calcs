What is 80% of 40?

80% of 40 is 32. 40 plus 80% is 72; 40 minus 80% is 8.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 40 × 80 ÷ 100 = 32.

As a decimal: 80% is 0.8, so 40 × 0.8 = 32.

As a fraction: 80% is 4/5 (80/100 simplified), so it's 40 × 4 ÷ 5 = 32.

In your head: 10% of 40 is 4, so 80% is 8 × 4 = 32.

Adding or taking off 80%: multiply by 1.8 to add it (72) or by 0.2 to take it off (8).

Working backwards: if 32 is 80% of a number, the number is 32 ÷ 80 × 100 = 40. And if 72 is a price with 80% added, the price before was 72 ÷ 1.8 = 40.
