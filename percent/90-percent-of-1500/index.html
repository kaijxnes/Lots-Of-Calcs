What is 90% of 1,500?

90% of 1,500 is 1,350. 1,500 plus 90% is 2,850; 1,500 minus 90% is 150.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 1,500 × 90 ÷ 100 = 1,350.

As a decimal: 90% is 0.9, so 1,500 × 0.9 = 1,350.

As a fraction: 90% is 9/10 (90/100 simplified), so it's 1,500 × 9 ÷ 10 = 1,350.

In your head: 10% of 1,500 is 150, so 90% is 9 × 150 = 1,350.

Adding or taking off 90%: multiply by 1.9 to add it (2,850) or by 0.1 to take it off (150).

Working backwards: if 1,350 is 90% of a number, the number is 1,350 ÷ 90 × 100 = 1,500. And if 2,850 is a price with 90% added, the price before was 2,850 ÷ 1.9 = 1,500.
