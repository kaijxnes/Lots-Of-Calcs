What is 20% of 25?

20% of 25 is 5. 25 plus 20% is 30; 25 minus 20% is 20.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 25 × 20 ÷ 100 = 5.

As a decimal: 20% is 0.2, so 25 × 0.2 = 5.

In your head: 10% of 25 is 2.5, so 20% is 2 × 2.5 = 5.

Adding or taking off 20%: multiply by 1.2 to add it (30) or by 0.8 to take it off (20).
