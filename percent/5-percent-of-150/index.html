What is 5% of 150?

5% of 150 is 7.5. 150 plus 5% is 157.5; 150 minus 5% is 142.5.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 150 × 5 ÷ 100 = 7.5.

As a decimal: 5% is 0.05, so 150 × 0.05 = 7.5.

As a fraction: 5% is 1/20 (5/100 simplified), so it's 150 × 1 ÷ 20 = 7.5.

In your head: 10% of 150 is 15, and 5% is half that: 7.5.

Adding or taking off 5%: multiply by 1.05 to add it (157.5) or by 0.95 to take it off (142.5).

Working backwards: if 7.5 is 5% of a number, the number is 7.5 ÷ 5 × 100 = 150. And if 157.5 is a price with 5% added, the price before was 157.5 ÷ 1.05 = 150.
