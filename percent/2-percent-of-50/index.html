What is 2% of 50?

2% of 50 is 1. 50 plus 2% is 51; 50 minus 2% is 49.

How to work it out

The formula: multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. 50 × 2 ÷ 100 = 1.

As a decimal: 2% is 0.02, so 50 × 0.02 = 1.

As a fraction: 2% is 1/50 (2/100 simplified), so it's 50 × 1 ÷ 50 = 1.

In your head: 1% of 50 is 0.5, so 2% is 2 × 0.5 = 1.

Adding or taking off 2%: multiply by 1.02 to add it (51) or by 0.98 to take it off (49).

Working backwards: if 1 is 2% of a number, the number is 1 ÷ 2 × 100 = 50. And if 51 is a price with 2% added, the price before was 51 ÷ 1.02 = 50.
