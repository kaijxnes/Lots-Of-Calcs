Interest is charged on tax paid late, at HMRC's late payment rate: 8% a year from 24 November 2026 (Bank of England base rate plus 4% (from 6 April 2025)). The calculator works it out day by day at today's rate; HMRC changes the rate when the base rate moves.

For a 2025/26 return and bill due on 31 January 2027, the daily penalties start on 1 May 2027, the 6-month penalty after 31 July 2027 and the 12-month one after 31 January 2028. To see what's due on time, use the payments on account calculator.

Frequently asked questions

What is the penalty for filing a Self Assessment return late?

£100 straight away. After 3 months, £10 a day up to £900; after 6 months, another 5% of the tax due or £300, whichever is greater; after 12 months, another 5% or £300.

What is the penalty for paying late?

5% of the tax unpaid at 30 days, again at 6 months and again at 12 months, plus interest on the amount owed.

What interest does HMRC charge on late tax?

The late payment interest rate: 8% a year from 24 November 2026. It's the Bank of England base rate plus 4%, so it changes when the base rate does.

How much is the penalty on £2,000 paid and filed 4 months late?

By 31 May 2027, a return and £2,000 of tax both unpaid since 31 January cost £410.00 in late filing penalties (£100 plus 31 days at £10), £100.00 in late payment penalties and about £52.60 interest: £562.60 in all.

Can I appeal a Self Assessment penalty?

Yes, if you have a reasonable excuse for filing or paying late. Penalties are otherwise due within 30 days of the date on the penalty notice.
