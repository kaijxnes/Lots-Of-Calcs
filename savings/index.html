Common questions

Do these include interest?

Yes, where it matters: the savings goal, emergency fund, 52-week challenge and compound interest calculators all take an interest rate and work out what it adds. Rates you see are examples, not offers.

How is compound interest calculated?

Each period's interest is added to the balance before the next period is worked out, so growth compounds: A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt). The compound interest calculator shows it building year by year.

What should I save for first?

Most people start with an emergency fund of three to six months of essential spending, then save for goals with dates, then invest for the longer term.
