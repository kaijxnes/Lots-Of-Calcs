350°F in Celsius

350°F is 177°C — listed in recipes as 180°C, 160°C fan or gas mark 4.

Conventional oven177°C
Fan oven157°C

Recipe chart equivalentGas 4 · 180°C · 160°C fan · 350°F (moderate)

A fan moves the hot air around, so recipes give fan temperatures about 20°C lower. Recipe charts round Fahrenheit to the nearest 25°F, so 180°C is listed as 350°F although it is exactly 356°F.

Frequently asked questions

What is 350°F in Celsius?

350°F is 177°C ((350 − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 = 176.7). Recipe charts list 350°F as 180°C, gas mark 4.

What is 350°F in a fan oven?

About 160°C fan.

What gas mark is 350°F?

Gas mark 4 — moderate.

Sources: Which?, Instant Pot UK, Liana's Kitchen. Ovens vary, so check your oven's manual — and check food is piping hot all the way through before serving.
