Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1972?

In 2026 you are 54 if your birthday has already been this year, or 53 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1972?

Generation X. Born 1965 to 1980, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1972 a leap year?

Yes — 1972 had a 29 February.

How do you write 1972 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen seventy-two"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and seventy-two. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXXII.
