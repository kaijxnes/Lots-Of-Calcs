Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1997?

In 2026 you are 29 if your birthday has already been this year, or 28 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1997?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 1997 a leap year?

No. 1997 had 365 days.

How do you write 1997 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen ninety-seven"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and ninety-seven. In Roman numerals it's MCMXCVII.
