Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2002?

In 2026 you are 24 if your birthday has already been this year, or 23 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2002?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 2002 a leap year?

No. 2002 had 365 days.

How do you write 2002 in words?

As a year it's said "two thousand and two"; in full, two thousand and two. In Roman numerals it's MMII.
