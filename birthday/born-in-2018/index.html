Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2018?

In 2026 you are 8 if your birthday has already been this year, or 7 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2018?

Generation Alpha. The generation after Gen Z, usually taken to start in the early 2010s.

Was 2018 a leap year?

No. 2018 had 365 days.

How do you write 2018 in words?

As a year it's said "twenty eighteen"; in full, two thousand and eighteen. In Roman numerals it's MMXVIII.
