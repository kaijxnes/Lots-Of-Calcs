Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 1959?

71 once your birthday has passed in 2030, or 70 before it. In 2027 you'll be 68 after your birthday.

How old am I if I was born in 1959?

In 2026 you are 67 if your birthday has already been this year, or 66 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1959?

The Baby Boomers. The post-war birth surge from 1946 to 1964.

Was 1959 a leap year?

No. 1959 had 365 days.

How do you write 1959 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen fifty-nine"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and fifty-nine. In Roman numerals it's MCMLIX.
