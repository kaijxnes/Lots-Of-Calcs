Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 1942?

88 once your birthday has passed in 2030, or 87 before it. In 2027 you'll be 85 after your birthday.

How old am I if I was born in 1942?

In 2026 you are 84 if your birthday has already been this year, or 83 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1942?

The Silent Generation. Born before the post-war baby boom.

Was 1942 a leap year?

No. 1942 had 365 days.

How do you write 1942 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen forty-two"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and forty-two. In Roman numerals it's MCMXLII.
