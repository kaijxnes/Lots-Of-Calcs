Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2003?

In 2026 you are 23 if your birthday has already been this year, or 22 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2003?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 2003 a leap year?

No. 2003 had 365 days.

How do you write 2003 in words?

As a year it's said "two thousand and three"; in full, two thousand and three. In Roman numerals it's MMIII.
