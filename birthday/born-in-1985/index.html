Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1985?

In 2026 you are 41 if your birthday has already been this year, or 40 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1985?

The Millennials (Generation Y). Born 1981 to 1996, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1985 a leap year?

No. 1985 had 365 days.

How do you write 1985 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen eighty-five"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and eighty-five. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXXXV.
