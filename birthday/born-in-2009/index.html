Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2009?

In 2026 you are 17 if your birthday has already been this year, or 16 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2009?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 2009 a leap year?

No. 2009 had 365 days.

How do you write 2009 in words?

As a year it's said "two thousand and nine"; in full, two thousand and nine. In Roman numerals it's MMIX.
