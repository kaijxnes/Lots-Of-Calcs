Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 1964?

66 once your birthday has passed in 2030, or 65 before it. In 2027 you'll be 63 after your birthday.

How old am I if I was born in 1964?

In 2026 you are 62 if your birthday has already been this year, or 61 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1964?

The Baby Boomers. The post-war birth surge from 1946 to 1964.

Was 1964 a leap year?

Yes — 1964 had a 29 February.

How do you write 1964 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen sixty-four"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-four. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXIV.
