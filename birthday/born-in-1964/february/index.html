Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born on 14 February 1964?

On 26 November 2026 you are 62 years, 9 months, 12 days old, and you have lived 22,931 days. You were born on a Friday.

What day of the week was 1 February 1964?

Saturday. February 1964 ended on a Saturday, 29 February.

How old is someone born in February 1964?

On 26 November 2026, between 62 years, 8 months, 28 days (born 29 February) and 62 years, 9 months, 25 days (born 1 February). Find your date in the table for your exact age.

When does someone born on 29 February 1964 celebrate their birthday?

On 29 February in leap years. In other years most people celebrate on 28 February or 1 March; in UK law you come of age on 1 March in a year without a 29 February.
