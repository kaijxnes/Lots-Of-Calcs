Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 2012?

18 once your birthday has passed in 2030, or 17 before it. In 2027 you'll be 15 after your birthday.

How old am I if I was born in 2012?

In 2026 you are 14 if your birthday has already been this year, or 13 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2012?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 2012 a leap year?

Yes — 2012 had a 29 February.

How do you write 2012 in words?

As a year it's said "twenty twelve"; in full, two thousand and twelve. In Roman numerals it's MMXII.
