Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 1999?

31 once your birthday has passed in 2030, or 30 before it. In 2027 you'll be 28 after your birthday.

How old am I if I was born in 1999?

In 2026 you are 27 if your birthday has already been this year, or 26 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1999?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 1999 a leap year?

No. 1999 had 365 days.

How do you write 1999 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen ninety-nine"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and ninety-nine. In Roman numerals it's MCMXCIX.
