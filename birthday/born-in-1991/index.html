Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 1991?

39 once your birthday has passed in 2030, or 38 before it. In 2027 you'll be 36 after your birthday.

How old am I if I was born in 1991?

In 2026 you are 35 if your birthday has already been this year, or 34 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1991?

The Millennials (Generation Y). Born 1981 to 1996, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1991 a leap year?

No. 1991 had 365 days.

How do you write 1991 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen ninety-one"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and ninety-one. In Roman numerals it's MCMXCI.
