Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1970?

In 2026 you are 56 if your birthday has already been this year, or 55 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1970?

Generation X. Born 1965 to 1980, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1970 a leap year?

No. 1970 had 365 days.

How do you write 1970 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen seventy"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and seventy. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXX.
