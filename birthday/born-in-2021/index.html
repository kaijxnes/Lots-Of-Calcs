Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2021?

In 2026 you are 5 if your birthday has already been this year, or 4 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2021?

Generation Alpha. The generation after Gen Z, usually taken to start in the early 2010s.

Was 2021 a leap year?

No. 2021 had 365 days.

How do you write 2021 in words?

As a year it's said "twenty twenty-one"; in full, two thousand and twenty-one. In Roman numerals it's MMXXI.
