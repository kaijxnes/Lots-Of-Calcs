Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1938?

In 2026 you are 88 if your birthday has already been this year, or 87 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1938?

The Silent Generation. Born before the post-war baby boom.

Was 1938 a leap year?

No. 1938 had 365 days.

How do you write 1938 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen thirty-eight"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and thirty-eight. In Roman numerals it's MCMXXXVIII.
