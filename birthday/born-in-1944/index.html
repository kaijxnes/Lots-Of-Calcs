Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1944?

In 2026 you are 82 if your birthday has already been this year, or 81 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1944?

The Silent Generation. Born before the post-war baby boom.

Was 1944 a leap year?

Yes — 1944 had a 29 February.

How do you write 1944 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen forty-four"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and forty-four. In Roman numerals it's MCMXLIV.
