Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1980?

In 2026 you are 46 if your birthday has already been this year, or 45 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1980?

Generation X. Born 1965 to 1980, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1980 a leap year?

Yes — 1980 had a 29 February.

How do you write 1980 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen eighty"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and eighty. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXXX.
