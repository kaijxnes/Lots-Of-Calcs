Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1973?

In 2026 you are 53 if your birthday has already been this year, or 52 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1973?

Generation X. Born 1965 to 1980, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1973 a leap year?

No. 1973 had 365 days.

How do you write 1973 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen seventy-three"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and seventy-three. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXXIII.
