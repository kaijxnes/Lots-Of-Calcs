Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1989?

In 2026 you are 37 if your birthday has already been this year, or 36 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1989?

The Millennials (Generation Y). Born 1981 to 1996, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1989 a leap year?

No. 1989 had 365 days.

How do you write 1989 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen eighty-nine"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and eighty-nine. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXXXIX.
