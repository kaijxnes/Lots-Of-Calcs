Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2008?

In 2026 you are 18 if your birthday has already been this year, or 17 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2008?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 2008 a leap year?

Yes — 2008 had a 29 February.

How do you write 2008 in words?

As a year it's said "two thousand and eight"; in full, two thousand and eight. In Roman numerals it's MMVIII.
