Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2015?

In 2026 you are 11 if your birthday has already been this year, or 10 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2015?

Generation Alpha. The generation after Gen Z, usually taken to start in the early 2010s.

Was 2015 a leap year?

No. 2015 had 365 days.

How do you write 2015 in words?

As a year it's said "twenty fifteen"; in full, two thousand and fifteen. In Roman numerals it's MMXV.
