Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2011?

In 2026 you are 15 if your birthday has already been this year, or 14 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2011?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 2011 a leap year?

No. 2011 had 365 days.

How do you write 2011 in words?

As a year it's said "twenty eleven"; in full, two thousand and eleven. In Roman numerals it's MMXI.
