Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2020?

In 2026 you are 6 if your birthday has already been this year, or 5 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2020?

Generation Alpha. The generation after Gen Z, usually taken to start in the early 2010s.

Was 2020 a leap year?

Yes — 2020 had a 29 February.

How do you write 2020 in words?

As a year it's said "twenty twenty"; in full, two thousand and twenty. In Roman numerals it's MMXX.
