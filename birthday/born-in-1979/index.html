Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1979?

In 2026 you are 47 if your birthday has already been this year, or 46 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1979?

Generation X. Born 1965 to 1980, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1979 a leap year?

No. 1979 had 365 days.

How do you write 1979 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen seventy-nine"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and seventy-nine. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXXIX.
