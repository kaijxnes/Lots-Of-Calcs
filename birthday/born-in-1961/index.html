Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1961?

In 2026 you are 65 if your birthday has already been this year, or 64 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1961?

The Baby Boomers. The post-war birth surge from 1946 to 1964.

Was 1961 a leap year?

No. 1961 had 365 days.

How do you write 1961 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen sixty-one"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-one. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXI.
