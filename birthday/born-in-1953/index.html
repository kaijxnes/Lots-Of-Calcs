Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1953?

In 2026 you are 73 if your birthday has already been this year, or 72 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1953?

The Baby Boomers. The post-war birth surge from 1946 to 1964.

Was 1953 a leap year?

No. 1953 had 365 days.

How do you write 1953 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen fifty-three"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and fifty-three. In Roman numerals it's MCMLIII.
