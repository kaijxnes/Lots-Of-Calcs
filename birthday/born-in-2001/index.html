Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 2001?

In 2026 you are 25 if your birthday has already been this year, or 24 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2001?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 2001 a leap year?

No. 2001 had 365 days.

How do you write 2001 in words?

As a year it's said "two thousand and one"; in full, two thousand and one. In Roman numerals it's MMI.
