Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1949?

In 2026 you are 77 if your birthday has already been this year, or 76 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1949?

The Baby Boomers. The post-war birth surge from 1946 to 1964.

Was 1949 a leap year?

No. 1949 had 365 days.

How do you write 1949 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen forty-nine"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and forty-nine. In Roman numerals it's MCMXLIX.
