Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2030 if I was born in 2024?

6 once your birthday has passed in 2030, or 5 before it. In 2027 you'll be 3 after your birthday.

How old am I if I was born in 2024?

In 2026 you are 2 if your birthday has already been this year, or 1 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 2024?

Generation Alpha. The generation after Gen Z, usually taken to start in the early 2010s.

Was 2024 a leap year?

Yes — 2024 had a 29 February.

How do you write 2024 in words?

As a year it's said "twenty twenty-four"; in full, two thousand and twenty-four. In Roman numerals it's MMXXIV.
