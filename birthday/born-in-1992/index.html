Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1992?

In 2026 you are 34 if your birthday has already been this year, or 33 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1992?

The Millennials (Generation Y). Born 1981 to 1996, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1992 a leap year?

Yes — 1992 had a 29 February.

How do you write 1992 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen ninety-two"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and ninety-two. In Roman numerals it's MCMXCII.
