Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1958?

In 2026 you are 68 if your birthday has already been this year, or 67 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1958?

The Baby Boomers. The post-war birth surge from 1946 to 1964.

Was 1958 a leap year?

No. 1958 had 365 days.

How do you write 1958 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen fifty-eight"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and fifty-eight. In Roman numerals it's MCMLVIII.
