Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1998?

In 2026 you are 28 if your birthday has already been this year, or 27 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1998?

Generation Z. Pew Research Center counts anyone born from 1997 as Gen Z; most researchers end it around 2012.

Was 1998 a leap year?

No. 1998 had 365 days.

How do you write 1998 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen ninety-eight"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and ninety-eight. In Roman numerals it's MCMXCVIII.
