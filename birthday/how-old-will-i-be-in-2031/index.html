Frequently asked questions

How old will I be in 2031 if I was born in 2000?

31 once you've had your birthday in 2031, or 30 before it.

How old will I be in 2031 if I was born in 1990?

41 after your birthday in 2031, or 40 before it.

How old will I be in 2031 if I was born in 1980?

51 after your birthday in 2031, or 50 before it.

How do I work out how old I'll be in 2031?

Take your birth year from 2031: that's your age after your birthday in 2031. Before your birthday you're one year younger. Someone born in 1995 is 36 after their 2031 birthday and 35 before it.

Who turns 18 in 2031?

Anyone born in 2013. People born in 2010 turn 21, and those born in 1966 turn 65.

How many years until 2031?

2031 is 5 years after 2026. It isn't a leap year.
