Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1967?

In 2026 you are 59 if your birthday has already been this year, or 58 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1967?

Generation X. Born 1965 to 1980, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1967 a leap year?

No. 1967 had 365 days.

How do you write 1967 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen sixty-seven"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and sixty-seven. In Roman numerals it's MCMLXVII.
