Frequently asked questions

How old am I if I was born in 1994?

In 2026 you are 32 if your birthday has already been this year, or 31 if it's still to come.

What generation is someone born in 1994?

The Millennials (Generation Y). Born 1981 to 1996, in Pew Research Center's definition.

Was 1994 a leap year?

No. 1994 had 365 days.

How do you write 1994 in words?

As a year it's said "nineteen ninety-four"; in full, one thousand nine hundred and ninety-four. In Roman numerals it's MCMXCIV.
