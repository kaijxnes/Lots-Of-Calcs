Frequently asked questions

How much Maintenance Loan will I get with a household income of £70,000?

In 2026 to 2027: £5,048 living away from home outside London, £7,060 in London, or £4,013 living with your parents, for a full year with no other children or pension contributions taken off.

Does having brothers or sisters increase the loan?

Yes: £1,130 comes off household income for each other child your parents support. With one, the loan outside London is £5,048 instead of £5,048.

Do my parents' pension contributions count?

They're taken off your parents' income, so paying into a pension can increase the loan.

Is the Maintenance Loan paid monthly?

No, it's paid into your bank account at the start of each term. £5,048 a year is about £421 a month if you spread it over 12 months.
