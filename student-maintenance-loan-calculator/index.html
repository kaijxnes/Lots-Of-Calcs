How your Maintenance Loan is worked out

Student Finance England starts from your household's residual income: your parents' income before tax and National Insurance (including a parent's partner), less their pension contributions and £1,130 for each other child who depends on them, plus any income of yours. Up to £25,000 you get the full loan. Above that it's reduced:

Student finance is changing for courses starting on or after 1 January 2027.

Frequently asked questions

How much Maintenance Loan can I get?

In 2026 to 2027, up to £9,118 living with your parents, £10,830 living away from home outside London, and £14,135 in London. With household income over £25,000 you get less, down to £4,013, £5,048 and £7,039.

How is household income worked out for student finance?

It's your parents' income before tax and National Insurance (including a parent's partner), less pension contributions and £1,130 for each other child who depends on them, plus any income of yours.

How much Maintenance Loan with £40,000 household income?

£8,512 living away from home outside London, £11,777 in London and £6,825 living with your parents (2026 to 2027).

At what household income do you get the minimum Maintenance Loan?

About £62,410 living away from home outside London, £70,131 in London and £58,387 living with parents.
