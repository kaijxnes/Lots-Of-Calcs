SSP is £123.25 a week or 80% of your average weekly earnings, whichever is lower, for up to 28 weeks. For 4 weeks off at the full rate that's £493.00 before tax and National Insurance, paid through payroll on your usual payday. That leaves 24 weeks of SSP if you're off again within 8 weeks. Your employer can pay more under a company sick pay scheme, but not less.

Frequently asked questions

How much SSP do I get for 4 weeks off sick?

£493.00 at the full rate of £123.25 a week (2026/27), before tax and National Insurance. If you earn less than £154.06 a week, it's 80% of your average weekly earnings instead.

Do I get sick pay from the first day?

SSP is paid for every full day you're off sick that you'd normally have worked, once you've been ill for at least one full working day. A day you started work and went home sick doesn't count.

Do I need a fit note for 4 weeks off?

Yes: you must give your employer a fit note if you're off work for more than 7 days in a row, including days you don't normally work.

How much SSP is left after 4 weeks?

24 weeks: SSP lasts up to 28 weeks, counted across spells of sickness 8 weeks or less apart.
