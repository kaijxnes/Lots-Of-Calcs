Inheritance tax calculator

Estimate UK inheritance tax on an estate, including the residence nil-rate band and a spouse's unused allowances.

Everything left to a spouse or civil partner is exempt, so this is for an estate passing to others. The residence band shrinks by £1 for every £2 the estate is over £2 million. The 36% rate applies when 10% or more of the estate (after the thresholds) goes to charity — a simplified test. Gifts in the 7 years before death aren't included. 2026/27 figures from GOV.UK (Inheritance Tax), checked 2026-11-21. A guide, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the inheritance tax threshold?

£325,000. It can rise to £500,000 if you leave your home to your children or grandchildren, and a married couple or civil partners can pass on unused allowances — up to £1,000,000 between them.

What is the inheritance tax rate?

40% on the part of the estate above the threshold, or 36% if 10% or more of the estate goes to charity.

Who pays inheritance tax?

It's paid out of the estate by the executors, usually before the estate is shared out, so beneficiaries normally don't pay it directly.
