Frequently asked questions

What does 7/1 (7 to 1) mean in betting?

7/1 means you win £7 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 7/1 makes £70.00 profit and returns £80.00 with your stake. It's said "seven to one".

What is 7/1 in decimal odds?

8.00: divide 7 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 7/1 in American odds?

+700: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 7/1 imply?

12.5% — 1 ÷ (7 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 7/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £107.50; if it only places, £27.50.
