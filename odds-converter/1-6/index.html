Frequently asked questions

What does 1/6 mean in betting?

1/6 means you win £1 for every £6 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 1/6 makes £1.67 profit and returns £11.67 with your stake. It's said "one to six on".

What is 1/6 in decimal odds?

1.17: divide 1 by 6 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 1/6 in American odds?

-600: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 1/6 imply?

85.7% — 6 ÷ (1 + 6). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
