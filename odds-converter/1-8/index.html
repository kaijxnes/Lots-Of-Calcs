Frequently asked questions

What does 1/8 (1 to 8) mean in betting?

1/8 means you win £1 for every £8 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 1/8 makes £1.25 profit and returns £11.25 with your stake. It's said "one to eight on".

What is 1/8 in decimal odds?

1.13: divide 1 by 8 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 1/8 in American odds?

-800: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 1/8 imply?

88.9% — 8 ÷ (1 + 8). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
