Frequently asked questions

What does 5/6 mean in betting?

5/6 means you win £5 for every £6 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 5/6 makes £8.33 profit and returns £18.33 with your stake. It's said "five to six on".

What is 5/6 in decimal odds?

1.83: divide 5 by 6 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 5/6 in American odds?

-120: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 5/6 imply?

54.5% — 6 ÷ (5 + 6). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
