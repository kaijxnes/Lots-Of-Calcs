Frequently asked questions

What does 4/11 mean in betting?

4/11 means you win £4 for every £11 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 4/11 makes £3.64 profit and returns £13.64 with your stake. It's said "four to eleven on".

What is 4/11 in decimal odds?

1.36: divide 4 by 11 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 4/11 in American odds?

-275: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 4/11 imply?

73.3% — 11 ÷ (4 + 11). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
