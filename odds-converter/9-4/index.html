Frequently asked questions

What does 9/4 mean in betting?

9/4 means you win £9 for every £4 you stake. £10 at 9/4 makes £22.50 profit and returns £32.50 with your stake. It's said "nine to four".

What is 9/4 in decimal odds?

3.25: divide 9 by 4 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 9/4 in American odds?

+225: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 9/4 imply?

30.8% — 4 ÷ (9 + 4). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 9/4 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £48.13; if it only places, £15.63.
