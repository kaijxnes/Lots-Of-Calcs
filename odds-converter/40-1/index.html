Frequently asked questions

What does 40/1 mean in betting?

40/1 means you win £40 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 40/1 makes £400.00 profit and returns £410.00 with your stake. It's said "forty to one".

What is 40/1 in decimal odds?

41.00: divide 40 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 40/1 in American odds?

+4000: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 40/1 imply?

2.4% — 1 ÷ (40 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 40/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £520.00; if it only places, £110.00.
