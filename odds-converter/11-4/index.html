Frequently asked questions

What does 11/4 (11 to 4) mean in betting?

11/4 means you win £11 for every £4 you stake. £10 at 11/4 makes £27.50 profit and returns £37.50 with your stake. It's said "eleven to four".

What is 11/4 in decimal odds?

3.75: divide 11 by 4 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 11/4 in American odds?

+275: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 11/4 imply?

26.7% — 4 ÷ (11 + 4). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 11/4 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £54.38; if it only places, £16.88.
