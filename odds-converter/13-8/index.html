Frequently asked questions

What does 13/8 (13 to 8) mean in betting?

13/8 means you win £13 for every £8 you stake. £10 at 13/8 makes £16.25 profit and returns £26.25 with your stake. It's said "thirteen to eight".

What is 13/8 in decimal odds?

2.63: divide 13 by 8 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 13/8 in American odds?

+162.5: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 13/8 imply?

38.1% — 8 ÷ (13 + 8). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 13/8 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £40.31; if it only places, £14.06.
