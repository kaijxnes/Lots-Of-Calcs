Frequently asked questions

What does 50/1 (50 to 1) mean in betting?

50/1 means you win £50 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 50/1 makes £500.00 profit and returns £510.00 with your stake. It's said "fifty to one".

What is 50/1 in decimal odds?

51.00: divide 50 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 50/1 in American odds?

+5000: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 50/1 imply?

2% — 1 ÷ (50 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 50/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £645.00; if it only places, £135.00.
