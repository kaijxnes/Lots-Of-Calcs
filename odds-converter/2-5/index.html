Frequently asked questions

What does 2/5 (2 to 5) mean in betting?

2/5 means you win £2 for every £5 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 2/5 makes £4.00 profit and returns £14.00 with your stake. It's said "two to five on".

What is 2/5 in decimal odds?

1.40: divide 2 by 5 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 2/5 in American odds?

-250: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 2/5 imply?

71.4% — 5 ÷ (2 + 5). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
