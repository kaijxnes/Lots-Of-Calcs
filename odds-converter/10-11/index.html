Frequently asked questions

What does 10/11 mean in betting?

10/11 means you win £10 for every £11 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 10/11 makes £9.09 profit and returns £19.09 with your stake. It's said "ten to eleven on".

What is 10/11 in decimal odds?

1.91: divide 10 by 11 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 10/11 in American odds?

-110: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 10/11 imply?

52.4% — 11 ÷ (10 + 11). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
