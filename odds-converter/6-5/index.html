Frequently asked questions

What does 6/5 (6 to 5) mean in betting?

6/5 means you win £6 for every £5 you stake. £10 at 6/5 makes £12.00 profit and returns £22.00 with your stake. It's said "six to five".

What is 6/5 in decimal odds?

2.20: divide 6 by 5 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 6/5 in American odds?

+120: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 6/5 imply?

45.5% — 5 ÷ (6 + 5). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 6/5 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £35.00; if it only places, £13.00.
