Frequently asked questions

What does 10/3 mean in betting?

10/3 means you win £10 for every £3 you stake. £10 at 10/3 makes £33.33 profit and returns £43.33 with your stake. It's said "ten to three".

What is 10/3 in decimal odds?

4.33: divide 10 by 3 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 10/3 in American odds?

+333.3: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 10/3 imply?

23.1% — 3 ÷ (10 + 3). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 10/3 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £61.67; if it only places, £18.33.
