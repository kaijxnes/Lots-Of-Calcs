Frequently asked questions

What does 33/1 (33 to 1) mean in betting?

33/1 means you win £33 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 33/1 makes £330.00 profit and returns £340.00 with your stake. It's said "thirty-three to one".

What is 33/1 in decimal odds?

34.00: divide 33 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 33/1 in American odds?

+3300: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 33/1 imply?

2.9% — 1 ÷ (33 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 33/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £432.50; if it only places, £92.50.
