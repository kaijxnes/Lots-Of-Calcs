Frequently asked questions

What does 5/4 mean in betting?

5/4 means you win £5 for every £4 you stake. £10 at 5/4 makes £12.50 profit and returns £22.50 with your stake. It's said "five to four".

What is 5/4 in decimal odds?

2.25: divide 5 by 4 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 5/4 in American odds?

+125: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 5/4 imply?

44.4% — 4 ÷ (5 + 4). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 5/4 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £35.63; if it only places, £13.13.
