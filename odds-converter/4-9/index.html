Frequently asked questions

What does 4/9 mean in betting?

4/9 means you win £4 for every £9 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 4/9 makes £4.44 profit and returns £14.44 with your stake. It's said "four to nine on".

What is 4/9 in decimal odds?

1.44: divide 4 by 9 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 4/9 in American odds?

-225: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 4/9 imply?

69.2% — 9 ÷ (4 + 9). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
