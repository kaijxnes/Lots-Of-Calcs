Frequently asked questions

What does 2/9 (2 to 9) mean in betting?

2/9 means you win £2 for every £9 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 2/9 makes £2.22 profit and returns £12.22 with your stake. It's said "two to nine on".

What is 2/9 in decimal odds?

1.22: divide 2 by 9 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 2/9 in American odds?

-450: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 2/9 imply?

81.8% — 9 ÷ (2 + 9). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
