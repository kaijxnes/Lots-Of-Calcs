Frequently asked questions

What does 8/13 (8 to 13) mean in betting?

8/13 means you win £8 for every £13 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 8/13 makes £6.15 profit and returns £16.15 with your stake. It's said "eight to thirteen on".

What is 8/13 in decimal odds?

1.62: divide 8 by 13 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 8/13 in American odds?

-162.5: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 8/13 imply?

61.9% — 13 ÷ (8 + 13). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
