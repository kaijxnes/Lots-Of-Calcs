Frequently asked questions

What does 11/1 (11 to 1) mean in betting?

11/1 means you win £11 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 11/1 makes £110.00 profit and returns £120.00 with your stake. It's said "eleven to one".

What is 11/1 in decimal odds?

12.00: divide 11 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 11/1 in American odds?

+1100: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 11/1 imply?

8.3% — 1 ÷ (11 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 11/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £157.50; if it only places, £37.50.
