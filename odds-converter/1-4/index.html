Frequently asked questions

What does 1/4 (1 to 4) mean in betting?

1/4 means you win £1 for every £4 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 1/4 makes £2.50 profit and returns £12.50 with your stake. It's said "one to four on".

What is 1/4 in decimal odds?

1.25: divide 1 by 4 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 1/4 in American odds?

-400: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 1/4 imply?

80% — 4 ÷ (1 + 4). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
