Frequently asked questions

What does 1/3 (1 to 3) mean in betting?

1/3 means you win £1 for every £3 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 1/3 makes £3.33 profit and returns £13.33 with your stake. It's said "one to three on".

What is 1/3 in decimal odds?

1.33: divide 1 by 3 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 1/3 in American odds?

-300: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 1/3 imply?

75% — 3 ÷ (1 + 3). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
