Frequently asked questions

What does 3/1 (3 to 1) mean in betting?

3/1 means you win £3 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 3/1 makes £30.00 profit and returns £40.00 with your stake. It's said "three to one".

What is 3/1 in decimal odds?

4.00: divide 3 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 3/1 in American odds?

+300: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 3/1 imply?

25% — 1 ÷ (3 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 3/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £57.50; if it only places, £17.50.
