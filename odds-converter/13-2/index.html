Frequently asked questions

What does 13/2 (13 to 2) mean in betting?

13/2 means you win £13 for every £2 you stake. £10 at 13/2 makes £65.00 profit and returns £75.00 with your stake. It's said "thirteen to two".

What is 13/2 in decimal odds?

7.50: divide 13 by 2 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 13/2 in American odds?

+650: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 13/2 imply?

13.3% — 2 ÷ (13 + 2). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 13/2 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £101.25; if it only places, £26.25.
