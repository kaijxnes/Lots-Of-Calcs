Frequently asked questions

What does 8/1 mean in betting?

8/1 means you win £8 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 8/1 makes £80.00 profit and returns £90.00 with your stake. It's said "eight to one".

What is 8/1 in decimal odds?

9.00: divide 8 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 8/1 in American odds?

+800: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 8/1 imply?

11.1% — 1 ÷ (8 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 8/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £120.00; if it only places, £30.00.
