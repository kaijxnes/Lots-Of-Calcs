Frequently asked questions

What does 4/5 mean in betting?

4/5 means you win £4 for every £5 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 4/5 makes £8.00 profit and returns £18.00 with your stake. It's said "four to five on".

What is 4/5 in decimal odds?

1.80: divide 4 by 5 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 4/5 in American odds?

-125: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 4/5 imply?

55.6% — 5 ÷ (4 + 5). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
