Frequently asked questions

What does 4/7 mean in betting?

4/7 means you win £4 for every £7 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 4/7 makes £5.71 profit and returns £15.71 with your stake. It's said "four to seven on".

What is 4/7 in decimal odds?

1.57: divide 4 by 7 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 4/7 in American odds?

-175: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 4/7 imply?

63.6% — 7 ÷ (4 + 7). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
