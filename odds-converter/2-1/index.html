Frequently asked questions

What does 2/1 mean in betting?

2/1 means you win £2 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 2/1 makes £20.00 profit and returns £30.00 with your stake. It's said "two to one".

What is 2/1 in decimal odds?

3.00: divide 2 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 2/1 in American odds?

+200: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 2/1 imply?

33.3% — 1 ÷ (2 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 2/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £45.00; if it only places, £15.00.
