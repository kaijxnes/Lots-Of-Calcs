Frequently asked questions

What does 15/8 (15 to 8) mean in betting?

15/8 means you win £15 for every £8 you stake. £10 at 15/8 makes £18.75 profit and returns £28.75 with your stake. It's said "fifteen to eight".

What is 15/8 in decimal odds?

2.88: divide 15 by 8 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 15/8 in American odds?

+187.5: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 15/8 imply?

34.8% — 8 ÷ (15 + 8). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 15/8 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £43.44; if it only places, £14.69.

18+. Odds are a price, not a prediction — the bookmaker's margin is built in. If gambling stops being fun, BeGambleAware offers free, confidential help.
