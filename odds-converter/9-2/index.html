Frequently asked questions

What does 9/2 mean in betting?

9/2 means you win £9 for every £2 you stake. £10 at 9/2 makes £45.00 profit and returns £55.00 with your stake. It's said "nine to two".

What is 9/2 in decimal odds?

5.50: divide 9 by 2 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 9/2 in American odds?

+450: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 9/2 imply?

18.2% — 2 ÷ (9 + 2). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 9/2 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £76.25; if it only places, £21.25.

18+. Odds are a price, not a prediction — the bookmaker's margin is built in. If gambling stops being fun, BeGambleAware offers free, confidential help.
