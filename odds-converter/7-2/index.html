Frequently asked questions

What does 7/2 (7 to 2) mean in betting?

7/2 means you win £7 for every £2 you stake. £10 at 7/2 makes £35.00 profit and returns £45.00 with your stake. It's said "seven to two".

What is 7/2 in decimal odds?

4.50: divide 7 by 2 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 7/2 in American odds?

+350: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 7/2 imply?

22.2% — 2 ÷ (7 + 2). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 7/2 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £63.75; if it only places, £18.75.
