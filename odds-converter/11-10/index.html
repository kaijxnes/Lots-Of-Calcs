Frequently asked questions

What does 11/10 (11 to 10) mean in betting?

11/10 means you win £11 for every £10 you stake. £10 at 11/10 makes £11.00 profit and returns £21.00 with your stake. It's said "eleven to ten".

What is 11/10 in decimal odds?

2.10: divide 11 by 10 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 11/10 in American odds?

+110: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 11/10 imply?

47.6% — 10 ÷ (11 + 10). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 11/10 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £33.75; if it only places, £12.75.
