Frequently asked questions

What does 8/11 (8 to 11) mean in betting?

8/11 means you win £8 for every £11 you stake — less than your stake, which is what odds-on means. £10 at 8/11 makes £7.27 profit and returns £17.27 with your stake. It's said "eight to eleven on".

What is 8/11 in decimal odds?

1.73: divide 8 by 11 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 8/11 in American odds?

-137.5: the stake needed to win 100 profit.

What chance of winning does 8/11 imply?

57.9% — 11 ÷ (8 + 11). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.
