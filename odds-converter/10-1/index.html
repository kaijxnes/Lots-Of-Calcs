Frequently asked questions

What does 10/1 (10 to 1) mean in betting?

10/1 means you win £10 for every £1 you stake. £10 at 10/1 makes £100.00 profit and returns £110.00 with your stake. It's said "ten to one".

What is 10/1 in decimal odds?

11.00: divide 10 by 1 and add 1 for your stake. Decimal odds are the total return for each £1 staked.

What is 10/1 in American odds?

+1000: the profit on a 100 stake.

What chance of winning does 10/1 imply?

9.1% — 1 ÷ (10 + 1). That's the chance the price corresponds to, including the bookmaker's margin, so the real chance is usually a little lower.

How much does a £10 each-way bet at 10/1 pay?

£20 in total (£10 to win, £10 to place). If it wins at 1/4 place terms it returns £145.00; if it only places, £35.00.
