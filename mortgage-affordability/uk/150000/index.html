Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £150,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £600,000 to £675,000 on £150,000. Some go to 5 times (£750,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £150,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £675,000 buys a home of about £750,000; with a 15% deposit, about £794,118. Stamp duty on £750,000 in England would be £27,500, or £27,500 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£3,752 a month on £675,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 49% of the £7,607 a month a £150,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £4,988.

What if I'm buying with someone?
