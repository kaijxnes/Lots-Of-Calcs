Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £40,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £160,000 to £180,000 on £40,000. Some go to 5 times (£200,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £40,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £180,000 buys a home of about £200,000; with a 15% deposit, about £211,765. Stamp duty on £200,000 in England would be £1,500, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,000 a month on £180,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 37% of the £2,693 a month a £40,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,330.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £40,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £360,000.
