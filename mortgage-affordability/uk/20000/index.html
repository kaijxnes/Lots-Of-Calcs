Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £20,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £80,000 to £90,000 on £20,000. Some go to 5 times (£100,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £20,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £90,000 buys a home of about £100,000; with a 15% deposit, about £105,882. Stamp duty on £100,000 in England would be £0, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£500 a month on £90,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 33% of the £1,493 a month a £20,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £665.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £20,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £180,000.
