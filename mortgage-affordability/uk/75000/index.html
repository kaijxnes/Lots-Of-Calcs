Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £75,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £300,000 to £337,500 on £75,000. Some go to 5 times (£375,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £75,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £337,500 buys a home of about £375,000; with a 15% deposit, about £397,059. Stamp duty on £375,000 in England would be £8,750, or £3,750 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,876 a month on £337,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 42% of the £4,505 a month a £75,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £2,494.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £75,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £675,000.
