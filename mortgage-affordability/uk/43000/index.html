Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £43,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £172,000 to £193,500 on £43,000. Some go to 5 times (£215,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £43,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £193,500 buys a home of about £215,000; with a 15% deposit, about £227,647. Stamp duty on £215,000 in England would be £1,800, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,076 a month on £193,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 37% of the £2,873 a month a £43,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,430.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £43,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £387,000.
