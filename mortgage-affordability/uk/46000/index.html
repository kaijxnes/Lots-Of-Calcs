Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £46,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £184,000 to £207,000 on £46,000. Some go to 5 times (£230,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £46,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £207,000 buys a home of about £230,000; with a 15% deposit, about £243,529. Stamp duty on £230,000 in England would be £2,100, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,151 a month on £207,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 38% of the £3,053 a month a £46,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,530.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £46,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £414,000.
