Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £57,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £228,000 to £256,500 on £57,000. Some go to 5 times (£285,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £57,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £256,500 buys a home of about £285,000; with a 15% deposit, about £301,765. Stamp duty on £285,000 in England would be £4,250, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,426 a month on £256,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 39% of the £3,635 a month a £57,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,896.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £57,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £513,000.
