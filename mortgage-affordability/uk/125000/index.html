Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £125,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £500,000 to £562,500 on £125,000. Some go to 5 times (£625,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £125,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £562,500 buys a home of about £625,000; with a 15% deposit, about £661,765. Stamp duty on £625,000 in England would be £21,250, or £21,250 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£3,127 a month on £562,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 48% of the £6,505 a month a £125,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £4,157.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £125,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £1,125,000.
