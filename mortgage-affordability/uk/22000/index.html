Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £22,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £88,000 to £99,000 on £22,000. Some go to 5 times (£110,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £22,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £99,000 buys a home of about £110,000; with a 15% deposit, about £116,471. Stamp duty on £110,000 in England would be £0, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£550 a month on £99,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 34% of the £1,613 a month a £22,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £732.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £22,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £198,000.
