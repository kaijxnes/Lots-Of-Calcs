Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £38,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £152,000 to £171,000 on £38,000. Some go to 5 times (£190,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £38,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £171,000 buys a home of about £190,000; with a 15% deposit, about £201,176. Stamp duty on £190,000 in England would be £1,300, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£950 a month on £171,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 37% of the £2,573 a month a £38,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,264.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £38,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £342,000.
