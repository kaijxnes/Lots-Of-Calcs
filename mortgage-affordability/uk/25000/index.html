Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £25,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £100,000 to £112,500 on £25,000. Some go to 5 times (£125,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £25,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £112,500 buys a home of about £125,000; with a 15% deposit, about £132,353. Stamp duty on £125,000 in England would be £0, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£625 a month on £112,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 35% of the £1,793 a month a £25,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £831.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £25,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £225,000.
