Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £41,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £164,000 to £184,500 on £41,000. Some go to 5 times (£205,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £41,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £184,500 buys a home of about £205,000; with a 15% deposit, about £217,059. Stamp duty on £205,000 in England would be £1,600, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,026 a month on £184,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 37% of the £2,753 a month a £41,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,363.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £41,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £369,000.
