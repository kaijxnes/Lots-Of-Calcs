Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £48,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £192,000 to £216,000 on £48,000. Some go to 5 times (£240,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £48,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £216,000 buys a home of about £240,000; with a 15% deposit, about £254,118. Stamp duty on £240,000 in England would be £2,300, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,201 a month on £216,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 38% of the £3,173 a month a £48,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,596.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £48,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £432,000.
