Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £49,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £196,000 to £220,500 on £49,000. Some go to 5 times (£245,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £49,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £220,500 buys a home of about £245,000; with a 15% deposit, about £259,412. Stamp duty on £245,000 in England would be £2,400, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£1,226 a month on £220,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 38% of the £3,233 a month a £49,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £1,629.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £49,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £441,000.
