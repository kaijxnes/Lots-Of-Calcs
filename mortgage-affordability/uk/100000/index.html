Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £100,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £400,000 to £450,000 on £100,000. Some go to 5 times (£500,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £100,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £450,000 buys a home of about £500,000; with a 15% deposit, about £529,412. Stamp duty on £500,000 in England would be £15,000, or £10,000 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£2,501 a month on £450,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 44% of the £5,713 a month a £100,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £3,325.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £100,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £900,000.
