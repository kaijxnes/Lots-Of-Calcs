Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £21,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £84,000 to £94,500 on £21,000. Some go to 5 times (£105,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £21,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £94,500 buys a home of about £105,000; with a 15% deposit, about £111,176. Stamp duty on £105,000 in England would be £0, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£525 a month on £94,500 at 4.5% over 25 years — 34% of the £1,553 a month a £21,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £698.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £21,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £189,000.
