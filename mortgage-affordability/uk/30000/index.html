Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on £30,000?

Most UK lenders lend around 4 to 4.5 times your income, so about £120,000 to £135,000 on £30,000. Some go to 5 times (£150,000) or more for higher earners. Each lender also checks your spending, debts and credit record, so the real figure can be lower.

What could I afford on £30,000 with a deposit?

With a 10% deposit, borrowing £135,000 buys a home of about £150,000; with a 15% deposit, about £158,824. Stamp duty on £150,000 in England would be £500, or £0 for a first-time buyer.

What would the mortgage cost a month?

£750 a month on £135,000 at 4.5% over 25 years — 36% of the £2,093 a month a £30,000 salary leaves after tax and National Insurance. If rates rose 3 points it would be £998.

What if I'm buying with someone?

Lenders add your incomes: with a partner also earning £30,000, 4.5 times your joint income is £270,000.
