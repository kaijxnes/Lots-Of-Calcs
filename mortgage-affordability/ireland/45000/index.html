How much can I borrow on €45,000 in Ireland?

Up to €180,000 for a first-time buyer, €157,500 for a mover, under the Central Bank's rules.

Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on €45,000 in Ireland?

Up to €180,000 as a first-time buyer — 4 times your gross income, the Central Bank of Ireland's limit — or €157,500 (3.5 times) if you're a second or subsequent buyer. Lenders can go above the limits for up to 15% of their lending, and every lender also checks you can afford the repayments.

How much deposit do I need?

At least 10% for first-time and second buyers (30% for buy-to-let). With a 10% deposit, borrowing €180,000 buys a home of up to about €200,000, so the deposit is about €20,000.

What would the repayments be?

€859 a month on €180,000 at an example 4% over 30 years — 28% of the €3,084 a month that €45,000 leaves after income tax, USC and PRSI. On €157,500 it would be €752.

Buying with someone?

The limit applies to your combined gross income: with a partner also on €45,000, first-time buyers could borrow up to €360,000.
