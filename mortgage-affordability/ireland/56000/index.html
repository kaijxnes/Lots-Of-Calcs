Frequently asked questions

How much can I borrow on €56,000 in Ireland?

Up to €224,000 as a first-time buyer — 4 times your gross income, the Central Bank of Ireland's limit — or €196,000 (3.5 times) if you're a second or subsequent buyer. Lenders can go above the limits for up to 15% of their lending, and every lender also checks you can afford the repayments.

How much deposit do I need?

At least 10% for first-time and second buyers (30% for buy-to-let). With a 10% deposit, borrowing €224,000 buys a home of up to about €248,889, so the deposit is about €24,889.

What would the repayments be?

€1,069 a month on €224,000 at an example 4% over 30 years — 30% of the €3,568 a month that €56,000 leaves after income tax, USC and PRSI. On €196,000 it would be €936.

Buying with someone?

The limit applies to your combined gross income: with a partner also on €56,000, first-time buyers could borrow up to €448,000.
