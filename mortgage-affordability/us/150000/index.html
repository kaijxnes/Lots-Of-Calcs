Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford on $150,000 a year?

About $560,837 with 20% down ($112,167), using the 28/36 rule: housing costs up to 28% of your $12,500 gross monthly income, $3,500 a month. With 10% down it's about $507,170, though that usually means paying mortgage insurance too.

What would the monthly payment be?

$3,500 a month: $2,836 principal and interest on $448,670 at 6.5% over 30 years, plus about $514 property tax and $150 insurance.

How do other debts change it?

The 36% part of the rule caps all your debt payments, so car loans, student loans and card minimums reduce what you can spend on housing: $600 a month of other debts brings the price down to about $560,837.
