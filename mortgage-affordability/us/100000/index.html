Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford on $100,000 a year?

About $365,521 with 20% down ($73,104), using the 28/36 rule: housing costs up to 28% of your $8,333 gross monthly income, $2,333 a month. With 10% down it's about $330,544, though that usually means paying mortgage insurance too.

What would the monthly payment be?

$2,333 a month: $1,848 principal and interest on $292,417 at 6.5% over 30 years, plus about $335 property tax and $150 insurance.

How do other debts change it?

The 36% part of the rule caps all your debt payments, so car loans, student loans and card minimums reduce what you can spend on housing: $600 a month of other debts brings the price down to about $365,521.
