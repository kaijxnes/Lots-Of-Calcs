Frequently asked questions

How much house can I afford on $65,000 a year?

About $228,799 with 20% down ($45,760), using the 28/36 rule: housing costs up to 28% of your $5,417 gross monthly income, $1,517 a month. With 10% down it's about $206,905, though that usually means paying mortgage insurance too.

What would the monthly payment be?

$1,517 a month: $1,157 principal and interest on $183,039 at 6.5% over 30 years, plus about $210 property tax and $150 insurance.

How do other debts change it?

The 36% part of the rule caps all your debt payments, so car loans, student loans and card minimums reduce what you can spend on housing: $600 a month of other debts brings the price down to about $200,897.
