Frequently asked questions

How many 400×400 tiles in a square metre?

6.25 tiles: one square metre is 1,000,000 mm², and each tile covers 400 × 400 = 160,000 mm², so 1,000,000 ÷ 160,000 = 6.25.

How many 400×400 tiles for 10 square metres?

62.5 tiles before any extra — 69 with 10% added for cuts and breakages.

Does grout change the number?

Slightly — grout lines mean each tile covers a little more than its own size, so you need a few fewer. The figures here ignore them, which leaves a small margin.
