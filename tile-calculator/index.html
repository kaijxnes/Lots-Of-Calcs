Frequently asked questions

How do I work out how many tiles I need?

Work out the area in square metres (length × width), multiply by the tiles per square metre for your tile size, then add extra for cuts and breakages. 10 m² of 600 × 600 tiles is 27.8 tiles, so 31 with 10% extra.

How many tiles are in a square metre?

1,000,000 ÷ (tile width × length in mm). For 300 × 300 tiles that's 11.11; for 600 × 600, 2.78; for 300 × 600, 5.56.

How much extra should I buy?

Enough for cuts, breakages and a few spares in case one needs replacing later; more for diagonal layouts or rooms with lots of corners. The calculator starts at 10%, which you can change.

How many boxes do I need?

Divide the tiles needed by the number in a box (it's on the box) and round up. Enter it in the calculator.
