Frequently asked questions

What is IR35?

The UK off-payroll working rules. If you'd be an employee were it not for your limited company, you're 'inside IR35' and taxed much like an employee. For medium and large clients, and all public sector clients, the client decides your status; for small private sector clients, your own company decides.

Why is take-home lower inside IR35?

Inside IR35 the whole rate is treated as employment income: employer National Insurance (15%) comes out of it, then income tax and employee NI. Outside IR35 you can pay yourself mostly in dividends, which carry no National Insurance.

How many days a year do contractors work?

Often around 220 to 230 once holidays, bank holidays and gaps between contracts are allowed for — use your own figure; it makes a big difference.
