Common questions

Is the borrowing figure what a lender will offer?

It's a guide based on typical income multiples and affordability rules. Lenders also look at your spending, credit history, deposit and the property, so the real offer can be higher or lower.

What's the difference between APR and the interest rate?

The interest rate is what the debt costs a year; APR adds compulsory fees spread over the term, which makes deals with different fees comparable.

Does overpaying a mortgage save much?

Often a lot: every overpayment cuts the balance that interest is charged on for the rest of the term. The overpayment calculator compares it with investing the same money instead.
