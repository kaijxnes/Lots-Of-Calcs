Frequently asked questions

What is £30 plus VAT?

£36.00 at the standard 20% rate: £30 × 1.2. The VAT is £6.00. At the reduced 5% rate it's £31.50.

How much VAT is in £30?

If £30 includes VAT at 20%, the VAT is £5.00 and the price before VAT is £25.00: divide by 1.2 to take VAT off. At 5% the VAT is £1.43.

Why isn't the VAT in a price just 20% of it?

Because the 20% is added to the price before VAT, so it's a sixth (20/120) of the VAT-inclusive total. Taking 20% off £30 would give £24.00, which is £1.00 too little.

Which VAT rate applies?

The standard rate of 20% covers most goods and services; the reduced rate of 5% applies to some, such as home energy and children's car seats; and zero-rated items, such as most food and children's clothes, have none (GOV.UK).
