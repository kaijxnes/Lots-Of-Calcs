Frequently asked questions

What is £1 plus VAT?

£1.20 at the standard 20% rate: £1 × 1.2. The VAT is £0.20. At the reduced 5% rate it's £1.05.

How much VAT is in £1?

If £1 includes VAT at 20%, the VAT is £0.17 and the price before VAT is £0.83: divide by 1.2 to take VAT off. At 5% the VAT is £0.05.

Why isn't the VAT in a price just 20% of it?

Because the 20% is added to the price before VAT, so it's a sixth (20/120) of the VAT-inclusive total. Taking 20% off £1 would give £0.80, which is £0.03 too little.

Which VAT rate applies?

The standard rate of 20% covers most goods and services; the reduced rate of 5% applies to some, such as home energy and children's car seats; and zero-rated items, such as most food and children's clothes, have none (GOV.UK).
