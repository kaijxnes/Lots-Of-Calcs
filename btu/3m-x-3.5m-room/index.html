Frequently asked questions

How many BTU do I need for a 3m × 3.5m room?

About 4,005 BTU/hr for a living room with a 2.4 m ceiling, one outside wall and double glazing; 3,204 BTU for a bedroom. More outside walls, single glazing or a north-facing room need more.

How many watts of radiator for a 3m × 3.5m room?

About 1,174 W for a living room (1 W = 3.412 BTU/hr). Radiator outputs are usually quoted at a 50°C temperature difference (ΔT50); a heat pump or low flow temperature system needs a bigger radiator for the same room.

How accurate is this?

It's a rule of thumb used for sizing a single radiator. For a whole new heating system or a heat pump, a heating engineer should do a room-by-room heat loss calculation, which allows for insulation, floor and ceiling and air changes.
