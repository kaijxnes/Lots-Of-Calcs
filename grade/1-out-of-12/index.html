1 out of 12 as a percentage

1 out of 12 is 8.3% (rounded) — an F on the common US scale.

Percentage8.3%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)F
Right / wrong1 / 11

Working: 1 ÷ 12 = 0.0833, and × 100 = 8.3%. The same percentage as 5 out of 60.

Grade boundaries vary: many UK and international tests use different ones. Set your own in the grade calculator, which also does weighted grades.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1 out of 12 as a percentage?

8.3% (rounded). Divide 1 by 12 to get 0.0833 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 1 out of 12?

On the common US 10-point scale, 8.3% is an F. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 12 and still get 90%?

1 — you need at least 11 right.
