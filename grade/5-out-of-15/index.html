5 out of 15 as a percentage

5 out of 15 is 33.3% (rounded) — an F on the common US scale.

Percentage33.3%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)F

Working: 5 ÷ 15 = 0.3333, and × 100 = 33.3%. The same percentage as 4 out of 12, 10 out of 30, 20 out of 60.
