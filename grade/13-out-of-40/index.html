Frequently asked questions

What is 13 out of 40 as a percentage?

32.5%. Divide 13 by 40 to get 0.325 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 13 out of 40?

On the common US 10-point scale, 32.5% is an F. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 40 and still get 90%?

4 — you need at least 36 right.
