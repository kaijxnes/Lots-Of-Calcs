2 out of 10 as a percentage

2 out of 10 is 20% — an F on the common US scale.

Percentage20%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)F
Right / wrong2 / 8

Working: 2 ÷ 10 = 0.2, and × 100 = 20%. The same percentage as 3 out of 15, 4 out of 20, 5 out of 25, 6 out of 30, 8 out of 40, 10 out of 50, 12 out of 60, 16 out of 80.

Grade boundaries vary: many UK and international tests use different ones. Set your own in the grade calculator, which also does weighted grades.

Frequently asked questions

What is 2 out of 10 as a percentage?

20%. Divide 2 by 10 to get 0.2 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 2 out of 10?

On the common US 10-point scale, 20% is an F. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 10 and still get 90%?

1 — you need at least 9 right.
