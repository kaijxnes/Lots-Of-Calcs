9 out of 10 as a percentage

9 out of 10 is 90% — an A on the common US scale.

Percentage90%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)A
Right / wrong9 / 1

Working: 9 ÷ 10 = 0.9, and × 100 = 90%. The same percentage as 18 out of 20, 27 out of 30, 36 out of 40, 45 out of 50, 54 out of 60, 72 out of 80.

Grade boundaries vary: many UK and international tests use different ones. Set your own in the grade calculator, which also does weighted grades.

Frequently asked questions

What is 9 out of 10 as a percentage?

90%. Divide 9 by 10 to get 0.9 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 9 out of 10?

On the common US 10-point scale, 90% is an A. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 10 and still get 90%?

1 — you need at least 9 right.
