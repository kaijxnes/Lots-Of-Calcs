20 out of 50 as a percentage

20 out of 50 is 40% — an F on the common US scale.

Percentage40%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)F
Right / wrong20 / 30

Working: 20 ÷ 50 = 0.4, and × 100 = 40%. The same percentage as 4 out of 10, 6 out of 15, 8 out of 20, 10 out of 25, 12 out of 30, 16 out of 40, 24 out of 60, 32 out of 80.

Grade boundaries vary: many UK and international tests use different ones. Set your own in the grade calculator, which also does weighted grades.

Frequently asked questions

What is 20 out of 50 as a percentage?

40%. Divide 20 by 50 to get 0.4 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 20 out of 50?

On the common US 10-point scale, 40% is an F. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 50 and still get 90%?

5 — you need at least 45 right.
