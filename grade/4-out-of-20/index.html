4 out of 20 as a percentage

4 out of 20 is 20% — an F on the common US scale.

Percentage20%

Working: 4 ÷ 20 = 0.2, and × 100 = 20%. The same percentage as 2 out of 10, 3 out of 15, 5 out of 25, 6 out of 30, 8 out of 40, 10 out of 50, 12 out of 60, 16 out of 80.

Frequently asked questions

What is 4 out of 20 as a percentage?

20%. Divide 4 by 20 to get 0.2 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 4 out of 20?

On the common US 10-point scale, 20% is an F. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 20 and still get 90%?

2 — you need at least 18 right.
