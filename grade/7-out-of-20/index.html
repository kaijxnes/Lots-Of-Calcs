7 out of 20 as a percentage

7 out of 20 is 35% — an F on the common US scale.

Percentage35%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)F

Working: 7 ÷ 20 = 0.35, and × 100 = 35%. The same percentage as 14 out of 40, 21 out of 60, 28 out of 80.

Frequently asked questions

What is 7 out of 20 as a percentage?

35%. Divide 7 by 20 to get 0.35 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 7 out of 20?

On the common US 10-point scale, 35% is an F. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 20 and still get 90%?

2 — you need at least 18 right.
