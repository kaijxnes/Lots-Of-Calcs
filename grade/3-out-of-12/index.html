3 out of 12 as a percentage

3 out of 12 is 25% — an F on the common US scale.

Percentage25%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)F
Right / wrong3 / 9

Working: 3 ÷ 12 = 0.25, and × 100 = 25%. The same percentage as 5 out of 20, 10 out of 40, 15 out of 60, 20 out of 80.

Grade boundaries vary: many UK and international tests use different ones. Set your own in the grade calculator, which also does weighted grades.

Frequently asked questions

What is 3 out of 12 as a percentage?

25%. Divide 3 by 12 to get 0.25 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 3 out of 12?

On the common US 10-point scale, 25% is an F. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 12 and still get 90%?

1 — you need at least 11 right.
