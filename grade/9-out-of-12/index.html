9 out of 12 as a percentage

9 out of 12 is 75% — a C on the common US scale.

Percentage75%
Letter grade (US 10-point scale)C
Right / wrong9 / 3

Working: 9 ÷ 12 = 0.75, and × 100 = 75%. The same percentage as 15 out of 20, 30 out of 40, 45 out of 60, 60 out of 80.

Frequently asked questions

What is 9 out of 12 as a percentage?

75%. Divide 9 by 12 to get 0.75 and multiply by 100.

What grade is 9 out of 12?

On the common US 10-point scale, 75% is a C. Schools and countries use different boundaries, so check your own; the grade calculator lets you set them.

How many can I get wrong out of 12 and still get 90%?

1 — you need at least 11 right.
