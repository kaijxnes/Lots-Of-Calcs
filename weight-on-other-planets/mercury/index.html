Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Mercury?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.378. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 26.5 kg on Mercury; 150 lb becomes 56.7 lb.

How old would I be on Mercury?

A year on Mercury lasts 88 Earth days, so divide your age by 0.241 — you'd be older there. At 30 you would be 124 Mercury years old.

What is the gravity on Mercury?

About 0.378 times Earth's, or roughly 3.71 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s².

Would my mass change on Mercury?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.

Why is it lighter on Mercury?

Almost identical to Mars despite being a good deal smaller — Mercury is far denser, with an outsized iron core taking up most of its volume.
