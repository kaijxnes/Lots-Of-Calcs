Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on the Moon?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.166. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 11.6 kg on the Moon; 150 lb becomes 24.9 lb.

What is the gravity on the Moon?

About 0.166 times Earth's, or roughly 1.63 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s².

Would my mass change on the Moon?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.

Why is it lighter on the Moon?

Why the Apollo astronauts bounced rather than walked. Their suits weighed more on Earth than the astronauts did on the Moon.
