Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Titan?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.138. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 9.7 kg on Titan; 150 lb becomes 20.7 lb.

What is the gravity on Titan?

About 0.138 times Earth's, or roughly 1.35 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s².

Would my mass change on Titan?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.

Why is it lighter on Titan?
