Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Pluto?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.063. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 4.4 kg on Pluto; 150 lb becomes 9.5 lb.

How old would I be on Pluto?

A year on Pluto lasts 90,560 Earth days, so divide your age by 247.97. At 30 you would be 0.1 Pluto years old.

What is the gravity on Pluto?

About 0.063 times Earth's, or roughly 0.62 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s².

Would my mass change on Pluto?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.
