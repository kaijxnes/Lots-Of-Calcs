Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Venus?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.907. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 63.5 kg on Venus; 150 lb becomes 136.1 lb.

How old would I be on Venus?

A year on Venus lasts 224.7 Earth days, so divide your age by 0.615 — you'd be older there. At 30 you would be 48 Venus years old.

What is the gravity on Venus?

About 0.907 times Earth's, or roughly 8.89 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s².

Would my mass change on Venus?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.
