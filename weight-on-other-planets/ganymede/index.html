Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Ganymede?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.146. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 10.2 kg on Ganymede; 150 lb becomes 21.9 lb.

What is the gravity on Ganymede?

About 0.146 times Earth's, or roughly 1.43 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s².

Would my mass change on Ganymede?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.

Why is it lighter on Ganymede?

Jupiter's largest moon and the biggest in the solar system, wider than Mercury — but made largely of ice, so it pulls far more gently.
