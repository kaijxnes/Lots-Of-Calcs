Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Saturn?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.916. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 64.1 kg on Saturn; 150 lb becomes 137.4 lb.

How old would I be on Saturn?

A year on Saturn lasts 10,747 Earth days, so divide your age by 29.43. At 30 you would be 1 Saturn years old.

What is the gravity on Saturn?

About 0.916 times Earth's, or roughly 8.98 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s², measured at the cloud tops since there is no solid surface.

Would my mass change on Saturn?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.

Why is it lighter on Saturn?

The surprise. Ninety-five times Earth's mass, yet so spread out that you would weigh slightly less at its cloud tops than you do at home.
