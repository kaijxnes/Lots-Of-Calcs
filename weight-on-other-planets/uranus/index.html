Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Uranus?

Multiply your Earth weight by 0.889. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 62.2 kg on Uranus; 150 lb becomes 133.4 lb.

What is the gravity on Uranus?

About 0.889 times Earth's, or roughly 8.72 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s², measured at the cloud tops since there is no solid surface.

Would my mass change on Uranus?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.

Why is it lighter on Uranus?

An ice giant with no solid surface to stand on. This is the pull at the cloud tops.
