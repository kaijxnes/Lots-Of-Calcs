Frequently asked questions

How much would I weigh on Jupiter?

Multiply your Earth weight by 2.36. Someone who weighs 70 kg (11 st) on Earth would weigh 165.2 kg on Jupiter; 150 lb becomes 354 lb.

What is the gravity on Jupiter?

About 2.36 times Earth's, or roughly 23.14 m/s² against Earth's 9.81 m/s², measured at the cloud tops since there is no solid surface.

Would my mass change on Jupiter?

No. Your mass — how much of you there is — is the same everywhere. What changes is weight, the force gravity pulls you down with, which is what scales measure.

Why is it heavier on Jupiter?

A gas giant with no surface at all. At the cloud tops you would weigh well over twice what you do here, and the pressure would finish you long before the weight did.
