Frequently asked questions

How much can I take tax-free from my pension?

Usually up to 25% of each pension pot, up to a lifetime maximum of £268,275. The rest is taxed as income when you take it.

How long will my pension last?

It depends on how much you take out and how the investments grow. Taking out less than the pot grows by makes it last indefinitely; the calculator shows the years for your figures.

Should I buy an annuity instead?

An annuity pays a guaranteed income for life however long you live, but you give up the pot. Drawdown keeps the pot invested and flexible, but can run out. Many people mix the two; free guidance is available from Pension Wise.
