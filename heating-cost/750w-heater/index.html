Frequently asked questions

How much does a 750W heater cost per hour?

19.7p on full power: it uses 0.75 kWh an hour, and electricity costs 26.32p per kWh under the energy price cap (1 October to 31 December 2026). On a thermostat it draws full power only part of the time once the room is warm, so it usually costs less.

How much does it cost to run a 750W heater all day?

£1.58 for 8 hours, £2.37 for 12 hours or £4.74 for a full 24 hours on full power.

Is a 750W electric heater cheaper than central heating?

Per unit of heat, no: electricity costs 26.32p per kWh and gas 7.97p, so even allowing for boiler losses (8.9p per kWh of heat at 90% efficiency) electric heat costs about 3 times as much. But a heater warms one room, while central heating warms the whole house — heating just the room you're in for a few hours can still cost less overall.

Does the type of heater matter?

Not for the cost per hour: every electric heater — fan, convector, oil-filled, halogen or panel — turns 1 kWh of electricity into 1 kWh of heat. What changes is how quickly it warms you and how well it holds the heat; a thermostat and timer make the biggest difference to the bill.
