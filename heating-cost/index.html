Frequently asked questions

Is it cheaper to use an electric heater or the central heating?

Per unit of heat, gas is cheaper: 7.97p per kWh against 26.32p for electricity, or about 8.9p per kWh of heat once a 90%-efficient boiler's losses are counted — electric heat costs about 3 times as much. An electric heater can still work out cheaper if it lets you leave the central heating off and warm only the room you're in.

How much does a 2kW heater cost per hour?

52.6p on full power at 26.32p per kWh. A 1kW heater costs 26.3p an hour and a 3kW heater 79p.

How do I work out what a heater costs?

Multiply the power in kW by the hours it runs and the price per kWh: a 2 kW heater for 3 hours at 26p uses 6 kWh and costs £1.56. The power is on the label or in the manual.

What efficiency is my boiler?

A gas boiler loses some heat up the flue: new gas boilers fitted in England must be at least 92% efficient (ErP, under Boiler Plus), and older ones are often less, so 90% is used here — change it to match yours.
