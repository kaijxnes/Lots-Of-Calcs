Frequently asked questions

If my last period was in January 2026, when is my baby due?

Between 8 October 2026 and 7 November 2026 — in October or November 2026 — depending on the day it started. The table gives the date for each day.

What is the due date if my last period started on 15 January 2026?

Thursday 22 October 2026, 280 days later. Full term (37 weeks) starts on 1 October 2026, and 42 weeks is 5 November 2026.

How is a due date worked out?

By adding 280 days — 40 weeks — to the first day of your last period, assuming a 28-day cycle. Weeks of pregnancy are counted from that day, not from conception.

How accurate is the due date?

It's an estimate. Pregnancy normally lasts from 37 to 42 weeks from the first day of your last period, so your baby may well arrive some days before or after the due date. The dating scan, usually at around 12 weeks, measures the baby and gives a more accurate estimate.

What if my cycle isn't 28 days?

The standard calculation assumes 28 days. The NHS due date calculator lets you adjust the cycle length, and your midwife will use the dating scan to set the date that's used for your care.

An estimate only, not medical advice. Your midwife or GP will confirm your due date, usually at the dating scan. Sources: NHS — Due date calculator, Government of Jersey — Working out your due date.
