Frequently asked questions

Where do these exchange rates come from?

The European Central Bank's euro reference rates, published every working day; these are for 20 November 2026. Cross rates (such as pounds to dollars) are worked out through the euro.

Why is my bank's rate different?

Reference rates are mid-market rates without any margin. Banks, card providers and bureaux de change add a margin or a fee, so compare the amount you'll actually receive.
