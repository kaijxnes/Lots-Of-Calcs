How much Paternity Pay is

Statutory Paternity Pay is £194.32 a week or 90% of your average weekly earnings, whichever is lower (2026/27), for up to 2 weeks of Paternity Leave. On average earnings of £215.91 a week or more you get the full £194.32. You can take the 2 weeks together or as two separate weeks, from the birth until 52 weeks after it, and you get the same leave for twins. To get the pay you must earn an average of at least £129 a week, have worked for your employer for 26 weeks by the 15th week before the due date, still be employed up to the birth, and give the right notice. Paternity Leave itself is a right from your first day as an employee.

Shared Parental Leave and Pay

You can share up to 50 weeks of leave and up to 37 weeks of pay between you, in the first year after the birth or adoption, by your partner ending maternity or adoption leave and pay early. Statutory Shared Parental Pay is the same £194.32 a week or 90% of earnings, whichever is lower (unlike maternity pay, there's no 90% of full pay for the first 6 weeks). GOV.UK's example: after 22 weeks of maternity leave and pay, you can share 30 weeks of leave and 17 weeks of pay. The mother must take at least the compulsory 2 weeks after the birth (4 in a factory), and you need to give your employer 8 weeks' notice. The maternity pay calculator covers the mother's side.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Statutory Paternity Pay?

£194.32 a week or 90% of your average weekly earnings, whichever is lower, for up to 2 weeks (2026/27). Two weeks at the full rate is £388.64 before tax.

Who gets Paternity Pay?

You need to earn an average of at least £129 a week, have been employed by the same employer for 26 weeks by the 15th week before the due date, still be employed up to the birth, and give the right notice.

How much is Shared Parental Pay?

The same as Paternity Pay: £194.32 a week or 90% of average weekly earnings, whichever is lower, for up to 37 weeks shared between you.

How much Shared Parental Leave can we get?

Up to 50 weeks of leave and 37 weeks of pay, less whatever maternity or adoption leave and pay has been used. After 22 weeks of maternity leave and pay, that's 30 weeks of leave and 17 of pay.

Is Paternity Pay taxed?

Yes. It's paid like your wages, weekly or monthly, with tax and National Insurance taken off.
