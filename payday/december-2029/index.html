December 2029 has 4 Fridays: Friday 7 December, Friday 14 December, Friday 21 December and Friday 28 December. None of them is a bank holiday. Fortnightly pay comes on every other one, depending on your pay cycle.

Frequently asked questions

When is payday in December 2029?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Monday 31 December 2029. On the last Friday, Friday 28 December 2029; on the 28th, Friday 28 December 2029; the 25th, Monday 24 December 2029; the 20th, Thursday 20 December 2029; the 15th, Friday 14 December 2029 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in December 2029?

Yes for some pay dates: the 25th is paid on Monday 24 December because Tuesday 25 December is a bank holiday and the 15th is paid on Friday 14 December because Saturday 15 December is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.
