Frequently asked questions

When is payday in July 2035?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Tuesday 31 July 2035. On the last Friday, Friday 27 July 2035; on the 28th, Friday 27 July 2035; the 25th, Wednesday 25 July 2035; the 20th, Friday 20 July 2035; the 15th, Friday 13 July 2035 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in July 2035?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 27 July because Saturday 28 July is a weekend and the 15th is paid on Friday 13 July because Sunday 15 July is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?
