Frequently asked questions

When is payday in October 2034?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Tuesday 31 October 2034. On the last Friday, Friday 27 October 2034; on the 28th, Friday 27 October 2034; the 25th, Wednesday 25 October 2034; the 20th, Friday 20 October 2034; the 15th, Friday 13 October 2034 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in October 2034?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 27 October because Saturday 28 October is a weekend and the 15th is paid on Friday 13 October because Sunday 15 October is a weekend.
