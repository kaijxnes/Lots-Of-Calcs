Paid weekly or fortnightly on a Friday

February 2027 has 4 Fridays: Friday 5 February, Friday 12 February, Friday 19 February and Friday 26 February. None of them is a bank holiday. Fortnightly pay comes on every other one, depending on your pay cycle.

Frequently asked questions

When is payday in February 2027?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Friday 26 February 2027. On the last Friday, Friday 26 February 2027; on the 28th, Friday 26 February 2027; the 25th, Thursday 25 February 2027; the 20th, Friday 19 February 2027; the 15th, Monday 15 February 2027 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in February 2027?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 26 February because Sunday 28 February is a weekend and the 20th is paid on Friday 19 February because Saturday 20 February is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.
