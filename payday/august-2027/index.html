Frequently asked questions

When is payday in August 2027?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Tuesday 31 August 2027. On the last Friday, Friday 27 August 2027; on the 28th, Friday 27 August 2027; the 25th, Wednesday 25 August 2027; the 20th, Friday 20 August 2027; the 15th, Friday 13 August 2027 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in August 2027?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 27 August because Saturday 28 August is a weekend and the 15th is paid on Friday 13 August because Sunday 15 August is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.
