Frequently asked questions

When is payday in December 2030?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Tuesday 31 December 2030. On the last Friday, Friday 27 December 2030; on the 28th, Friday 27 December 2030; the 25th, Tuesday 24 December 2030; the 20th, Friday 20 December 2030; the 15th, Friday 13 December 2030 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in December 2030?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 27 December because Saturday 28 December is a weekend, the 25th is paid on Tuesday 24 December because Wednesday 25 December is a bank holiday and the 15th is paid on Friday 13 December because Sunday 15 December is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the next payday after December 2030?

If you're paid on the last working day, Friday 31 January 2031: 31 days after December's.
