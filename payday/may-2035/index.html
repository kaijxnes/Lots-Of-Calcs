Frequently asked questions

When is payday in May 2035?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Thursday 31 May 2035. On the last Friday, Friday 25 May 2035; on the 28th, Friday 25 May 2035; the 25th, Friday 25 May 2035; the 20th, Friday 18 May 2035; the 15th, Tuesday 15 May 2035 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in May 2035?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 25 May because Monday 28 May is a bank holiday and the 20th is paid on Friday 18 May because Sunday 20 May is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?
