Frequently asked questions

When is payday in June 2026?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Tuesday 30 June 2026. On the last Friday, Friday 26 June 2026; on the 28th, Friday 26 June 2026; the 25th, Thursday 25 June 2026; the 20th, Friday 19 June 2026; the 15th, Monday 15 June 2026 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in June 2026?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 26 June because Sunday 28 June is a weekend and the 20th is paid on Friday 19 June because Saturday 20 June is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the next payday after June 2026?
