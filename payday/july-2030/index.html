Frequently asked questions

When is payday in July 2030?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Wednesday 31 July 2030. On the last Friday, Friday 26 July 2030; on the 28th, Friday 26 July 2030; the 25th, Thursday 25 July 2030; the 20th, Friday 19 July 2030; the 15th, Monday 15 July 2030 (England and Wales).

Is payday early in July 2030?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 26 July because Sunday 28 July is a weekend and the 20th is paid on Friday 19 July because Saturday 20 July is a weekend.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the working day before — but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the next payday after July 2030?
