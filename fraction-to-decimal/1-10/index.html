Long division: 1 ÷ 10

Write 1 as 1.000… and divide by 10, one digit at a time. Each remainder is multiplied by 10 for the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1/10 as a decimal?

0.1. Divide 1 by 10 and the division comes out exactly.

What is 1/10 as a percentage?

10%. Divide 1 by 10 and multiply by 100.

Is 1/10 a recurring decimal?

No. It terminates because 10 = 2 × 5 has no prime factors other than 2 and 5, the factors of 10.
