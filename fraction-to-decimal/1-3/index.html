Frequently asked questions

What is 1/3 as a decimal?

What is 1/3 as a percentage?

About 33.33%. Divide 1 by 3 and multiply by 100.

Is 1/3 a recurring decimal?

Yes. It recurs because 3 has a prime factor other than 2 or 5, so the division never comes out exactly.
