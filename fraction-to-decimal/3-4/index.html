Frequently asked questions

What is 3/4 as a decimal?

0.75. Divide 3 by 4 and the division comes out exactly.

What is 3/4 as a percentage?

75%. Divide 3 by 4 and multiply by 100.

Is 3/4 a recurring decimal?

No. It terminates because 4 = 2 × 2 has no prime factors other than 2 and 5, the factors of 10.
