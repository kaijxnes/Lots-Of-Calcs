Frequently asked questions

What is 1/12 as a decimal?

What is 1/12 as a percentage?

About 8.33%. Divide 1 by 12 and multiply by 100.

Is 1/12 a recurring decimal?

Yes. It recurs because 12 = 2 × 2 × 3 has a prime factor other than 2 or 5, so the division never comes out exactly.
