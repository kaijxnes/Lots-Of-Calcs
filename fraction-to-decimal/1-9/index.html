Frequently asked questions

What is 1/9 as a decimal?

0.1111…, with the 1 repeating forever. Rounded, it's 0.11 to 2 decimal places or 0.111 to 3.

What is 1/9 as a percentage?

About 11.11%. Divide 1 by 9 and multiply by 100.

Is 1/9 a recurring decimal?

Yes. It recurs because 9 = 3 × 3 has a prime factor other than 2 or 5, so the division never comes out exactly.
