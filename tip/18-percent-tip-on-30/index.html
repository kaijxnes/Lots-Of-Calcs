What is a 18% tip on $30?

$5.40: $30 × 18 ÷ 100. With the tip the total is $35.40.

How do I work out a 18% tip in my head?

Should I tip on the amount before or after tax?

Etiquette guides usually say to tip on the pre-tax amount, but many people tip on the total for simplicity — the difference is small. Enter whichever you prefer in the tip calculator.
