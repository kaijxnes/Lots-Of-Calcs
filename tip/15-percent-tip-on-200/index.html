Frequently asked questions

What is a 15% tip on $200?

$30: $200 × 15 ÷ 100. With the tip the total is $230.

How do I work out a 15% tip in my head?

Take 10% ($20), then add half of that ($10): $30.

Should I tip on the amount before or after tax?

Etiquette guides usually say to tip on the pre-tax amount, but many people tip on the total for simplicity — the difference is small. Enter whichever you prefer in the tip calculator.

How much is $230 split between 4 people?

$57.50 each, tip included.
