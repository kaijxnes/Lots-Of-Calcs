The benefits are Pension Credit, Universal Credit and income-related Employment and Support Allowance. The qualifying week is 21 to 27 September 2026.

Who gets it and when

You can get a Winter Fuel Payment for winter 2026 to 2027 if you were born on or before 27 June 1960 and usually live in England, Wales or Northern Ireland. Most people get it automatically and receive a letter in October or November; most are paid in November or December 2026. If you need to claim, the deadline is 31 March 2027. It doesn't affect your other benefits. In Scotland you can't get it, but you might get Pension Age Winter Heating Payment. If you live in a care home, you're not eligible if you get Universal Credit, Pension Credit or income-related ESA and have lived there the whole time from 29 June 2026 or earlier.

If your own total income is over £35,000, HMRC takes the payment back, usually through your tax code, or through your Self Assessment tax bill if you send a return. Your partner's income doesn't count. You can opt out of getting it instead.

Frequently asked questions

Who gets the Winter Fuel Payment?

People born on or before 27 June 1960 who usually live in England, Wales or Northern Ireland, for winter 2026 to 2027. Your circumstances in the qualifying week, 21 to 27 September 2026, set the amount.

How much is the Winter Fuel Payment?

Between £100 and £300: £200 if you live alone and were born between 28 September 1946 and 27 June 1960, or £300 if you were born before 28 September 1946. Less each if you live with someone else who's eligible and neither of you gets Pension Credit, Universal Credit or income-related ESA.

What's the income limit for the Winter Fuel Payment?

If your own total income is over £35,000, HMRC takes the payment back, usually through your tax code. Your partner's income doesn't count towards it.

When is the Winter Fuel Payment paid?

Most eligible people are paid in November or December 2026, after a letter in October or November saying how much they'll get.

Do I need to claim the Winter Fuel Payment?

Usually not: you get it automatically if you get the State Pension or certain other benefits. If you've never had it before, or have deferred your State Pension since your last one, you may need to claim by 31 March 2027.
