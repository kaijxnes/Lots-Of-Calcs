Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between GMT and China time?

China Standard Time is 8 hours ahead of UK time in January and 7 hours ahead of UK time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm GMT in China time?

10pm for most of the year. In January it's 11pm.

What is 9am GMT in China time?

4pm for most of the year.
