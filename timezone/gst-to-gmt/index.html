Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between Dubai time and GMT?

UK time is 4 hours behind Gulf Standard Time in January and 3 hours behind Gulf Standard Time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm Dubai time in GMT?

12pm for most of the year. In January it's 11am.

What is 9am Dubai time in GMT?

6am for most of the year.
