Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between IST and UTC?

UTC is 5½ hours behind India Standard Time. It doesn't change during the year.

What is 3pm IST in UTC?

9:30am for most of the year.

What is 9am IST in UTC?

3:30am for most of the year.
