Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between AEST and GMT?

UK time is 11 hours behind Sydney time in January and 9 hours behind Sydney time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm AEST in GMT?

6am for most of the year. In January it's 4am.

What is 9am AEST in GMT?

12am for most of the year.
