Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between EST and AEST?

Sydney time is 16 hours ahead of US Eastern time in January and 14 hours ahead of US Eastern time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm EST in AEST?

5am (next day) for most of the year. In January it's 7am.

What is 9am EST in AEST?

11pm for most of the year.
