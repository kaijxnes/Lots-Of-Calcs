Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between CET and IST?

India Standard Time is 4½ hours ahead of Central European time in January and 3½ hours ahead of Central European time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm CET in IST?

6:30pm for most of the year. In January it's 7:30pm.

What is 9am CET in IST?

12:30pm for most of the year.
