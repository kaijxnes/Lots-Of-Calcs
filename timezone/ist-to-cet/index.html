Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between IST and CET?

Central European time is 4½ hours behind India Standard Time in January and 3½ hours behind India Standard Time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm IST in CET?

11:30am for most of the year. In January it's 10:30am.

What is 9am IST in CET?

5:30am for most of the year.
