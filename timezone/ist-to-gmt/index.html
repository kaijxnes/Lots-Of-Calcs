Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between IST and GMT?

UK time is 5½ hours behind India Standard Time in January and 4½ hours behind India Standard Time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm IST in GMT?

10:30am for most of the year. In January it's 9:30am.

What is 9am IST in GMT?

4:30am for most of the year.
