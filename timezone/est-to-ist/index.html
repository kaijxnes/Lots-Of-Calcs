Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between EST and IST?

India Standard Time is 10½ hours ahead of US Eastern time in January and 9½ hours ahead of US Eastern time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm EST in IST?

12:30am (next day) for most of the year. In January it's 1:30am.

What is 9am EST in IST?

6:30pm for most of the year.
