Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between AEST and EST?

US Eastern time is 16 hours behind Sydney time in January and 14 hours behind Sydney time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm AEST in EST?

1am for most of the year. In January it's 11pm.

What is 9am AEST in EST?

7pm (previous day) for most of the year.
