Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between PST and EST?

US Eastern time is 3 hours ahead of US Pacific time. It doesn't change during the year.

What is 3pm PST in EST?

6pm for most of the year.

What is 9am PST in EST?

12pm for most of the year.
