Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between UTC and IST?

India Standard Time is 5½ hours ahead of UTC. It doesn't change during the year.

What is 3pm UTC in IST?

8:30pm for most of the year.

What is 9am UTC in IST?

2:30pm for most of the year.
