Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between IST and PST?

US Pacific time is 13½ hours behind India Standard Time in January and 12½ hours behind India Standard Time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm IST in PST?

2:30am for most of the year. In January it's 1:30am.

What is 9am IST in PST?

8:30pm (previous day) for most of the year.
