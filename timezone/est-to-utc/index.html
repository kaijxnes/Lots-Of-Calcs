Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between EST and UTC?

UTC is 5 hours ahead of US Eastern time in January and 4 hours ahead of US Eastern time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm EST in UTC?

7pm for most of the year. In January it's 8pm.

What is 9am EST in UTC?

1pm for most of the year.
