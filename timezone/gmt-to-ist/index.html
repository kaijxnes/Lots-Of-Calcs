Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between GMT and IST?

India Standard Time is 5½ hours ahead of UK time in January and 4½ hours ahead of UK time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm GMT in IST?

7:30pm for most of the year. In January it's 8:30pm.

What is 9am GMT in IST?

1:30pm for most of the year.
