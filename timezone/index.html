How the conversion works

Pick a date and time, the zone it's in and the zone you want. The calculator looks up each zone's offset from UTC for that exact moment — including whether daylight saving is in force there — and converts. It uses the time zone database built into your browser, the same one your phone and computer use to set their clocks.

Worked example: a call at 14:00 in London on 20 March 2026 is 10:00 in New York. On 20 January it would be 09:00 in New York. The gap is usually five hours, but the US moves its clocks forward on 8 March 2026 and the UK not until 29 March, so for three weeks it is only four.

Because the southern hemisphere's summer is the northern winter, London is 11 hours behind Sydney in January but 9 hours behind in July. Some places, including Arizona and Queensland, don't use daylight saving at all.

GMT, BST, UTC — what's the difference?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the reference every time zone is measured from, and never changes for daylight saving. GMT is the UK's winter time and is the same as UTC. BST, British Summer Time, is the UK's summer time, one hour ahead (UTC+1), from the last Sunday in March to the last Sunday in October.

Frequently asked questions

Does this handle daylight saving time?

Yes. The conversion uses your browser's time zone data, so daylight saving is applied automatically for the specific date you enter — which matters, since the offset between two zones changes across the year.

Why is the time difference between two cities not always the same?

Because countries start and end daylight saving on different dates. For a few weeks each spring and autumn the usual gap between two zones shifts by an hour.

What time is it in New York when it's 9am in London?

Usually 4am — New York is five hours behind London for most of the year. For a few weeks in March and late October to early November, when only one of the two has changed its clocks, it is four hours behind, so 5am.

Is the UK on GMT or BST now?

The UK is on BST (UTC+1) from the last Sunday in March to the last Sunday in October, and on GMT (UTC+0) the rest of the year. Press “Use current time” and the calculator converts from the time on your device.

What is the best time for a meeting across time zones?

Convert a few candidate times and look for one inside working hours in both places. London and New York overlap best between about 13:00 and 17:00 UK time; London and Sydney have almost no overlap, so one side usually takes an early morning or evening call.

Why does a time zone show something like GMT+5:30?

Not every zone is a whole number of hours from UTC. India is 5 hours 30 minutes ahead, Nepal 5 hours 45 minutes, and parts of Australia half an hour off their neighbours. The calculator handles these offsets exactly.
