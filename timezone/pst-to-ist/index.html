Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between PST and IST?

India Standard Time is 13½ hours ahead of US Pacific time in January and 12½ hours ahead of US Pacific time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm PST in IST?

3:30am (next day) for most of the year. In January it's 4:30am.

What is 9am PST in IST?

9:30pm for most of the year.
