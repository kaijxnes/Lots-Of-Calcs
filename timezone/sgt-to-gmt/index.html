Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between Singapore time and GMT?

UK time is 8 hours behind Singapore time in January and 7 hours behind Singapore time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm Singapore time in GMT?

8am for most of the year. In January it's 7am.

What is 9am Singapore time in GMT?

2am for most of the year.
