Frequently asked questions

What is the time difference between JST and GMT?

UK time is 9 hours behind Japan Standard Time in January and 8 hours behind Japan Standard Time in July. The table of dates shows exactly when it changes.

What is 3pm JST in GMT?

7am for most of the year. In January it's 6am.

What is 9am JST in GMT?

1am for most of the year.
