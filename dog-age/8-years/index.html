Frequently asked questions

How old is a 8-year-old dog in human years?

Between about 48 and 66, depending on size: a small dog ages more slowly than a giant breed. A medium-sized dog of 8 is about 54 in human terms — mature — while a giant breed is about 66.

Is one dog year really seven human years?

No — that's an old rule of thumb, which would make a 8-year-old dog 56. Dogs grow up much faster than people in their first two years (about 15 human years in the first, 9 more in the second), then age at a steadier rate that depends on size.

What does the DNA-based estimate say?

A 2020 study at UC San Diego compared changes in DNA (methylation) in dogs and humans and came up with human age ≈ 16 × ln(dog age) + 31. For 8 years that gives about 64. It ages young dogs very quickly and older dogs slowly, and was worked out on Labradors, so treat it as a second opinion.
