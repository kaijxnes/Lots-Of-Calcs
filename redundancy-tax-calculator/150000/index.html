Frequently asked questions

How much tax will I pay on £150,000 redundancy pay?

£30,000 is tax-free and £120,000 is taxed. On a £35,000 salary, leaving in September with no more income that tax year, that's about £47,092.00 of Income Tax (2026/27), so you keep £102,908.00.

How much tax is taken off £150,000 redundancy pay?

Paid with your final salary, PAYE takes about £49,905.25. If it's paid after your P45, your employer uses an 0T code on a month 1 basis and about £52,850.25 comes off. If you think you've paid too much tax, contact HMRC.

Do I pay National Insurance on £150,000 redundancy pay?

No: there's no employee National Insurance on redundancy or severance pay, even over £30,000. Your employer pays Class 1A National Insurance on the part over £30,000.

Is £150,000 redundancy pay taxed more in Scotland?

It's taxed at Scottish rates: about £52,688.02 on a £35,000 salary, leaving in September (2026/27).
