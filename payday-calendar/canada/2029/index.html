Bold dates are earlier than usual because the normal day is a weekend or bank holiday. Bank holidays are the days Payments Canada closes the systems that settle direct deposits; one on a weekend closes them the next weekday.

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the business day before. Provincial holidays such as Family Day and the August Civic Holiday don't close the payment system, so deposits usually arrive as normal; your employer's pay policy has the final say. Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2029 if I'm paid on the last business day?

January — 31 January; February — 28 February; March — 29 March; April — 30 April; May — 31 May; June — 29 June; July — 31 July; August — 31 August; September — 28 September; October — 31 October; November — 30 November; December — 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the business day before. Provincial holidays such as Family Day and the August Civic Holiday don't close the payment system, so deposits usually arrive as normal; your employer's pay policy has the final say.

When is the 25th paid early in 2029?

February (23 February), March (23 March), August (24 August), November (23 November) and December (24 December): the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday in those months.

When are semi-monthly paydays in 2029?

On the 15th and the last business day of each month, moved earlier for weekends and bank holidays. The 15th is paid early in April, July, September and December of 2029.

Which Fridays are bank holidays in 2029?

30 March (Good Friday). A Friday payday then is usually paid on the Thursday.
