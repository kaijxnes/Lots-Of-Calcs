Bold dates are earlier than usual because the normal day is a weekend or public holiday. National public holidays from Employment New Zealand, observed on the Monday (or Tuesday) when they fall on a weekend; Matariki from MBIE. Regional anniversary days are left out.

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before; it's set in your employment agreement. Your region's anniversary day can also bring pay forward.

2030 dates are expected: they follow the usual rules, as the official 2030 list isn't out yet.

Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2030 if I'm paid on the last working day?

January — 31 January; February — 28 February; March — 29 March; April — 30 April; May — 31 May; June — 28 June; July — 31 July; August — 30 August; September — 30 September; October — 31 October; November — 29 November; December — 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before; it's set in your employment agreement. Your region's anniversary day can also bring pay forward.

Which Fridays are public holidays in 2030?

19 April (Good Friday), 21 June (Matariki). A Friday payday then is usually paid on the Thursday.
