Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2026 if I'm paid on the last working day?

January — 30 January; February — 27 February; March — 31 March; April — 30 April; May — 29 May; June — 30 June; July — 31 July; August — 31 August; September — 30 September; October — 30 October; November — 30 November; December — 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before; it's set in your employment agreement. Your region's anniversary day can also bring pay forward.

Which Fridays are public holidays in 2026?

2 January (Day after New Year's Day), 6 February (Waitangi Day), 3 April (Good Friday), 10 July (Matariki), 25 December (Christmas Day). A Friday payday then is usually paid on the Thursday.
