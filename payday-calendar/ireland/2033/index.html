Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2033 if I'm paid on the last working day?

January — 31 January; February — 28 February; March — 31 March; April — 29 April; May — 31 May; June — 30 June; July — 29 July; August — 31 August; September — 30 September; October — 28 October; November — 30 November; December — 30 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before, but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

When is the 25th paid early in 2033?

June (24 June), September (23 September) and December (23 December): the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday in those months.

Which Fridays are public holidays in 2033?

None: no public holiday closes a Friday in 2033.
