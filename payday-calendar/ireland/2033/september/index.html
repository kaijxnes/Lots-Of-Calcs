Frequently asked questions

When is payday in September 2033?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Friday 30 September 2033; on the last Friday, Friday 30 September; on the 28th, Wednesday 28 September; on the 25th, Friday 23 September; on the 20th, Tuesday 20 September; on the 15th, Thursday 15 September (Ireland).

Is payday early in September 2033?

Yes for some pay dates: the 25th is paid on Friday 23 September because 25 September is a Sunday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before, but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

How long is it from September's payday to the next one?
