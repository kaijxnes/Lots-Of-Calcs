Frequently asked questions

When is payday in August 2033?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Wednesday 31 August 2033; on the last Friday, Friday 26 August; on the 28th, Friday 26 August; on the 25th, Thursday 25 August; on the 20th, Friday 19 August; on the 15th, Monday 15 August (Ireland).

Is payday early in August 2033?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 26 August because 28 August is a Sunday and the 20th is paid on Friday 19 August because 20 August is a Saturday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before, but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

How long is it from August's payday to the next one?

30 days, if you're paid on the last working day: Wednesday 31 August to Friday 30 September 2033.
