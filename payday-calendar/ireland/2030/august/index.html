Frequently asked questions

When is payday in August 2030?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Friday 30 August 2030; on the last Friday, Friday 30 August; on the 28th, Wednesday 28 August; on the 25th, Friday 23 August; on the 20th, Tuesday 20 August; on the 15th, Thursday 15 August (Ireland).

Is payday early in August 2030?

Yes for some pay dates: the 25th is paid on Friday 23 August because 25 August is a Sunday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

How long is it from August's payday to the next one?
