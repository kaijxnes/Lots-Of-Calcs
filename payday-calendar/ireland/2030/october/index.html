Frequently asked questions

When is payday in October 2030?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Thursday 31 October 2030; on the last Friday, Friday 25 October; on the 28th, Friday 25 October; on the 25th, Friday 25 October; on the 20th, Friday 18 October; on the 15th, Tuesday 15 October (Ireland).

Is payday early in October 2030?

Yes for some pay dates: the 28th is paid on Friday 25 October because Monday 28 October is October bank holiday and the 20th is paid on Friday 18 October because 20 October is a Sunday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before, but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

How long is it from October's payday to the next one?

29 days, if you're paid on the last working day: Thursday 31 October to Friday 29 November 2030.
