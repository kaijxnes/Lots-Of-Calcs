When is payday in February 2035?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Wednesday 28 February 2035; on the last Friday, Friday 23 February; on the 28th, Wednesday 28 February; on the 25th, Friday 23 February; on the 20th, Tuesday 20 February; on the 15th, Thursday 15 February (Ireland).

Is payday early in February 2035?

Yes for some pay dates: the 25th is paid on Friday 23 February because 25 February is a Sunday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

How long is it from February's payday to the next one?
