Frequently asked questions

When is payday in December 2029?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Monday 31 December 2029; on the last Friday, Friday 28 December; on the 28th, Friday 28 December; on the 25th, Monday 24 December; on the 20th, Thursday 20 December; on the 15th, Friday 14 December (Ireland).

Is payday early in December 2029?

Yes for some pay dates: the 25th is paid on Monday 24 December because Tuesday 25 December is Christmas Day and the 15th is paid on Friday 14 December because 15 December is a Saturday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before, but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

How long is it from December's payday to the next one?

31 days, if you're paid on the last working day: Monday 31 December to Thursday 31 January 2030.
