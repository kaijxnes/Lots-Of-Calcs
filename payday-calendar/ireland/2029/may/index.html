Frequently asked questions

When is payday in May 2029?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Thursday 31 May 2029; on the last Friday, Friday 25 May; on the 28th, Monday 28 May; on the 25th, Friday 25 May; on the 20th, Friday 18 May; on the 15th, Tuesday 15 May (Ireland).

Is payday early in May 2029?

Yes for some pay dates: the 20th is paid on Friday 18 May because 20 May is a Sunday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before, but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

How long is it from May's payday to the next one?

29 days, if you're paid on the last working day: Thursday 31 May to Friday 29 June 2029.
