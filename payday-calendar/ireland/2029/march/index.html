Frequently asked questions

When is payday in March 2029?

If you're paid on the last working day of the month, Friday 30 March 2029; on the last Friday, Friday 30 March; on the 28th, Wednesday 28 March; on the 25th, Friday 23 March; on the 20th, Tuesday 20 March; on the 15th, Thursday 15 March (Ireland).

Is payday early in March 2029?

Yes for some pay dates: the 25th is paid on Friday 23 March because 25 March is a Sunday.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the working day before, but it's set by your contract, so check with your employer. December pay is often earlier still, before Christmas.

How long is it from March's payday to the next one?

31 days, if you're paid on the last working day: Friday 30 March to Monday 30 April 2029.
