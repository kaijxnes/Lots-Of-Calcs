Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2031 if I'm paid on the last business day?

January — 31 January; February — 28 February; March — 31 March; April — 30 April; May — 30 May; June — 30 June; July — 31 July; August — 29 August; September — 30 September; October — 31 October; November — 28 November; December — 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the business day before, but some pay the next one: it's in your employer's pay policy and some states set rules. Direct deposits can't arrive on days the Federal Reserve is closed.

When is the 25th paid early in 2031?

January (24 January), May (23 May), October (24 October) and December (24 December): the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday in those months.

When are semi-monthly paydays in 2031?

On the 15th and the last business day of each month, moved earlier for weekends and bank holidays. The 15th is paid early in February, March, June and November of 2031.

Which Fridays are bank holidays in 2031?

4 July (Independence Day). A Friday payday then is usually paid on the Thursday.
