Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2030 if I'm paid on the last business day?

January — 31 January; February — 28 February; March — 29 March; April — 30 April; May — 31 May; June — 28 June; July — 31 July; August — 30 August; September — 30 September; October — 31 October; November — 29 November; December — 31 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or bank holiday, most employers pay on the business day before, but some pay the next one: it's in your employer's pay policy and some states set rules. Direct deposits can't arrive on days the Federal Reserve is closed.

When is the 25th paid early in 2030?

May (24 May), August (23 August) and December (24 December): the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday in those months.

When are semi-monthly paydays in 2030?

On the 15th and the last business day of each month, moved earlier for weekends and bank holidays. The 15th is paid early in June, September and December of 2030.

Which Fridays are bank holidays in 2030?

None: no bank holiday closes a Friday in 2030.
