Bold dates are earlier than usual because the normal day is a weekend or public holiday. National public holidays observed in every state and territory, from the Reserve Bank of Australia. State holidays (King's Birthday, Labour Day, Melbourne Cup and others) can move pay too.

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the business day before; your award, agreement or contract sets it. Many Australians are paid fortnightly, and a state public holiday can also bring pay forward.

2028 dates are expected: they follow the usual rules, as the official 2028 list isn't out yet.

Frequently asked questions

When is payday in 2028 if I'm paid on the last business day?

January — 31 January; February — 29 February; March — 31 March; April — 28 April; May — 31 May; June — 30 June; July — 31 July; August — 31 August; September — 29 September; October — 31 October; November — 30 November; December — 29 December.

What if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday?

When payday falls on a weekend or public holiday, most employers pay on the business day before; your award, agreement or contract sets it. Many Australians are paid fortnightly, and a state public holiday can also bring pay forward.

When is the 25th paid early in 2028?

March (24 March), April (24 April), June (23 June), November (24 November) and December (22 December): the 25th falls on a weekend or public holiday in those months.

Which Fridays are public holidays in 2028?

14 April (Good Friday). A Friday payday then is usually paid on the Thursday.
