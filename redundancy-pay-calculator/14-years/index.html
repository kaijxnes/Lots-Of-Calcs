Statutory redundancy pay after 14 full years with your employer, by age and weekly pay.

Frequently asked questions

How much redundancy pay do I get for 14 years' service?

Between 11 and 21 weeks' pay, depending on your age, with weekly pay capped at £751: up to £15,771. Aged 45 on £600 a week, it's 16 weeks: £9,600.

Do I qualify for redundancy pay?

You need to be an employee with at least 2 years' continuous service with the employer.

Is redundancy pay taxed?

Statutory and contractual redundancy pay are usually tax-free up to a limit, but pay in lieu of notice and holiday pay are taxed as earnings. HMRC's guidance on termination payments has the details.
