What day of the week was I born?

Enter your date of birth: the calculator gives the day, and the age calculator also shows it with your exact age.

How does it work out the day?

From the calendar itself — it counts the days from a date whose weekday is known, using the Gregorian calendar (the one in use in Britain since 1752).
