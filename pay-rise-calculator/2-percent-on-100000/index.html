Frequently asked questions

How much is a 2% pay rise on £100,000?

£2,000 a year before tax, so your salary goes to £102,000. After tax and National Insurance that's £760.00 a year, or £63.33 a month, in England, Wales and Northern Ireland; £50.83 a month in Scotland.

Why do I only keep 38% of it?

Because a rise is taxed at your highest rates: between £100,000 and £125,140 the personal allowance is withdrawn — £1 for every £2 earned — so each extra pound is taxed at an effective 62%. Student loan repayments take another 9% of the rise if you're above the threshold, and a pension contribution set as a percentage of pay goes up with it.

Which tax year is this?

2026/27, using the same rates as the salary calculators. A rise in April may fall in the next tax year, which can have different thresholds.
