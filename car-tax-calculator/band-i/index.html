Frequently asked questions

How much is band I car tax?

£360 for 12 months from 1 April 2026, or £378 in total paid monthly by Direct Debit. 6 months costs £198 (£189 by Direct Debit).

Which cars are in tax band I?

Cars first registered between 1 March 2001 and 31 March 2017 with CO2 emissions of 176 to 185g/km. The CO2 figure is on the V5C registration certificate.

Does a newer car pay the same?

No. Cars registered from 1 April 2017 pay a first payment based on CO2, then the standard rate of £200 a year, plus £440 a year for 5 years if the list price was over £40,000 (£50,000 for an electric car).

Rates from GOV.UK: Vehicle tax rates: vehicles registered on or after 1 April 2017, Vehicle tax rates: cars registered on or after 1 March 2001, Vehicle tax rates: cars registered before 1 March 2001. Last checked 27 November 2026.
