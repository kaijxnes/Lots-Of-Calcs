How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.65 has 2 digits after the point, so put 65 over 100: 65/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 65 and 100 is 5.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
65 ÷ 5 = 13 and 100 ÷ 5 = 20, so 0.65 = 13/20.
