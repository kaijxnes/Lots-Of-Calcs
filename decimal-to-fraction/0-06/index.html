How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.06 has 2 digits after the point, so put 6 over 100: 6/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 6 and 100 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
6 ÷ 2 = 3 and 100 ÷ 2 = 50, so 0.06 = 3/50.
