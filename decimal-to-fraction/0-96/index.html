How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.96 has 2 digits after the point, so put 96 over 100: 96/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 96 and 100 is 4.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
96 ÷ 4 = 24 and 100 ÷ 4 = 25, so 0.96 = 24/25.
