How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.86 has 2 digits after the point, so put 86 over 100: 86/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 86 and 100 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
86 ÷ 2 = 43 and 100 ÷ 2 = 50, so 0.86 = 43/50.
