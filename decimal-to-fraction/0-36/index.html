How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.36 has 2 digits after the point, so put 36 over 100: 36/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 36 and 100 is 4.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
36 ÷ 4 = 9 and 100 ÷ 4 = 25, so 0.36 = 9/25.
