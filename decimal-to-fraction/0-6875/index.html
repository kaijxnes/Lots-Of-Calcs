How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.6875 has 4 digits after the point, so put 6875 over 10000: 6875/10000.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 6875 and 10000 is 625.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
6875 ÷ 625 = 11 and 10000 ÷ 625 = 16, so 0.6875 = 11/16.
