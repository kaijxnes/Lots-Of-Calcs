How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
1.2 has 1 digit after the point, so put 12 over 10: 12/10.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 12 and 10 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
12 ÷ 2 = 6 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5, so 1.2 = 6/5.
4
As a mixed number
6 ÷ 5 = 1 remainder 1, so 6/5 = 1 1/5.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1.2 as a fraction?

6/5, which is 1 1/5 as a mixed number. 1.2 is 12/10, and dividing both by 2 simplifies it to 6/5.

How do you convert 1.2 to a fraction?

Put the digits after the point over 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits (12/10), then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor (2).
