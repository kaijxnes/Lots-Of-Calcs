0.4 as a fraction

How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.4 has 1 digit after the point, so put 4 over 10: 4/10.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 4 and 10 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
4 ÷ 2 = 2 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5, so 0.4 = 2/5.

Frequently asked questions

What is 0.4 as a fraction?

2/5. 0.4 is 4/10, and dividing both by 2 simplifies it to 2/5.

How do you convert 0.4 to a fraction?

Put the digits after the point over 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits (4/10), then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor (2).
