1.25 as a fraction

How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
1.25 has 2 digits after the point, so put 125 over 100: 125/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 125 and 100 is 25.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
125 ÷ 25 = 5 and 100 ÷ 25 = 4, so 1.25 = 5/4.
4
As a mixed number
5 ÷ 4 = 1 remainder 1, so 5/4 = 1 1/4.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1.25 as a fraction?

5/4, which is 1 1/4 as a mixed number. 1.25 is 125/100, and dividing both by 25 simplifies it to 5/4.

How do you convert 1.25 to a fraction?

Put the digits after the point over 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits (125/100), then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor (25).

What is 1.25 as a percentage?

125%. Multiply the decimal by 100, which moves the point two places to the right.
