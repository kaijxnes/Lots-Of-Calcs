How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.4375 has 4 digits after the point, so put 4375 over 10000: 4375/10000.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 4375 and 10000 is 625.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
4375 ÷ 625 = 7 and 10000 ÷ 625 = 16, so 0.4375 = 7/16.
