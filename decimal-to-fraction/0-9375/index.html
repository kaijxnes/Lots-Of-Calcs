How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.9375 has 4 digits after the point, so put 9375 over 10000: 9375/10000.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 9375 and 10000 is 625.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
9375 ÷ 625 = 15 and 10000 ÷ 625 = 16, so 0.9375 = 15/16.
