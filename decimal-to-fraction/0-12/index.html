0.12 as a fraction

0.12 = 3/25 in its simplest form.

How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.12 has 2 digits after the point, so put 12 over 100: 12/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 12 and 100 is 4.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
12 ÷ 4 = 3 and 100 ÷ 4 = 25, so 0.12 = 3/25.

Frequently asked questions

What is 0.12 as a fraction?

3/25. 0.12 is 12/100, and dividing both by 4 simplifies it to 3/25.

How do you convert 0.12 to a fraction?

Put the digits after the point over 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits (12/100), then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor (4).

What is 0.12 as a percentage?
