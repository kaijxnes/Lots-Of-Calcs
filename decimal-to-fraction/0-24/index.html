How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.24 has 2 digits after the point, so put 24 over 100: 24/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 24 and 100 is 4.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
24 ÷ 4 = 6 and 100 ÷ 4 = 25, so 0.24 = 6/25.
