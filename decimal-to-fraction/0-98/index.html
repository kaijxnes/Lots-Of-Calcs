How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.98 has 2 digits after the point, so put 98 over 100: 98/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 98 and 100 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
98 ÷ 2 = 49 and 100 ÷ 2 = 50, so 0.98 = 49/50.
