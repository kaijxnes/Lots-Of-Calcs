How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.94 has 2 digits after the point, so put 94 over 100: 94/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 94 and 100 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
94 ÷ 2 = 47 and 100 ÷ 2 = 50, so 0.94 = 47/50.
