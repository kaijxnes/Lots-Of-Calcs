How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.625 has 3 digits after the point, so put 625 over 1000: 625/1000.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 625 and 1000 is 125.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
625 ÷ 125 = 5 and 1000 ÷ 125 = 8, so 0.625 = 5/8.
