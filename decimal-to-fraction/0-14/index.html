How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.14 has 2 digits after the point, so put 14 over 100: 14/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 14 and 100 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
14 ÷ 2 = 7 and 100 ÷ 2 = 50, so 0.14 = 7/50.
