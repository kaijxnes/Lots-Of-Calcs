How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.8 has 1 digit after the point, so put 8 over 10: 8/10.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 8 and 10 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
8 ÷ 2 = 4 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5, so 0.8 = 4/5.
