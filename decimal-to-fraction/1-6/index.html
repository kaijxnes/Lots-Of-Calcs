How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
1.6 has 1 digit after the point, so put 16 over 10: 16/10.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 16 and 10 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
16 ÷ 2 = 8 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5, so 1.6 = 8/5.
4
As a mixed number
8 ÷ 5 = 1 remainder 3, so 8/5 = 1 3/5.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1.6 as a fraction?

8/5, which is 1 3/5 as a mixed number. 1.6 is 16/10, and dividing both by 2 simplifies it to 8/5.

How do you convert 1.6 to a fraction?

Put the digits after the point over 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits (16/10), then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor (2).

What is 1.6 as a percentage?

160%. Multiply the decimal by 100, which moves the point two places to the right.
