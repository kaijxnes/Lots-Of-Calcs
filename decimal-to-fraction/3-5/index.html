How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
3.5 has 1 digit after the point, so put 35 over 10: 35/10.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 35 and 10 is 5.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
35 ÷ 5 = 7 and 10 ÷ 5 = 2, so 3.5 = 7/2.
4
As a mixed number
7 ÷ 2 = 3 remainder 1, so 7/2 = 3 1/2.

How do you convert 3.5 to a fraction?

Put the digits after the point over 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits (35/10), then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor (5).
