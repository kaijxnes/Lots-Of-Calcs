How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
1.4 has 1 digit after the point, so put 14 over 10: 14/10.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 14 and 10 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
14 ÷ 2 = 7 and 10 ÷ 2 = 5, so 1.4 = 7/5.
4
As a mixed number
7 ÷ 5 = 1 remainder 2, so 7/5 = 1 2/5.

How do you convert 1.4 to a fraction?
