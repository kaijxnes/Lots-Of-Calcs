How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
2.5 has 1 digit after the point, so put 25 over 10: 25/10.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 25 and 10 is 5.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
25 ÷ 5 = 5 and 10 ÷ 5 = 2, so 2.5 = 5/2.
4
As a mixed number
5 ÷ 2 = 2 remainder 1, so 5/2 = 2 1/2.

Frequently asked questions

What is 2.5 as a fraction?

How do you convert 2.5 to a fraction?

Put the digits after the point over 1 followed by as many zeros as there are digits (25/10), then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor (5).
