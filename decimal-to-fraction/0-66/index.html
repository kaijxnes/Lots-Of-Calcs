How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.66 has 2 digits after the point, so put 66 over 100: 66/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 66 and 100 is 2.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
66 ÷ 2 = 33 and 100 ÷ 2 = 50, so 0.66 = 33/50.
