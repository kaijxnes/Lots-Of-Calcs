How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.0625 has 4 digits after the point, so put 625 over 10000: 625/10000.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 625 and 10000 is 625.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
625 ÷ 625 = 1 and 10000 ÷ 625 = 16, so 0.0625 = 1/16.
