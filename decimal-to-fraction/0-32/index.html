How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
0.32 has 2 digits after the point, so put 32 over 100: 32/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 32 and 100 is 4.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
32 ÷ 4 = 8 and 100 ÷ 4 = 25, so 0.32 = 8/25.
