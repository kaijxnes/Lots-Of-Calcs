How to work it out

1
Write it over a power of ten
3.75 has 2 digits after the point, so put 375 over 100: 375/100.
2
Find the biggest number that divides both
The greatest common divisor of 375 and 100 is 25.
3
Divide top and bottom by it
375 ÷ 25 = 15 and 100 ÷ 25 = 4, so 3.75 = 15/4.
4
As a mixed number
15 ÷ 4 = 3 remainder 3, so 15/4 = 3 3/4.
