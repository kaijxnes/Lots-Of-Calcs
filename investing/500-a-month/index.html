Frequently asked questions

How much will £500 a month be worth in 10 years?

About £77,182 at 5% a year, from £60,000 paid in. At 3% it would be £69,724, and at 7% £85,526. In today's money, with 2% inflation, the 5% figure is worth about £63,316.

How much will £500 a month be worth in 20 years?

About £202,902 at 5% a year from £120,000 paid in, or £136,547 in today's money.

How much will £500 a month be worth in 30 years?

About £407,688 at 5% a year and £584,726 at 7%, from £180,000 paid in. The longer the money is left, the more of the total is growth rather than what you put in.

How long would it take to reach £100,000?

Investing £500 a month, about 13 years at 4%, 13 years at 5%, 12 years at 7%.

What return should I use?

The returns here are examples, not predictions. Cash savings pay whatever interest rate the account offers. Shares have grown faster than cash over long periods in the past, but can fall sharply and stay down for years, so a range of returns is more useful than a single figure. The table shows 2% to 10%.

Does this include fees and tax?

Fees are set to 0% to start with; add yours in the calculator or see the fee table above. In the UK, a stocks and shares ISA or a pension lets investments grow without income or capital gains tax; outside them, tax may be due on gains and income above the yearly allowances. These figures are before any tax.
