Frequently asked questions

How much does the 50p 100 envelope savings challenge save?

£2,525: 50p × 5,050. Fill 100 envelopes, one a day in any order, with 50p to £50 — envelope n gets 50p × n.

How long does it take?

100 days at one envelope a day: starting on 1 January 2027, the last goes in on 10 Apr 2027. Doing two a week instead takes a year — any pace works, as long as every envelope gets filled.

Can I start on a different date?

Yes. Open it in the savings challenge calculator, set your start date and tick off each envelope as you go; your progress is saved in your browser.
