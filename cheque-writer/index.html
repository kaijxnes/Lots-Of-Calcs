Frequently asked questions

How do I write pence on a cheque?

Write the pounds in words, then "and" and the pence: "One thousand two hundred and fifty pounds and fifty pence only". Writing "and 50p" is also accepted. In the box, write £1,250.50.

How do I write cents on a US check?

Write the dollars in words and the cents as a fraction of 100: "One thousand two hundred fifty and 50/100 dollars". With no cents, write "and 00/100".

Why write "only"?

"Only" (or a line drawn through the rest of the space) closes off the words line so nobody can add to the amount. Start writing at the far left for the same reason.

What if the words and figures don't match?

Banks generally go by the amount in words, or may return the cheque. Check that the two agree before you sign it.

How do I write a cheque in India?

Start with "Rupees", write the amount in the Indian system (lakh and crore) and end with "Only": "Rupees Two Lakh Fifty Thousand Only" for ₹2,50,000. In the box write ₹2,50,000/-.
