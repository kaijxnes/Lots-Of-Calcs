Frequently asked questions

How are loan repayments worked out?

A personal loan is repaid in equal monthly instalments: each covers the month's interest on the balance plus some of the loan, so the balance falls to zero at the end of the term. The APR includes the interest and any compulsory fees, so it's the figure to compare loans on.

What are the repayments on a £10,000 loan?

About £308.77 a month at 7% APR over 3 years, £1,115.75 in interest altogether. Over 5 years it's £198.01 a month but more interest overall.

Is a shorter loan better?

It costs more each month but less in total, because you pay interest for fewer months. Pick the shortest term whose monthly payment you can comfortably afford.

What APR will I get?

The rate you're offered depends on your credit record, income and the amount and term; advertised 'representative' APRs only have to be offered to 51% of successful applicants. Use the tables to see how much a higher rate would cost.
