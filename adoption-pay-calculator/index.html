How much adoption pay you get

Statutory Adoption Pay is paid for up to 39 weeks: 90% of your average weekly earnings for the first 6 weeks, then £194.32 or 90% of your average weekly earnings (whichever is lower) for the next 33 weeks. It's paid like your wages, weekly or monthly, with tax and National Insurance taken off. Adoption leave is 52 weeks (26 weeks of Ordinary and 26 weeks of Additional Adoption Leave), so the last 13 weeks are unpaid unless your employer pays more. Only 1 person in a couple can take adoption leave; the other partner could get paternity leave instead.

Who can get it: you must have been continuously employed by your employer for at least 26 weeks by the week you were matched with a child, earn on average at least £129 a week before tax, give at least 28 days' notice and give proof of the adoption or surrogacy. For an overseas adoption, the 26 weeks are counted when you start getting adoption pay; in a surrogacy arrangement, by the 15th week before the baby's due. You can't get it for a private adoption, as a special guardian or kinship carer, or if you adopt a stepchild or family member. If you're not eligible, your employer must give you form SAP1 explaining why.

Frequently asked questions

How much is Statutory Adoption Pay?

90% of your average weekly earnings for the first 6 weeks, then £194.32 or 90% (whichever is lower) for the next 33 weeks (2026/27).

How much adoption pay on £30,000 a year?

About £519.23 a week for the first 6 weeks, then £194.32 a week for 33 weeks: £9,527.94 in total before tax.

How long is adoption leave?

52 weeks, of which up to 39 weeks are paid. Only 1 person in a couple can take adoption leave.

Who can get Statutory Adoption Pay?

Employees with at least 26 weeks' continuous employment by the week they're matched with a child, who earn on average at least £129 a week before tax and give the right notice and proof.

Is adoption pay the same as maternity pay?

The amounts are the same: 90% for 6 weeks, then £194.32 or 90% for 33 weeks. The maternity pay calculator covers birth mothers.
