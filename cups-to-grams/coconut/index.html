Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of desiccated coconut (unsweetened)?

53 g, using King Arthur Baking's weight chart (1 cup = 53 g). A US cup is 236.6 ml.

How many grams is ½ cup of desiccated coconut (unsweetened)?

27 g.

How many cups is 100 g of desiccated coconut (unsweetened)?

1.89 cups (just under 2 cups).

Why do different charts give different weights?

A cup measures volume, so the weight depends on how the cup is filled: flour spooned in and levelled weighs less than flour scooped and packed. These figures are for spooning in and levelling off, King Arthur Baking's method. Weighing ingredients is always more reliable than cups.
