Frequently asked questions

How many grams is a cup of flour?

120 g of all-purpose (plain) flour, spooned in and levelled. Scooped straight from the bag it can be 150 g or more, which is why weighing is more reliable.

How many grams is a cup of sugar?

198 g of granulated sugar.

How many grams is a cup of butter?

226 g — a US stick of butter is ½ cup, 113 g.

Is a US cup the same as a UK or metric cup?

No. A US cup is 236.6 ml; a metric cup (used in Australia and elsewhere) is 250 ml, about 6% more. Old British recipes sometimes used a 284 ml cup. These conversions are for US cups.
